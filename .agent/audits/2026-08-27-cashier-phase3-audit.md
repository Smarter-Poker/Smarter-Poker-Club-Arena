## Verified clean (no change needed)

- RLS write policies on every cashier table: `cashout_requests` carries
  explicit client deny policies; `chip_escrow`, `union_wallets`, `agents` are
  service-only; `chip_requests`, `tournament_tickets`, `chip_transactions`,
  `union_wallet_transactions`, `wallet_transactions`, `notifications`,
  `diamond_transactions` all have RLS enabled with no client write policy
  (default deny). No direct-write bypass of the money RPCs exists.
- `fn_mint_chips_from_diamonds` otherwise: advisory-lock op_id idempotency
  held to commit, union/standalone split, union-minter gate, 100x rate.
- UnionWalletModal is fully compatible with the phase-2 rewrite of
  `fn_union_send_to_member` (return keys preserved; new keys additive).
- `cashout_requests`, `chip_requests`, `chip_escrow` indexing already fits
  their query shapes (including the one-pending-per-player partial unique).

## Left as documented dead weight (service_role-only, uncalled)

`distribute_promo_chips`, `transfer_promo_agent_to_player`,
`transfer_promo_club_to_agent`, `redeem_promo_to_chips`, `get_promo_status`
and the WH `distribute-promo` route are now uncalled by any Club Arena
surface. They are unreachable from browsers (service_role grants) and are left
in place rather than dropped — removing the WH route is World Hub repo work
and the playthrough model they implement is a policy question for Dan (the
current law, 2026-08-24, is that promo to a player wallet is just as good as
cash, with no playthrough). If the playthrough model is ever wanted back, it
needs a deliberate design pass, not these leftovers.
