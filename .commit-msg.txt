feat(ads): reach in people, and a subset that admits it

IMPRESSIONS ARE NOT PEOPLE. fn_ad_stats counted events, which is the right
unit for "how often was this shown" and the wrong one for almost every
question an operator asks - and the two can differ by an order of magnitude
with nothing on the panel saying which you are reading.

The lobby logs one impression per advert per PAGE LOAD, so a player who
reloads thirty times is thirty impressions and one person. Today's numbers:

  spins_jackpot    lobby_strip    65 impressions    5 viewers   13.0 each
  bbj_running      lobby_strip    44                5            8.8
  referral_invite  lobby_strip    35                5            7.0

Read as reach, 65 is a campaign doing well. It is five people, one of them a
test account refreshing the page. A click-through rate computed against 65
says one thing and against the people who saw it says another, and the second
is what decides whether a campaign lives.

viewers and clickers now sit BESIDE the event counts, not instead of them: the
ratio between them is frequency, and a campaign with a high one is either
working hard or nagging. That is a judgement the panel should let somebody
make rather than make for them. The panel reads "65 Views To 5 People".

The migration refuses to apply if any row reports more people than events. If
reach exceeds frequency the DISTINCT is on the wrong column and every ratio
built on it is wrong in a way nobody would catch by eye.

AND THE LAST TWO SILENT CEILINGS. The stats read used to carry .limit(50000)
and was removed earlier today. Two of the same shape remained, further away:
ad_catalog at 200 rows and ad_placement at 1,000. A 201st campaign would stop
being mentioned and the page would look complete. Both reads now ask PostgREST
for an exact count, and the panel says "Showing Part Of The List Only" when
what it shows is a subset.

The limits stay - loading ten thousand rows into an editor helps nobody.
Presenting a partial catalog as the whole one is the part that had to go.
