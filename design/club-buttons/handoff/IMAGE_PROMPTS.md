Successful lessons:

- Describe approved art as hardware and application data as the display.
- Request transparent empty value zones for dynamic overlays.
- Specify symmetry, exact zones, restrained blue crystal accents, and no decorative tails.
- Use realistic complete data while validating alignment.
- Produce distinct family artwork rather than recoloring one generic card.

Failed lessons:

- “Make it premium” alone produces generic CSS-like panels.
- Image-generated text and numbers distort and cannot support live values.
- Unspecified right edges often acquire unwanted protrusions.
- Scaling desktop artwork down without a mobile composition clips content.
