## 0. The honest answer to Dan's directive

**Is the diamond economy on the standard? No, but it is now measurable, which it was not this morning.** At 22:30 UTC on 09-02 the Mint register had 0 rows, the supply snapshot double-counted 619,879, the journal was one-sided and deleted from hourly, 208,000 diamonds had entered supply since 09-01 with no row anywhere, and no trial balance existed. At 01:10 UTC on 09-03: `fn_ca_mint_supply('diamonds')` = `SUM(profiles.diamonds)` = 1,030,092; the hourly trial balance reads difference 0 on every account; every journal row names its class and counterparty; a deleted balance is a recorded burn; the balance cannot go negative by constraint; every earn engine has a budget line and a daily-cap ledger; disputes have a handler. Everything that could refuse a live movement is log-only (`ca_diamond_incidents`) until Dan flips it.

**Was money moved? No.** Eleven migrations, every probe rolled back, supply unchanged at 1,030,092 before and after. No backfill of the 208,000 unjournaled horse grants (the standard forbids moving diamonds by migration; the acknowledged baseline squares the identity without inventing per-user history).

**What still does not meet the standard.** Four areas, graded below: purchased-vs-earned is a lot table without consumption attribution; the 897,095 unclaimed-challenge liability is unbudgeted until Dan rules on horses; transfers are unchanged (Dan's call); the Diamond Arena accounting clone is designed, not built. One operational lesson: six lanes applied DDL within twenty minutes and one chip-side overlay funding deadlocked (18.00 chips, tournament `1068cd04`, `ca_ledger_write_failures` 704). The DDL rule applies to swarms.

**Were the legacy paths removed? No, and most must not be yet.** Ten functions are safe to delete after one repo grep; seven were re-pointed or instrumented tonight; the dead stores (37,241 diamonds across `user_progress`, `bot_profiles`, two club columns, `club_diamond_wallets`) are gated on seven days of measured zero writes (`ca_diamond_dead_store_writes`, opens 2026-09-10).

## 2. The phases

Order is by money at risk, then by what unblocks the next phase. Every item ships as one PR, one migration, rolled-back probes, its own law test; anything that can refuse a live movement ships log-only first and flips on Dan's word. **DDL rule for swarms: at most two lanes applying in any twenty-minute window, never during a running overlay lock or a tournament start wave; the 00:31:49 deadlock is the reason.**

1.1 **Run `scripts/setup-stripe-webhook.js`** against live Stripe (a human with the Stripe key) so `charge.dispute.*` reaches the new handler; confirm with one test event in Stripe's dashboard.
1.2 **The union diamond grant** (`fn_union_send_to_member_zd3core` kind = diamonds, up to 100,000 per call, no debit, no caller on disk): remove the branch or fund it, on Dan's ruling; today it is instrumented (`DR2:union_diamond_grant_unfunded`).

1.4 **DR4 from log to refuse** after 24h with 0 `DR4:credit_without_reference` rows.

### Phase 2 - the liabilities (before the Diamond Arena opens)

2.1 **Daily challenges** (897,095 completed-unclaimed, 11,662 horse rows): Dan's ruling on auto-claim for horses and expiry; then cap at assignment by the `daily_challenges` budget line so the liability can never exceed the budget.
2.2 **Real budget numbers**: replace the eleven 2,500,000 placeholders and the two seeded caps with Dan's figures; referrals and `vip_stipend` inside the caps or on their own line.
2.3 **FIFO lot consumption** in `deduct_diamonds` (purchased lots consumed before promotional balance) so `purchased_liability = SUM(issued - consumed - refunded)` stops overstating; then the breakage aging report (D4).

2.5 **The VIP price oracle**: cost read from a `vip_plans` table in `purchase_vip_with_diamonds_atomic_v3`; v1 and v2 retired; `buy_streak_freeze` reads `feature_pricing`.

## 4. How to read progress

`SELECT * FROM fn_ca_diamond_trial_balance(now() - interval '24 hours')` names the account that drifted; `SELECT rule, severity, count(*) FROM ca_diamond_incidents WHERE resolved_at IS NULL GROUP BY 1, 2` counts the refusals the rules did not make; `SELECT fn_ca_mint_supply('diamonds'), SUM(diamonds) FROM profiles` must agree; `ca_diamond_dead_store_writes` is the delete gate; `ca_diamond_snapshots.unexplained` is the deploy gate. When the first three read zero for seven days and the fourth reads zero writes, Phase 3's deletions are due and Phase 5 may open.
