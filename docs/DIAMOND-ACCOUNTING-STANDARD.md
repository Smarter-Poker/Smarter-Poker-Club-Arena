# DIAMOND ACCOUNTING STANDARD - how diamonds are supposed to be issued, held, spent and retired

**Status: BINDING from 2026-09-03. Author: Claude (Cowork), written from production evidence pulled 2026-09-02 22:30 to 2026-09-03 00:30 UTC.**
**Scope: every diamond movement on the platform - purchase, refund, chargeback, every earn engine, every sink, transfers and gifts, the diamonds-to-chips bridge, account deletion, the Mint, and the accounting the Diamond Arena (one platform club playing cash, MTTs, SNGs, spins and satellites in diamonds) must be built on.**
**Companion documents: `docs/CHIP-ACCOUNTING-STANDARD.md` (the chip standard this one clones), the four audits in `docs/audits/2026-09-02-diamond-economy/`, and `docs/DIAMOND-ACCOUNTING-ROADMAP.md` (the scorecard and phases).**

This document does three things, in order:

1. States the **industry standard** for a purchased, closed-loop virtual currency that is also the play money of a poker room (sourced from ASC 606 / IFRS 15 practice, FinCEN and state closed-loop rulings, the 2025 sweepstakes statutes, Apple 3.1.1 and Google Play Billing, Stripe webhook and dispute practice, and the GLI-19 / NJ DGE principles that carry over unchanged from chips).
2. Shows **exactly what we built**, path by path, with production numbers, and names each root cause.
3. Sets the **standard we are moving to** - the chart of accounts, the lifecycle of every movement, the hard rules, the layers of protection - and the fix lanes that get us there.

Nothing in section 2 is inferred from the repo alone. Function bodies are the live `pg_proc` bodies; the repo mirror is stale for several of them (`fn_purchase_time_banks`, `award_purchase_diamonds`, `fn_union_send_to_member_zd3core`).

Two things make diamonds different from chips and shape everything below. **Diamonds are bought with real money and earned by play**, so the economy is a virtual-currency liability with real-money on-ramps: refunds, chargebacks, breakage, deferred revenue, the promotional-versus-purchased split and anti-farming are part of the standard, not extras. And **diamonds will be the chip of the Diamond Arena**, so everything the chip standard built for tables and tournaments must exist for diamonds, and the design has to decide up front whether a diamond at a table is the same unit as a diamond in the wallet.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The diamond economy is nearly idle (2 journal rows in the last 24 hours, 570 in 30 days across 11 users) and the money that does move mostly moves outside every ledger. **Architectural: there is no issuance door in use and no double entry.** `fn_ca_mint` / `fn_ca_burn` / `ca_mint_ledger` exist and have never written a row; every diamond in existence was issued around them (Stripe, seven earn engines, the signup grant, a union grant that mints up to 100,000 per call, agent adjustments). The journal `diamond_transactions` records one side of every movement (a user and a signed amount, no counterparty), is deleted from hourly through the `app.ledger_maintenance` bypass and cascades away when a profile is deleted, so it cannot be replayed into a balance. **Operational: (1) six stores hold the same number (one canonical, four mirrors, one partial) and the hourly snapshot adds a mirror to the canonical, so the supply gate has been double-counting 619,879 since 17:29 UTC on 09-02; (2) the largest promotional source, the signup 500, is unjournaled: 416 horses seeded since 09-01 put 208,000 diamonds into supply with no row anywhere; (3) 77 profile deletions on 09-02 destroyed 198,525 diamonds and their history with no burn; (4) daily challenges are an unbudgeted, uncapped promise of 897,095 diamonds (87 percent of supply) that nobody has ever claimed, 11,662 of the rows belonging to horses who have no button to press; (5) purchased and earned diamonds are one integer, so no customer liability can be computed and the three lifetime purchases (300 diamonds, 3.00 USD, one of them a Stripe test-mode session) were never journaled.** The primitives underneath are sound: `add_diamonds_to_balance` and `deduct_diamonds` lock the row, refuse negatives, journal in the same transaction and dedupe by a UNIQUE constraint whenever the caller passes a reference. The defects are in the callers that bypass them, the references they omit, and the accounts that do not exist.

The fix is not another detector. It is: **one issuance and retirement door (the Mint) that every grant and every burn passes through, with a house account so promotional issuance is funded and rake is banked rather than burned by omission; a journal with both sides named and an issuance class on every row; a purchased-diamond sub-ledger so the customer liability, refunds and chargebacks are computable; every earn engine budgeted and capped at the constraint; a supply identity that reads the canonical store once; a retirement path for deleted accounts; and, for the Diamond Arena, one platform club whose member wallet is the arena wallet, so the chip machinery (buy-in, cash-out, obligations, settle, escrow, guarantees, rake, BBJ, kill switch, four-eyes, trial balance) is reused rather than rewritten.**

## 1. Industry standard (what "supposed to be done" means)

### 1.1 Principles, with sources

The chip principles S1 to S18 (`docs/CHIP-ACCOUNTING-STANDARD.md` 1.1) apply to diamonds unchanged wherever a diamond sits at a table or in a tournament. The following are the additional principles for a purchased, closed-loop currency. Sources are in `docs/audits/2026-09-02-diamond-economy/lane2-ramps-bridge-and-standard.md` part D with URLs.

### 1.2 The conservation identity

```
ISSUED = purchased_issuance + promotional_issuance
       - retired (burns: bridge, deletion, admin, chargeback clawback)

ISSUED = player_wallets (profiles.diamonds, canonical, one row per player)
       + diamond_debts (receivables from chargebacks, a negative that lives in its own table)
       + [Diamond Arena] arena_wallets + felt + tournament_escrow + diamond_house + diamond_bbj + spin_reserve
```

ISSUED changes only through the Mint. Every other movement is a transfer between two of those accounts and must appear as one journal row with both sides named. If ISSUED moves and the Mint shows no row, an account was written outside the ledger and the trial balance must say which one. The purchased pool and the promotional pool are each tracked on their own sub-ledger so that `purchased_liability = purchased_issuance - purchased_consumed - purchased_refunded` is computable at any moment.

### 2.2 The journal

`diamond_transactions` (1,432 surviving rows; columns `user_id, type, amount, balance_after, description, reference_id, transaction_type, source, metadata`):

- **Idempotency is enforced by constraint, and better than the chip side:** `UNIQUE (reference_id) WHERE NOT NULL` (global) and `UNIQUE (user_id, reference_id) WHERE NOT NULL`. Any writer that passes a reference gets a DB-enforced dedupe. Nine core writers pass NULL and get none. The global index means two users can never share a reference, which every two-sided movement (transfer, PvP stake and refund, a prize list) must design around by suffixing the user.
- **One side only.** No counterparty, no from/to. A transfer is two rows (or two calls); the in-use P2P path (`deduct_diamonds` then `add_diamonds_to_balance`, two transactions from `store/diamond-transfer.js` and `live/gift.js`) shows the expected failure signature: `diamond_gift_sent` 6 rows / -10,155 against `diamond_gift_received` 3 rows / +10,135.
- **Not append-only in practice.** `trg_ca_append_only` refuses UPDATE and DELETE unless `app.ledger_maintenance` is set; measured use of that bypass since 09-01: 2,860 rows / 1,896,161 diamonds deleted (2,718 rows / 635,761 by the certification fleet, hourly and ongoing; 141 rows / 1,259,900 by the 09-02 00:57 test-account retirement through `mgmt-api`). Two `ON DELETE CASCADE` FKs (to `auth.users` and `profiles`) and `pages/api/auth/delete-account.js:213` erase a player's history with the player.
- **Classification is inconsistent.** `type` and `transaction_type` are set differently by different writers; `source` is NULL on 570 of 570 rows in 30 days; there is no issuance class (purchased / promotional / earned / transferred / seeded).
- **Attribution is impossible from the audit table.** `ca_diamond_balance_audit`: 603 rows, all `db_role = postgres`, all `app_name = 'PostgREST 14.5'`, all `journaled = false` (the column is never set by anything). 601 of the 603 changes were cert-fleet accounts with no journal row; the 2 real movements were journaled.

### 2.5 Transfers and gifts

- `send_wallet_diamond_transfer` and `send_stream_gift` (authenticated, SECURITY DEFINER): correct in shape - both rows locked in id order, conditional debit, two journal rows in one transaction, anti-farming checked - and **never called in production** (0 rows ever). Their graceful duplicate pre-check compares the bare reference to suffixed rows and can never match, so a replay surfaces as a unique-violation error, and a NULL client reference becomes a fresh uuid (a retry is a second send).
- The paths in use are two-call: `deduct_diamonds` then `add_diamonds_to_balance` from the API, with a JS-side compensating credit if the second call fails.
- Legacy `transfer_diamonds_deduct` / `transfer_diamonds_credit` (service_role): two independently callable halves, no reference, invisible to the anti-farming trigger (its `transaction_type` is NULL and its source is not on the trigger's list).
- Anti-farming `fn_check_anti_farming_gift_cap`: pair 5,000 / 24h, user 50,000 / 24h, burst 2,000 / 60s, fresh-paid 500 / 24h; **waived entirely** for a hard-coded uuid (KINGFISH), for any unflagged account 120 days old, and for anyone 7 days after a single purchase of any size; no device or IP linking in the DB; no recipient-side aggregation; no earn-side velocity; no reversal window.

### 2.6 Horses (D22)

Horses hold 456,781 of 1,030,092 diamonds (44.3 percent; 1,000 profiles). No diamond writer filters `is_horse` (clean on 10.5 where the DB is the actor: the trivia payout paid horses through the same function a human gets, challenge assignment is identical). Where the browser is the input device, horses get nothing: 0 of 45 `daily_login` rows, 0 easter eggs, 0 share streaks, and above all no way to press Claim on 11,662 completed challenges worth 897,048 diamonds. Under 10.5 the engine is supposed to supply the horse's input device; for diamonds it does not.

### 2.7 Controls

- `trg_guard_profile_privileged_columns` refuses a `diamonds` write outside a service context or an allowlist of six call-stack names (`deduct_diamonds`, `fn_union_send_to_member`, `claim_daily_challenge`, `claim_daily_challenges`, `fn_ca_mint`, `fn_ca_burn`); every other writer passes only because it is SECURITY DEFINER. A stack-string allowlist is a fragile substitute for a grant.

### 3.2 The lifecycle, per movement

**Purchase (Stripe today; Apple / Google when added)**

1. Checkout creates a `diamond_purchases` row `pending` from a DB price table (`diamond_packages`, replacing the API constant), with `checkout_request_id` UNIQUE.
2. Webhook: signature over the raw body; `event.id` claimed under the PK before any side effect (as today); `stripe_checkout_session_id` and `stripe_payment_intent_id` UNIQUE on the purchase row; test-mode sessions refused in live mode.
3. Settle `settle_diamond_card_purchase_atomic`: compare-and-set on status (as today), then **`fn_ca_mint(asset = diamonds, destination = player, op_id = purchase id)`** with issuance class `purchased`, which writes the journal row (both sides: `purchase_clearing -> player`), the mint register row, and the purchase lot.
4. Refund `charge.refunded`: pro-rata reversal through **`fn_ca_burn`** with class `purchased_refund`, chained through any redemption the platform can revoke; the remainder, if the balance is short, is a `diamond_debts` row, never a negative balance.
5. Dispute `charge.dispute.created`: freeze the lot (log-only first), open an evidence task with the deadline; `funds_withdrawn` burns like a refund; `closed` won restores. All journaled with the provider reference.
6. Daily: provider balance report vs `diamond_purchases` and the mint register; every charge, refund, dispute and fee matches one row or is an incident (D11).

**Earn (every engine)**

1. The engine computes the award and calls **one credit function** (`add_diamonds_to_balance`, hardened: reference REQUIRED for every positive amount, issuance class REQUIRED, engine name REQUIRED).
2. The credit function debits the engine's line in `diamond_reward_budgets` (refuses at the cap: log-only first), applies the per-user per-day cap for that engine from a versioned catalog, and writes one journal row `promo_budget:<engine> -> player` under a reference of the shape `<engine>:<user>:<event>` that the UNIQUE index enforces.
3. Catalog edits are versioned: `diamond_reward_catalog_history` written by trigger; the version stamped on every award row.
4. Daily challenges: the completed row IS the obligation. The claim is the same function for humans and horses: **the engine claims for a horse at the moment a human would be shown the button** (D22), or, if Dan rules that challenges expire, they expire for both. Either way the snapshot sum is capped at assignment by the challenge budget line, so the liability can never exceed the budget.
5. Signup grant: one row through the Mint (`fn_ca_mint` destination `player`, class `promotional`, op_id `signup:<user>`) fired by `handle_new_user` on INSERT and on the ON CONFLICT path alike; the horse seeder inserts profiles with `diamonds = 0` and lets the trigger grant.
6. Union diamond grants: either funded (the union holds a diamond balance debited by the send) or removed. A one-sided credit is a mint and belongs to the Mint.

**Spend (every sink)**

1. The sink reads its price from the database (`feature_pricing`, `diamond_packages`, `merchandise_*`, `promo_vault_catalog`; `vip_pricing` rebuilt to the real plans or dropped), never from the caller; a client-supplied cost is compared, not trusted.
2. It calls **one debit function** (`deduct_diamonds`, hardened: reference REQUIRED, consumable/durable class REQUIRED), which consumes purchased lots FIFO before promotional balance, writes one journal row `player -> revenue:<sink>` and refuses below zero.
3. Refunds of a sink credit back through the same function pair with class `refund` and the original reference in metadata.

**Transfer / gift** (only if Dan keeps them: 6.4)

1. `send_wallet_diamond_transfer` / `send_stream_gift` are the only paths; the two-call API route and the legacy pair are retired.
2. Promotional balance only (a purchased lot is never transferable, D6); both rows in one transaction with the counterparty in both; reference per side; a reversal window during which the recipient's credit is `pending` and the sender can recall it; caps that do not vanish at 120 days.

**The bridge (diamonds to chips, owners only)**

`fn_mint_chips_from_diamonds` becomes a thin wrapper: `fn_ca_burn(diamonds, player, op_id)` and `fn_ca_mint(chips, union | club, op_id)` in one transaction, the rate read from `ca_bridge_rate` (one row), both register rows carrying the same op_id. Player conversion stays revoked.

**Account deletion**

`fn_ca_journal_profile_deletion` becomes `fn_ca_retire_profile`: burn the balance through `fn_ca_burn` (class `deletion`, op_id `deletion:<user>`), settle or write off `diamond_debts` by rule, anonymize the journal rows (user_id to a tombstone id, never delete), keep `diamond_purchases` for the provider window; the CASCADE FKs are replaced by `ON DELETE RESTRICT` with the retire function as the only path. `delete-account.js` stops deleting journal rows.

**Diamond Arena (one platform club)**

1. `clubs` gains `asset text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'chips' CHECK (asset IN ('chips','diamonds'))` and one row `asset = 'diamonds'`, `union_id NULL`, `is_platform true`. Tables and tournaments inherit the club's asset; `fn_seat_club_for_user` refuses a cross-asset seat (log-only first).
2. `fn_arena_deposit(user, amount, key)`: `profiles.diamonds -> platform club_members.chip_balance`, one transaction, journaled in `diamond_transactions` (class `arena_deposit`) and `chip_ledger` (asset diamonds). Purchased lots inside their dispute window are not depositable (log-only first; Dan's window). `fn_arena_withdraw` is the mirror.
3. Inside the arena the chip standard 3.2 applies verbatim: `atomic_table_buyin`, hand settle, `atomic_seat_cashout_locked`, `fn_register_for_tournament`, `tournament_obligations` + `fn_settle_tournament_obligation`, `fn_apply_prize_guarantee` drawing from the platform treasury (`diamond_house`), rake to `diamond_house`, BBJ on one `bbj_pools` row for the platform club, spins on the platform's `spin_bonus_pools` row. Freerolls are FREE BUY funded from the house or they pay nothing and alert.
4. Horses: a horse bankroll on the platform treasury funded by `fn_ca_mint(diamonds, destination = house)`, `fn_horse_fund_from_treasury` unchanged; horses deposit like humans.
5. Reporting learns `asset` before the first diamond table opens: `fn_ca_trial_balance(asset)`, `fn_ca_supply_snapshot` excludes the platform club, `ca_diamond_snapshots` includes it, `ca_manual_adjustments.asset`, `ca_payout_freeze` scopes `diamond_tournament_payouts` and `arena_withdrawals`.

### 3.4 Layers of protection (in order of when they fire)

1. **Constraint** - CHECK on balance, budgets, caps; UNIQUE on references, provider ids, op_ids. Fires inside the transaction; cannot be bypassed by a retry, a migration or a second arm.
2. **Single path** - one credit, one debit, one mint, one burn, one deposit, one withdraw; the trigger refuses everything else (log-only first, then refuse on Dan's word).
3. **Assertion at boundaries** - settle asserts the purchase row transitioned; retire asserts the balance reached zero and the register moved by the same amount; the arena deposit asserts both sides moved.
4. **Trial balance** - hourly per account, names the writer.
5. **Detectors** - the snapshot, the mint velocity watch, the trivia economy audit; each gets an owner, an auto-resolve and a 24h SLA; a detector that fires on a condition DR1 to DR16 make impossible is retired.
6. **Kill switch** - `ca_payout_freeze` scope `diamond_issuance`: a trial-balance break on `player_diamonds` or `diamond_house` above Dan's threshold in one hour freezes promotional issuance and arena withdrawals (purchases keep settling; obligations keep accruing) until a human clears it.
