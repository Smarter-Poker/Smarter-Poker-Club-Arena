- (a) "Fund guarantees and promotional entries from authorized diamond house/budgets."

Nobody can finish these until Dan approves numbers: the programme's settings section, citing CLAUDE.md 10.9, reserves future rates, caps, guarantees and prize structures to him ("do not silently copy chip amounts or invent future player entitlements"). ## 2. What Exists Today

### 2.1 The Super guarantee of PR #4958 is not a tournament guarantee

PR #4958 (`d0e04ed4fe`, applied as `20260920073858 diamond_bonus_games_have_one_setting_and_super_guarantees_the_entry`, file `supabase/migrations/20260919220610_...sql`, fixture `tests/sql/diamond-one-setting-super-guarantee.sql`):

- **Product.** Diamond Spins: the bonus games (Plinko, Crash, Donkey Cross, Diamond Mines) that the club Diamond wheel awards. A Super award comes from an Upgrade spin and plays a doubled stake (the fixture: a 100 Diamond spin plays "200 diamonds, 2 chips").
- **The guarantee.** `fn_diamond_bonus_minimum(p_bet, p_boost)` returns `ceil(p_bet*50)/100` for boost 2 (half the doubled stake, which is the spin entry) and `ceil(p_bet*10)/100` otherwise. A Super round returns at least the entry however it ends. The migration keeps the survival law, so every stopping target still carries an expectation of 0.8 of the stake: the minimum is priced inside the game's own 0.80 return, not added on top of it.
- **Funded from.** The hosting club's or union's chip accounts: `fn_diamond_game_pay_chips` pays from the host's promo wallet first and then its bank (`clubs.promo_balance` and `clubs.chip_treasury`, or `union_wallets.promo_wallet` and `chip_balance`) into the player's `club_members.chip_balance`. The fixture asserts the payout on `club_members.chip_balance`.
- **Approved by.** Dan, quoted verbatim in the migration header (2026-09-19): "They must all pay a minimum of 1:1 value even if they lose and don't cash out."
- **Tournaments.** None of it applies. It touches no tournament, no arena custody, no `ca_diamond_house` and no Diamond payout; it is a per-round floor on a chip payout in a member club.

### 2.2 How the chip estate funds a guarantee, a freeroll and a promotional entry

- **Guarantee check at creation.** Trigger `trg_tournaments_guarantee_affordable` (md5 `91f977eaec85de7f6b478d37184e9732`, on INSERT and UPDATE of `tournaments`): the union bank (`union_wallets.chip_balance`) for a union club, otherwise `clubs.chip_treasury`, less `clubs.guarantee_treasury_floor`, less the unfunded guarantees of the club's or union's live events, must cover the new guarantee. With `guarantee_enforcement_enabled` it raises `Club % cannot guarantee % chips: ...` (55000); otherwise it files a critical `financial_alerts` row.
- **Overlay at lock.** Trigger `zz_ca_fund_overlay_on_lock` runs `fn_ca_fund_overlay_on_lock` (md5 `93f3e46a957abb7a42d4a2cfaff42fcb`) when status moves from ANNOUNCED or REGISTERING to RUNNING: shortfall is the guarantee (or, for a satellite, target buy-in plus fee times seats) less `prize_pool`; it debits the union bank, falls back to the club treasury when the union bank is short ("Dan 2026-09-04"), and writes a `chip_ledger` row, category `overlay`, to `prize_liability`. When both are short it files a critical alert and returns without topping up.
- **Overlay at prize-pool close.** The engine calls `fn_apply_prize_guarantee` (md5 `b7e8618d519c4aa6d714ff3e248234d0`) at the sites that close a prize pool (`TournamentManagerBase.ts` line 8548); its core writes `tournament_guarantee_overlays` and one `chip_ledger` overlay leg keyed `tourney:<id>:guarantee_overlay`, and raises `guarantee bank holds % but the advertised overlay requires %` when the bank is short.
- **Freeroll.** A 0-entry MTT is a Free Buy (`fn_is_free_buy_event`: buy-in 0, fee 0, MTT, not spin or SNG). Its prize is the guarantee, funded as above; rebuys and add-ons are priced by Dan's rule of 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-04 (`docs/changelog/2026-09-04-free-buy.md`).
- **Promotional entry.** A staff member issues a tournament ticket with `fn_issue_tournament_ticket_phase2_core_20260831`, which debits the issuer's own `club_members.chip_balance` into escrow (category `ticket_issue`, counterparty `escrow`) before the ticket exists, so the ticket is funded before anyone can redeem it. Live: 631 tickets, of which 630 name a source satellite (575 unredeemed, 55 redeemed) and one, cancelled, does not.
- **Horses.** A chip horse enters with a ticket or its own club wallet; chip cash seats are funded from the club treasury (`fn_horse_fund_from_treasury`, category `horse_funding`).

## 3. The Design That Holds Whatever He Answers

### 3.1 Five rules that keep the supply identity whole

The house and the arena float mean different things to the books, and the design depends on keeping them apart.

- **R1. Who owns what.** Player-owned Diamonds are in `profiles.diamonds` or in an arena holding that `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()` counts. Platform-owned Diamonds are in `ca_diamond_house`. `fn_ca_diamond_trial_balance` attributes every change in the arena float to players (its player row adds the float's change), so nothing platform-owned may be parked in the arena float and nothing player-owned in the house. If an answer names a platform account other than the house (A1, B3, B11, B20), it is either a partition of the house kept by earmarks (R3) or a new house-side row that the identity, the trial balance and the snapshot all learn in the same migration: the last two read only `ca_diamond_house` row 1 today.
- **R2. Crossing the line is a registered pair, in one transaction.** Player to house: the payer's spend journal row (the register retires it from the player) plus a house `mint` row. This is exactly what `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_settle_fee` writes today. House to player: a house `burn` row plus the recipient's credit journal row with issuance class `promotional` (the register issues it to the player). Nothing crosses with one row, and nothing crosses through `ca_diamond_house_ledger`, which the register does not see (section 6, item 4, is what happens otherwise). A house-to-player credit is also filed by the earn ledger, so its journal type must be mapped by `fn_ca_diamond_engine_of` to a named engine of its own (not `other`, and not `trivia_tournaments`, where `tournament_prize` lands today), that engine's `diamond_reward_budgets` lines must be written from Dan's answers before its first credit (for an engine with no earlier line the earn ledger creates one of 2,500,000 by itself), and an engine that pays a tournament prize must have no row in `diamond_engine_daily_caps`, because a per-user cap must never shorten a prize.
- **R3. A promise is an earmark, not a movement.** A guarantee set aside, an unredeemed promotional entry and a jackpot seed are rows in an append-only earmark ledger on the house. An earmark lowers the house's available balance (balance less open earmarks) and moves nothing, so the identity never sees it. When the promise is paid to a specific player, R2 applies to exactly the paid amount and the earmark closes.
- **R4. Per-hand money never touches the house row.** Every house write locks `ca_diamond_house` row 1. Per-hand rake and jackpot drops therefore accrue in append-only player-side rows inside the arena float and cross to their destination by R2 in a periodic sweep: one house write per sweep, not per hand.
- **R5. A new holding is counted in the migration that creates it.** Any new place Diamonds sit is added to `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()` in the same migration, and to the trial balance's fixture split (`v_arena_fix`, which today sums custody and spin days owned by fixture accounts) whenever a fixture account can own a row.

### 3.2 A configured-but-unset value refuses by name

One table owned by Dan, with no defaults anywhere:

- `ca_diamond_economics`: append-only rows of (`name`, `scope`, `value`, `units`, `approved_quote`, `approved_on`, `recorded_by`, `recorded_at`). `name` is from a closed list (one per question in section 1), `scope` is `all` or a stake key such as `bb:2`, `units` is checked against the name, `approved_quote` is Dan's words verbatim and may not be empty. An account-valued answer names an account from a closed list whose storage exists. A row is never updated or deleted; a new answer is a new row, and the current value is the latest row for its name and scope.
- `fn_ca_diamond_economic(p_name, p_scope)`: returns the current value, and when there is none raises `diamond_economics_unset:<name>/<scope>` under one SQLSTATE no Diamond door uses today. It never returns NULL and never falls back to another scope, to a chip value or to a literal.
- Every door that needs a number reads it at the moment it needs it and lets the refusal reach the client as a named reason: the creation door refuses a guarantee with the unset name of A4, the settler refuses a raked hand with the unset name of B5, and so on.
- A feature switch is itself an answer (A10, A12, B4, B14). Switch absent or off: the amount is exactly zero and the six layers keep refusing anything else. Switch on with any required value unset: refuse by name. Never "on with a default".
- The Diamond table rows keep `rake_percent`, `rake_cap_bb` and `bbj_percent` at an explicit 0 whatever Dan answers, so layers 1 and 2 never let the chip schedule reach a Diamond table. The Diamond rake is read from `ca_diamond_economics`, never from `tables.rake_percent`, `RAKE_SPEC`, `ca_rake_tier` or `ca_bbj_policy`.
- Pins for its migration: the table, its append-only trigger, its closed name list, the reader and its SQLSTATE; no seed row except those that quote an answer; a law test that no Diamond money door contains a numeric literal for a name on the list (the shape of the Super guarantee migration's calibration pins).

The two values that run today without a recorded approval (B1, B2) are handled like this: the migration that introduces the table seeds no fee row. Until Dan answers, the creation door keeps its inherited rule, and its law says so in words. When he answers, one migration writes his row, the door reads the reader, and from then an unset fee refuses.

### 3.3 The guarantee top-up (line a)

- **Creation.** `fn_poker_diamond_create_tournament` admits `guarantee` (whole Diamonds) when A1 names an account, and refuses `guaranteedPrize`, the chip builder's key, by name rather than ignoring it. It checks A4 (per event), A5 (exposure: open earmarks plus unfunded guarantees of live events), A9 (authority) and, per A7, either creates an earmark for the whole guarantee on the account named by A1 or only checks the account's available balance. Per A8, a failed check refuses or reports.
- **The chip triggers are fenced in the same migration.** `trg_tournaments_guarantee_affordable` and `fn_ca_fund_overlay_on_lock` raise by name for a Diamond event instead of reading the Diamond Arena's `clubs.chip_treasury` (0 today). Without this, the first Diamond guarantee would be refused with a chip message at insert, and a Diamond satellite would start without its seat overlay after a critical alert.
- **Close.** The overlay is computed once, where the engine already closes a prize pool: `fn_apply_prize_guarantee` gains a Diamond arm, as `fn_credit_and_log` did. Overlay = max(0, guarantee less the prize parts of every entry, rebuy, re-entry and add-on), with bounty parts included or not per A6. Every term is a whole Diamond, so the overlay is whole. It is written as a new ledger kind `overlay` (`prize_part` = amount, no user, no custody) so `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_escrow` reports it in `overlay_in` and the prize bank includes it. Per R3 the Diamonds stay in the account named by A1: the earmark becomes firm and any excess is released.
- **Payment.** `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_pay` drains the player-funded prize parts from custody first, exactly as today, and pays any remainder of a place from the overlay earmark by R2 (house burn plus the winner's `promotional` credit row). The invariant every Diamond door asserts becomes: prize plus bounty plus fee balances = custody plus unpaid overlay.
- **What is left.** Overlay that no whole-Diamond place can use, and the whole overlay of an event cancelled after close, is released back to the account. Nothing moved, so nothing moves back.
- **Pins.** `fn_poker_diamond_create_tournament` (`6d82bede...`, redefined in full, not watched), the ledger's `kind`, `inflow` and `outflow` constraints, `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_escrow` (`850410a4...`), `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_open_shadow` (`15beba29...`, watched), `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_drain` (`abaf068c...`, watched), `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_pay` (`e246c03b...`, watched), `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_charge` (`76d337b6...`, watched) and `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_settle_fee` (`4e947c94...`, watched) for the new invariant; `fn_apply_prize_guarantee` (`b7e8618d...`), `trg_tournaments_guarantee_affordable` (`91f977ea...`) and `fn_ca_fund_overlay_on_lock` (`93f3e46a...`) by asserted substitution; the overlay credit's journal type in `fn_ca_diamond_journal_origin` (`ba8e79df...`, registered as a mint to the player), `fn_ca_diamond_engine_of` (its own engine, no daily cap row) and `fn_diamond_kind_bucket` (`f0a76e45...`, a Title Case bucket, with the diamonds-only law repinned to the new file); `fn_ca_declare_guard_redefinition` for every watched name; final assertions that the identity difference is 0 and `tournaments_enabled` is still false.

### 3.4 Promotional entries and freerolls (line a)

- **Issue.** A promotional entry is an earmark on the account named by A1 (R3), for one holder and one event or any event, with an expiry from A17, checked against A13 to A15 and A9. No Diamond moves at issue.
- **Redeem.** Redemption is the ordinary Diamond registration with the funding source changed: in one transaction the house pays the full entry (prize, bounty and fee parts, identical to a paid entry) into a new custody row for the holder by R2 (house burn plus a `promotional` credit journal row on the holder whose balance_after is unchanged, because the Diamonds land in custody, not the wallet), then the charge door writes the `entry` ledger row carrying that journal row. The ledger's `inflow` constraint is satisfied as it stands, the seat guards (P0810 to P0815) admit the seat against the custody row as they do today, and the fee part returns to the house at settlement through the existing fee door.
- **Refund.** `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_refund` and the cancellation door read the funding source from the ledger row and follow A16: back to the account (the holder's spend row plus a house mint, R2), back to the holder as a new earmark, or to the wallet. Never the wallet by default.
- **Freerolls.** If A10 is yes, the creation door admits a zero buy-in only together with a guarantee, since a freeroll's prize is its guarantee. The entry itself needs its own admission rule: the registration core's Diamond arm runs only when the charge is above zero (`IF v_split.charge > 0 AND v_unit = 100`), the reserve door refuses an amount of 0, the ledger refuses an amount under 1, and the seat guards admit a Diamond tournament seat only against an active entry (the charge door's own comment on P0810 and P0812). Without that rule a Diamond freeroll player would register and then be refused a seat. Rebuys and add-ons use A11.
- **Horses.** Per A18 the Diamond arm of `fn_register_horse_for_tournament` (md5 `84c0354e68fb129b5373bc6024cba334`) calls either the ordinary Diamond registration (the horse's own balance, horse parity) or the promotional door; per A19 and A20 the overlay guard and the freeroll fill gain Diamond arms. None of them may reach `fn_horse_fund_from_treasury`.
- **Pins.** The registration core's Diamond arm (`fn_register_for_tournament_before_atomic_capacity_20260907`, `591867b9...`), the charge, refund and cancel doors, `fn_ca_diamond_journal_origin` (`ba8e79df...`) so the promotional credit registers as `promotion` and the return as `spend`, `fn_ca_diamond_engine_of` with an engine of its own whose budget lines come from A15 (A13 is a count of entries, which the Diamond-denominated `diamond_engine_daily_caps` cannot express, so the promotional door enforces it), `fn_diamond_kind_bucket` (`f0a76e45...`) with a Title Case bucket for the new kinds and the diamonds-only law repinned to the new file, and the identity assertion.

### 3.5 The tournament fee (line b)

It exists, reaches the house and keeps the identity whole. What changes is where its numbers come from.

- **Rates.** After B1 and B2, the creation door and the Diamond arm of the recovery fee read `ca_diamond_economics`. `fn_ca_tournament_fee_ratio` (md5 `858c3f3cda609a0b427f294d1c9edf36`) keeps its chip behaviour; the Diamond purchase path (`fn_ca_process_tournament_chip_purchase_money_v1`, `9ff346ac...`) stops calling it for a Diamond event and refuses by name when the Diamond rebuy rate is unset, so the chip fallback of 10 percent can never price a Diamond rebuy.

- **Pins.** `fn_poker_diamond_create_tournament`, `fn_ca_process_tournament_chip_purchase_money_v1` by asserted substitution, `fn_poker_diamond_tournament_settle_fee` if the destination changes.

### 3.6 BBJ (line b)

- **Drop.** Taken by the engine from the pot like rake (section 5), in whole Diamonds per B15 to B17, and carried to `fn_poker_diamond_settle_cash_hand` as its own amount. The settler moves it from the payers' custody to a jackpot accrual inside the arena float (R4, R5): custody to accrual is arena to arena, so no register row.
- **Pool.** The pool is owed to future winners, so it is player-side: an append-only jackpot ledger whose balance is the sum of its rows, counted by `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()`. A seed from B20 is a house earmark (R3) and pays out by R2 only when a hit uses it.

- **Pins.** The settler, the commit door's Diamond clause, the ledger's append-only trigger, `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()` (`86863a12...`), the fixture split in `fn_ca_diamond_trial_balance` (`f744e044...`), and a law that no Diamond jackpot row is ever written to `bbj_pools` or `bbj_contributions`.

`the-diamond-arena-is-diamonds-only` keeps its six pins and gains lines for the new journal kinds (their own Title Case buckets, never `club_chips`, never "chip"). `the-books-do-not-depend-on-the-arena` keeps its pins unchanged: every new player-side holding is inside `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()`, every platform-side amount is in the house, and no accounting surface reads a new arena object directly. A new law pins the step 0 fences, and a second pins that no Diamond door holds a literal for a name in `ca_diamond_economics`.

## 5. Cash-Game Rake Is Not The Tournament Fee

The two paths differ in three ways, and each changes the design.

- **Where the money sits.** A tournament fee sits in custody as a known part of every entry from the moment it is paid and is drained once, at the terminal. A cash rake does not exist until a pot is won: it is taken out of the pot, so the stacks after the hand sum to less than the stacks before. Today the settler refuses exactly that (`diamond_hand_does_not_conserve`: the deltas must sum to zero).
- **How often.** A fee is one house write per event. Rake is one per raked hand, on every Diamond table at once. Writing it to `ca_diamond_house` per hand would serialize every Diamond cash hand behind row 1 (R4).
- **Who computes it.** The fee is computed by a database door at creation. Cash rake is computed by the engine (`RakeConfig.ts` today, for chips) and arrives at the commit door as `p_rake`; the database checks the envelope agrees with itself but does not recompute it.

The design:

1. **The engine** computes Diamond rake from `ca_diamond_economics` only (B5 to B10), for Diamond tables only, in whole Diamonds, and never from `RAKE_SPEC`, `ca_rake_tier` or the table's rake columns, which stay 0. `HandController` (layer 3) admits a non-zero rake, and a jackpot drop, on a Diamond hand only when it came from the Diamond settings; `assertDiamondAcceptedHand` (layer 4) admits `rake` and `bbj` equal to the Diamond amounts and still refuses insurance. Layers 1 and 2 do not change: the table columns stay 0.
2. **The commit door** (layer 6) keeps refusing a chip `rake` object on a Diamond hand. The Diamond rake travels as `p_rake` into the stack core and on to `fn_poker_diamond_settle_cash_hand`, so it is settled in the same transaction as the stacks, not as a post-commit obligation.
3. **The settler** does not take the engine's number on trust. It recomputes the rake from the facts the hand row carries (pot size, contributions, uncalled returns, whether a flop was dealt, how many were dealt in) and the Diamond schedule, and refuses a disagreement by name. Conservation becomes: the stack deltas sum to minus (rake plus jackpot drop).
4. **Attribution.** The rake is attributed to the pot's contributors by the estate's WEIGHTED_CONTRIBUTED rule, the method the commit door already requires for chip rake, split into whole Diamonds by a deterministic remainder rule the migration states and pins. This moves no Diamond: it only decides whose spend row records each Diamond of rake. The purchased-lot consumption needs no change: the settler already consumes lots for every loss, and every rake Diamond is part of some player's loss. The attribution matters to the books only for the register rows, and to players only if B12 is yes.
5. **Accrual and sweep (R4).** Per hand, the rake moves from the payers' custody rows into an append-only Diamond rake accrual (`table_id`, `hand_number`, payer, amount) counted by `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()`: arena to arena, no register row, keyed by the hand's existing receipt (`poker_diamond_hand_receipts`) so a replay moves nothing twice. A sweep moves the accrued rake to the destination named by B11 by R2, one payer spend row per payer per sweep and one house `mint`, or, if B11 is "retired from supply", the spend rows alone.
6. **Never a chip table.** Diamond rake never writes `rake_records`, `rake_attributions`, `rake_distribution_legs` or `club_wallets`; the step 0 fence makes that a refusal. So VIP points (B13) and rakeback (B12) exist for Diamonds only if Dan says so, and then through Diamond doors.
7. **Pins.** `fn_poker_diamond_settle_cash_hand` (`3aab9170...`, watched), `fn_ca_commit_hand_settlement` (`c5f1e51d...`), `fn_ca_settle_hand_stacks_absolute` (`04e0218b...`) if its routing changes, `fn_poker_diamond_plain_cash_table` unchanged (`94fb4dd7...`, watched), `fn_ca_arena_diamonds()`, the trial balance's fixture split, `fn_ca_diamond_journal_origin` and `fn_diamond_kind_bucket` for the rake kind, and a fixture that plays a raked hand and a replay through the real doors and proves the identity whole before and after.

## 6. What Is Already Wrong Today

Each item was read, not inferred, on 2026-09-21. None has cost anyone anything yet: items 1, 2, 3 and 6 wait behind the two arena switches (no Diamond seat, Diamond tournament or non-NLH Diamond table has ever existed), item 4 waits behind a trivia entry nobody has made since February, and items 5, 7 and 8 are numbers nobody approved rather than Diamonds that moved.

1. **The chip supply meter will count Diamonds as chips.** `fn_ca_supply_snapshot` (md5 `261db1022bd41955c49ea8248390ae92`, the hourly chip meter and an armed kill-switch detector) sums `felt` as every live seat's `stack` on a non-tournament table, and `tourn_liab` as every live tournament's pool (from its escrow row, or from `prize_pool + bounty_pool - bounty_pool_paid + total_rake` when it has none), with no asset filter on either; only the club-level sums and the member wallets exclude platform clubs. A Diamond cash seat's stack and a Diamond event's pools (which the Diamond registration core increments) move no `chip_ledger` row, so they read as unexplained chip supply: a drift incident once an hour's figure passes 100 with 300 over four hours, and at 1,000 in one hour the kill switch's escalation ("critical incident, senior page, alert; a person opens the payout freeze"). The first busy hour of Diamond cash play, or the first well-attended Diamond tournament, would page as a chip leak. Measured: no Diamond seat or Diamond tournament has ever existed; the meter took 24 snapshots in the last 24 hours.
2. **The commit door refuses every Diamond cash hand that is not hold'em.** `fn_ca_commit_hand_settlement` still refuses `p_hand_row->>'game_variant' IS DISTINCT FROM 'nlh'` for a Diamond table. The change that opened nine games on 2026-09-12 (#4411, migration `20260912070000_the_diamond_arena_deals_the_games_the_estate_deals`) reached the creation door, the plain-cash rule, the settler, the TypeScript boundary and `HandController`, but not this door, and `tests/a-diamond-table-has-one-shape.law.test.ts` checks only the TypeScript files for the `'nlh'` literal. A Diamond PLO, short deck, fixed-limit or pineapple table would admit, seat and deal, then refuse every hand at commit with `atomic hand commit refused (diamond_chip_obligation_or_fractional_fact)`. Today all 17 Diamond tables are NLH.
3. **The Diamond creation door drops a guarantee silently.** It refuses `guarantee` but never reads `guaranteedPrize`, the key the estate's own builder sends (`src/services/TournamentService.ts` line 1050), nor `isRebuy`, `isReentry`, `addOnAvailable`, `addOnCost` or `addOnFromStart` (it reads `rebuy`, `reentry`, `addOn` and `addonCost` instead). A config built by that builder would create a Diamond event with no guarantee, no rebuys and no add-on, and report success. No client calls the door today.
4. **A paid trivia tournament entry breaks the Diamond identity.** `enter_trivia_tournament_v2` (md5 `e636c45d5552ce1484e602bbb5d07917`) retires the player's full fee through the journal, credits the 10 percent cut to `ca_diamond_house` and writes a `ca_diamond_house_ledger` row, but writes no house `mint` row to `ca_mint_ledger`. `fn_ca_diamond_register_vs_supply()` would read a difference equal to the cut, which is the assertion every Diamond migration ends on. No trivia entry has been made since 2026-02-12, before the register existed, so it has never fired.
5. **Two Diamond fee rules run without a recorded approval.** The Diamond entry fee (10 percent, 5 percent heads-up) and the rebuy fallback (10 percent when the entry fee is zero) are chip rules copied into Diamond doors on 2026-09-12 and 2026-09-14. No document found records Dan approving them for Diamonds; B1 and B2 ask.
6. **A Diamond satellite or guarantee would reach a chip treasury.** Unreachable today because the creation door refuses both first, but if either were admitted without step 0, `trg_tournaments_guarantee_affordable` would refuse it with a message in chips, and `fn_ca_fund_overlay_on_lock` would read the Diamond Arena's `chip_treasury` (0), file a critical alert and start the event without its overlay.
7. **Three reward budget lines are fiction** (section 2.6), as the health function says. They refuse nobody and are Dan's to set.
8. **The earn ledger writes a budget nobody set.** When a credit lands under an engine with no earlier `diamond_reward_budgets` line, `fn_ca_diamond_earn_ledger` creates the line with `budget_diamonds` 2,500,000 (`COALESCE(<previous period's line>, 2500000)`). Under ruling 21 it refuses nobody, but the health function then judges real issuance against a plan no one approved. Any new Diamond engine (section 3.1, R2) must have its line written from Dan's answer before its first credit.
