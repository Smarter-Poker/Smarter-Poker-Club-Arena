## What a Diamond player can reach

`src/components/arena/diamondArenaRoutes.ts` is the arena's allowlist: under `/clubs/diamond-arena` a player may open the lobby, `tournaments`, `messages`, `members`, `members/:userId` and `members/:userId/statistics`; every other route renders the safe shell. The footer (`DiamondBottomNav`, `DIAMOND_FOOTER_DOORS`) adds two global doors and the wallet: Hand History (`/hand-history?arena=diamond`), Stats (`/stats?club=diamond-arena`) and the Diamond wallet modal. A seated player also reaches the table and its menu. The global header and its menu are shown on every non-table page, the arena included.

### The gap that keeps line 1 open

**No leaderboard reachable in the arena has a Diamond source.** The in-table Leaderboard reads `player_stats` for the table's club. The post-commit projection keeps Diamond hands out of `player_stats` on purpose (its second projection is gated on `NOT COALESCE(v_diamond,false)`: that table has no asset dimension). Read today: 0 `player_stats`, 0 `player_stats_snapshots` and 0 `club_member_daily_stats` rows for the arena club. So the board is scoped, but at a Diamond table it will read empty forever. Closing it is a build: a Diamond branch in `fn_club_leaderboard_period_v2` (or a Diamond leaderboard reader) over the Diamond stat rows (`ca_hand_player_stat` where `asset = 'diamonds'`, which holds every seat, horses included) or the settlement facts (`ca_hand_facts`, human seats only). Which source, and which metrics the board offers in Diamonds, is a product choice; horses count (see line 6).

## Line 2: public and private fields, and opponent card privacy

### Opponent cards: verified

- **At the table (source).** The engine never broadcasts a hole card outside a showdown or an all-in runout: `broadcastCurrentState` (`server/src/engine/ServerTableEngine.ts`, the `players` map) sends `cards` only when the stage is showdown or a runout is being revealed and the seat has neither folded nor been mucked, plus the cards a player chose to show; `getTableState(requestingUserId)`, which serves `GET /state` on a resync, adds only the requester's own cards; `getObserverState` applies the same gate and only when the table allows it. A player's own cards travel through `table_hole_cards`, whose RLS reads only `auth.uid() = user_id` and whose INSERT policy is `WITH CHECK (false)`. Diamond tables run the same engine.
- **After the hand (live data).** `hand_history` rows are readable only by the hand's own players (`players @> [{"userId": auth.uid()}]`); anon has no policy. Its `hole_cards` column is the table's reveal record. Measured: in the last 15 minutes, 12,896 hands, 0 with a hole card for a player who did not show at showdown, 0 with cards in `players[].cards` for such a player, 0 with a `seed`, 0 with cards in `actions`; the same in 5-minute windows 1 and 7 days back (4,271 and 2,512 hands) and in a 0.2 percent page sample across the table (8,756 hands, 27 August to today; 1,244 of them with revealed cards, every one matched by the showdown record).
- **Private card stores.** `ca_hand_facts` (own rows), `hand_discards` (own rows), `hand_private_state` (own rows, 0 rows), `table_hole_cards` (own rows), `horse_hand_reviews` and `rabbit_hunt_offers` (RLS on, no policy: nobody reads them), `hand_state_snapshots` (no grant). None of them is in the Realtime publication.
- **Definer reads the client calls.** `ca_player_class_hands` returns hole cards from the caller's own facts behind `ca_assert_self`; `ca_player_hands` returns the viewer's own cards only (`v_self`); the stats readers refuse another player's figures (42501, proven in both rehearsals). `fn_ca_operator_read_hand` returns every hole card of a hand, but only to a club owner or admin, only with a written reason of at least eight characters, and writes an `audit_trail` row; the arena has no club admin (Phase 2), so for a Diamond hand that is the arena's owner account.

### Private fields: one exposure

`profiles` has one SELECT policy, `profiles_select` (`USING true`, to `authenticated`), and column grants that let any signed-in account read these columns of every account, among others: `diamonds`, `diamond_balance`, `diamond_multiplier`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `full_name`, `birth_year`, `city`, `state`, `country`, `last_seen`, `last_login`, `referred_by`. Email and phone are not granted. No screen shows another player's Diamond balance (`PublicProfilePage` reads none), but any signed-in account can read everyone's through the API. The Diamond wallet stores themselves are private: `diamond_transactions` and `poker_diamond_custody` are own-rows, `diamond_purchase_lots`, `diamond_debts` and `poker_diamond_tournament_ledger` have no browser grant.

This is not a small fix: this repository selects `profiles.diamonds` in eleven places (almost all the viewer's own balance) and 67 `select` calls in World Hub name a Diamond column, so the columns cannot simply be revoked. It needs an owner-only read for the balance, both repositories moved to it, then the revoke.

Also readable by anyone, and noted rather than judged: `table_seats` (a public policy) exposes `horse_id`, so any client can tell a horse's seat; `player_stats` and `player_position_stats` are public, but hold no Diamond rows.

## Line 6: agent panels, union menus, chip metrics and synthetic players

- **Agent panels: none reachable.** Every operator route under the arena renders the safe shell (the allowlist above). `members/:userId` rendered `MemberManagementPage`, the chip club's member screen with its agent and downline panels; for a Diamond player `ca_club_member_detail` returned NULL (self and another player), `ca_club_member_statistics` returned `restricted` and `ca_club_member_downline` returned no rows (read as an ordinary player), so it showed "Member Not Found" and no panel rendered. A typed member URL in the arena now opens the arena's Players page instead (fix 2 below); `members/:userId/statistics` is unchanged, and its reader answers `restricted` in the arena. The one agent panel a Diamond player could reach, the Stats page's Rake-tab downline, is removed in the arena by #5623 (the client half of fix 2). (The roster and member pages themselves are line 3's rebuild.)
- **Union menus: one question.** The arena has no union (`union_id` NULL, enforced by `poker_arena_diamond_identity`) and shows no union surface. The global menu (`HamburgerMenu.tsx`) is the same in every club: in the arena it offers "Create Club", and "Create Union" to an account allowed to create one. Whether the global menu should drop club and union creation while the player is in the Diamond Arena is Dan's question below.
- **Chip metrics: none known after today.** The lobby hides the Bad Beat Jackpot strip in the arena, the table menu says "Top Up", tournament surfaces print Diamonds at the event's unit (#4954 and the 2026-09-21 pass), and the Stats door, which printed chip figures, now reads Diamonds.
- **Synthetic players: none showing; one rule not yet enforced.** The rulings decide horses: "Fixture accounts are not players; horses are" ([DIAMOND-RULINGS](DIAMOND-RULINGS.md), "Two rulings the review made necessary"), and ruling 16 keeps horses at player parity; so a horse is real activity and a certification fixture is not. Production today: 17 Diamond tables, 0 seat rows ever, 0 Diamond tournaments, 0 registrations, so nothing synthetic is on show. The arena's one live figure, ACTIVE (`get_club_players_playing`), counted distinct users in live seats without excluding fixture accounts (`fn_ca_is_fixture_account`); it now applies the same rule as the Players page (fix 1 below).

## Questions for Dan

1. **Which profile fields are public?** Any signed-in account can read every account's Diamond balance, legal name, birth year and city through the API, though no screen shows them. The recommendation is that balances and real-name fields be owner-only; the build is an owner-only balance read, both repositories moved to it, then the column revoke.
2. **Should the global menu drop "Create Club" and "Create Union" inside the Diamond Arena?** They are platform actions, not the arena's, but they are the only club and union controls a player sees while in it.
3. **What does a Diamond leaderboard rank, and from which record?** The in-table board has no Diamond source. The stat rows hold every seat, horses included; the settlement facts hold human seats only.
