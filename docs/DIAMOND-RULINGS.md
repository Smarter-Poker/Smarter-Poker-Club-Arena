## Two rulings the review made necessary

**Fixture accounts are not players; horses are.** `fn_ca_is_fixture_account` (sentinel uuid, active `ca_cert_accounts` row, `.invalid` email) scopes incident severity and the promotional budget. It never matches a horse. `fn_ca_is_cert_account` matches `@horses.smarter.poker` and must never be used to scope a player rule (CLAUDE.md 10.5).

**The certification harness must fund and spend through the money paths.** 151 direct `UPDATE profiles SET diamonds` through PostgREST in five days (500 to 0), all fixture accounts, no journal row, no register row. It is filed at info (`DR6:fixture_harness_unjournaled`, `DR6:fixture_harness_unregistered_movement`) and kept out of the player identity and the deploy gate. The root fix is the harness calling `fn_ca_mint` and `deduct_diamonds`; until then the info feed is the measure of it.
