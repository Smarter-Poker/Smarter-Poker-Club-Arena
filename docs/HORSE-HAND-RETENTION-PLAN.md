## Acceptance contract

- Eight full days must have elapsed from both the original hand and successful settlement completion. A hand settled yesterday remains protected even if dealt earlier.
- Mixed human/horse hands are human history. Human, unknown, malformed, disputed and unresolved originals remain protected; current profile status never substitutes for original classification.
- Age, an atomic row, historical `settled=true`, F06 accepted status or snapshot completion alone do not establish successful postcommit. Accounting, bounty/escrow, reports, F06 permits, movement and other applicable custody protections must hold.
- Financial balances, earnings, settlement identities and replay fences remain intact. Compact derived statistics and accounting identities may outlive erased cards/actions so lifetime summaries and duplicate-payment protection continue to work.
- Every disposal is bounded, atomic, authorized, idempotent and safe under concurrent publication/replay. Preservation of original accepted statistics is part of the same authoritative transition, not a later repair loop.
- Source qualification, protected merge, installation, engine publication, activation, executed cleanup and measured disk results are separate states. None substitutes for the others.
