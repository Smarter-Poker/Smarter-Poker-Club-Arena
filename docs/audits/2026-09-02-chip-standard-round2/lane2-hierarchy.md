**Short answer:** the _shape_ of what we built matches the agent-model industry (union bank, club treasury, agent
float with a credit line, commission as a share of downline rake, weekly union close, rakeback paid down the chain,
send/claim with a clawback window). Five things do not match the standard, and two of them are money defects:
(1) the weekly union close as rewritten on 08-31 debits the union twice and would destroy a week of rake or refuse
forever; (2) cash-game commission is accrued for only ONE agent per hand, so every other agent at the table earns
nothing; (3) hierarchy sends are journaled as two unrelated suspense legs with `table_stack` invented as the
player-side counterparty; (4) issuance happens through five doors and the Mint register (`ca_mint_ledger`) has
never been written; (5) no hierarchy balance column has a `CHECK (>= 0)`, and hierarchy liabilities (708,635 chips of
unsettled commission, 299,026 of pending rakeback) are outside the trial balance.

## PART 1 - INDUSTRY: how the agent-model apps and the regulators account for a hierarchy

### 1.1 What the published material actually says

The app vendors (PokerBros, PPPoker, ClubGG, Pokerrrr 2, Upoker, X-Poker) publish almost nothing about back-office
accounting; the operating model is documented by the agents, rakeback sites and the incident reports. What is
consistent across the sources:

- **The app never holds real money.** "ClubGG never holds those funds ... real-money settlement happens between a
  player and an agent, outside the app" ([Deep Poker](https://deep.poker/learn/clubgg)); "PokerBros doesn't handle
  funds - the club agents do" ([GipsyTeam](https://www.gipsyteam.com/poker/pokerbros-diamond-union)); PPPoker's
  developer "doesn't process cashouts itself"
  ([Professional RakeBack](https://professionalrakeback.com/pppoker-room-pokerclub-fails-effectively-stealing-player-funds)).
  In-app chips are the only ledgered instrument; fiat lives with the agent.
- **Chips are issued to the club owner against a real-money instrument.** On PPPoker "club owners purchased chips
  using Diamonds (PPPoker's in-house currency bought with real money), then sell their chips to players they
  recruit for real funds and then buy them back later at an agreed-upon exchange rate"
  ([Professional RakeBack](https://professionalrakeback.com/pppoker-room-pokerclub-fails-effectively-stealing-player-funds)).
  The club sets the chip value ("one chip = one USD")
  ([PokerNet](https://pokernetai.com/blog/how-to-create-pppoker-club-2026-guide/)).
- **Agents are the cashier.** They "process deposits and withdrawals", "pay rakeback", handle "transfer between
  accounts within one club or different clubs", and "accounting is settled with the club and the agent gets cash to
  pay his players" ([Worldpokerdeals](https://worldpokerdeals.com/blog/pppoker-agent-all-you-need-to-know),
  [Pokeroff FAQ](https://pokeroff.com/en/news/faq-poker-club-app/)). The player "transfers funds to your agent ...
  your agent credits your chip balance inside the club" ([The Poker Agent](https://thepokeragent.com/poker-app-agent/)).
- **Commission is a share of the rake your players generate, and it cascades.** "Every hand played creates rake
  which flows through a distribution chain. Agents receive a percentage of rake generated by their players, then
  retain a portion and return the remainder as rakeback to players ... rakeback percentages between agents differ
  ... it represents where they exist in the chain" ([The Poker Agent](https://thepokeragent.com/poker-app-agent/)).
  Union baselines are published (Diamond Union: 30% standard, agents add 5%
  ([GipsyTeam](https://www.gipsyteam.com/poker/pokerbros-diamond-union)); ClubGG agents quote 30-60%
  ([Deep Poker](https://deep.poker/learn/clubgg))). "Sub-Agents are able to set up their own Sub-Agents"
  ([PPH.Poker](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/167/poker-software/pph-poker-version-1-report-amp-agent-management-app-pppoker-1745599/)).
- **The cycle is weekly.** "Rakeback in club poker applications is usually fixed at a certain percentage, paid weekly
  or monthly depending on the agent's rules"; "Reports are received weekly"; new players cannot withdraw until the
  agent "receives a player activity report from the club" ([Pokeroff FAQ](https://pokeroff.com/en/news/faq-poker-club-app/)).
  Automation vendors sell "instant" union settlement precisely because the weekly manual square-up is the pain point:
  "the agent gets paid their share instantly after every single game ends ... union settlements are completely
  instant ... zero risk of a club stiffing the union on payday"
  ([PokerClubHub review](https://thepokeragent.com/pokerclubhub-review-join/)).
- **Agent P&L is payable/receivable.** Third-party tools exist to produce "Agents Summary showing payable and
  receivable" and "Agent Details for reviewing profitability"
  ([PPH.Poker](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/167/poker-software/pph-poker-version-1-report-amp-agent-management-app-pppoker-1745599/)).
- **The club can take chips back; the agent can too, under rules.** "The club itself can deduct funds from a player's
  account if they have violated the rules"; agents may deduct on exchange-rate changes
  ([Pokeroff FAQ](https://pokeroff.com/en/news/faq-poker-club-app/)). A player switching agents "request[s] a
  withdrawal of your full chip balance ... The transfer should be processed without delay"
  ([Worldpokerdeals](https://worldpokerdeals.com/blog/pokerbros-agent-all-you-need-to-know)).
- **Credit is the failure mode.** The $84k PokerClub.ag collapse: the operator "was redirecting incoming deposits
  directly to the accounts of withdrawing users" - a chip issuer with no reserve
  ([Professional RakeBack](https://professionalrakeback.com/pppoker-room-pokerclub-fails-effectively-stealing-player-funds)).
  Two more union/club vanishings on ClubGG are on 2+2
  ([TMT Union](https://forumserver.twoplustwo.com/28/discussion-poker-sites/clubgg-club-owner-vanishing-2-400-tmt-union-careful-one-time-poker-1846092/)).
- **Unions share the player pool, not the books.** "Clubs in the same Union share a common player pool ... they can
  have different logos, chip values, and rake structures"
  ([AsiaPokerClubs](https://asiapokerclubs.com/blog/clubgg-unions-what-you-need-to-know/)).

### 1.2 What the regulators say that applies to an intermediary ledger

- **NJ 13:69O-1.3** ([LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-13-69O-1-3)): account adjustments
  over $500 need supervisory authorisation BEFORE entry; every adjustment needs documented notification to the
  patron; the operator must hold a segregated account >= cashable balances + funds on game + pending withdrawals;
  no limitation on a patron withdrawing their own funds.
- **NJ 13:69O-1.9** ([LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-13-69O-1-9)): a daily Patron
  Account Adjustments Report (who, when, why, how much) reviewed daily by the operator or intermediary and weekly by
  the licensee; variance reports; non-cashable promotional balances reported separately.
- **GLI-19 v3.0** ([gamingcompliance.io](https://gamingcompliance.io/gli-19-v3-0-what-every-online-casino-game-must-meet-to-pass-certification/)):
  "a wager must not be accepted if it would cause a negative balance"; theoretical-vs-actual comparison with logged
  escalation of deviations.

### 1.3 The principles for a hierarchy ledger (what "industry standard" means for this lane)

| #   | Principle                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Source                                                                         |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| P1  | **One issuer, one register.** Chips enter circulation only when the platform issues them to a club (or union) bank against a real-money instrument (diamonds). Every issuance and retirement is a row in one register; ISSUED = sum of that register.                                                                                        | PPPoker diamonds model; NJ 1.3(k) reserve; standard S17                        |
| P2  | **Every hierarchy account is a real balance that cannot go negative** (union bank, union rake treasury, club treasury, agent float, agent promo float, player wallet). A send that would overdraw is refused, not carried. Credit is a separate, capped, visible line - never a negative balance.                                            | GLI-19 negative balance; NJ 1.3; PokerClub.ag collapse                         |
| P3  | **Double entry with both sides named on one row.** A club-bank send is `club_treasury -> agent_wallet(agent X)`; the journal never invents a counterparty and never parks a real transfer in suspense. Suspense is zero at every trial balance.                                                                                              | S3, S9/R9                                                                      |
| P4  | **Rake is attributed at source.** Each raked hand credits the rake account (club treasury or union rake treasury) and, in the same settlement, books a commission ACCRUAL for every agent in the chain of every contributing player, weighted by that player's contributed share. Super-agent override = rate x (downline slice remaining).  | The Poker Agent chain; PPH.Poker cascades; Dan 2026-08-29 weighted-contributed |
| P5  | **Accruals are liabilities, on the balance sheet.** Unsettled commission and unpaid rakeback are owed money: they appear in the trial balance and in the club's solvency check, and the club bank cannot be drawn below what it owes its agents without a warning.                                                                           | PPH.Poker payable/receivable; NJ 1.9                                           |
| P6  | **One settlement cycle, one payer per obligation.** Weekly close: union rake treasury -> clubs (their share) -> agents (commission) -> players (rakeback), each leg idempotent per (period, payee), each obligation paid by exactly one function under exactly one key. Two payers for one obligation is a double payment waiting to happen. | Pokeroff weekly; S5, R2/R3                                                     |
| P7  | **One rate spec.** The rakeback and commission rate a player or agent is paid is the rate on their contract row (negotiated deal, agent default, then a published ladder), read by one function.                                                                                                                                             | The Poker Agent (rate = position in chain)                                     |
| P8  | **Send / claim / clawback are logged, windowed and visible to the loser.** Agent claim-back of an unspent send inside a short window is standard; taking a player's chips outside that window is a club-authority action that notifies the player and reports daily.                                                                         | Pokeroff (club may deduct); NJ 1.3(d),(g), 1.9(k)                              |
| P9  | **Four eyes and a threshold on manual money.** Issuance, treasury adjustments and any credit outside the automated settlement above a threshold require an approver distinct from the initiator, a reason, and appear on a daily adjustments report.                                                                                         | NJ 1.3(i), 1.9(k); standard R6                                                 |
| P10 | **Promo is not cash until it is earned.** Promotional balances are tracked separately and convert to cashable chips only through the playthrough rule; a promo wallet cannot credit a cash wallet directly.                                                                                                                                  | NJ 1.9 non-cashable report; existing `promo_apply_playthrough` design          |
| P11 | **Union settlement is a receivable/payable ledger, with credit exposure per club, and the union bank never goes negative to cover a club.** A short club is an invoice with a shortfall and a deadline, not a silent overdraft.                                                                                                              | PokerClubHub "stiffing the union"; Pokeroff; NJ 1.3                            |
| P12 | **A player can always get their own chips out** through their agent; a club or union may confiscate only under a rule, with a record the player can see.                                                                                                                                                                                     | NJ 1.3(s); Worldpokerdeals (full balance on switching agents)                  |
| P13 | **Every money door is a registered function with an owner.** Orphan RPCs still granted to `authenticated` are attack surface and audit noise; if it has no caller, it is revoked.                                                                                                                                                            | GLI-19 access controls; `ca_money_rpc_registry`                                |
| P14 | **Horses are players** on every leg above: they earn commission for their agents, are paid rakeback, count in the P&L.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | CLAUDE.md 10.5                                                                 |

## PART 2 - OURS: the route map, from the live bodies

### 2.0 How the hierarchy is journaled (this determines half the findings)

Hierarchy balances live in columns: `union_wallets.{chip_balance, rake_wallet, promo_wallet, ...}`, `clubs.chip_treasury`,
`club_wallets.chip_balance` (rake accumulator), `agents.{agent_wallet_balance, promo_wallet_balance, credit_used}`,
`club_members.{chip_balance, promo_balance}`. The hierarchy RPCs write **`chip_transactions`** (their own receipt
table, with `op_id` in metadata and partial unique indexes per transaction_type) - NOT `chip_ledger`. `chip_ledger`
rows come from **triggers**: `fn_ca_autoledger` on every balance column (clubs, club_wallets, union_wallets, unions,
agents, bbj_pools, spin_bonus_pools, club_members.promo_balance) and `fn_club_members_ledger_writer` on
`club_members.chip_balance`. Both write ONE single-leg row per column change, with:

- category = `current_setting('app.ledger_category')` else **`adjustment`**;
- counterparty = `current_setting('app.ledger_counterparty')` else **`settlement_suspense`** (autoledger) or
  **`table_stack`** (the club_members writer);
- no `idempotency_key`, no `correlation_id`, three levels of exception-swallow into `ca_ledger_write_failures`;
- `app.ledger_autoskip_<table>='1'` suppresses the row entirely.

So a hierarchy function that does not call `fn_ca_declare_ledger` before its writes produces a journal that is
balanced in total but wrong in every detail. **Verified on production** for `agent_wallet_send` at
2026-09-01 14:23:18 (10,000.00 to a player): the journal holds `agent_wallet -> settlement_suspense` (adjustment)
and `table_stack -> player_wallet` (adjustment) - two unrelated rows, no key, no correlation, and a phantom 10,000
leaving the felt. Of the 21 money-moving functions in this lane, **6 declare** their ledger shape
(`fn_agent_claim_commission`, `fn_union_send_to_member`, `fn_union_settle_player_pnl`, `fn_union_weekly_rakeback_close`,
`fn_ca_mint`/`fn_mint_club_chips`, `fn_ca_fund_overlay_on_lock`, `increment_union_wallet`, `fn_payout_leaderboard`
club branch) and **the rest do not** (club bank send/claim/reverse, agent wallet send/claim/self-stake, admin removal,
wallet/cashier claim-back, promo wallet send, union clawback, union send-to-club, `fn_mint_chips_from_diamonds`,
round 2, round 3, `fn_close_settlement_period`'s treasury leg, `fn_payout_leaderboard` union branch).

### 3.2 Do today's chip-std changes touch or conflict with the hierarchy routes?

Checked against the live catalogue:

- **`tournament_obligations` + `fn_settle_tournament_obligation`**: the body references none of `clubs.chip_treasury`,
  `union_wallets`, `agents.*` or `agent_commissions`. It pays players from tournament escrow. No overlap with C1-C8.
  One adjacency: `fn_settle_tournament_rake` (existing, not today's) is what feeds U1 and the tournament half of C4;
  if lane A's completion path calls it in the same transaction as the obligation settle, the 57 attribution deadlocks
  above are the thing to watch (F14).
- **R3 logger (`fn_ca_money_path_log` on `wallet_transactions`)**: fires only for credits whose category is
  `prize`, `bounty`, `refund`, `tournament_prize`, `tournament_refund` or `tourney%`. Hierarchy credits use
  `commission`, `rakeback`, `transfer`, `leaderboard_payout` - not logged, no conflict. (If R3 is later hardened to
  a refusal, C5a/C5b/C6a/C6b must be added to the allow-list explicitly or they will be refused.)
- **Escrow shadow (`tournament_escrow_shadow`, `ca_escrow_shadow_runs/results`)**: read-only; no hierarchy account.
- **Trial balance (`fn_ca_trial_balance`)**: covers `club_treasuries`, `union_banks` (`union_bank`+`union_wallet`+

  `commission_payable` / `rakeback_payable` (F8), and its `total_supply` is the sum of accounts, not the Mint (F4).
  It correctly showed the hierarchy accounts exact for 23h except `union_banks` +42.50 - which is the same
  `ledger_autoskip` back-pay path the standard already names.
- **Satellite pool transfer (`fn_award_satellite_seat`)**: touches no hierarchy account.

No conflict. Two dependencies: F1 must land before the first real close (week of 09-07, executed on or after 09-14),
and F3's category additions must be coordinated with whoever owns `chip_ledger_category_check` today.

### 3.3 What matches the industry and should be kept as-is

- The agent float + capped credit line with "square up or prepay" (C3a) is exactly the PokerBros/PPPoker agent model
  and better than most: the float cannot go below zero and the debt is a visible column.
- The 10-minute agent claim-back and 7-day club-bank reversal windows (C2b, C3b) are the send/claim controls the apps
  advertise, with proportional credit repayment that the apps do not have.
- The weekly union P&L clearing (U5) with invoices, pro-rata payment when the bank is short and no overdraft is the
  right P11 shape; the state machine and rollback make it restart-safe.
- Union -> member sends (U4) and the Mint proper (M1) are the model the rest of the lane should copy: declared
  ledger, op-claim idempotency, one clean journal row.
- Rakeback and commission rates are contract rows with a cap at upline minus ten points (`fn_player_rakeback_rate`),
  which is the "rate reflects your position in the chain" rule from the field - it just is not the rate the payer uses.

### 3.4 Top five recommendations, in order

1. **Fix the weekly close before 09-14 (F1).** Separate pots, one debit per pot, retained share credited to the bank,
   ledger rows for both, a conservation assert inside the guarded section, rolled-back probe in the PR. This is the
   only item that can destroy chips this month.
2. **Book every agent's commission (F2) and make one function the payer (F6, F7).** Remove the source-only guard;
   `fn_agent_claim_commission` is the single commission payer (round 2 calls it); one rakeback payer at
   `fn_player_rakeback_rate` from the funder Dan names.
3. **Declare the journal on every hierarchy write (F3)** and stop the `table_stack` default. This alone drains the
   +182k/day suspense flow the trial balance is complaining about and makes `chip_ledger` say who paid whom.
4. **One Mint (F4) and `CHECK >= 0` on every hierarchy balance (F5).** Route the diamond mint, the free top-up and the
   opening grant through `fn_ca_mint`; add a retire door; add the constraints `NOT VALID` then validate off-peak.
5. **Close the orphan doors and add the second signature (F9, F10).** Revoke the seven dead RPCs; require an approver
   and a reason above a threshold for mints, large sends, clawbacks and statement settlement; publish a daily
   adjustments report. That is the part of NJ 1.3/1.9 an intermediary is expected to have, and it is the control the
   PokerClub.ag and TMT-union collapses lacked.
