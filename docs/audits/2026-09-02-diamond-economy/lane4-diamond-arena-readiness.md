## 0. Summary in one screen

1. **What "Diamond Arena" is today: three unrelated things, none of them a poker room.** (a) A World Hub shell page (`pages/hub/diamond-arena.js`) that iframes `https://diamond.smarter.poker`, which returned HTTP 404 when fetched tonight; (b) a per-user quiz result log `diamond_arena_events` (0 rows, no function references it); (c) a dead repo `Smarter-Poker-Diamond-Arena` (last substantive commit 2026-04-05) holding a CLIENT-SIDE poker engine and an arena staking module whose seven RPCs and two tables do not exist in production.
2. **The first diamond tournament that actually ran is trivia, not poker:** `trivia_tournaments`, 5 events all-time (3 completed, 2 cancelled), 208 entries, 5,200 diamonds in fees, 4,784 pooled, 4,600 paid in 16 `tournament_prize` rows on 2026-08-01, every recipient a horse. 0 tournaments in the last 30 days. The 10% house cut is an unjournaled implicit burn. `training_tournaments` has 0 rows all-time.
3. **The chip machinery is 10 of 16 components hard-wired to `club_members.chip_balance` / `clubs` / `unions`.** Asset-agnostic today: `ca_rake_schedule` + `fn_effective_rake`, `fn_ca_settle_hand_stacks_absolute` (stack identity), the Mint (`fn_ca_mint` / `fn_ca_burn` already accept `asset = 'diamonds'`), `ca_money_rpc_registry`, and the `tournament_obligations` table shape. Near-agnostic (one CHECK each): `ca_payout_freeze` scope, `ca_manual_adjustments.target_kind`.
4. **The engine is not the blocker.** `server/src` (538 .ts files) moves money exclusively through named RPCs (47 distinct money-related RPC names in non-test code); `club_members` appears in 14 non-test files, all reads. There is no single wallet adapter, but there is also no direct balance write to abstract away. The wallet lives in the database, so a Diamond Arena is a DATABASE clone job first.
5. **There is no platform club.** `clubs` has 17 rows, no `is_platform` / `is_system` / `asset` column, none named platform/system/house/smarter/diamond/official. `tables.club_id` and `tournaments.club_id` are nullable in DDL but 0 of 121,002 tables and 0 of 62,436 tournaments (30d) have NULL.
6. **Top three recommendations:** (1) one `clubs` row with `asset = 'diamonds'` and a wallet-resolution seam (`fn_seat_club_for_user` / `fn_credit_player_wallet_once` / `fn_ensure_club_wallet`) that reads the club's asset, rather than a parallel schema; (2) rake as a HOUSE ACCOUNT (`diamond_house`), ledgered, never a burn by omission, with the Mint's `p_destination` widened so the house can hold diamonds; (3) do NOT build the Diamond Arena on `profiles.diamonds` directly: the platform club's `club_members.chip_balance` row becomes the arena wallet, funded by an explicit, journaled `profiles.diamonds -> arena wallet` deposit and drained by an explicit withdrawal, so purchases, chargebacks and the GLI-19 transfer question all stay in the platform layer.

## E. Design decisions for Dan (trade-off, then recommendation)

**(1) Same asset at the table, or an arena escrow wallet?**
Option A: a diamond at the table is `profiles.diamonds` moved straight into `table_seats.stack`. Every buy-in/cash-out then rewrites the platform's canonical store, which is also written by purchases, daily login, gifts, refunds and Stripe chargebacks (24 distinct journal types today); every one of those writers becomes a table-money writer for R3 purposes, and the trial balance would have to reconcile `profiles.diamonds` against the whole platform.
Option B: the platform club's `club_members.chip_balance` row IS the arena wallet. A player deposits `profiles.diamonds -> arena wallet` (one journaled RPC, mint-shaped, reversible) and withdraws the same way. Inside the arena every existing function works unchanged (section B seams). The arena's conservation identity is closed: `arena wallets + felt + escrow + house + bbj = deposits - withdrawals`.
**Recommendation: B.** It is the smaller change (one deposit/withdraw RPC pair plus reporting), it keeps GLI-19-style separation between "account" and "game", and it makes E(4) and E(8) tractable. Cost: a two-step UX (deposit, then sit) unless the client does it in one call.

**(2) Rake as burn (sink) or as a house account (platform revenue)?**
Burn is deflationary and simple, but a burn by omission (what `enter_trivia_tournament_v2` does today) is invisible to every identity and indistinguishable from a leak. A ledgered burn through `fn_ca_burn` is honest but forfeits revenue and cannot fund guarantees, freerolls or a BBJ. A house account (`diamond_house`, the platform club's treasury) receives rake and fees, funds overlays and jackpots from it, and appears as one line in the trial balance; Dan can burn from it later with a four-eyes adjustment.
**Recommendation: house account, never burn by omission.** The chip machinery already does this (`atomic_distribute_rake -> club_treasury`). Burning is then a policy Dan can run monthly from the house, journaled.

**(3) BBJ in diamonds.**
A jackpot is a liability funded by drop. With E(2) as a house account and E(6) as a real club, one `bbj_pools` row for the platform club and the existing `bbj_record_contribution` / `bbj_atomic_payout_v2` handle it; the standard's `fn_bbj_reconcile` (not yet built, Roadmap 4.2) covers both assets if it takes a pool id. Risk: the allocator's fixed 50/25/25 and the empty-backup silent restart (Roadmap BBJ row) would be inherited.
**Recommendation: reuse, but only after Roadmap 4.2/4.3 land; a diamond BBJ with the known allocator defects is a second copy of a known bug.**

**(4) Purchased and earned diamonds at the same tables.**
Same tables is what Dan said ("diamonds that they buy or earn"). The hazard is a Stripe chargeback on a purchase whose diamonds were already lost at a table: the platform cannot claw back from other players (standing ruling: no clawback), so the loss is the house's. `diamond_purchases` (3 rows, 300 diamonds, `refunded_diamonds`, `refunded_amount_cents` columns) already models refunds. With E(1)-B the arena wallet gives a natural control: a deposit is refused (log-only first) while a purchase is inside its chargeback window, or purchased diamonds carry a `settled_at` and only settled diamonds may be deposited.
**Recommendation: same tables; add a purchase-settlement window on the DEPOSIT, not on play; chargebacks after deposit are a house write-off through four-eyes.** Needs Dan's number for the window (UNVERIFIED what Stripe's dispute window is for this account; typically weeks).

**(5) Horses in the arena.**
CLAUDE.md 10.5: horses are players, identical treatment, no exemption. Horses already hold 456,781 of 1,030,092 diamonds (44.3%; 1,000 horse profiles, 900 non-zero) versus 573,311 across 308 humans (181 non-zero, max 493,960 in one profile). Their arena bankroll must come from somewhere ledgered: today the pattern is `scripts/horse-tournament-test.js` writing `profiles.diamonds` directly, and in chips it is `fn_horse_fund_from_treasury` from the club treasury.
**Recommendation: a horse bankroll account on the platform club (its treasury), funded by `fn_ca_mint(asset='diamonds', destination='house')` once that destination exists, with `fn_horse_fund_from_treasury` unchanged.** Horse diamonds are then inside the arena identity and never touch the human supply figure. Dan decides whether the 456,781 horse diamonds already in `profiles.diamonds` are burned (Mint retire) or deposited.

**(6) One `clubs` row with `asset = 'diamonds'` vs a parallel schema.**
Parallel schema (the `diamond-arena` repo's approach: `arena_prize_pools`, `poker_tables`, its own RPCs) means re-implementing and re-auditing 47 engine RPCs, 16 controls and every law test, and it is exactly how `trivia_tournaments` became an unaudited second payout engine. One `clubs` row (`asset` column, `union_id NULL`, `is_platform true`) means the seams in section B resolve to it unchanged, and the work is REPORTING: `fn_ca_trial_balance`, `fn_ca_supply_snapshot`, `ca_direct_balance_writes`, `ca_manual_adjustments.target_kind` and `ca_payout_freeze.scope` learn `asset`, and the Mint gets a `house` destination for diamonds. Hazard: until reporting learns `asset`, the platform club's diamonds are silently summed into the chip supply.
**Recommendation: one club row, asset-tagged, and the reporting change ships in the SAME migration set (log-only) before any diamond table opens.** Also add `CHECK (asset)` consistency: a table/tournament inherits its club's asset; a member of the platform club cannot be seated at a chip table with that wallet (`fn_seat_club_for_user` must never pick the platform club for a chip table or vice versa).

**(7) Guarantees and freerolls funded from what?**
A guarantee is money in escrow or it is not a guarantee (standard 3.2). In chips the bank is `union_bank | club_treasury`. For diamonds the only candidates are the house account E(2) and `diamond_platform_budget` (7,500,000 budgeted, 2,493 spent across 3 periods).
**Recommendation: `fn_apply_prize_guarantee` draws from the platform club treasury (house), and the house is topped up by the Mint against `diamond_platform_budget` as the cap.** If the house cannot fund it, the event pays what it holds and raises `guarantee_unfunded`, same as chips.

**(8) Player-to-player transfers inside the arena.**
`send_wallet_diamond_transfer(p_recipient_id, p_amount, p_message, p_reference_id)` is SECURITY DEFINER and executable by `authenticated`; 0 transfer rows in the last 30 days. GLI-19 treats account-to-account transfer outside a game as prohibited (chip dumping, collusion, laundering). Inside the arena wallet (E(1)-B) there is no transfer primitive at all today, which is the right default.
**Recommendation: no transfers inside the arena; keep or revoke the platform-level `send_wallet_diamond_transfer` as Dan rules (Lane 1/2 scope), but the arena wallet must never be a transfer source or target.**
