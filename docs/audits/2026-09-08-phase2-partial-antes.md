# Phase 2: Partial Individual Antes

W08/C05/C06 review, September 8, 2026. Before changes: HandController.postBlinds posts live blinds before either ante format and labels all antes as undifferentiated deadInvested. PokerEngine.calculatePots pools all deadInvested into a shared pot. Thus an individually short ante can contend for the full antes of every opponent, rather than matched contribution levels. The existing dead-money tests cover full ante-only stacks and BBA shared money, but not unequal individual ante payments.

Reproduce three four-player hands with ante10, blinds1/2 and stacks3/7/100/100, placing the 3-chip stack away from blinds, in the small blind, and in the big blind. Expected individual ante caps are12/24/34, with final awards12/12/10 to different eligible winners after a two-player checkdown. Individual antes must be distinct from the table-wide BBA and dead small blinds; neither shared form may become a private side pot or an uncalled-bet refund. TDA2024 RP11 recommends big-blind-first for the single-payer BBA, which remains unchanged.

Benchmarks reviewed: https://www.pokertda.com/view-poker-tda-rules/ (2024 version, retrieved September8); https://www.pokerstars.com/help/articles/rules-explanation-antes/ ; https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/rules/ . Individual-ante contribution caps apply the ordinary matched-contribution side-pot principle; this is not an external fairness certification.
