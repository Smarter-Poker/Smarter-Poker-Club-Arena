## Confirmed Corrections

- RIT consent: spectators could decline offers through the authenticated HTTP route. The public dispatcher also reported success for invalid/final decisions and could announce three runs after two were accepted, or announce an unclamped request. Both source and public dispatcher now enforce participation; final offers reject requests before events; broadcasts use the stored count. Removed the unused unguarded setChosenRuns setter. Seven new cases initially produced six failures and one pass, then all passed. A real resolver case submits a late choice from rit_result after two complete boards are published and verifies rejection and retained count.
- Omaha: the fewer-than-four-card utility fallback used Holdem selection and ignored the third available hole card. All supplied subsets now enumerate exactly two holes and three board cards. Insufficient cards use the existing No Hand result; high/low share the selection boundary. Actual deals still use VariantRules for four/five/six cards. Traced HandController, determineWinners and insurance/equity callers. No payout exception or recovery path was added. Five new cases reproduced five failures and now pass, alongside the independent oracle.
- Cash button: prediction treated two eligible veterans among a larger roster as heads-up. The real deal forced a sole veteran's button onto a newcomer, violating the existing entry rule and disagreeing with prediction. Heads-up now requires an actual two-player roster; a sole veteran can retain the button for the entry hand. Once dealt, newcomers join normal rotation. Eight cases exercise the actual deal and prediction, including the following hand, initialization, newcomers and all three heads-up departure cases. The obsolete source-text assertion pinning the offending override was superseded by behavioral coverage.
- Published rules: all nine live variant codes are recognized. PLO8/FLO8 show four-card Omaha eight-or-better; FLO8/FLH show fixed-limit sizes, completion/reopening and the house cap; SHORT_DECK shows the 36-card deck and correct ranking/straight rules; Pineapple names the actual discard timing. Cash button/entry/missed-blind rules and short-blind pot-limit sizing are disclosed. All 21 rendered component cases pass. The three chip-continuity sentences are unchanged.
- Winning-card display: RIT sent correct award names but recomputed the first winner's high hand for highlights. The event now carries the exact five from the per-pot EvaluatedHand retained for settlement/history. The client uses those cards for all tied, side-pot, high and low awards, preserving original hole indices. Older payloads without cards retain amounts/names without invented highlights. The real resolver wire assertion failed before the missing field was added, then passed. Four client cases cover low selection, multiple winners, hidden placeholders and older payloads. Financial calculations are unchanged.
