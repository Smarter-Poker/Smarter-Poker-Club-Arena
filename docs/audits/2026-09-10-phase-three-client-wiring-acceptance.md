## Current Corrections

`TournamentResultsPage` now binds archive responses to the requested event and discards superseded responses, including two overlapping refreshes for the same event. Standings, hand history, and mystery awards show a visible retry state after failure. Old rows cannot appear under the next event's name. The list distinguishes unavailable results from a successful empty query. The Mine view does not query all players' results while signed out.

Expanded-panel interactions stop propagation so switching tabs, changing sorting, or retrying does not collapse the event. A direct event URL resolves even when the filtered list is empty and inserts the resolved event into the rendered list. Biggest Hits uses only the recorded winner prize: zero stays zero, missing/non-finite is unknown, and buy-in times multiplier is never substituted for an individual's award.

No money policy, SQL, wallet, event, engine, host, or settlement was mutated by this lane. It adds no financial repair loop or production test double.
