# Prospective Captured Rakeback ABI Version 2

This is the gated integration contract for the new cumulative funding path. It supersedes the earlier per-week cash DTO only when the coordinated source, bank, funding, player and legacy-exclusion transaction is ready. The earlier native payer and browser checkpoints remain reproducible. No endpoint is activated by this contract.

The claim keeps its existing arguments: fn_claim_captured_rakeback(p_request_id uuid, p_expected_user_id uuid, p_club_id uuid DEFAULT NULL). The getter remains fn_get_captured_rakeback(p_club_id uuid DEFAULT NULL). Successful version 2 responses must match captured-rakeback-api-v2.ts. A version 1 or malformed response cannot authorize a version 2 claim or clear its unresolved request.

The immutable financial grouping is the original booked club, original Union, bank route, admitted source generation and captured payer. A source can have valid entitlement before its bank receipt creates a UUID pool. Such a balance has pool_id null and retains the same scope tuple when funding later arrives. Pool IDs must agree with their immutable tuple; different payers remain separate even within one pool. Current memberships never retag a source.

Exact decimal strings preserve PostgreSQL NUMERIC precision. Canonical exact grammar is 0 or a positive integer without leading zero, optionally followed by a decimal part ending in a nonzero digit; values below one begin 0. Cash grammar requires exactly two decimal places, including 0.00. No sign, exponent, whitespace, NaN or Infinity is accepted. Sum/reconciliation uses exact decimal arithmetic, not binary Number. Counts are nonnegative safe integers. Canonical UUIDs and ISO UTC timestamps are required.

For each balance, closed_entitlement_exact is the sum of valid captured player entitlement for closed UTC earning weeks in that exact scope. consumed_exact is the sum of immutable payment allocation slices and equals paid_amount. unpaid_exact is closed_entitlement_exact minus consumed_exact. pending_amount is floor(closed_entitlement_exact \* 100) / 100 minus paid_amount. It is unpaid cash entitlement, not a promise of wallet liquidity or funding admission. A later compatible week may complete an earlier fractional cent. Never calculate weekly pending cash by rounding a week and subtracting fractional consumption.

Each earning week preserves exact entitlement, consumed and remaining allocations. Open weeks remain visible but contribute nothing to closed totals, pending cash or payout admission. The week is Monday through Sunday inclusive. earning_closed_through is a Monday date and excludes that week. Source counts are exact nonnegative integers; funding_admitted_source_count cannot exceed source_count. A zero payment does not erase a newly admitted source.

Unresolved source contracts are shown in unresolved_earnings with explicit reasons and source counts. They do not become invented cash entitlements. Genuine unassigned policy, invalid assigned payer, invalid funding route/rate and unresolved hierarchy remain visible and deferred. No historical source is admitted or backpaid by this DTO.

cash_payments contains actual immutable whole-cent transfers and paid_at timestamps, separate from exact earning_slices. Each payment has a positive amount; its slices sum exactly to that amount, retain original hand/contributor/week identity and belong to its scope. Repeated hand/contributor slices within one payment are invalid. Global payment IDs are unique; payment sums reconcile to paid_amount and per-scope paid_amount. Earning-week consumption reconciles to its exact payment slices. Payments cannot consume open or later-than-receipt-cutoff earning weeks.

A claim result lists only NEW payments created in that request, never every prior receipt. payment_count equals payments.length, top new_payout equals their amounts, scope payment_ids partition those same IDs exactly once, and each scope new_payout equals its listed payments. A zero or partial valid result may have deferred reasons; it is an authoritative completed attempt and its request UUID can clear after full validation. A malformed response, transport failure or unknown commit state retains the exact UUID for recovery. Replay returns the original result, including original payment IDs and timestamps, byte-equivalent as canonical JSON; it performs no new payment. New requests remain disabled while an unresolved request is being recovered.

source_active true must mean the REAL version 2 capability and coordinated cutover are installed. Capture generation alone is insufficient. A valid false response has zero source monetary totals and empty source arrays. The browser keeps the separate legacy history visible and never routes to legacy claims after it has observed true for that account. The legacy SQL mutation must reject captured mode to close the activation race. No bootstrap getter will be published simply to ship this UI early.

Authorization remains current and separate from financial identity. A beneficiary can recover their own exact request even after agent deactivation or club departure; the source debt is not erased. Removed Union administrators cannot act merely because they held authority when the hand was played. Administrative actions check current authority for the original funding scope.

Required native and browser cases include cross-week .009+.009 club carry, player fractional carry across closed weeks, changing payer with no fractional wallet mixing, no pool before bank admission, source admission with zero new agent cash, partial and zero deferral, exact concurrent/lost-response replay, old and new account isolation, departed club/original Union authorization, open UTC week exclusion and Pacific DST bank-window attribution. Full monetary and UI composition remains a release gate.
