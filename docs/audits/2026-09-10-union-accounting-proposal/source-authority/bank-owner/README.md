# Prospective Cash Bank Owner — Native Checkpoint

This is an unapplied candidate, not a production activation. It extends the
captured actual bank owner to consume the immutable accepted hand route and
record the real destination credit in the same transaction. Historical rows
remain outside source funding admission; no history or balances are repaired.

Run `bash docs/audits/2026-09-10-union-accounting-proposal/source-authority/bank-owner/run-local.sh`.
The PostgreSQL 17 runner uses a private Unix socket with no network listener,
actual captured functions and triggers, and synthetic fixture identities. It
cleans only its own stopped database data. The fixture contains 120 tables,
224 functions and 174 enabled trigger definitions; this inventory does not
claim that every trigger branch or game variant was exercised.

The verified bank result is 62 checks: 49 direct native cases, 9 activation
and concurrency cases, and 4 old compiled legacy route-flip cases. The
expanded fixture also passes the separate 15 accepted-owner checks.
`proof-manifest.json` pins the executable inputs and individual proof outputs.

The direct cases run the actual accepted owner, durable post-commit dispatcher,
bank owner, and cash commission batch. They prove exact replay, changed-payload
refusal, captured route/rates after membership changes, actual destination row
updates, gross rake versus net-after-BBJ accounting, and final receipt failure
rollback across every public table. The deferred failure case observes both
applied contributor receipts before forcing rollback of the entire transaction.

Observed PostgreSQL blockers cover activation waiting after an old bank has
inserted its leg, old new-record and existing-record recovery owners paused
before record admission, duplicate exact requests, and both old compiled
legacy route flips. A bounded DDL timeout leaves activation atomic. Guards on
actual record/leg writes prevent a resumed old function from committing an
unclassified prospective credit. Real and derived hand aliases are fenced.
These tests deliberately separate source and bank activation to exercise
pending accepted sources; that fixture order is not a rollout instruction.

`ca_cash_bank_receipts.hand_id` is the immutable funding witness. It binds the
accepted generation/hash, actual rake record and spendable leg, original route,
Union/club destination, gross credit and BBJ/net distinction, and exactly one
actual Union transaction or treasury ledger row. `bank_credit_at` is that real
credit row's returned `created_at`, preserving the installed Pacific calendar
basis. A pre-credit leg or positive wallet balance is insufficient evidence.
The receipt and its referenced money rows reject mutation and truncation,
including the actual maintenance setting. Service access is read-only.

`00-bank-legacy-index.sql` is an online expansion step outside activation.
`01-bank-receipts.sql` installs source admission and body-independent guards.
`02-bank-owner.sql` is generated by `build-owner.py` from the exact captured
owner (MD5 `56fe5715421d7e35bc386a669bb483a2`); anchor drift refuses generation.
The proposal does not rewrite the actual accepted-owner body. Final source,
bank and downstream classification must activate together under bounded locks.

The booked agent policy remains unchanged: raw fractional commission is capped
at 0.70 by the actual agents constraint and create/admin producers. The actual
cash batch calls the fractional credit owner. A different normalization helper
does not establish a live overcredit defect; the read-only aggregate of stored
rates above 1 was zero. Native cases prove the real constraint and admin owner
refuse 0.85 and whole-percent 80 without mutation. Raw invalid source terms
remain explicit and deferred; this candidate neither clips nor widens policy.
See `commission-policy-evidence.json` for exact producer and storage provenance.

Remaining release gates are the reviewed source-compatible Round1 writer and
legacy exclusion, actual released-cash capacity with exact source consumption,
full Union-to-club-to-hierarchy-to-player cascade proof, and final coactivation
ordering with current catalog witnesses. This bank receipt is not a downstream
release receipt, a source-final assertion, or permission to spend historical
funds. Publication of this audit checkpoint must not enable these SQL stages.

Independent bank reviews by the payer and Round2 lanes found no remaining
bank-specific blocker at this checkpoint. Both reviews explicitly reserve
full funding/cascade, legacy exclusion and finality approval for the shared
integration proof. Their disposition is limited to this unapplied candidate.
