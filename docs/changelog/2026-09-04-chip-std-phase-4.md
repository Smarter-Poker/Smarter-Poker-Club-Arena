## 4.3 One allocator, and every door declares itself

1. **Two allocators disagreed.** The engine (`RakeConfig.ts`) splits a drop 50/25/25 main/backup/promo below the pivot and 25/25/50 at or above 100,000 in main (Dan, 2026-08-18) and sends the portions; `bbj_record_contribution` ignored them and always allocated 50/25/25 by cumulative rounding, while `fn_bbj_repair_unbanked`, a third allocator, honoured the pivot. The union pool crossed 100,000 at **2026-09-03 07:13:51.966 UTC** (read from the journal's post-balance) and never dipped below, so every drop for 38 hours put 50% into main where the rule says 25%. Now there is one allocator, `fn_bbj_allocate`, reading `ca_bbj_policy` (pivot 100,000; 0.50/0.25 below, 0.25/0.25 at; promo the remainder), used by the drop and the repair; the engine's portions are accepted and ignored. Live within a minute: union pool drops 25.9% main / 48.1% promo, club pool 51.6% main.
2. **The over-allocation moved where the rule put it.** At apply, 50,550 post-pivot drops / 17,011.50 chips had put 4,245.25 more into main than the policy allows; that amount was moved main to promo inside the pool (`ca_bbj_bucket_moves` row 1, op `pivot-correction:<pool>:2026-09-03`, asserted from the rows before and the balances after). Chips are conserved; nobody is paid or charged; the sweep that already runs carried it to the union's promo wallet within the hour, which is what the pivot is for. Union main after: 104,254.07.
3. **A bank move inside a pool is declared and recorded.** The reseed (backup to main on a 100% hit) and the union's backup-to-main transfer wrote the columns and nothing else, journalled by the autoledger as `bbj_pool -> settlement_suspense`. `fn_bbj_move_between_banks` (service only): declared `bbj_pool -> bbj_pool` (one leg per bank label), a `ca_bbj_bucket_moves` row with a reason and an op id, replay returns the original, refused when the source bank cannot cover it.
4. **An empty reserve is said out loud.** A 100% hit with backup at 0 restarted main at 0 in silence; the reseed now files a `bbj_error` incident (`bbj-reserve-empty:<pool>:<hour>`). Probed: a 50-chip main with an empty reserve paid 50, restarted at 0 and raised the incident; with a 70-chip reserve it reseeded main to 70 through a recorded move.
5. **The payout declares itself and pays the right wallet.** `bbj_atomic_payout_v2`'s pool debit fell to suspense (no payout in 7 days is the only reason suspense read 0), and a departed recipient's share went to their HOME club wallet via `credit_player_wallet`, not the wallet they bought in from. Now: pool debit declared `bbj_payout` `bbj_pool -> table_stack` keyed `bbj_payout:<payout>`; a seated recipient's seat is credited (the felt is derived; delta-mode hand writes preserve it); a departed recipient's share is a keyed credit to the TABLE's club wallet declared `table_stack -> player_wallet`, key `bbj:<payout>:<user>`, with a `chip_transactions` row. Probed on the Deep Stack pool: a 15% hit of 2,765.27 wrote one pool leg, three keyed wallet legs, zero suspense legs, three history rows; the replay paid nobody and moved no wallet. `bbj_credit_one_recipient` was executable by `authenticated` (RLS would have refused its recipient insert, so not exploitable; still a money door a browser could call): service only, like every BBJ door now.
6. **Funding and the backup transfer declare.** `fn_union_fund_bbj_pool` (World Hub `fund_bbj_pool`) debited the union bank and credited the banks undeclared, and also added to `pool_amount`, a dead counter the autoledger still journalled as `bbj_pool`, so a funded amount was journalled twice; `fn_union_bbj_backup_transfer` likewise. Both declare (`transfer` `union_wallet -> bbj_pool` per bank; `promo` `bbj_pool -> union_wallet`); `pool_amount` is neither written nor journalled. Probed: funding 30 wrote exactly three legs and no stray one.

## 4.2 The jackpot has an opening balance it can prove

The same shape the supply meter and the Mint register were given. `ca_bbj_pool_snapshots` carries one **opening balance per bank per pool**, taken at 21:17:14 UTC and labelled as a baseline, not a movement: union main 104,267.07 / backup 31,952.04 / promo 6.36; Deep Stack 18,447.58 / 8,723.35 / 2.49; the empty Midway club row 0/0/0. The baseline note carries the lifetime identity at that instant (inflow 442,342.22, outflow 205,573.73, balances 163,400.79, gap 73,367.70) and its decomposition from the earlier investigation, kept untouched in `bbj_conservation_baseline`: 41,096.65 is 49,714 contribution rows of 2026-03-03..07 whose bank portions fall short of their amount (phantom inflow, no chip missing); the remainder is `total_paid_out` on the pool rows exceeding the `bbj_payouts` rows from before that table was kept. Nothing is written off, credited or moved: nobody is owed it, and the pools hold exactly what the opening balances record. The lifetime figure stays readable (`fn_bbj_conservation_check.gap`, `lifetime_healthy`) and the pre-journal residue is named for the epoch reset gate with the other historic items.

From that instant the identity is the journal's. `fn_bbj_reconcile(pool)` reads the three banks and every `chip_ledger` leg labelled `bbj_pools.<bank>` since the previous snapshot **in one statement** (one snapshot, so a write committing between two reads cannot show in one and not the other), and writes `journal_*`, the business flows (drops, payouts, sweeps, funding, bank moves, ledger write failures) and `unexplained_*` per bank. `fn_bbj_reconcile_all()` runs it for every active pool from the hourly `rake-bbj-invariant-audit` that already exists (no new cron; Dan's standard) and files a `bbj_error` incident when the trailing two snapshots disagree by more than a cent or a write failure landed. `fn_bbj_conservation_check` keeps its lifetime figures and adds `epoch` (opened_at, snapshots, unexplained_since_opening); `healthy` now means the journal identity has held since the baseline. Probed: a declared 10-chip bank move reconciled to the cent; 5 chips written past the journal read as `unexplained_main 5` and filed the incident; the hourly audit's return carries `meter`.

After apply: `fn_bbj_conservation_check` -> `healthy true`, `lifetime_healthy false`, gap 73,367.70 (unchanged by every move above: conserved). First scheduled meter run 21:38 UTC.
