# Free Buy - Dan 2026-09-04

## The law conflict, and how it was resolved

Two of Dan's own instructions collided, and CLAUDE.md 10.8 forbids an agent
resolving that unilaterally, so it was put to him.

- **2026-09-02** (PR #2846, `zz_freerolls_are_free_buy`): every 0-buy-in MTT is
  a Free Buy, and rebuys and add-ons cost **$1**, forced.
- **2026-09-04**: the $250 tier is $1/$1 but the **$500 tier is $2/$2**.

The trigger forced `rebuy_cost` and `addon_cost` to 1.00 unconditionally, so the
feature event would have been created at $2 and silently rewritten to $1 before
a single player registered.

**Dan's ruling: tier-aware.** $1 stays the default for every freeroll on the
platform; a _scheduled_ Free Buy that deliberately sets its own price keeps it.
Neither law was deleted, and the law-test pin moved to the new migration in the
same commit.

**What did not need changing.** `rebuy_chips`, `addon_chips`, `rebuy_levels` and
`addon_levels` were only ever filled in when `<= 0`, so the 10,000-chip add-on
on a 3,000 stack already survived the trigger untouched. Only the two costs were
forced. The narrower the change, the less of a working law is disturbed.

**Proven, then rolled back** (11.5 - never spend real chips to test a rule):

    plain freeroll (7, 7, 0)     -> rebuy 1.00, addon 1.00, chips 3000
    scheduled tier (2, 2, 10000) -> rebuy 2.00, addon 2.00, chips 10000

## The add-on window is ONE window

"Add on as soon as they sit down, and also at the break" plus "one hour for late
reg, then the add on period" is not two windows. It is one window that opens at
the start and closes after the break. `tournament_players.add_on` is a boolean:
two windows would need a second add-on slot per player. One add-on each, taken
whenever the player likes inside the window.

The engine's level-based early close is skipped for these. A TURBO structure
passes the level cap long before the hour is up, so closing on levels would shut
the add-on window while late registration was still open and silently cost every
horse its add-on.

## Overlay funding

Dan: overlays come from the union main bank and the DSS main bank, "and if the
main bank didn't have enough chips, your back up plan is pulling from the
treasury wallet".

The routing already existed - union events draw `union_wallets.chip_balance`,
DSS draws `clubs.chip_treasury` - but a short union bank used to raise a
critical alert and **return without topping up**, which advertises a guarantee
and then does not pay it. It now falls back to the club treasury for the whole
shortfall, warns naming the fallback, and only reserves `critical` for both
banks being short.

Patched from the function's own `pg_get_functiondef` source rather than retyped:
a ~200 line money path with a deadlock-retrying ledger write, where hand
transcription is how a payout bug gets introduced. Assertions confirm the
satellite-seat guarantee, the club-treasury path and the 40P01 retry survived.

## Numbers worth saying out loud

- 5 events/day/host at 250/250/250/500/250 = **1,500 per host**, **3,000 across
  both**. Confirmed by Dan.
- Rebuys and add-ons feed the same pool, so the guarantee is a **ceiling on
  exposure, not a cost**. A 100-entrant standard event with 2.5 rebuys each and
  a full add-on take collects 350 and retires the 250 guarantee outright.
- The union bank held **66,596.34** when this was written - roughly 44 days at
  absolute worst case (nobody rebuys, nobody adds on). The treasury fallback now
  exists behind it.
- The five slots leave an **8 hour overnight gap** (midnight to 08:00). Five
  events cannot cover 24 hours at a 4-hour cadence; six can. This is the
  schedule as specified, recorded rather than quietly rounded away.

## Not built yet

The 5-a-day scheduler itself. `FreeBuy.ts` holds the tiers, slots, window and
horse behaviour with 26 tests; the board that creates the 10 events a day at
Chicago wall-clock time is the remaining piece. The existing recurring board
matches on `getUTCHours()`, which would drift an hour twice a year and move the
8PM feature event to 7PM every November, so it needs a Chicago-anchored pass
rather than another UTC block.
