# 2026-09-04 - Six table complaints from Dan's screenshots, fixed at the root

Dan, with four screenshots of a PLO6 1/2 table in Shark Club, in one message.
Each item below names what he saw, what was actually wrong, and what changed.

## 1. Every horse alias looked like the same person

**Seen:** RakeHunter, CheckCowb, RiverMonk, WheelMonk, RakeJester - "two word
names, first letter of both words capitalized, no one word names, no
variation in capitalization, no numbers spaces or characters."

**Measured:** 1,000 horses, 867 of them `CapitalCapital`, only 803 distinct
aliases. `identityFor()` in `server/src/services/HorseOnboarding.ts` composed
every alias as `ALIAS_A[i] + ALIAS_B[j]` from two twenty-word lists: 400
shapes, so 197 horses shared a name with another horse. `CheckCowb` is
`CheckCowboy` cut by the seat plate's 11-character budget.

**Changed:**

- `server/src/services/horseAliasStyles.ts` - fifty styles (single words,
  lowercase, ALLCAPS, `snake_case`, `first.last`, birth years, lucky numbers,
  city codes, `xX...Xx`, leet, honorifics with a space, and so on), drawn from
  pools of poker words, everyday words, first names, surnames and city codes.
  Deterministic in the horse id with an `attempt` salt for collisions. Every
  alias is 3-11 characters so the plate never cuts it.
- `identityFor()` now uses it; `username` derives from the alias and always
  satisfies `fn_protect_profile_username_and_gate`'s rule.
- `supabase/migrations/20260904190000_every_horse_gets_a_human_alias.sql` -
  generated by `server/scripts/generate-horse-alias-migration.ts`, renames all
  1,000 horses. Unique case-insensitively against every alias AND username on
  the platform, humans included. Dry-run inside a rolled-back transaction
  first, then applied with psql and recorded in
  `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations`. Result: 1,000 distinct aliases,
  180 CamelCase (18%), 213 with digits, 130 with underscores, 58 with a space,
  217 all-lowercase, max length 11.
- Only `profiles.alias` moved. `display_name` (the "real" name),
  `username` and `content_authors.alias` (the social handle) are untouched.
  A horse already seated shows its old name until it next sits or the hourly
  restart reloads the fleet.

## 2. "Reconnecting To The Table" on a table that had loaded fine

**Seen:** the banner ~20s into a table; sometimes "Connection Lost. Trying To
Get You Back."

**Measured, in Dan's own browser against production:** a raw socket to
`wss://engine.smarter.poker/ws/multi` that answers nothing is closed by the
engine at ~60-77s with `1001 heartbeat timeout`. That is the shared mux
socket: the engine PINGs every 25s and closes any socket silent for 60s, and
the only PONGs on the mux came from the per-table facades' clients. A socket
with NO facade - the pre-warmed lobby socket from `ServiceBootstrap`, and the
linger after leaving a table - never answered. So: boot at t+0, open a table
at t+42s (facade attached, felt live), engine closes the socket at t+60s for
the PINGs that went unanswered before the facade existed, every facade fails,
"Reconnecting To The Table." Anyone who spent 35-60s in the lobby after boot
got this; anyone who spent longer got a dead pre-warm and a cold handshake.

**Changed (`src/services/EngineSocketMux.ts`):**

- The physical socket answers every PING itself, exactly once, facades or
  not. Facades still receive the PING (their staleness watchdogs stamp it);
  their own PONGs are dropped in `sendFor` so four tables do not mean four
  replies.
- Linger after the last table 60s -> 10 minutes, now that the socket can
  outlive 60s at all. A player reading the lobby between tables keeps the
  warm socket; every table open is a SUBSCRIBE frame, not a TLS handshake.
- The lobby warm-up (`services/tableWarmup.ts`, shipped 2026-09-03: roster +
  SUBSCRIBE the moment a game card opens) is unchanged and now actually
  survives to the join.

Pinned in `tests/engine-socket-mux.test.ts`.

## 3. "Authentication Required" and no way to leave a table

**Seen:** spectating with 0 chips, Leave Table produced "Authentication
Required" and the table stayed. Dan: "you should never be denied the ability
to leave a table, even if you aren't logged in. The leave table acts like the
back button or takes you back to the lobby, that's the default."

**Wrong, three ways:** `handleLeaveTable` began with
`if (!tableId || !userId) return;` (silence for an unhydrated session); a
spectator (heroSeat 0) was routed through `tableService.leaveTable`, which
asks the ENGINE to release a seat that does not exist, and the engine's 401
came back verbatim as the reason; and `getAuthHeaders()` fell through to a
request with NO Authorization header whenever `getSession()` was empty (the
whole of a token refresh), which is what produced the 401.

**Changed (`src/pages/TablePage.tsx`, `src/services/GameServerAPI.ts`):**

- Leaving the VIEW is always allowed; cashing out is the engine's to grant.
  No seat -> close the tab, lobby. Seat and engine agrees -> cash out,
  results card, lobby (unchanged). Seat and engine refuses (auth not ready,
  engine restarting, stay clock) -> the engine's reason is said, the player
  goes to the lobby, and the TAB STAYS OPEN: the seat is live, the chips are
  safe on the server, and the tab is the way back to cash out.
- The tab X follows the same rule.
- `getAuthHeaders()` tries `refreshSession()` before ever sending an
  unauthenticated request.

Pinned in `tests/unit/theDoorIsNeverLocked.test.ts`.

## 4. Previous Hand / Chat / Time Bank / Rabbit Hunt 50% larger

`--sp-hud-tile-size` 44 -> 66px, radius 12 -> 18px (`TableHUD.css`); the chat
button, mounted outside the HUD, carries the same numbers in `TableChat.css`
and the test pins the two equal. Overlaid numerals and badges scale with the
tile. A dead `@media (min-width: 1024px)` block in `PreviousHandCard.css` that
read undeclared `-lg` tokens (fallback 44px) is deleted - it would have pinned
desktop at the old size the moment the token moved.

`--sp-hero-clear` is NOT grown to fit the taller row. The reserve is the
hero's half-seat strip and every previous attempt to grow it for chrome shrank
the felt, which Dan has rejected three times; the tiles sit at the page's left
edge under the stadium's rounded corner. The HUD-slot test's pin inverts
accordingly: hero-clear stays the measured half-seat number.

## 5. Dealer button twice the chips in the pot

`--dealer-btn-size: calc(var(--cp-chip-size) * 2)` - 7.2% of the table, 20px
floor, 52px ceiling (25px on a phone, 52px on desktop). `tableGeometry.ts`
mirrors it as `CHIP_* x 2`.

The geometry followed: the puck's felt margin is now its own radius (3.6%)
plus daylight, with the chips' margin left at 2.5% (growing the shared
constant was tried and narrowed the board by two chip widths). Daylight is
1.9%, the largest the ownership rule admits (at 2.1%+ an 8-max phone's
bottom-right puck stood nearer the seat above it). `overlapsBoard` moved from
the DealerButton wrapper into `dealerButtonPosition`'s own predicate so the
module never hands out a puck on the cards. 56 raw placements now overlap a
plate (36 before) and the swing resolves every one; pinned in
`tests/unit/seatCardsAndPlate.test.tsx` and `tests/table-geometry-chips.test.ts`.

## 6. Folding when you could check froze the game

**Wrong:** a fold with a free check opened `FoldProtectionDialog` ("Check Or
Fold?") - a modal that ignored input for 350ms, could not be dismissed by its
backdrop, and was never closed by the game. When the clock ran out under it
the server checked for the player and the modal stayed up over a hand that
had moved on.

**Changed:** a fold is a fold. The server has always accepted a voluntary fold
with a free check (`ServerActionValidator.validateFold`). The dialog, its CSS,
`commitFold` and the dialog-only `handleCheck` are deleted. Pre-actions are
unchanged (an armed fold still maps to `auto_check_fold`). Pinned in
`tests/unit/foldIsAFold.test.ts`.

Dan, with three more screenshots ("Session Lost. Refresh To Rejoin The

### 7. The disconnect audit

`docs/audits/2026-09-04-disconnect-protection-audit.md`: what is in place
(transport, the presence FSM, what happens to the hand, reconnect, eviction,
the hourly restart, every connection surface and where it sits), what shipped
today, and twelve ranked remaining gaps with file references.

### 8. Reconnect is an event; every connection message is on the felt

- `DisconnectToast` moved from `position: fixed` at the top of the page to an
  absolute child of `.table-surface` on the wordmark line beside
  `TableConnectionBanner`, gated on `isActive` and on the socket being
  connected (its map is stale otherwise). "Session Lost. Refresh To Rejoin"
  is gone: a refresh was never needed. It says "Reconnecting Your Seat, Ns
  Until Auto Action" / "Reconnecting Your Seat, Your Chips Are Safe On The
  Server", and hides at zero.
- `TablePage`: the socket's edge back to `connected` resets GameServerAPI's
  circuit breaker (which refused our own heartbeats for 30s after the network
  returned), heartbeats immediately (the engine's un-away and auto-action
  cancel), and re-arms the hole-card read for a seated hero in a live hand
  with no cards on screen.
- `EngineStateClient.onopen`: the RESYNC on reconnect read `seq` after
  `resetInbox()` zeroed it and had been dead since 2026-08-25. It reads first
  now.
- Engine: `collectAbandonedSeatEvictions` releases a seat whose player has
  been DISCONNECTED or page-left for 5 minutes at a table too quiet for
  blinds or turns (reason `abandoned_seat`, explained on the next visit);
  `/away` registers a seated player on demand so the beacon is tracked at a
  table that has not dealt yet.

### 9. One I'm Back

The floating bottom-right button (which also called `setSitOut` directly,
bypassing the in-flight guard) and the sit-out pill's button are deleted. The
footer bar's I'm Back is the one; the pill keeps Leave Table.

### 10. "Authentication Required" on I'm Back after a reload

`getAuthHeaders` used `supabase.auth.getSession()` alone and sent NO
Authorization header when it came back empty, which it does for the first
moments after a reload while auth-js restores the session. It now uses the
token cache -> getSession -> localStorage -> refreshSession, and every engine
call goes through `engineFetch`, which retries once on 401 with a refreshed
token (a 401 is refused before it runs, so replay is safe for every endpoint).

### 11. Folded hero cards were dimmed twice

`.seat__cards--folded` (opacity 0.45) sits inside `.seat--folded` (opacity
0.65): 0.29 on the felt. Now 0.85 inside the same ancestor, ~0.55 effective,
grayscale 35%. Still a folded hand; readable.

### 12. A closed table said "Reconnecting To The Table" forever

Found while verifying on production: opening a table the fleet had just
closed showed the banner for as long as the tab was open, with SUBSCRIBE
answered TABLE_NOT_FOUND every time. `EngineStateClient`'s 4404 handler set
`idle` and `scheduleReconnect()` overwrote it with `reconnecting` one line
later, on every retry. A `tableMissing` flag keeps the slow ladder silent
(`idle`) until the table answers; the "This Table Has Closed" overlay is
driven by the error count as before. Pinned in
`tests/engine-state-client-recovery.test.ts`.
