## Finding 1, the -153.80: spins were read from counters that agree with the journal at no instant

Read per event over a five minute window (every live event's liability term captured twice, five minutes apart, against the `prize_liability` legs between), every mismatch was a SPIN. Part four had the meter read a spin from its counters (`prize_pool + total_rake`), and a spin's `prize_pool` is its multiplier prize from creation to completion. The journal moves a spin's money in five legs: entries into `prize_liability`; `prize_liability -> spin_reserve` for the pool net of fee when the third player sits (`spin_entry`); `spin_reserve -> prize_liability` for the drawn prize (`spin_prize`); the prize out; the fee out. So the exact liability of a spin is: its entries while registering, its fee alone between the pool filling and the draw, fee plus the drawn prize until the payout, the fee until settlement, zero. The counter is none of those, in either direction (below the exact bank after a large draw, above it while registering), which is why the offset over the live spins read +237.92 at 07:41, +421.64 at 07:45, -22.56 at 07:55:05 and -508.56 at 07:55:38. Each hourly boundary landed somewhere on that curve. An oscillation, not a leak: 1,699 completed spins read exactly 0.00 with the reserve terms and their lifetime journal is zero.

**The escrow now knows the reserve.** `tournament_escrow.reserve_out` and `reserve_in`, fed in the same transaction by one trigger on the `spin_entry` and `spin_prize` legs (`zz_ca_escrow_reserve_leg`, `fn_ca_escrow_on_reserve_leg`) through the one door (`fn_ca_escrow_apply`, now 14 arguments, the 12-argument shape dropped), and `prize_balance = (gross_in - fee_entries_in - bounty_in) + overlay_in + satellite_in - reserve_out + reserve_in - prize_out - refund_prize`. A spin opened at first sight reads its reserve legs too. Backfilled from the legs for all 1,859 spin rows; the migration asserts every completed or cancelled spin at zero and no negative prize bank (COMPLETING is not judged: a spin there still holds its drawn prize until the payout lands and its fee until settlement). The meter reads the escrow for every event with a row and the counters only for an event with no row yet. The shadow comparison (`fn_ca_escrow_balance_drift`) adds the reserve terms so a spin is not a permanent disagreement.

**The step is on the register.** When the meter stopped believing the spin counters, the exact banks exceeded the counters by 508.56 over 105 live spins (a large draw was pending); recorded as `register-opening-baseline-correction:supply-meter-redefinition:2026-09-05-spins`, a mint by sign, bounded by the migration at 1,500 either way. Incident `supply-unexplained:2026-09-05-07` resolved with this as its correction. The 07:05 to 08:05 snapshot straddles the switch and reads the offset at 07:05 as one more unexplained figure; it is named below.

Verified live after apply: 76 settled live spins, banks against journal to the cent, 0 disagree; `fn_ca_escrow_balance_drift(2)` 1,229 checked, 0 disagree (probe). Spins stay `enforced = false` (tracked, not refused) until a soak of measured non-negative live banks; the exactness is what makes the flip possible, and it is named for the next cut.

## Finding 2, the BBJ write failures: the freeze refused the journal and let the bank write stand

`ca_ledger_write_failures` held 104 rows (9.69 chips) with sqlstate 55006 PLATFORM_FROZEN, every one a `chip_ledger` INSERT made by `fn_ca_autoledger` for a `bbj_pools` write at :55 or :00. The writer was `fn_ca_auto_reconcile_tick` (pg_cron, every minute, runs as postgres with no service_role claim, does not stop for the break) calling `fn_bbj_repair_unbanked`. `bbj_pools` is not one of the seven tables the freeze guards, so the bank write stood; `chip_ledger` is, so the leg was refused; the autoledger logged it and let the write through, as it is built to. A bank moved and the journal did not, which is exactly what the BBJ meter flagged.

Fixed on both sides. `fn_refuse_while_frozen` passes a `chip_ledger` row written from inside another trigger (`pg_trigger_depth() > 1`): that row is the record of a write the freeze already permitted, and refusing it is the one outcome the standard cannot allow. A direct client INSERT on `chip_ledger` (depth 1) is refused as before. `fn_bbj_repair_unbanked` returns empty while `fn_platform_frozen()` and re-banks at the next tick after play resumes (CLAUDE.md section 13 rule 5). Probed inside the live 07:55 freeze: a `bbj_pools` write as postgres produced its leg and no failure row; the repair returned 0 rows; a direct insert was refused with PLATFORM_FROZEN. Both `bbj-meter` incidents resolved. No write failure since 07:58.
