# Party Face Performance Draft

Added a transparent premium atlas and a preview-only performance for party_face. Its horn extends at 500 ms, its mouth opens from 633 to 1167 ms, its eyes close at 1300 ms, and ribbons burst at 1567 ms before the 4000 ms cut. Mouth and eye layers use an instance-scoped clip, preserving the original artwork. Ribbons sit behind the head to retain expression visibility.

This is deliberately unregistered: it adds no claimed live catalogue coverage. The final matching picker still, dedicated horn/whistle/crowd recordings, production flow and device acceptance remain pending. The generated opaque checkerboard edit was rejected and is not a project asset. Browser checks cover 15 normalized speed samples, reduced motion and page errors. The original transparent source is art-source/animated/party_face.png; the renderer uses its fingerprinted WebP atlas.
