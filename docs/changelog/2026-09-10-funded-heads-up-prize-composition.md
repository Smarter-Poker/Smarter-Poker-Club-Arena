# Funded Heads-Up prizes share the accounting CI proof

The existing Heads-Up native fixture verified 16 paid admission, launch, refund and rollback groups. Its prize amount and cumulative payout writers were tested in separate fixtures, so there was no evidence that the funded launch could supply the amount those writers actually paid.

The new composition starts with two real 200-chip entries and lease-bound launch completion. Across NLH/PLO4 and 300/1000 starting score chips, the installed reader derives 380 prize custody. The actual cumulative writer then credits that 380 once under overlapping calls, with one matching obligation, payout, key and wallet receipt. A late receipt failure rolls back every public fixture table. Replays, a competing recipient and payout-history mutation preserve the committed evidence.

The isolated accounting CI command now passes --with-heads-up-payout. It replaces the old 16-group HU segment with 32 groups, giving 69 unique groups: 37 other funding/refund groups, 16 existing HU groups, four funded ladder groups and 12 payout groups. The no-flag command still runs the original 53-group composition. Both focused HU selectors remain available.

This proof takes winner identity as synthetic input. It conserves 2000 opening custody as 1980 wallet balances and 20 fee escrow; it intentionally leaves the event RUNNING, both seats occupied and zero terminal receipts. Authoritative elimination, current terminal readiness, fee attribution and seat-exit closure remain open. No production mutation or Stage B installation is part of this change.

The source manifest and exercised catalog are in docs/audits/2026-09-10-heads-up-funded-payout-native.json. The captured SQL is byte-identical to its archived source; the composition then imports the pinned already-applied obligation and guard successors.
