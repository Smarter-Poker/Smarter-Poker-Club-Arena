# The welcome spin is funded

**2026-09-11. Dan: "THATS ON YOU TO DECIDE."**

The welcome spin has been built, probed and live since phase 2 and unreachable
the whole time, because `welcome_budget_chips` was `0` on both hosts. It is
funded now. This is the reasoning, so the numbers can be argued with rather than
guessed at later.

## What it actually costs

A welcome spin is the real wheel at the real price, and the table returns 80
percent over time. So the expected cost of one is about **0.80 chips**: eighty
cents of prize against a spin the host chose not to charge a dollar for. The
variance is the part that matters: the top prize is 50 chips, one spin in 667.

## The queue is not the new members

The rule is once per member **ever**, so turning it on does not only greet
future arrivals: every existing member who has never taken one becomes eligible
at once.

    Deep Stack Society   417 members waiting
    Midway Union       1,508 members waiting across its clubs

At 0.80 expected, the standing back catalogue is roughly 334 chips of liability
for Deep Stack and 1,206 for Midway if every single one claims. Against promo
wallets of 20,652 and 54,976 that is affordable, but paying it in one lump on
day one is not the shape anybody wants.

## The window is what controls it

    Deep Stack Society   300 chips per 7 days
    Midway Union         500 chips per 7 days

Seven days rather than the 30 day default, deliberately. The sliding window
drains the back catalogue at a controlled rate either way, but a shorter window
reopens the door sooner: a member who arrives the day after a month's budget is
spent would otherwise wait three weeks to be welcomed. At these numbers Deep
Stack clears its 417 in about a week and a half, Midway its 1,508 in about
three weeks, and after that the budget is only ever meeting genuinely new
arrivals.

The maximum a host can lose in any window is exactly the budget. That is the
whole point of the phase 2 design, and it is why these can be set without
hedging: 300 a week is 6 percent of Deep Stack's promo wallet per month and 500
a week is 4 percent of Midway's, and the real spend will be far under both,
because uptake is never total.

A welcome spin is the whole wheel or it is not offered, so the offer closes
while roughly 50 chips of budget remain unspent, in reserve for a top prize
that may never land. That reserve is the price of never handing a new member a
wheel with its best tiers locked out.

## Verified on the live configuration

A rolled-back probe took a real waiting member on each host, read the page and
the lobby as that member, and took the spin: the page says available, the entry
read that four player surfaces use says ready, the door pays, and not one tier
is locked for the budget. Nobody's once-ever spin was spent, because the probe
rolls back.

The owners hold 494,590 diamonds between them against a top diamond prize of
250, so the diamond side of the table is covered too.
