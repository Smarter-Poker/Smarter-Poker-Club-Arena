# Diamond Spins Twelve-Prize Wheel

Players choose 25 to 2,500 diamonds before spinning a dimensional chrome wheel. Twelve sectors contain the four bonus games, Upgrade, 1x/2x/3x chip prizes, Diamonds, Throwables, Time Bank and Rabbit Hunt. Each new spin awards a prize. A winning sector opens a full prize reveal; game wins open their setup, and Upgrade plays a second games-only wheel with a doubled base. Peg sounds follow visible movement. Tab changes pause the paid animation and reveal.

The server commits both draws and persists the complete result. Game awards reserve their maximum liability before the wheel outcome is shown, are account/club/game bound, and pay the base once. Double Down adds only the original entry. Interrupted requests retain their identity and resume without another debit. Existing open rounds and historical receipts remain recoverable. Owner consent, owner diamond custody, Promo-first/Main Bank shortfall payments, and maintenance freezes remain enforced. Welcome entries and claimed tenth-day Mint-funded entries remain 100 diamonds; the host pays rewards.

The inactive migration was qualified through the maintained isolated PostgreSQL accounting runner, including all previous financial probes, all twelve outcomes, all four Upgrade games at 7,500 total diamonds, replay/refusal boundaries and complete row rollback. Real PostgreSQL receipts feed the client service and cryptographic verification tests. Required protected CI and publication provide the final release evidence; production wagers are not part of agent verification.

The reveal exposed a shared text-fitting defect: a scaled opening animation was mistaken for a smaller text zone. Fitting now measures the untransformed content box, with before/after regression coverage. No shared frame artwork changed.
