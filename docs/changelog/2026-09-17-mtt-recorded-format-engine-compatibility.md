# MTT Engine Uses Recorded Tournament Formats

Tournament discovery, launch receipts, resumed managers, table sizing, prize-structure selection, synchronized-break eligibility and Horse context now use the database's immutable format contract. Historical purchased heads-up satellites, SNGs and Spins retain their fixed format. Missing or mismatched format authority refuses the affected admission; intentionally unclassified historical COMPLETING rows retain the existing stored-payout recovery preflight.

Eligibility and funding projections consume the installed admission snapshot, preserving numerical MTT capacity while the database contract remains `legacy-capacity-v1`. The engine supports the prepared unlimited contract without activating it. Physical table seats remain finite. Recurring satellites send both real legacy and scheduled configurations to the database owner; restart inserts carry their immediate source identity. New MTT and satellite fee quotes agree between client and server, without repricing existing funded rows.

The recurring satellite board now applies the database admission horizon before selecting its three targets. Otherwise three expensive targets one hour away occupy every slot after activation, while the scheduled creator requires three hours and refuses all of them. The regression exercises the actual board and creator calls: the old composition fails two cases, while the correction preserves the legacy board and selects the eligible future target. Unknown admission snapshots cannot authorize creation.

Scheduled feeders also retain their existing MTT tournament type. The actual installed legacy classifier rejects the proposed SATELLITE/satellite writer shape, while accepting MTT/satellite with its authored target. Four creator cases reproduce the incompatible type before this correction. An obsolete two-entry draft remains subject to the database's refusal until unlimited admission activates; no replacement numeric cap or historical conversion is introduced.

Validation: 1,028 distinct focused assertions across 48 existing/new test files, with the directly affected creation and resume fixtures supplying real contract-shaped responses and checking completed calls. Both application and server TypeScript checks pass. Newer booked-start, atomic break-resume, balance-continuation and lifecycle diagnostics remain composed. Local tests mock database transport; they do not establish production activation or financial settlement. Protected CI, publication and live behavior remain separate. Six retained creation/edit/restart concurrency cases and the real activation transition still require their authoritative native qualification; replica-switched fixtures are not activation proof.
