# Diamond Spins: Approved Cards, Replay And Daily Statements

The wheel now uses the owner's approved twelve illustrated prize cards and eight upper cards, fitted to the curved wheel sectors. Chip cards print the selected entry's actual chip amount; the upper tiers are MINI 5x, MINOR 10x, MAJOR 25x and GRAND 100x. The matte selector holder follows the rim, and its hinged pointer responds to the passing dividers. Only an upgrade activates the upper selector.

The global header and one framed control panel fit the wheel page at phone, desktop and landscape sizes. Club Chips reads only the current club membership balance. A visible 30-second countdown can start the selected spin once, with Hold available; hidden pages, open details, loading and unavailable play pause it. A completed spin does not silently arm another countdown.

Completed normal and Super bonus games have private replay history and explicit share actions. Public random-token links expose only the finished game, and posting to the social feed uses the canonical duplicate-safe social path. Replay animation preserves the recorded outcome and shows the terminal crash or collision before completion. Future Crash rounds use a slower curve and a 100x ceiling; existing round snapshots remain unchanged.

Daily Diamond accounting keeps entry receipts and Diamond or inventory expenses in durable server custody. It settles one owner wallet net transfer after the Chicago business day, with an itemized statement and one notification. Promo-first and Main-Bank chip funding are unchanged. Claimed Daily Bonus entries remain Mint funded, and their transfer does not consume the player's purchased Diamond lots.

Verification includes real isolated PostgreSQL accounting and all-row rollback, concurrent duplicate settlement, forced notification failure, privacy and canonical social sharing; component, service, pointer and responsive wheel reveal tests are retained in the existing checks. Production wagering and social posting are not performed by these tests. Supabase installation timestamps replace the initially reserved filenames; the qualified SQL and guard-declaration identifiers remain unchanged.
