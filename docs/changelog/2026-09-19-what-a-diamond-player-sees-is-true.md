# What a Diamond player sees is true

Lane cw-diamond-player, branch `agent/cw-diamond-player/fix/what-a-diamond-player-sees-is-true`. Client only: no engine, no database. Every change is asset-aware, and a chip club renders exactly as it did before, which the tests pin sentence by sentence.

## What was wrong

Four things a Diamond Arena player could read were untrue or dead.

1. **The freeroll clock said 0:00.** `useNextDiamondFreeroll` finds no Diamond freeroll (there is no `REGISTERING` tournament with a zero buy-in in the arena club), and both the Diamond Arena card in the lobby carousel and the lobby's identity rail printed that absence as `0:00` under NEXT FREEROLL. On a countdown that reads "starting now". The hook folded "no freeroll", "not answered yet" and "the read failed" into one null, so the surfaces could not have said anything else.

2. **A Diamond seat spoke chips on the way out.** The five eviction explanations ("Your Chips Are Back In Your Wallet"), the generic removal toast, the three refused-leave toasts ("Your Seat And Chips Stay On The Table"), the reconnect warning ("your seat and chips are safe on the server"), the header top-up tooltip ("Add Chips") and the seat-first buy-in sheet ("Taking Your Chips", "Not Enough Chips", "Your Seat And Chips Are Safe") were bare literals in `TablePage.tsx`. A Diamond player who sat out too long was told their chips were back in a wallet they do not have.

3. **The chip bridge was mounted inside the Diamond lobby.** `ArenaAccessBoundary` put the Diamonds To Chips button under the closed-arena notice and `ClubHomePage` mounted `DiamondBustPrompt` ("Out Of Chips? ... Chip Prizes Paid Into Your Club Wallet") for the arena too. Both lead to the host club's diamonds-to-chips wheel, which credits that club's `club_members.chip_balance`. They were silent only because the Diamond host club has no wheel configured, which is a row staff can add; the programme's rule is that the arena club must never acquire chip balances.

4. **Top Up controls at a seat that cannot top up.** The multi-table tab bar rendered "Top Up" and "Auto Top Up" for every table tab. Both emit a bus action the owning table page answers with `seatCanAddFunds`, and at a Diamond tournament seat (reachable since #4938) the page broke out of the switch in silence: two items, two taps, nothing.

## What changed

- `src/hooks/useNextDiamondFreeroll.ts` returns an explicit `state` (`loading`, `scheduled`, `none`, `error`) and `useDiamondFreerollCountdown` prints a word for each shape that is not a clock: `None Scheduled` when the read answered and nothing is ahead, `Unavailable` when the read failed, zeros while loading (the same rule every figure on a club card follows). The tooltip says which. `DiamondArenaCard` and the identity rail (`ClubIdentityCard`, via `ClubHomePage`) print the word with a modifier class sized to fit the half-rail a clock was drawn for, and read it aloud without "Starts In".
- `src/components/table/seatExitCopy.ts` is the one place those exit sentences live, keyed by the seat's asset. `seatUnits` answers Chips for a chip seat and for a seat whose asset has not landed, so a chip seat reads byte for byte what it read before; a Diamond seat reads the same sentence with Diamonds in it. `TablePage.tsx` reads every one of the sentences from it, through `tableState.arenaAsset` or, where the callback is registered once, `tableStateRef.current.arenaAsset`.
- `ArenaAccessBoundary` no longer imports the bridge; its closed-arena notice stands alone. `ClubHomePage` mounts `DiamondBustPrompt` only behind `!isAutomaticArena`, the server's own answer. `DiamondBustPrompt` also asks the boundary itself, switches its `fn_diamond_games_entry` read off inside the arena and renders nothing there. Chip clubs keep both, untouched.
- `TablePage` reports `canAddFunds: seatCanAddFunds(arenaAsset, isTournament)` beside the other tab facts; `MultiTablePage` passes it through on `TabInfo`; `createDefaultMenuSections` drops Top Up and Auto Top Up when it is `false` and keeps both when it is absent.

## How it was verified

- `tests/components/diamondArenaCard.test.tsx` and `tests/unit/arenaIdentityRail.test.tsx`: none, loading, scheduled and error cases at the card, the rail and the hook, with the live read mocked; the seam (`null` is a known none, a number is scheduled) issues no read. Eleven of the new pins fail against the previous source.
- `tests/unit/aDiamondSeatSpeaksDiamonds.test.ts`: every exit sentence derived for both assets, the chip sentences pinned verbatim, the Diamond sentence pinned as the chip sentence with one word changed, Title Case and no em dash, and a source pin that reads every literal in `TablePage.tsx` containing "Chip" or "Chips" out to its enclosing expression and requires that expression to have asked `=== 'diamonds'` first (two remain, both ternaries on the asset). `TableMenu.tsx` names no denomination. The two existing pins that named the old literals (`removedFromTableIsVisible`, `humanIsNeverLeftWaiting`) moved to the module in the same commit.
- `tests/the-diamond-arena-has-no-chip-bridge.law.test.tsx` (registered in `docs/laws.d/`): component tests that the boundary's notice carries no bridge for the arena and that the bust prompt renders nothing and reads nothing inside it while still inviting a felted chip member, plus source pins on all three files. Five of its pins fail against the previous source.
- `tests/unit/tabBarTopUpFollowsSeatCanAddFunds.test.tsx`: the menu builder drops exactly the two items on `false` and keeps them otherwise; the rendered tab bar's hamburger agrees; the answer travels from the table page through `MultiTablePage` to the bar and is derived nowhere else. The `seatCanAddFunds(` census in `aDiamondSeatTopsUp` now lists the seventh caller.
- `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `check-title-case`, `check-ui-text`, `check-painted-text-case` OK; the focused suite (`tests/components/`, `tests/unit/aDiamond*`, `tests/unit/diamond*`, `tests/*diamond*`, the in-tab footer test, the one-name and law-registry laws, and every file touched) green; the full root suite run on the Mac before push.
