# Diamond Phase 10: A Diamond Table Ranks Its Own Hands

September 30, 2026. Phase 10 of the Diamond Arena build programme, line 1 ("Scope histories/replays, earnings, stats, leaderboards and wallet records to Diamond"), the piece [the lines 1, 2 and 6 audit](../DIAMOND-PHASE-10-LINES-1-2-6-2026-09-29.md) left open: the Leaderboard a player opens at a Diamond table. Both arena switches stay closed. Nothing is priced and no Diamond moves.

## What Was Wrong

- **The table Leaderboard had no Diamond source.** It asks `fn_club_leaderboard_period_v2` for the table's club: net profit (winnings minus what the seat put in) over a UTC calendar window, measured from `player_stats` against its daily snapshots. The post-commit projection keeps every Diamond hand out of `player_stats` on purpose (that table has no asset dimension), so at a Diamond table the board was empty for ever.
- **The Diamond stat rows cannot hold a window.** Since #5621 a Diamond hand keeps its own stat rows, and they hold the same figures the chip board ranks (`won_amt`, and `invested_actions + my_blind`, everything the seat put in net of a returned bet). But the forward roll keeps each player's newest 1,000 rows per asset: on September 30 the busiest players, all horses, held 1,135 to 1,163 rows covering two to eight hours. A week, a month or all time cannot be read back from them, and a horse's day often cannot. The chip board survives the same retention because `player_stats` keeps running totals and a snapshot marks each window's start.

## The Database

Migration `a_diamond_table_ranks_its_own_hands` (`20260930043000`, md5 `79b61a54fa9131b8c98fd4853ad91aa0`), rehearsed, then applied and recorded at 04:37 UTC:

- `ca_diamond_player_day`, new: the Diamond twin of those running totals, one row per player per UTC day (`hands_dealt`, `total_winnings`, `total_losses`, `sum_big_blind`, the `player_stats` names). A day is the grain the chip board's snapshots measure windows in. Private: row security on, no browser grant.
- The post-commit projection folds each Diamond cash hand into it, by asserted substitution with the live md5 (`daf6adcc7fb0784004f60a28a70b97cb`) pinned and the reverse proved. It reads that hand's own Diamond stat rows, whichever writer wrote them, so a hand is folded once, when it is projected, and the retention can never take it back. It keeps chip Projection 2's population: cash hands, seats with a profile, horses included. A chip hand never enters the branch.
- `fn_diamond_arena_leaderboard_period(period, limit, offset)`, new: the chip profit board over those totals, clause for clause. The same window (`fn_leaderboard_period_window`), the same score, the same order and ties, the same activity rule (hands, a tournament won, winnings or losses in the window; a tournament won is counted from the arena's own events by the rule of `trg_tournament_completed_stats`), and the same rank change against the standings before yesterday. It returns the chip board's columns except `total_rake`, which a stat row does not carry. Fixture accounts are left out and horses are not: "Fixture accounts are not players; horses are" ([DIAMOND-RULINGS](../DIAMOND-RULINGS.md)). Signed-in players and the service role may call it; a visitor may not.
- `fn_club_leaderboard_period_v2` is not touched (its md5 is asserted), so a chip table's board is what it was.

The rehearsal (one rolled-back transaction on production) dealt three Diamond hands at the arena's NLH 1/2 table through the real post-commit projection: one in August, one on September 22 whose stat rows were written first, the way the forward roll can reach a hand before the projection, and one today with a person, a horse, a certification fixture and a seat with no profile. Eight Diamond stat rows; seven day rows in whole Diamonds, the September hand folded once, the fixture folded (it has a profile) and the seat with no profile not. Read as an ordinary signed-in player, every period ranked as the chip board would: today the horse +24 first and the person -22 second; this week the same, with the person's rank change -1 against the tie before yesterday; this month the horse +18 first (+1) and the person -16 second (-1); all time the horse +16 first (+1) and the person -14 second (-1). The fixture and the seat with no profile were off the board, a visitor was refused, the busiest chip club's board and the players' chip rows were unchanged, the arena's chip board stayed empty, and the Diamond supply identity did not move. The fixture ran in 0.58 s.

## The Client

`TablePage` reads the table's arena from the one source it already has (`tableState.arenaAsset`, parsed by `parseTableArenaIdentity`). At a Diamond table the Leaderboard asks `LeaderboardService.getDiamondArenaLeaderboard`, shows Loading Rankings while it asks, No Rankings Yet when the answer is empty, and Rankings Unavailable when the server could not answer, never an empty board in its place; its subtitle names the unit (Winnings In Diamonds), and figures print in whole Diamonds. At a chip table the arena test is false, the same call is made and the panel receives exactly the props it did.
