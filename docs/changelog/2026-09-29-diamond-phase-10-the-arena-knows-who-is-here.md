# Diamond Phase 10: The Arena Knows Who Is Here

September 30, 2026. Phase 10 of the Diamond Arena build programme: line 3 ("Show real member/online/seated/table counts with meaningful zero/error states") and the last open item of line 6 ("Verify no agent panels, union menus, chip metrics or synthetic players appear as real activity"). Both arena switches stay closed. Nothing is written and nothing is priced.

## What Was Wrong

- **Online Now usually read Unavailable.** The arena's Players page shows four figures from `fn_diamond_arena_counts` (#5616). Members, At Tables and Tables were real. Online was a number only while World Hub's messenger presence (`profiles.is_online` with `last_seen` inside five minutes) could see the person asking, because nothing in Club Arena writes that feed. No player's `last_seen` is newer than September 27, 18:34 UTC, so a player who uses only the arena read Online as Unavailable.
- **The global menu offered Create Union inside the arena.** Ruling 16 keeps unions, agents and commissions out of the Diamond Arena, and it has no union, but the menu's "Create Union" quick action was shown there to an account allowed to create one.

## The Database

Migration `the_arena_knows_who_is_here` (`20260930044500`, md5 `11cde6b42e8a3f43159baac66e8a7870`), rehearsed, then applied and recorded at 04:31 UTC:

- `fn_diamond_arena_counts` is redefined in full over its pinned live md5 (`62640c4a82bd621861fc6d0b84d5dd86`), with the same signature, definer and grants. Members, tables and seated are word for word what they were. New live md5 `e26051a45722a0660c36f231abdaf936`.
- **Online** is now the players who are here right now by any of three live sources: a live Realtime feed, the messenger's presence (the chip roster's rule) or a live seat at an open arena table. It is counted through `fn_diamond_arena_is_player` (fixtures and deleted accounts out, horses in) and returned as a number only; no name or id leaves the function.
- **What it can tell.** The sources say who has the platform open (Club Arena, or World Hub), not which club page they are looking at. Every player is a member of the arena, so for the arena that is who is online, the same way a chip roster counts a member online wherever they are. The names on the Players page keep their own presence marks, which still follow the messenger; this change adds a count, not a list.
- **When it says Unavailable.** The caller is online by definition, so if none of the sources shows the caller they are not seeing where players are, and Online is NULL (`presence_not_reported`) rather than an undercount. Without a caller it is NULL (`no_caller_to_witness`), and a read that fails is NULL with its SQLSTATE. A real zero is printed as 0.
- The migration proves at apply that the register exists and can be read, and that the two feeds every signed-in page holds (`table_waitlist`, `notifications`) are in the Realtime publication.

**Rehearsal** (one rolled-back transaction on production, 04:24 UTC, `REHEARSAL OK`): an ordinary player with no feed read Online as NULL (`presence_not_reported`) with members 1,149, tables 17 and seated 0, exactly what the pinned text answers; given a live feed (two rows standing in for the waitlist and notifications feeds, inside the rolled-back transaction), the same player read Online 2, equal to an independent recount (1 without them), and feeds for a certification fixture, an account that does not exist, the service role, a visitor and a malformed id were not counted; another player with no feed still read NULL; a fixture asking was answered and not counted; a visitor was refused; a player holding a real live feed read the recount. The call took 339 ms; the text it replaces took 401 ms asked without a caller. The members rule is most of both (about 310 ms), and the new read of the register takes under 1 ms.

**After the apply** (a fresh read-only transaction, 04:31 UTC): the migration is recorded under its version with its exact text, the `@live-proof` holds, the grants are unchanged (authenticated and service role; no visitor). A player holding a live feed read `{"members": 1149, "online": 1, "seated": 0, "tables": 17}` in 375 ms; before this change the same player read Online as Unavailable (their messenger `last_seen` is September 27). A player with no feed read Online as NULL with `presence_not_reported`.

## The Client

- **The Players page asks an unknown Online once more.** A page loaded cold can ask before its own feeds have registered, so an unknown Online stays "..." and is asked again after three seconds; the second answer is final and changes Online alone (`DiamondPlayersPage.tsx`, `DIAMOND_ONLINE_RECHECK_MS`).
- **The menu offers no union inside the arena.** "Create Union" is hidden while the arena is the club in the menu's context: the route's club (path or `?club=`), that club resolved to its id, or the arena's lobby opened as a tab over a table, judged by the client's one arena identity, `isDiamondArenaClubKey` (`src/components/navigation/menuUnionDoor.ts`). "Create Club", "Invite Players", every other entry and every chip club are unchanged.
- Measured the way CI does (`vite build`, then `scripts/ci/bundle-size.mjs`), paired builds with the same dependencies: the whole app grows by 186 bytes gzipped (2,942,726 to 2,942,912 bytes, 2874 of 2880 kB), and initial load is unchanged at 314 of 320 kB.
