# Diamond Phase 9: A Diamond Spin Draws A Whole Prize

Status: the Spin tournament format (three seats, a multiplier drawn at launch, a prize pool of that multiplier times the buy-in) is built in Diamonds - its draw and its reserve - behind the switch. Diamond Tournaments Remain Refused At Every Door (`tournaments_enabled` is false), and no reserve source is authorized, so every Diamond Spin is also refused by name at creation and at the draw until Dan names one. No real entry has been written against a real wallet. This is the tournament format, not the Diamond Spins bonus wheel, which is untouched.

## The Database

Migration `a_diamond_spin_draws_a_whole_prize` (applied as `20260929163000`; stored text byte-identical to the repo file, md5 `4dac22680e9c10c1f3a4536db0b3ced1`):

- `poker_diamond_spin_reserve_source`: the authorization, one row or none, written only by a values migration that quotes Dan. It names the account that underwrites a Spin (the only one the code can move against is the house, `ca_diamond_house`, which holds 0 today) and the most one Spin may take from it. This migration writes no row.
- `poker_diamond_spin_contracts`: the multiplier table a Diamond Spin was created with, validated at its buy-in, pinned by sha256, immutable. The draw rolls over this table and nothing else; the engine's compiled manifest cannot change it. No foreign key to `tournaments` (adding one would lock every tournament write for the migration's length); the creation door writes the contract in the same transaction as its tournament.
- The rule, `fn_poker_diamond_spin_contract`: the chip draw authority's manifest rules (the expectation equals three seats less the chip rake rate, 2.76), the estate ladder for every multiplier the terminal prices from it, the Spin blind ladder, and the whole-Diamond rule - every tier's pool and every place of its ladder a whole Diamond at the configured buy-in - each refused by name. The published table (`spin_tier_spec` with the estate ladders) is whole at a buy-in of one Diamond and so at every whole buy-in; the migration's closing block proves it.
- The creation door admits `spin` through `fn_poker_diamond_create_spin` under the chip seat-first door's rules (three seats; the buy-in the whole charge, no fee; a multiplier drawn, never configured; NLH, PLO4, PLO5 or PLO6; no key a Spin would not keep), and refuses a mismatch by name. It requires the authorized source, within its cap, holding the table's full cover. A Spin key on any other format is refused (`diamond_tournament_spin_requires_a_spin_format`).
- The draw, `fn_poker_diamond_spin_draw`, is the Diamond arm of the one Spin authority: `fn_spin_draw_and_settle_atomic` routes a Diamond Spin to it after its own lease, launch-receipt and freeze proofs. It proves three whole entries in custody, the pinned contract and the authorized source (cap and cover again), rolls `extensions.gen_random_bytes` over the pinned table, floors the pool to the unit and proves the residue is zero, then moves the difference from the three entries against the source in whole Diamonds: a pool above them is underwritten into custody (a house burn on the register and a player-side register mint per custody share); a pool below them releases the surplus to the source (the drain's player-side burn and a house mint). It writes the immutable `spin_draw_receipts` row in the chip receipt's shape and stamps the tournament. Owner-only.
- The banks carry the legs: two ledger kinds (`spin_underwrite`, `spin_surplus`, prize bank only, per custody row); the Diamond escrow reads them into the prize bank; the chip-facing router keeps the chip shadow convention; the shadow opens with them as `reserve_in` / `reserve_out`; the drain holds an underwritten share as prize and names a surplus `arena_spin_surplus`.
- No chip reserve touches a Diamond Spin: `fn_spin_draw_and_settle` and `fn_spin_settle_game` refuse one by name (`diamond_spin_is_drawn_by_its_own_authority`), the unbooked sweep skips one, and the third seat books no chip entry for one.
- The Spin contract trigger and the launch completion read the Diamond draw (`fn_poker_diamond_spin_draw_proof`); the refund door refuses an entry whose Spin has been drawn (`diamond_spin_entry_already_booked`), so neither a withdrawal nor a cancellation can unwind a drawn Spin.

Every chip edit is an asserted substitution with the live md5 pinned and the reverse substitution proved; every Diamond door is pinned and redefined in full with the same signature. The creation door moved twice under this work (the satellite seat, `20260929161500`, and the chip-legs fence, `20260929160000`); each time the change was rebuilt on the live body, re-pinned and re-rehearsed before the apply. No price, rate, budget or source is invented.

## The Engine

- `TournamentManagerBase` (the Spin paid-gate at start): a chip Spin's payment evidence is its refund entitlement; a Diamond Spin's is its custody entry row in the Diamond tournament ledger, which the gate now reads for a tournament whose unit is the Diamond. The rest of the gate and the reveal anchor are unchanged.
- `spinLaunchParking`: the draw's four data refusals (`diamond_spin_reserve_source_not_authorized`, `diamond_spin_reserve_over_its_authorized_cap`, `diamond_spin_reserve_cannot_cover_the_table`, `diamond_spin_contract_missing`) park the launch; each lifts only when its data changes.

## The Rehearsal

One rolled-back transaction through the real doors, before the apply, with the house given a balance by hand and a source row written inside the transaction only: with no source authorized the creation door refused a Spin by name; with a source row (cap 5,000 Diamonds) a Spin key on a scheduled event, a bounty, a payout ladder, six seats, a configured multiplier and an FLH Spin were each refused by name, as were a table off the approved edge, a 10x paying 70/30 against the estate's 80/20 and a blind ladder without its continuation; 2.5x and 3.5x were refused at a buy-in of 3 (a 7.5-Diamond pool) and admitted at 2; a whole 7x pool whose 80/20 ladder splits a Diamond was refused at 1; a table beyond the cap and one beyond the house's balance were refused. A Spin at the published table (worst excess 970, cover 1,500 at a buy-in of 10) filled with three Diamond seats (three whole entries in custody, nothing booked chip-side); its launch began and the draw refused by name - no source, over the cap, without cover - writing nothing; then it drew 2x: a pool of 20, the surplus of 10 released from custody to the house as a registered pair, the receipt and the stamped row in the chip shape. The replay moved nothing; the row's pool and multiplier could not be rewritten; the chip draw, the chip settle and the chip entry booking refused it; the contract refused an update; the launch completed on the Diamond draw proof. A second Spin at a 1x/4x table drew 4x: the house underwrote 10 into custody as a registered pair. Both finished through the real terminal paying whole Diamonds (20 and 40 to their winners), every bank, custody row and obligation closed and the shadow's reserve_in and reserve_out equal to the legs; the house held exactly what the legs moved. A third Spin, drawn and held before its launch completed, refused its unregistration and its cancellation by name (`diamond_spin_entry_already_booked`). The supply identity was whole in production before the fixture (difference 0), and nothing any Spin did moved it.

## Dan's Question

Which Diamond account underwrites a Diamond Spin - pays the part of a drawn prize pool above its three entries, and receives the entries above a smaller pool? The only one the code can move against today is the house (`ca_diamond_house`), which holds 0 Diamonds. If it is the house: how is the house funded (may the Mint issue into it, and how much), and what is the most one Spin may take from it (`max_underwrite_per_spin`, in Diamonds)? The answer is one values migration inserting the `poker_diamond_spin_reserve_source` row that quotes the ruling. Two design choices ride on it and are Dan's to overrule: a Diamond Spin requires the source to cover its whole table (no tier is locked out, where the chip authority locks out tiers its pool cannot cover), and no per-game rake is booked - the table's edge is realised against the source over volume.

## What Is Still Not Here

- A played-Spin launch recovery (a launch interrupted after hands were played) proves itself from chip evidence only: `fn_prove_played_spin_launch_recovery` reads the refund entitlements, the chip ledger and the chip reserve draw, so a Diamond Spin in that state answers `played_spin_launch_recovery_unproven` and stays parked. Its Diamond proof is not built.
- The lobby advertises a filling Spin as "Win Up To" the chip table's top multiplier (`SPIN_MAX_MULTIPLIER`); a Diamond Spin created with a table of its own should show its pinned top multiplier (the creation door returns `max_multiplier`). At the published table the two agree.
- No staff client creates a Diamond Spin; the Spin door takes only Spin keys, so the estate's full builder config would be refused by name.
