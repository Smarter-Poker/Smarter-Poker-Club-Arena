# Create Club Certificate Cleans Every State

## What Existed

The production Create Club certificate exercised the owner reset as a committed write, then depended on cleanup helpers that rejected a freshly created board for several minutes and did not recognize the exact successful post-reset state. Interrupted runs could therefore leave reserved certification clubs and accounts behind. The harness also trusted the reset response's own removed-ID lists, accepted an `already_gone` cleanup result without proving the 100,000-chip retirement, and did not read back asset deletion.

## What Changed

- The three reserved welcome-board cleanup helpers now admit a fresh board only when the complete twelve-game allowlist, zero activity, provenance, lease and F06 custody guards all pass.
- A separate private post-reset preparer recognizes only the exact reserved certification identity, owner and receipt lineage, complete package graph, returned BBJ and Spin principal, terminal unused games, exact tournament origins, zero-use Diamond configuration and no player activity. It removes only proved fixture state and preserves financial ledgers and configuration history.
- The owner reset is now executed as the authenticated certification player inside one direct PostgreSQL transaction. Its complete graph and zero-state are independently read before the transaction is always rolled back.
- Startup and final cleanup inventory every club owned by the reserved account, require the guarded retirement to report exactly 100,000 chips, verify clubs and logo assets are absent, and fail closed on any refusal, unreadable result or unknown state.
- The production certification job has a bounded 60-minute budget so its exact-live wait, direct certificate, published-browser flow and `always()` cleanup can all finish.

## Why

Certification must prove that a brand-new player can create, inspect, reset and retire a fully preloaded club without changing real clubs, players or chips, and it must leave no synthetic residue even after an interrupted earlier run. A response generated by the mutation is not independent proof of what existed, and a successful functional check is not complete if its fixture cleanup is ambiguous.
