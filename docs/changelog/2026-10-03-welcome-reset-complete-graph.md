# Welcome Reset Uses One Stable, Data-Safe Package Graph

The production Create Club certificate exposed two independent reset defects. It read the expected club graph before entering the same global settlement lane used by incremental Spin and Sit-N-Go materialization, so a legitimate opening row could appear between the preimage and the reset receipt. The reset functions installed by `20261002152207` had also dropped `tournament_schedule_spawns` backlinks while retaining a broad tournament-type predicate. That could leave an opening schedule tournament outside preview and reset, or absorb an unrelated owner-created tournament into a destructive reset.

The certificate now takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock(530090,1)` and then locks the club, its active welcome-package items, the selected package schedules, their schedule-spawn rows, and every same-club schedule/Spin/SNG/Satellite tournament that could enter the package graph before reading the independent preimage. The reset authority uses the same ordering before it derives the final graph. Those transaction-scoped locks remain held through the owner-authenticated reset, exact receipt comparison, financial readback, and rollback. Exact comparisons remain fail-closed and the mutation is never retried after an ambiguous outcome.

Migration `20261003021809` rewrites both the reset preview and unwind authorities from their exact installed source digests. The selected tournament graph contains opening schedule tournaments reached either by a direct `schedule_id` or by a `tournament_schedule_spawns` backlink; it also contains only `SATELLITE` feeders whose `satellite_target_id` or legacy `satellite_target` points to one of those selected package schedule tournaments. It does not select tournaments merely because their type is `SPIN`, `SNG`, or `SATELLITE`, so unrelated custom tournaments in all three formats remain intact.

The only unscheduled board rows selected by identity are the canonical pristine twelve: `NLH Heads-Up 1` and `PLO4 Heads-Up 1` (SNG, 0.95 buy-in plus 0.05 fee, two seats, 1,000 chips); `NLH Heads-Up 1 Turbo` and `PLO4 Heads-Up 1 Turbo` (the same SNG economics and seats, 300 chips); `1 Chip Spin NLH`, `1 Chip Spin PLO4`, `1 Chip Spin PLO5`, and `1 Chip Spin PLO6` (SPIN, 1 buy-in and no fee, three seats, 300 chips); and the matching four `1 Chip Deep Stack Spin` rows (SPIN, 1 buy-in and no fee, three seats, 1,000 chips). Each match also requires the pristine owner scope and lifecycle shape. Zero canonical rows is a valid not-yet-materialized board; otherwise all twelve identities must appear exactly once. A partial board or an identical owner-created lookalike makes preview fail closed and makes mutation refuse with `WELCOME_PACKAGE_BOARD_LINEAGE_AMBIGUOUS` instead of guessing which row to cancel.

The migration is single-transaction, replay-safe, reverse-substitution checked, preserves catalog metadata and ACLs, and changes no club, game, player, wallet, chip, receipt, or consent row. The native PostgreSQL 17 harness installs the actual production reset chain through the new migration and covers direct and backlink schedule tournaments, target-linked satellite feeders, all exact canonical board rows, identical-lookalike refusal, preservation of unrelated custom SPIN/SNG/SATELLITE tournaments, target-materialization and selected-row-update races, exact receipt arrays, a receipt identical to the returned result, conserved 100,000 opening chips, same-operation replay, later-operation replay, and atomic refusal when an operation ID is reused for another club.

Production certification then caught a fixture-schema blind spot before any player-facing reset: the legacy `tournaments.satellite_target` column is UUID in production, while the native fixture had declared it as text. The installed predicate consequently compared text to UUID and PostgreSQL refused the reset-impact read with `42883`. Migration `20261003040449` forward-repairs both preview and unwind authorities to compare the current and legacy satellite targets as UUIDs, validates both target-column catalog types, and preserves function ownership, ACLs, volatility, security, and configuration. The native fixture now matches production, installs and replays the repair, and exercises both target columns through preview and rollback-protected unwind. The failed certificate retired its isolated club, returned the exact 100,000-chip opening grant, and retired its reserved account; no real club, player, wallet, game, or chip balance was changed.
