# Stats Launch Re-Audit

The Club Arena Stats experience now keeps every owner and shared view bound to the active player, club, asset, range, and request identity. Multi-club comparison remains sortable only by fields the contract can truthfully supply, malformed saved layouts are normalized, privacy-sensitive owner details stay closed until the saved preference is verified, and stale asynchronous responses cannot repaint a newer scope.

The Study Workspace now reports bounded collection coverage per collection, preserves evidence and goal provenance, and prevents duplicate mutations. Exact cash sessions, financial reporting, positional analysis, evidence links, and retry states retain their existing behavior while using distinct approved Club Arena Console families, whole compact chip values, touch-sized actions, and console-glass dividers instead of nested generic frames.

Database hardening preserves void and correction history, serializes same-key revisions, synchronizes repaired legacy projections, keeps nullable incomplete session closes honest, includes sessions opened before the selected range, and retains the Diamond-table exclusion. The three native PostgreSQL 17 Stats fixtures are wired into the accounting CI shard so these contracts run on every affected protected candidate.
