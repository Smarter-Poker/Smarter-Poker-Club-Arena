## Scope and result

The enabled catalogue has **80 items**: 79 specs rendered by `ThrowablePlayer` and the separately implemented `boxing_glove`. This audit enumerates each entry below and adds a regression check for picker coverage, rig source, animation beats, cue mapping, cue timing, and both shipped audio formats. It closes the source inventory and file-presence gap in `scripts/dev/throwable-coverage.mjs`.

The existing reference implementation remains the comparator: the water gun’s large, avatar-covering splash and timed water loop, and the boxing glove’s multi-hit `KnockoutFlurry` with its timed synthesized impacts. Rigged effects share the 300-unit viewBox / 100-units-per-avatar coordinate contract, the common payload wrapper, and measured timing in each spec. Cue gain remains authored per item; it is not normalized to one loudness.

**Source and media audit: 80/80 rows mapped; 79/79 rigged effects have animation specs and at least one real cue; boxing glove has its separate animated and synthesized-sound path.** The full app-rendered screenshot/playback pass remains unverified: this session’s browser policy rejected local harness pages and explicitly disallowed alternate routes around that restriction. The darkroom generated its real-component harness for 79 rigs but captured no screenshots. Therefore this record does not claim a visual screenshot comparison or human listening test for each row, and it cannot conclude that no further visual/audio improvement is possible.
