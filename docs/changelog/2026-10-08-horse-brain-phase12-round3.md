Record: [horse-brain-phase12-round3-2026-10-08.md](../horse-brain-phase12-round3-2026-10-08.md).

- **Cause, from the locked matrices and development-seed attribution:** the round-1 Short Deck, Crazy Pineapple, FLH and FLO8 packs replaced every eligible reference decision and lost on every matrix. Their big blind folded hands the reference profitably defends (the shared defence bar ignores the price), they called and value-bet postflop on sampled equity measured against a range that ignores board contact, and they three-bet less than the reference profitably does.
- **Fix:** `REMAINING_VARIANT_PACKS[variant].round3` and `evaluateRemainingVariantPolicy` keep the reference action everywhere (every postflop decision included) except declared preflop spots that won on development seeds against both the horse population and the human-calibrated population (`human-calibrated-v1-20261008`): open the heads-up button and defend the big blind wider in every pack, and in fixed limit also fold reference first-in opens below the pack's open bar from middle, cutoff and button (three or more dealt) and steal wider from the folded-to small blind. Versions `short-deck-round3-v1`, `crazy-pineapple-round3-v1`, `fixed-limit-holdem-round3-v1`, `fixed-limit-omaha8-round3-v1`. Budgets, eligibility refusals, input binding, P12-B economics and the `illegal_candidate` guard are unchanged.
- **Measured and removed:** no-limit open tightening and a no-limit flop check below 0.95 sampled equity beat the reference against horses but lost to it against the human-calibrated population (Short Deck -69.54, Pineapple -54.61 bb/100): horse-versus-horse exploits, not upgrades.
