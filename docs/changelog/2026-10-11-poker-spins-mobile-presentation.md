Validation includes real browser geometry at 320, 375, 393, 430, 768 and 1440px, PLO4/5/6 live and tabled hands, two/three-player layouts, home-indicator clearance, no plate/utility/card overlap, and static sizing across footer collapse. Updated existing component/source contracts cover the new presentation and preserve server result/timing and currency boundaries.
