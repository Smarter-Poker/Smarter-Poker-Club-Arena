# Lightning Correlation Uses Measured Paired Hands

The pilot's first high timing signal reported correlation 0.9949, but only one window with four shared hands supplied that coefficient. `fn_lightning_integrity_report` weighted window correlations by sequential actions while testing their result against shared-hand counts from other windows with no correlation measurement. That let a short measurement borrow sample size, and also discarded the information needed to detect correlation across many short windows. This is an evidence aggregation defect; the observations do not establish uniform horse timing as its cause.

The engine now sends additive moments of per-hand mean decision times, in seconds, and the count of hands where both players actually made timed decisions. A hand spanning a window boundary contributes once when it ends. The database pools these moments and applies the existing 20-hand correlation sample floor to those measured pairs. It retains the existing sequential-action and fast-follow thresholds, authentication, hidden-card refusal, evidence retention and duplicate-window behavior. Legacy coefficients cannot establish a new correlation finding without the missing measurements. Existing findings remain open and unchanged for operator review. No horse is excluded and no action timing, seat, session or money record is changed.

Both new engine regressions failed before the repair and passed afterward. The real PostgreSQL 17 chain reproduces an under-supported positive and a missed genuinely correlated pair before the change. Afterward it refuses the borrowed-sample finding, detects 20 genuinely paired correlated hands across ten short windows, rejects negative correlation despite a contradictory legacy coefficient, and still detects fast-follow evidence. Duplicate windows add neither evidence rows nor findings. These witnesses run after the existing twenty Phase 11 qualification sections in the same maintained CI command.

Install the qualified database migration before its containing engine release. Old runtime reports continue to supply fast-follow evidence but cannot supply the missing paired correlation measurement. This change does not resolve old findings, certify new pilot play, or override the open A/A calibration and launch gates.
