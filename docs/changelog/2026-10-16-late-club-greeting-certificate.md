# Lobby certificate owns late Club Messages

The maintained lobby helper now handles Club Messages that arrive after initial preparation or after Diamond invitation retirement. It finds the real named dialog and takes its public Enter Lobby control; the obsolete global Close Club Message selector no longer supplies readiness.

The greeting owner registers before the Diamond owner, respects the real popup stack, and retains bounded real-control clicks and hidden checks. Diamond retirement drains existing callbacks, preserves original failures, settles any known open doors without racing duplicate clicks, and leaves the greeting owner available for later reads. Unknown covering layers still fail rather than being forced through.

The real-Modal fixture now uses Enter Lobby and can open either door after preparation. Browser regression coverage preserves both stack orders, delayed offer controls, failed declines, in-flight retirement and missing-action checks, and adds late greeting, direct retirement with both doors open, and unknown-cover refusal. The exact standalone late-greeting case fails on the original helper with modal pointer interception; all 32 affected browser checks pass in Chromium and WebKit, and all five connected invitation-owner unit cases pass. Product code, preference writes, persistence assertions and production data are unchanged.
