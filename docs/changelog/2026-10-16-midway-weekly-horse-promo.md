# Midway weekly horse promo distribution

The owner requested a weekly distribution of half of Midway Union's Promo Wallet, divided equally among active horses whose chip balance is below 10,000. The owner explicitly confirmed that each club balance is assessed separately. A horse can qualify in one member club while its wallet in another club is ineligible, or receive one share in each of two qualifying clubs.

The existing weekly accounting completion transaction now performs this distribution through `fn_promo_disburse`. The existing enabled weekly close admits Monday at 04:00 America/Chicago. The first eligible period ends October 19, 2026. No historical payout or immediate off-schedule payment is introduced. The trusted no-request pg_cron authorization convention is aligned with `fn_caller_is_engine`; browser owner authorization remains in the existing disbursement door.

Eligibility requires an enabled horse profile and an active, approved/active, lifecycle-active club membership. The tested threshold is each membership's `chip_balance`, without combining clubs or capping the resulting payment at 10,000. Funds land as ordinary chips, matching the existing Promo disbursement rule. Half of an odd cent stays in the source; remaining distributable cents are apportioned in deterministic club/user order so shares differ by at most one cent. No eligible recipients or a balance below two cents produces a recorded zero payout and leaves the money in the wallet.

The source wallet and eligible memberships are locked before the split. One receipt per union/week and one recipient per club/user/week prevent duplicate payments. A failed payout aborts the close's completion and all payout legs. Existing chip transactions, union wallet transactions and chip ledger guards remain authoritative. New tables are private with service read access; the trigger function has no application EXECUTE grants. There is no new scheduler, client publication or engine replacement.

Native PostgreSQL qualification loads the maintained captured financial schema and its existing catalog checks, overlays captured current disbursement/calendar authorities, and exercises the exact migration and trigger. Only future weekly due-time admission is simulated; the money/ledger functions are real. Production verification distinguishes installed/enabled behavior and rolled-back probes from the first natural weekly execution.

Required CI integration adds one native qualification step to the maintained accounting shard. The six current workflow input fingerprints were refreshed for that additive change; their financial sources, historical fingerprints and qualification statuses are preserved. Local qualification passed the exact installer/rollback/replay, eligibility, conservation, authorization, platform freeze, late rollback, concurrent close/restart, historical/unfinished close and tiny-budget cases. The native scale case paid 2,002 wallets with all deferred financial guards in about 12 seconds on this Mac; this is fixture timing, not a production timing guarantee.
