# Platform Ads Are Not Club Settings

Clubs and unions cannot change or turn off Smarter.Poker advertisements. Banner Displays now manages only the club-owned banner, with a read-only notice explaining platform ad ownership. The former ad visibility component and save function are removed, and the lobby ignores legacy ad visibility settings. Platform creative resolution, tracking, and other placements retain their existing behavior.

The shared Shark Club Championship Series campaign is retired as the automatic lobby/editor fallback. A club without its own banner displays no club banner. Known retired campaign URLs are also suppressed if supplied as an old banner value. Clubs, including union clubs, can still upload or remove their own banner through the existing owner and stale-write guards. Published asset history is preserved.

Scoped regression and the maintained production club certificate verify there are no club ad controls, the old shared campaign is absent, and club-banner upload/removal leaves platform ads intact. Delivery and live union-club screenshots are recorded in the existing task checkpoint.
