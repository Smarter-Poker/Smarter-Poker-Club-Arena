# MTT population qualification before HUD observation

The hosted MTT certificate admitted a running tournament with four players, completed its hand and reconnect proof, and then found three players before starting the two-context HUD witness. All ten clock reads correctly rejected the unchanged four-player eligibility floor.

A distinct pre-HUD population-attrition outcome now permits bounded selection of another eligible subject. It requires the same running tournament, an admitted population of at least four, a fresh `insufficient_players` result with fewer than four players, and an otherwise valid clock. Every transport, ownership and spectator invariant must still pass before replacement. Natural completion remains a separate outcome.

Replacement uses the existing attempt limit, shared case deadline and excluded-table list, and repeats the full proof on the new subject. Missing, paused, malformed, unreadable and unknown subjects still fail. The existing natural-clock sizing rules remain in place, with no additional timeout extension mechanism. The four-player floor, clock caps, reconnect budgets, assertions, required coverage and production behavior are unchanged.

Focused qualification tests cover the actual four-to-three transition and invalid, changed, paused, terminal and malformed inputs. No production data or engine changes are involved.
