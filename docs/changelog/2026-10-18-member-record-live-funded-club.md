# Member records follow funded hands and live source changes

The reported Shark Club member had 12 hand facts and -100.00 cash net filed under Midway Union, while the immutable buy-in receipt and 16.55 rake belonged to Shark Club. The member record and win/loss readers filtered on the table club, so they displayed zero hands and net. Wallet invalidation refreshed wallets only; identity, presence, role, downline, activity, watch location and the separately mounted win/loss card could remain stale.

Funded member daily facts now project the club recorded on the table's buy-in receipt, tournament entry, or rake attribution, with the original fact club as the final fallback. The insert/update/delete transaction maintains the projection. A separate one-time snapshot initialization subtracts already captured deltas and retains concurrent additions, without locking the hot facts table or rewriting money, memberships or hand facts. Accounting cutoff generations prevent a later launch reset from exposing earlier totals. Member detail, performance and both win/loss readers use the indexed daily projection. A raw per-member scan was rejected after a busiest-player read took 23.46 seconds.

Private identity-only invalidations originate from hand facts, seat changes, profile fields, membership/notes, fee ledgers and chip transactions. Existing agent-wallet signals remain in use. The page coalesces events, reconciles on subscription recovery/focus/online, preserves note drafts and downline expansion, and refreshes win/loss and the table locator. Every authoritative read still checks the viewer's permissions. No polling, repair job, scheduled release or engine replacement is introduced.

Regression evidence is retained in the member-record PostgreSQL fixture and component tests. Native qualification reproduces zero-before/twelve-after, verifies funded attribution, ranges, statistics, both win/loss readers, access refusal, private payloads, source signals and migration replay refusal. The existing required Data Console PostgreSQL job invokes the fixture; fixture-only changes select that job. Publication and installed/live evidence are tracked in the task checkpoint until directly verified.
