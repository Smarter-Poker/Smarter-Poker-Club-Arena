# Every earning-time payer remains responsible for the same player entitlement

The original weekly calculator refused players whose agent changed within the
week. The independent October 12–19 read found 150 such player/club positions;
the real split checker reproduced its historical-payer refusal. Selecting one
current agent would charge the wrong payer and erase earning-time agreements.

The original calculator now records a split payer on the same single weekly
period. Every source keeps its own observed rate and payer, including the
existing direct-owner 70 percent rule. The player's entitlement remains
`round(sum(source rake * observed effective rate), 2)`. The original certificate
insert atomically records immutable payer contracts. Fractions of a cent go by
descending remainder and then payer identity, preserving that exact total.

The existing R3 transaction locks and checks every funder before moving any
chips. It records each historical payer's funded wallet/ledger leg, native paid
invoice and credit receipt under the one original payout claim. Single-payer
behavior and zero contributors remain included. Direct and cached source
proofs, replay checks, balance checks, and retired-account reserves understand
the same contracts. No second payout route, clearing wallet or repair job exists.

paid period back when the whole round was unfinished. That guard now accepts
the original paid period/payout/certificate evidence for its exact closed-week
scope. It still requires every funded leg, invoice and receipt, and does not
mark the overall week complete. The original owner can resume remaining
payments without paying completed periods again.

calendar dates from timestamps in the selected report timezone.
