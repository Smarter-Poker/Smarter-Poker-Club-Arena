that can naturally depart. The owner delegated the choice of preparing an

existing internal players; it does not execute membership, funding, assignment
or operator controls.

with game load at most two and no active cash exposure. The selected 18 have
valid existing Auth identities, membership capacity and at most two known

already daily-capped, have no active chair or cash session, and have zero
pilot escrow, holds, cashouts, pool sessions or event obligations. Fresh
eligibility and wallet readback are still required at execution.

amount per incoming player. Its gross funding scope is 3,600 chips; observed

reduction of 1,118.53. This is a prepared scope proposal, not a financial
authorization or a claim those balances will remain unchanged. The two
previous roster manifests and all historical operations remain retained.
