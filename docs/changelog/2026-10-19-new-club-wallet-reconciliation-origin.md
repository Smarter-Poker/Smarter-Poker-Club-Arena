# New club wallet reconciliation origin

Deep Stack and Lightning have no legacy player-baseline rows. The conservation report therefore reads no cutoff or wallet legs for them and reports every current player as incomplete. Independent full-journal reconciliation shows their player wallets balance exactly; the missing coverage is in the report.

For a club created after the original journal-baseline fence, with no explicit player baselines, the report now reconciles wallets from zero at club creation. It reads every booked leg from that origin through the existing raw/sealed-day path. The response labels this basis and its origin timestamp. No historical baseline is inserted, no amount is inferred from the current balance, and no chips move. An unjournaled opening balance is a mismatch, not a new opening amount. Older clubs with unknown openings, invalid/future creation times and members predating the club remain incomplete. Explicit baselines retain precedence.

The authorized roles, authentication, bank-by-entity reconciliation, sealed-manifest validity, per-position comparison and existing legacy cutoffs remain intact. PostgreSQL 17 reproduces the missing coverage before the change and verifies exact bank/player totals afterward, unchanged whole reports across six legacy controls, missing-leg drift, raw/sealed equivalence, stale/missing seals, explicit-baseline precedence and role/session refusals. Auth/role helpers are controlled fixture inputs; the actual conservation report is executed. The existing required accounting parent invokes the fixture unconditionally.

Equivalent read-only production queries verify Deep Stack 420 current player wallets (3,774,333.99) and Lightning 73 current wallets (8,904.63) with zero drift, plus exact treasury reconciliation. Separate full-journal Lightning reconciliation also includes removed memberships: 121 positions, zero drift. These are query results before installation, not evidence that the updated report is installed or that weekly settlements are finished.
