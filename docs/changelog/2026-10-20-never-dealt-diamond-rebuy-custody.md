# Diamond rebuys retain their original restart proof

Coffee Break Freeroll (PLO4), tournament dee444f3-55d1-4626-9769-ddbfef3a00c7 starting October 19 at 18:00 UTC, remained RUNNING with 18 players. Two never-dealt tables held ten untouched starting stacks, including six Diamond rebuys. Their F06 park operations could not admit movement because `f06_movement_never_dealt_prior` required chip-wallet ledger rows and `asset='chips'` for every rebuy. Legitimate Diamond purchases instead fund tournament custody. Repeating the original read could never make those chip rows exist.

The original proof reader now accepts Diamond funding only when its immutable tournament ledger and custody movement match the completed purchase, amount, player, tournament, registration, arena, journal and active tournament custody. All existing original-chair, settled-hand, whole-roster, generation and no-later-funding constraints remain. Chip funding keeps its original proof branch. The migration reads proof only; it does not replay purchases, pay prizes, rewrite stacks or add a repair job. The serving engine can continue the same owning F06 operation after installation.

The existing required native PostgreSQL CI runner proves refusal before the migration, accepts original Diamond rebuys afterward, and rejects missing/foreign/mismatched payment, journal, custody, seat, roster, generation and hand evidence. It retains duplicate-read and ACL checks and repeats chip boundaries against the successor. Production installation and affected continuation are recorded separately in CHECKPOINT-coffee-mtt-root.md.
