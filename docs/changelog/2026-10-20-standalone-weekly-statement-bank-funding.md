The original standalone weekly bank restores retired cash and tournament rake through `fn_ca_fund_club`. The statement summary recorded the original retirement but omitted the separate spendable bank mint. A 4078.94 bank with 204.02 direct player payments therefore showed funding 0 and retained −204.02. The original issuer used those incorrect values for the consolidated statement.

The summary now recognizes `standalone_rake_funding` only for a standalone period whose exact original weekly key has one posted system-mint treasury leg and one matching native mint-register receipt. It validates club, amount, category, holder, asset, original producer, balances, time and linkage against the original bank's cash and tournament components and accepted private earning basis. The provenance fields are `standalone_bank_ledger_ids` and `standalone_bank_mint_ids`. An unrelated mint is excluded; a missing, wrong or inconsistent original bank receipt prevents issuance. There are no additional payments or changes to historical financial rows.

Private earnings still equal the original banked plus burned dispositions: the 4078.94 retirement remains burned and private banked remains zero. The later original mint adds 4078.94 funding, giving 3874.92 retained after 204.02 direct payments. Union 90 percent receipts and coordinated private retirement retain their existing formulas. Issued historical reports remain frozen. The current v3 source fingerprint and access contract are unchanged.
