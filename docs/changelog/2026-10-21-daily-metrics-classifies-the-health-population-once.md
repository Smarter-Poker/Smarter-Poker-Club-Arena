# Daily metrics classifies the health population once

The daily snapshot timed out three times in its health statement because PostgreSQL rebuilt the original human classifier for every collusion signal. Resolve that unchanged classifier once in a materialized CTE and reuse it for the two existing membership tests. Every output field, classifier predicate, schedule and timeout remains unchanged.

The production read-only health query exceeded 15 seconds before the change and completed in 2.324 seconds with the CTE over the same daily range. This is query evidence, not installed snapshot proof. Isolated PostgreSQL qualification compares the complete health JSON and original classifier across membership, null, duplicate and boundary cases. The existing snapshot and incident remain subject to actual post-install readback.
