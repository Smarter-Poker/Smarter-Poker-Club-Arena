# Standalone rakeback closes its own coordinator book

Deep Stack Society had no standalone earning sources for the week, but its standalone R3 selected Union-certified player periods by club ID and refused the foreign certificates. The same failure was reproduced with the original R3 and its historical-wallet successor.

The stage's two period selections and its preparation plan now exclude only exact-week foreign Union certificates whose latest identity, published amounts, producer fingerprint, weighted display rate and complete original allocation/source cohort agree, and no allocation makes the player its own payer, with no actual standalone source for that player. Unknown, malformed or missing certificates remain in the original refusal path. Any actual standalone cash, tournament or zero-rake source prevents exclusion. All original payer, wallet, ledger, receipt, invoice, timing and duplicate guards remain unchanged.

Isolated PostgreSQL 17 qualification proves both fail-before owners, zero close/replay without paying the five foreign periods, nine retained refusal boundaries, native empty-plan behavior and cached Union settlement of all five original players for exactly 0.16 followed by rollback. Both owner bodies reverse exactly to their predecessors outside the three selection anchors. Existing required native accounting CI invokes this finite qualification.

The migration depends on the historical-wallet predecessor and its engine runtime proof. Installation, production settlements and the full union audit remain separate root-owned work; no production fixture or payment was executed here.
