# Occasional horse chat and gestures

Horse chat previously gave every horse a positive speaking and throwing rate. Greetings rolled the arriving player's rate, and all-in chat was admitted through the horse's throwing habit.

Stable independent persona habits now leave approximately 25% of horses silent and 45% without throws or reactions. Active horses retain their vocabulary and typing style, with lower, varied participation rates. No event can give one horse more than a 35% chat chance or 25% gesture chance; existing hourly caps, hand spacing, mute checks and claims remain intact. A greeting rolls the selected greeter's rate. All-in chat uses talking probability, while throws and reactions use gesture probability, allowing chat-only and gesture-only horses.

Regression protection covers the four habit combinations, population proportions, rate bounds, silent greetings and shove chat, no gesture writes for quiet horses, and chat-only all-in behavior. The silent-persona regression fails on the prior implementation. Integrated with PR #7715's pending emoji-count, streak, typo and audience improvements, preserving all four. It requires engine publication and live verification before being described as live.
