# Unfinished MTT Entries say what they hold

Club Data and Union Data showed "Running MTT Entries" for every MTT that was not
finished. A live read of the Midway Union at 20:10 Chicago on 2026-10-22 split that
figure (-53,656.38) into -22,386.38 for the 9 MTTs actually running and -31,270.00
of buy-ins for 98 MTTs still registering, which had not started. The figure was
correct; its name was not.

The line is now "Unfinished MTT Entries" on the union panel, the simple Club Data
page and the advanced report, and the basis note says it holds buy-ins for MTTs
still running or not yet started. Finished MTT Winnings (36,108.99 at that read,
equal to the wallet ledger to the cent) and every total are unchanged. Client copy
only; no database or engine change.
