# Classic And Lightning Games

The owner replaced Action and Madness with one Lightning format at every stake. Classic keeps regular ring-game rules. Lightning uses 40% minimum VPIP, a big blind ante, six seats, and at least five ready participants. Six form immediately; a selected group of five waits three seconds from its latest ready arrival. An older arrival in another group cannot shorten or extend that deadline.

Pineapple remains Classic under the owner's explicit launch choice. Its physical discard round is not available in the Lightning host. Existing Pineapple seats, balances, buy-in limits and session clocks stay intact; creation refuses Pineapple Lightning before any game is written, and the picker explains the unavailable combination.

The database owns formation and dealing admission. The source-bound native qualification reproduces the former short-handed fallback, applies the exact migration to captured installed function bodies, then exercises actual matcher, formation and dealing calls. Isolated boundaries for eligibility, anchor admission and economic helpers are identified in the fixture; these tests do not certify production wallet permissions or installation. The TypeScript matcher mirrors this rule for its next ordinary engine release.

Only Classic and Lightning Games appear in creation and lobby controls. Historical Action/Madness identifiers remain readable, and saved style selections resolve to Lightning. Lightning creation uses the cluster mode; manual individual tables remain a Classic choice. Duplicate legacy populations retire through the original conversion/drain owner, while new admission resolves to the canonical exact-stake game. Existing hands, seat custody and balances remain with their original owners until normal settlement and departure.

Preselected Fold in an active Lightning room uses the existing fast-fold request immediately, with a duplicate-request guard. A seated player whose physical game converts sees View Game for their verified pool session. Conversion events and reconnect reads update that notice; stale account and out-of-order responses cannot redirect the view.

Horse hours, rests and daily schedules remain part of the separate scheduled-fleet delivery. Neither assignment rows nor enabled configuration count as live seating proof. Installation, client publication, current engine compatibility and actual seated/hand evidence must be recorded separately in the task checkpoint before this change is called live.
