# Horse premium raises and funded continuation decisions

The real decision path could cold-call single-suited AA in PLO5 and double-suited KK in PLO6 against one open and four callers. Their raw percentile and marginal non-AA flatting gates defeated the hand-class intent. Real-card suited AA and double-suited KK now anchor value 3-bets at normal opening prices. Credible tight-opener protection for KK, oversized prices, further re-raises, tournament/satellite gates, pot limits and legality remain separate decisions. Hold’em AA/KK now use their exact pair identity for the value squeeze, so percentile jitter cannot demote KK below a tight style’s bar. Both game modes are tested. No name-specific rule is added.

An all-in call or effective-stack jam now realizes its full equity: future betting cannot make that hand fold. The EV mixing window is bounded by chips still at risk, so a large existing pot cannot mix away a meaningful fraction of a tiny remainder. In no limit, a raise leaving less than the call it just chose to continue finishes the commitment immediately when legal. Pot limit preserves its ceiling. After a preflop raise, a flop remainder smaller than the ordinary one-third-pot ladder is priced as its actual terminal wager before deep-stack check/protection mixes. This remains an EV comparison, not a command to shove hopeless boards or exceed the pot limit.

Weak made hands heads-up can price up to two later barrels before paying for the present street. The bounded scenario uses the current bettor’s sampled postflop bet frequency, current pot fraction and actual remaining stacks; it chooses whether to fund later calls or fold. Explicit continuation replaces the generic realization discount. Live draws, solver-covered decisions, multiway/multi-board pots, fixed limit and missing reads retain their previous paths. Profitable draws that miss and genuine scare-card folds remain legitimate; chips already invested are not a reason to call.
