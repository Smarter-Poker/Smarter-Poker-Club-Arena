# Announce The Champion And Close The Finished Table

A tournament winner broadcast was consumed only by the winning player's browser. Spectators remained at the closed felt with the winner still shown in the last seat snapshot. Durable completion recovery deliberately returned for spectators without closing their tab.

The existing celebration now shows the champion name, 1st Place and the committed prize in the event's asset unit. All viewers receive the celebration through the existing winner event or durable completion reader. Champions and spectators close their finished table after 20 seconds. Spectators never receive another player's personal result card and never clear another table's playing session. The embedded close targets only the finished tab. Optional entrant-result enrichment starts during the display interval so a slow transport cannot extend the 20-second dwell.

No engine replacement, payout, settlement or schema change. Existing paid finishes and satellite qualification semantics remain intact. Regression coverage executes the maintained spectator handler with duplicate/in-flight events, delayed/error reads, unmount and exact 20-second timers, alongside existing winner/qualifier recovery and award animation coverage.
