# Every Tournament Finish Announces Before Closing

Committed completion now gives entrants and spectators the same 20-second announcement. A champion's name, first place and committed prize are read from tournament_players; each entrant still receives only their own result card. MTT v1/v2, SNG, Spin and seat-first satellite contracts use the same terminal path, independent of poker variant, rebuy/add-on, bounty, mystery bounty, progressive bounty, speed and multi-day settings. Final-table deals use their committed first-place row. Between-day BAGGED states do not finish the event.

Multi-qualifier satellites announce the committed qualifiers and award values without assigning an invented first place. Entrants retain their signed-in seat/ticket/cash receipt. Completion supersedes a shorter final-hand elimination exit, rejects late elimination presentation, deduplicates terminal signals and cancels only the finished table's timer. Ordinary eliminations and rebuy holds remain unchanged before terminal completion. Public reads fail visibly and retain recovery when the result is unavailable or incomplete; no financial writes or new engine contract are introduced.

Regression law executes the maintained terminal reader and celebration with browser/REST boundaries controlled for every persisted format and champion, eliminated entrant, authenticated spectator and guest. It checks the final-hand timer race, exact 19,999/20,000ms boundary, individual result ownership and all three named completion events. The new 21-case law failed against the previous source and passed after the repair. Existing closeout tests retain duplicate, timeout, late response, unmount and embedded-table checks; real overlay tests cover chip/Diamond units and unranked qualifier rendering. These files run in the existing required client unit check and publisher suite, with normal source-contract/related pre-push selection.
