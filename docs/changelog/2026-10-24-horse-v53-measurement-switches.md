# Horse continuation and PLO-discipline layers are measured on and off (V53)

#7838, #7866 and #7876 shipped the premium raises, terminal commitment, the call-side barrel budget and a set of PLO river and recovery repairs with no switch, so the nightly league could not say whether any of them earns. Following the V52 precedent (#7915), each layer that changes a live decision now has its own default-on `HorseDecideOpts` switch. Production play is unchanged: with no opts, the decision stream of a 300-pair NLH league and 80-120-pair PLO4/5/6/8 and short-deck leagues is byte-identical (sha256 over every action, amount and guard) to main `ebe9363e`.

| switch                  | layer                                                                                                                              | pre-change behavior with it off                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `v53PremiumRaise`       | exact AA/KK, Omaha suited-AA / double-suited-KK value 3-bets                                                                       | the bars alone decide                                                           |
| `v53TerminalCommitment` | preflop remainder jams, flop remainder priced, all-in lines realize full equity, mix window capped by chips behind                 | old sizing, realization and pot-only window                                     |
| `v53BarrelBudget`       | heads-up one-pair / weak Omaha two-pair calls price the bettor's observed next barrel                                              | no continuation built                                                           |
| `v53TripBoardBoat`      | Omaha river trip-board under-full boat flats                                                                                       | value raise                                                                     |
| `v53ColdFlushPressure`  | cold bet + price-raising jam is raised-line evidence against a dominated Omaha river flush                                         | no penalty, no guard                                                            |
| `v53CalledThroughFlush` | third-nut-or-worse river flush checks after two callers called two barrels                                                         | no forced check                                                                 |
| `v53ManiacRecovery`     | recovery keeps weak-flush stack folds, guarded and multi-board folds; granted-sample boundary with a fresh 2,500-deal confirmation | the pre-#7866 recovery (1,800 samples on the retained fixture instead of 4,300) |
| `v53PhysicalDraws`      | Omaha implied credit needs an actual draw                                                                                          | any live-draw board gets the credit                                             |
| `v53OmahaBlockers`      | Omaha physical two-hole nut-flush blocker                                                                                          | generic blocker read                                                            |
| `v53RangeRunout`        | Omaha made-range runout comparison withdraws initiative                                                                            | terminal-equity range read alone                                                |

Off restores the exact pre-change path: 200,000 random `evaluateSpot` and `decidePreflopV7` inputs match the pre-#7838 modules byte for byte with the switch off. With every V52/V53 switch off, PLO4, PLO5, PLO6 and PLO8 league decision streams equal the pre-#7838 tree's exactly; NLH and short deck differ only through the paid-delta range-reading repair in `HorseMind.bandFor` (#7866), a correctness fix to public-money accounting rather than a strategy layer (swapping in the historical band reproduces the pre-#7838 digest exactly).

The nightly league gains cards only where the layer was measured to change a decision in self-play: `plo4/plo5/plo6_v53_premium_raise`, `plo6_v53_terminal_commitment`, `plo4/plo5/plo6_v53_omaha_river` (the three river layers grouped), `plo4/plo5/plo6_v53_omaha_blockers`, `plo4/plo5/plo6_v53_physical_draws` and `plo4/plo5/plo6_v53_range_runout`. The legacy all-off arm includes every switch, and the data ledger registers each one.

Not carded, with the measurement: NLH premium raise and terminal commitment changed nothing in 2,000 6-max pairs at 100bb, 1,000 at 40bb or 1,000 heads-up, and terminal commitment changed nothing in 1,000 PLO4 or 2,000 PLO5 pairs; the barrel budget and maniac recovery need an opponent read outside the horse envelope, which a mirror league never produces (`HorseMind.opponentRead` was null on all 32,048 reads of a 300-pair NLH run). Those are pinned by spot tests on the retained incidents instead.

PLO5 coverage gap closed: `plo5_v40_omaha`, `plo5_v46_classes`, `plo5_v24_price` and `plo5_v15_discipline` join their plo4/plo6 cards (56, 48, 72 and 37 divergent pairs at 300 pairs).
