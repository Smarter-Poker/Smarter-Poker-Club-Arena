# Tournament open-shove line by seat

Dan, 2026-10-25 20:33 Chicago, with a screenshot of a horse open-shoving about 20 big blinds from UTG+1 in the Sunday Funday Championship (hand 48258730, after shove discipline was live): "SINCE WHEN IS IT GTO TO OPEN RIP 20BB FROM UTG?!"

Shove discipline had one first-in line, 20bb, for every seat. It is now set by seat: <=12bb effective from UTG through the hijack, <=15bb from the cutoff, <=20bb from the button, the small blind and heads-up. Deeper, the open is a 2.2x raise. The maniac law keeps its claim (a 20bb stack never raise-folds into a maniac behind): late seats still shove into the maniac and the earlier seats' small opens call the shove.
