# A fresh balance preparation makes bounded progress

The real table-balance owner could restart its read prefix forever. A live-table read plus table, seat and roster snapshots taking 5.7 seconds exhausted the admission’s five-second work budget before its first durable park. Re-admitting the same cursor repeated the same reads and moved nobody, even in the consolidation lane. A separate ambiguity treated fully completed slow planning as unfinished, retaining that lane and the same cursor unnecessarily.

Reserve one fresh preparation in its originally admitted pass, only when no existing break was visited and no durable break, ambiguous park/move or mixed recovery owns the work. It may finish the current bounded board read and its first durable park/fence. Then the normal budget applies: a spent admission yields before member dispatch or another intent. A slow gap plan fences only its first source for the existing hand-boundary owner, which rereads the board and uses the original atomic move path. Stop, abort, freeze and unknown outcomes remain authoritative. The scoped preparation window closes on every return and exception.

Completed planning has an explicit result. The elimination cursor can advance and clear an unnecessary consolidation mark even when the completed read consumed the budget; attempted or unfinished work retains its continuation. No timeout constant, money/seat transaction, migration, timer, repair loop or scheduler capacity changes.

Regression protection drives the real manager, board loader and planner under controlled per-read latency. The baseline repeats three admissions and creates zero parks; the fix creates one, fences after the durable request, and yields before dispatch. Stop/abort/freeze, refused intents and slow gap planning are covered, alongside the existing destination/deadline contracts. Historical alert attribution and live delivery evidence remain in the task checkpoint.
