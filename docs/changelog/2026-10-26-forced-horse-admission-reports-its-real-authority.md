# Forced Horse admission reports its real authority

The private fleet interpreter keeps accepted owner-forced decisions active while reporting their authority as owner-forced rather than independently qualified. Corrective decisions also retain that distinction when a selected baseline dependency uses forced authority. Admission, policy selection and action execution are unchanged.

The existing canonical fleet summary now exposes validated phase count axes and aggregate authority provenance without horse identifiers, cards, frames or arbitrary reason strings. Only the new explicit independent-qualification reason supplies independent provenance; ambiguous older reader output stays unknown. Canonical packet/source binding remains required, and all-phase, complete-population and replay certification remain separate.
