# Earned rakeback precedes club settlement

The owner specified union fees to club banks at 90%, clubs to agents, agents to players, then club settlements and invoicing. Preparation instead gated every fee stage on the later club P&L report. Existing jackpot and treasury-seat report gaps therefore withheld independently certified fee entitlements.

Move the same P&L gate into the original cascade after its fully funded, receipt-backed and conserved fee rounds, before any club statement, settled status, ECO or weekly invoice. Preserve every preparation source, historical contract, recomputation, authorization, timing and payment guard. A warming book retains truthful proof progress; a failed book still refuses settlement and invoicing. No new payer, schedule or balance writer.

Qualification separates actual-owner phase-order tests with controlled stage contracts from maintained native monetary-writer regressions. Actual production payment still requires a rolled-back full-path proof, assertions matching the proven amounts and beneficiaries, and wallet/ledger/duplicate readback. Installation and payment evidence remain separate.
