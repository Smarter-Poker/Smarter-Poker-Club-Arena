# Conditional river position defense candidate

The consolidated owner report identifies players who bet rivers far too often after opponents check. The old betCheckedTo read pools every postflop street, and riverBetOpps counts facing river bets. Neither measures this conditional opportunity.

A public-action reducer now records a last-to-act river opportunity only when every other remaining actor has checked. It uses the original observer context dealt count, rejects incomplete participants, repeated checks, out-of-order streets and oversized histories, removes earlier folds/all-ins, and records check-backs in the denominator. No opponent holding is read and a bet is never labeled a bluff.

The original-hand-bound HorseMind path learns these counters separately from legacy action dedupe. Each horse rebuilds them from its own accepted public-history window after a fresh worker; other observers, variants and formats remain isolated. The legacy pooled database has no authority for this individual conditional read and returns zero for those counters. This is bounded own-history reconstruction, not durable lifetime-counter storage or all-history recall.

Private decision read codec v7 appends two counters to its existing sidecar. Evidence without them preserves exact v1-v6 encoding. The replay validator checks nonnegative integral opportunities and bets no greater than opportunities; retained allocated plan handling accepts v7 under the existing identity and digest rules. No public payload gains private beliefs.

The default-off riverPositionDefense candidate lets the existing maniac call-down price a formerly rejected river call only for supported high-only NLH/PLO4/PLO5/PLO6, heads-up single-board cash, a credible wide entrant, a current opening bet after all checks, and at least eight conditional observations with a one-sided99percent Wilson lower bound of at least0.65. That bound is a declared candidate gate, not measured population calibration or a bluff probability. Existing own-hand equity, public preflop range, effective call, rake, survival and protected-fold checks remain the arbiter. It never forces a call or expands a raise war. Independent incremental qualification against the current brain, protected delivery and accepted live use remain required.

Initial helper tests failed before implementation. Seven reducer boundaries passed; subsequent observer/frame/mirror tests passed, and four controlled read/price consumer cases cover the four variants. Those consumer cases explicitly exercise the recovery function on a supplied reference fold, not proof that the complete prior brain folds those same nut hands. The real own-public-history packet is replayed twice into two fresh worker banks to prove original-window reconstruction and dedupe. These are functional and source-authority tests, not positive-edge qualification or live activation. The separate double-check betting continuation improvement remains open.
