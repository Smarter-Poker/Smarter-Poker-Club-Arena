# Incremental pressure qualification requires its own protected plan

The existing manual feature-ablation workflow now supports the five incremental
pressure targets and their exact five-flag package through a separate v2
registry. That registry is empty: this change neither sizes nor authorizes a
heldout experiment, qualifies a strategy, changes defaults, nor activates horses.
The original V24/V53 registry, seeds and statistical gates remain unchanged.

The runner authenticates the actual protected plan merge before the provider run,
full executable source closure, original current-worker identity, exact treatment
flags, fixed public warm histories, population and frozen store/admission inputs
before dealing. Every applicable component requires its own prospectively
registered positive coverage minimum. Original two-dealt check-raise coverage is
not inferred from multiway package results; the package is not certification of
its individual components. The continuation counter derives from the original
accepted turn outlook effect without a new plan and a successful worker commit
acknowledgment, without changing engine telemetry or decision behavior.

Assembly re-downloads each original provider artifact, verifies its SHA256 and
exact allowlisted member bytes before rebuilding the paired after-fee statistics.
Missing plans, changed sources, unknown provider provenance, omitted coverage,
foreign flags, substituted artifacts and reserved tournament seeds in development
are refused. Synthetic stress outcomes remain distinct from calibrated human
profitability. No new seeds, production operations or qualification deals are
introduced by this change.
