# Phase 8 has a separate isolated catastrophe guard candidate

The continuation's three-funded-seat work limit also prevented its existing deep one-pair protection from assessing full tournament tables. The new isolated `catastrophe_guard_v1` family evaluates that exact protection against the unchanged Phase 7 decision and modeled legal safe actions. It does not run future-hand continuation or change the live horse brain. Original production-derived full-table fixtures cover the guard; a small real-controller league fixture covers its request, result and transport wiring.

The existing evaluator and manual workflow accept an explicit family, defaulting to the legacy `continuation_v2` family. Results, manifests, summaries and assembler outputs bind the family, version and work kind. Guard assessments have their own bounded work count and zero continuation work. Mixed families, mismatched manifests and invented continuation work are refused. Old receipts without a family retain their legacy meaning.

Guard assembly binds the full maintained baseline source closure plus the new guard and its benchmark/evaluator plumbing. It emits a separate isolated qualification schema with activation disallowed and no selected policy. The three fixed seeds, six objectives, 1,024 pairs per run, positive per-seed CI99 lower bound, genuine action changes, 5 ms latency and integrity gates remain unchanged. Current human monitoring requirements remain unchanged. No qualification run or live activation is claimed, and the original source-9e runs and artifacts remain immutable.
