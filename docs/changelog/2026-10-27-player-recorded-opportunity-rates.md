# Player Stats recorded opportunity rates

Burls' club comparison printed 0.0% 3-bet even though persisted facts contained accepted re-raises. The club overview predates nullable opportunity facts and still returned a literal zero. The comparison, profile, advanced cards and alerts did not consistently honor measurement availability.

The migration patches the installed readers in place, preserving their funded-club source, authorization, ACLs, calendar bounds and query configuration. Club 3-bet and flop C-bet use matching recorded action/opportunity populations. Position rows carry 3-bet opportunities. All Clubs now derives these advanced rates, fold-to-3-bet, showdown counts and WTSD from the complete selected fact window rather than inheriting a latest-750 legacy sample. WTSD divides showdowns by flops seen. Tournament-with-hands counts cover the full window; recent finishes use the consumer's finish_rank field and bounty counts reach the tournament aggregate.

Comparison/coaching and alerts omit unmeasured 3-bet values. Profile and advanced cards retain unavailable state. CSV availability applies to All Clubs as well as selected clubs. Tournament-only play does not fabricate a cash BB/100 comparison.

The existing fact passive_actions counter includes checks and calls. It cannot establish the call-only Aggression Factor denominator. Readers stop certifying that ratio until complete call-only coverage exists. A daily-maintenance migration captures call-only counts in the original fact insertion transaction. Historical rows remain unmeasured; no repair loop or invented backfill. Legacy summed hand duration is likewise not certified as elapsed play time.

Validation: local PostgreSQL executes the actual funded-club predecessor before the candidate, reproduces zero despite a known re-raise, then verifies measured rates, cash/tournament participation, nullable historic opportunities, full-window showdown rates, tournament result fields and unchanged self-only access. The existing required Data Console PostgreSQL suite invokes this harness. Render tests cover unavailable/recorded comparison values, tournament-only cash suppression and advanced-card unavailable states. Delivery and live evidence are recorded in CHECKPOINT-burls-stats.md.

Field comparisons also had independent calculation defects. Their maintained snapshot producer now weights rates by recorded hands, calculates each hand's BB result before aggregation, and separates chips from Diamonds. It uses the existing narrow recent-action cache; its UI explicitly identifies reconstructed recent samples rather than claiming complete or settlement-exact history. The broad lifetime-fact scan was rejected after live read-only performance qualification. The narrow aggregate measured2.55s on production. Empty or stale missing asset cohorts produce an explicit unavailable state, and scope changes cannot temporarily paint the previous asset's ranks. Static strategy bands are labeled general references, with unsupported claims about measured winning-player ranges removed.

Hands Won now counts pot awards rather than only net-profitable hands, including split and side pots; money and profit fields retain their existing definitions.

Two Burls action-cache rows incorrectly classified short all-in calls as raises. Both maintained action readers now compare against the existing live bet level; the two named cache booleans are corrected only when settled facts independently say false. Monetary fields and reconstruction remain unchanged. Engine fact derivation also respects forced blinds and straddles.

The Mini-HUD had destructured record methods with no event callers. It now consumes only committed, allowlisted public summaries from physical and Lightning settlement paths. Per-observer hand receipts and counters persist atomically, preventing reconnect duplicate counting. Missing call evidence never fabricates a style classification. This new summary contract requires the daily engine release; independent reader/client corrections do not. Stats cache calculation keys invalidate old computed results without changing the server contract or logout purge prefix.

Validation also includes the actual summary builder through the real hook and rendered Mini-HUD, before/after all-in classification, privacy allowlisting, delayed/duplicate events and persisted receipts. Source binding CI uncovered an already-stale main workflow hash; the protected treasury qualification additions were reviewed and the binding/audit updated without changing the workflow.
