# Preserve priced refusals when new public investment changes the hand

The PLO4 pressure fixture's unconditional raise-fold cap counted newly multiway decisions as though the initial public line had stayed unchanged. On the fixed 120-hand diagnostic, ten preflop raise-folds contained seven new third-party voluntary contributions after hero's raise: five gained callers at the return price, while two gained an intervening caller still owing the re-raise. The remaining three had no new third-party investment. The diagnostic does not prove those original opens profitable.

The complete raiseThenFold and rtfByStreet totals remain unchanged. A separate unchanged-public-line counter retains the same maximum of three for the PLO4 pin. Only positive finite third-party calls, raises or all-ins after hero's latest raise change applicability. Forced postings, checks, folds, earlier limps and an untouched pending seat do not. NLH, tournament and postflop limits remain unchanged.

Independent classification boundaries and newly multiway priced-refusal controls, including aces, preserve the distinction. Forcing calls or suppressing legitimate opens solely to lower the unconditional count would reward unsafe continuation after new information. No strategy, sampler, price threshold or production behavior changes in this alignment.
