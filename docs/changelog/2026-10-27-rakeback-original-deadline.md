# Rakeback retains its original close deadline

R3 previously started another90second batch after preparation could consume most of the original close attempt. The same owner now clamps its existing stop to the attempt's recorded deadline when batching. Nonchunk calls retain their original behavior, including ignoring an unused malformed deadline.

The exact b07 original R3 owner is required. Its OID, permissions, configuration and every other body byte remain unchanged. Native PostgreSQL17 runs the original R2/R3 monetary and invoice family, proves old deadline failure then new partial progress, checks deferred receipts before replay, and retains existing refusal/concurrency coverage. This source does not certify production payment or installation.
