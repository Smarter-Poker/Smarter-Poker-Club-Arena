# Conditional river bluff check-raise candidate

The existing river bluff-raise ladder is NLH-only. Omaha can reach the river fold/call arbiter without a priced bluff-raise candidate; the paired-board call-off rule also folds all raises below a full house in its commitment domain.

`riverBluffCheckRaise` is a separate **default-off** candidate for NLH/PLO4/PLO5/PLO6 originally heads-up single-board cash. After the ordinary signed wager rules have folded, it can select a legal three-times-bet river check-raise. It requires a credible wide entrant, a conditional last-to-act river betting read, actual own-card nut blockers, and a positive zero-showdown-equity payoff bound after conservative fee deduction. Unknown economics, pending reads, continuation guards, calls/value raises, all-in or committed bettors, raise wars, tournaments, multi-board/bomb pots and unsupported games retain the reference. The final legalizer and near-pot rounding must preserve the exact priced amount; otherwise the reference stays.

Fold response evidence is deliberately specific: completed public heads-up check/bet/3x-raise responses, with calls and re-raises retained in the denominator even when further actions follow. Ordinary bet-fold rates are not check-raise-fold rates. New independent observer counters are reconstructed from original accepted public history, deduped by original hand and scoped like existing reads. Legacy pooled persistence supplies zero for these new fields. Read-frame v8 appends them without changing v1–v7 bytes when the new evidence is absent; allocated journal context and validation retain their existing fences. No opponent holding is inspected or transported. Original multiway hands are excluded from this response sample, including folded third players and all-in side pots. Public action history does not carry starting stacks: a historical raise action may itself exhaust a stack. Same-size response estimates therefore remain an unqualified model across historical stack and pot fractions, not a guarantee of the current hand's fold probability.

Paired/trips boards require boat/quads blockers; flush boards require a naked nut-suit ace; straight boards require a missing rank of the highest physically possible straight. Made straights and better remain with the value/call owner. This is not a rule to bluff every paired board, force every double barrel, or assume every high-frequency bettor is bluffing.

Connected tests exercise all four complete brain paths, signed policy ownership, real controller acceptance and completed-hand chip conservation including rake. Three Omaha cases show the original paired-board call-off guard still fires while the separately priced bluff candidate can raise; all-in/call/unknown-fee/no-read/changed-size cases retain the reference. Codec tests cover v8 invalid counters, v7 byte preservation, and allocated-context transport. Review caught and fixed denominator selection bias and legalizer metadata loss before submission.

These are implementation and regression results, **not independent strength qualification or live activation**. The same-size response estimates still need current-brain held-out qualification, exact source binding, protected delivery and accepted-use proof before promotion. Sparse evidence remains unknown; no default or production selector is enabled by this change.
