# Small-pot near-nut river value candidate

`smallPotNearNutRiverValue` is an explicit default-off candidate. In PLO4/5/6 heads-up remaining, single-board high-only cash, it removes only the existing value band's random call when a strong full house blocks all reachable quads and at most one stronger boat holding rank remains. It runs after the original equity bar and all early value/raise-war guards. The PLO6 final flat-only strategy rule has the narrow exception described below. All other final wager rules and controller legality remain authoritative; the existing raise sizing is unchanged.

The board must have exactly one pair. The opponent must have made the opening river bet, with no raise war, all-in opponent, or contested side pot. Settlement-owned contribution arithmetic must prove that the opponent's only unmatched amount is that opening bet. Full finite seat identity, investments, legal bounds, fee terms and board context are required. NLH is unsupported: the motivating queens-plus-seven blocker structure needs three hole cards. Missing data preserves the original decision.

The initial implementation choices are pot before bet <= 25% of effective stack, bet <= 10% of effective stack and bet <= half the pot before bet. Effective stack is min(hero stack + own street bet, opponent stack + opponent street bet). These ratios require qualification; they are not calibrated economic boundaries. The original random gate draw is consumed even when the candidate overrides its call. Excluded/default-off decisions retain original action and RNG; newly chosen raises legitimately consume the existing sizing RNG.
