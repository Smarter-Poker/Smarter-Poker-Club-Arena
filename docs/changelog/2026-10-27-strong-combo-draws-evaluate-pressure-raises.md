# Strong combo draws evaluate pressure raises

The consolidated owner feedback identifies passive flop/turn draws and repeated call–fold lines against wide aggressive players. In current source, randomized value/semi-bluff raise gates can decline; the later EV menu explicitly sets maxBet0 and sizes[] and can only fold or call. A draw above the semi-bluff band but below the made-value threshold can therefore never ask that menu to compare a legal raise.

This candidate extends that same maintained EV comparison for physically strong NLH/PLO4/PLO5/PLO6 combo draws, heads-up on an unpaired single-board cash flop/turn against a public wide-bettor read. It offers legal three-quarter-pot, pot and affordable terminal raises, using existing fold models, continuing-range equity, realization, effective stacks, rake and call deductions. It returns a pressure raise only when its modeled EV beats both folding and calling and legalization preserves the exact priced amount. Dominated naked flush draws, paired boards, multiway/multi-board, all-in opponents, missing legal bounds, existing call-only plans, tournaments and later-street raise wars keep their previous paths. No opponent private holding enters a horse's choice.

The option comboDrawPressure defaults off pending incremental paired qualification against the current brain with V53 already enabled, protected adoption and accepted live-use verification. It must not be smuggled into a broader V53-vs-off result or treated as qualified by aggregate telemetry. The existing final-source 18-phase and feature-ablation bindings must include the changed bytes.

Nine initial physical-domain/behavior checks fail on the original source. After implementation the actual HandController deals fixed legal hands, plays six/fifteen/call-nine preflop and a20 flop bet, and accepts new formerly-flat pressure actions in each of NLH/PLO4/PLO5/PLO6. The original high-equity NLH fixture already raised in the prior value branch; a lower made-value nut-flush/gutshot fixture tests the missing branch. Its first draw classifier missed the overlapping suited straight out because a made flush masks its straight category; the corrected rank mask recognizes both components without adding or double-counting equity. This is connected reachability and legality evidence, not proof of profitability.

The owner's river-stab and double-check continuation feedback remains a separate conditional-read/continuation acceptance item. No claim that this draw change alone fixes those patterns or that any player can be beaten every hand.
