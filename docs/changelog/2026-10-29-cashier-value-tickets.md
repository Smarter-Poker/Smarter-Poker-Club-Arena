# Cashier ticket values

Send Ticket grants a selected value, including 10,25 and40, without choosing a tournament. The holder may use it once for an eligible tournament in that club/union with the same total entry price, including fees and bounty allocation. Redemption uses the selected event’s exact funded split and the existing atomic admission path.

Existing event-specific tickets and pending retries keep their original contract. Satellite/refund entitlements, club eligibility and Diamonds/chips boundaries remain guarded.
