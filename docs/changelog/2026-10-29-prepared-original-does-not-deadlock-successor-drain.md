# A prepared original does not deadlock its successor drain

Mongo's Midday Free Buy event stopped advancing across 39 tables. Read-only production evidence found a stopped original hand 51935187 retained at sequence 10 and another dealer with hand 51935600 retained at sequence zero with handStarted false. The manager had paused the field to hand recovery to a successor generation, but its drain required every running controller to become null. The unstarted retained controller waited at that same next-hand pause, so it could neither start nor satisfy the drain. Mongo's own last hand and post-commit envelope had already completed; the old loop-phase label was not evidence of an ongoing settlement.

The successor drain now also accepts the exact positively retained, unstarted original at its explicit physical owner park. The engine requires its current controller, matching attempted permit and hand number, current ownership, and absence of unresolved retention, settlement, reads, or other accepted mutations. A started hand still finishes normally. The existing manager stop, custody transfer, lease release, successor admission and immutable original remain authoritative. No hand is started, discarded, settled or voided by this predicate, and no financial or maintenance gate changes.

The actual manager and dealer park regression failed before the repair and passed afterward. Negative cases retain the barrier for a started hand, uncertain write, replaced controller or permit, missing permit, pending settlement/read and lost owner. Runtime activation and natural live resumption require their separate delivery evidence.
