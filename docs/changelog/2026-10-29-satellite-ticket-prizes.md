# Satellite ticket prizes

The quick lobby shows satellite ticket awards instead of an ordinary percentage payout ladder. The target tournament appears prominently above the game card and in a larger in-card label.

Scheduled satellite entry prices fund approximately one target entry per five to ten entries after fees. For example15 into35 becomes5 into35. The data transition corrects schedules and empty unstarted MTT feeders; purchased events and frozen economic/settlement receipts keep their original commitments. Existing settlement preserves ticket entitlements and residual/closed-target value.
