# The club message switches are tick wells again

A render review of the shipped Table Management surfaces found four faults, and
each one was a selector reaching further than it meant to.

The groove that dresses a text field caught the announcement composer's two
switches, so the kit's 21 pixel tick well came out 299 pixels wide with "Pin
Banner" and "Display Now" shoved off the glass, and the page carried a
horizontal scrollbar at phone widths. The rule now says text fields only. The
description well cut its last line through the middle of the letters, because
the padding came out of the rows; it is sized to whole line boxes now. Each
announcement's Edit, Pin, Hide and Delete took a full width row of its own,
because the article's grid rule outranked the row of lit words; the row is
flex again. A hidden announcement dimmed its Delete past reading, and the
limits line fell under nine pixels on a small phone.

Two faults belonged to the console kit. A painted plate is a fixed slice of its
master, so on a phone the shark's single plate stood 39 pixels tall: the art
keeps its size and the button now reaches 44 pixels through a transparent pad,
exactly as the close word already did. Inside a dialog the global popup chassis
was drawing that close word as a bevelled rounded box half off the rail; the
plates were already armoured against it and the close word now is too.

The ticker's font select and speed slider arrived as a rounded browser box and
a pill with a round thumb; their appearance is reset and the parts are drawn
square, and the three colour wells line up with each other. Heads that fitted
their text down to six, eight and nine pixels say less: the board's eyebrow,
the refusal's line, the checking head and the ticker's subtitle. A blank
tournament form no longer paints its name field with the refusal ink before
anyone has been in it.

No service, command, contract, realtime or access behaviour changes.
