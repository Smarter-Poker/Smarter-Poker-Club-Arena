# Roadmap

## Phase 1 of 7 — Foundation and Regression Harness

Isolate dependencies, establish a clean test baseline, inventory current contracts,
lock the redesign behind regression tests, and document acceptance gates.

## Phase 2 of 7 — Create Club

Atomic/idempotent creation, eligibility and availability checks, drafts, identity
tooling, moderated uploads/generation, and guided post-create setup.

## Phase 3 of 7 — Find Player

Server-authoritative indexed search, cancellation, pagination, filters, ranking,
realtime presence, privacy preferences, and contextual actions.

## Phase 4 of 7 — Join Club

Atomic invitation redemption, confirmation preview, QR/clipboard/deep links,
complete application lifecycle, rate limiting, and recovery across auth/offline.

## Phase 5 of 7 — Shared Trust Layer

Analytics, observability, audit events, feature flags, accessibility, privacy,
security, abuse controls, drafts/recovery, and operations surfaces.

## Phase 6 of 7 — Casino Realism and Performance

Purpose-built Create/Find/Join renders, responsive crops, asset optimization,
motion/sound preference integration, bundle budgets, and visual regression tests.

## Phase 7 of 7 — Release Gate

Role matrix E2E, RLS verification, load/performance tests, operational runbooks,
staged rollout, rollback validation, and final release evidence.
