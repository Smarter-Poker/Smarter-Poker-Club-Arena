Horse Brain Phase 13: natural completion records, predeclaration
==================================================================

Declared after the Phase 13 build (PR #6280, squash 886cc9655461c1049ece829c1fc86c14e2d3ba75) merged to main and
before any window below opens. The commit time of this file on main is the
declaration time; no window that began before that time can be used.

1. Serving release
------------------
The first club-arena-engine release (label sp.release.sha) activated in an
hourly break after this declaration is on main, provided it contains the
squash commit above and, for each of the nine variants, its policy digest
computed from `git show <release>:server/<file>` (horsePhase13PolicyDigest,
definition horse-phase13-policy-digest-v1) equals the digest of that squash
commit. The release must be unchanged for the whole window: sp.release.sha and
State.StartedAt are read before the window opens and after it closes, and must
match. If the first such release is replaced before its window closes, the
window moves to the next full hour on the next qualifying release, every other
rule unchanged.

2. Window and population (complete, not a sample)
-------------------------------------------------
Minutes :02:00 to :50:00 UTC of the first full hour that release serves which
begins after this declaration is on main. Every Horse decision record in the
engine host's journal archive segments (archive/segments and
archive-shard-1/segments; closed segment files only, sha256-name verified,
read-only, nice 19 / ionice idle) with snapshot.decisionTimeMs in the window
whose sourceRelease is exactly that release; records of any other release are
counted and excluded by name. Only DECIDE_FAST records are counted (a DEEP
second look is excluded by name, excluded_deep_second_look); Pineapple discard
records are excluded by name. Multi-board and bomb hands are included and
counted in their board-count cells.

One record per variant (nlh, short_deck, flh, pineapple, plo4, plo5, plo6,
plo8, flo8): server/scripts/phase13-completion-extract.py and
server/src/scripts/phase13CompletionRecord.ts, read at the squash commit,
definition horse-phase13-completion-definition-v1 (receipt
body.decision.jointPolicy, receipt version joint-multiway-round1-v4; outcomes
workBudget, samplerBudgetExhausted, responseBranchUnavailable,
sampleUnavailable, governorReduced, completed). Tournament decisions are not
counted.

3. Verdict
----------
A variant's record meets the requirement only if every street cell and every
board-count cell has at least 127 eligible decisions and a Wilson lower bound
at the contract z (JOINT_STRENGTH_Z99) at or above 0.95. The verdict is
reported for all nine variants, whatever it is; a variant below the floor is
not re-measured on another window to look for a pass, and a cell with too few
natural decisions is reported as such, not extended. A record is used for
admission only together with that variant's qualification measured at the same
policy digest; with every PHASE13_PROTECTED_RELEASE_SELECTIONS entry null, no
record admits anything.
