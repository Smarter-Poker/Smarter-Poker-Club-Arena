## Why It Was Superseded

`playTournament` in `server/src/benchmark/HorseTournamentLeague.ts` paid a full bounty to every winner of a split knockout pot (`attributeKnockout` gives each splitter weight 1), so one knockout was paid more than once. In mystery seed 8102203, pair 36 paid 19,000 from an 18,000 bounty pool and the champion residual check fired on both the candidate and the baseline side, which ends the run at its first incomplete pair. PKO had the same overpayment without going negative, so the PKO numbers below are also wrong. The defect is in the league harness, not in the candidate: both sides of every pair were affected equally.

Fix: [#5786](https://github.com/Smarter-Poker/Smarter-Poker-Club-Arena/pull/5786) (`knockoutBountyShares` splits one bounty per knockout by claim weight, matching the engine's `fn_mystery_bounty_reserve` and `fn_collect_bounty`; the strict checks are kept). The full 18-run matrix is rerun on the fixed source.
