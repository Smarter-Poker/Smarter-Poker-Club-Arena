3. Comparison (independent re-settlement)
-----------------------------------------
Reference: server/src/benchmark/OmahaReference.ts with the reused
p9-natural-compare.mts, extended for one board and tournament units.
Inputs as recorded: the board (community_cards), recorded hole cards,
contributions replayed from actions (same rules as declaration.txt: blinds,
call, bet, straddle, antes are chips added; raise and all_in resolved
against the next captured invested totals, else unambiguous with no street
bet, else one unresolved amount by pot_size), production's pot rule
(individual antes matched; a shared big blind ante and a dead blind post are
pooled dead money), the button seat, and the chip unit from the hand's
captured public node (if absent: 1 for a tournament, 0.01 for chip cash,
noted).
Odd units: the engine's stated rule (PokerEngine.distributePot, Bible V8
2.7): a pot or half that does not divide evenly gives its odd units one at a
time to the winners in clockwise order from the button, the button last; in
hi-lo the odd unit of a pot goes to the high half (PokerEngine.calculatePots
caller). OmahaReference orders winners the same way (seats after the button
ascending, then seats up to and including the button).
Checks per hand:
  (a) pots: reference pots (amount and eligible seat set, in order) equal the
      recorded pots exactly;
  (b) conservation: recorded winners sum equals pot_size - rake_amount -
      bbj_amount exactly (tournament: equals pot_size);
  (c) per player:
      cash: recorded payout strictly within one cent of the exact pro-rata
        share  reference award x net / gross  (|recorded - exact| < 1 cent).
        The count within one cent inclusive (<= 1 cent) is reported beside it
        as a secondary band; the strict rule decides the verdict;
      tournament: recorded payout equals the reference award exactly, in
        whole chips;
  (d) per half (winners_by_board, written for single-board hi-lo), where
      recorded:
      cash: each player's entries sum exactly to that player's recorded
        payout, and each entry is within fewer cents than that player's
        number of entries of its exact pro-rata share (the engine's stated
        per-player penny repair onto the largest entry);
      tournament: each entry equals the reference half award exactly;
      not recorded -> (d) unknown (verdict MATCH_PARTIAL, never a match);
  (e) deductions, reported separately:
      cash: expected rake = min(round(pot x pct), cap tier by players dealt),
        pct at most 5 with two dealt, no rake when noFlopNoDrop and no flop
        was dealt; expected BBJ = feeBB x big blind when enabled and dealt
        >= minPlayersDealt; from the hand's own captured configuration; none
        captured -> unknown;
      tournament: rake_amount and bbj_amount must both be 0.
Verdicts: MATCH (a)-(d) hold with every input recorded; MATCH_PARTIAL (a)-(c)
hold, (d) unknown; MISMATCH any of (a)-(d) fails, with exact numbers per hand
(hashed hand id only); EXCLUDED by reason, never zero-filled:
live_eligible_cards_not_recorded, replay_disagrees_with_capture,
replay_pot_size_disagrees, unresolved_raise_amount, unhandled_action:<name>,
pots_not_recorded, winners_not_recorded, pot_size_not_recorded,
board_incomplete_contested, chip_unit_unknown, actor_not_in_players,
pooled_dead_money_encoding_does_not_hold, reference_threw.
Uncontested hands (one non-folded contributor) are re-settled (the whole pot
to that player) and reported separately from contested ones; their missing
board cards are stood in by unused deck cards because no hand is scored.
A mismatch is a finding; money is never changed.
