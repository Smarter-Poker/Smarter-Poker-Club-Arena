2. Population W (per window, every natural hand, any variant, any mode)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
W1 walks: hands in which every player action other than forced posts
  (sb, bb, ante, post, bomb_ante) and uncalled returns is a fold, every fold
  is preflop, at least one fold exists, a bb was posted, and there is no
  straddle or kill blind (everyone folded to the big blind).
W2 one-player pots: hands whose recorded pots contain at least one pot with
  exactly one eligible player.
W = W1 union W2. Nothing is sampled. Split by mode (cash chips, cash
diamonds, tournament) and variant.
Export: psql on the operator Mac, SELECT only, BEGIN READ ONLY ... ROLLBACK,
statement_timeout 170 s, one 15-minute slice per statement; an interval that
cannot be read even in 5-minute slices is reported as unknown_not_exported.

3. Rule (engine's stated rule, returnUncalledBet at 5c7d15db)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions are replayed from actions before any return (same replay as
p9-natural-compare.mts). Matched contribution = total - shared dead money,
where shared dead money is a shared big blind ante (ante type big_blind) and
a dead blind post; individual antes (per_player ante, bomb_ante) count as
matched. With at least two contributors, a UNIQUE non-folded top contributor
has uncalled excess = top matched - second matched. That excess must be
refunded (a recorded return to that player of exactly that amount, to the
cent) and must never form a one-player pot. A one-player pot is allowed only
when it is genuinely contested money as calculatePots defines it: its level,
built from post-refund matched contributions, has at least one other
(folded) contributor, or it is the pooled dead-money pot.
Counts per window, mode and variant:
  one-player pots (hands with one; pots), split genuine / uncontested-money;
  walks with a refund equal to the expected excess;
  walks with no refund and the excess recorded inside a one-player pot;
  walks with no excess expected (nothing to refund);
  any other refund disagreement (wrong amount, refund where none is due).
Cash rake on W hands: expected per the engine's stated rules: no rake and no
BBJ when no flop was dealt; otherwise rake = min(round(pot after refund x
pct), cap tier by dealt), pct at most 5 heads-up, BBJ = feeBB x big blind
when enabled, a flop was dealt and dealt >= minPlayersDealt; from the
hand's captured configuration (none -> unknown). Tournament: rake 0, BBJ 0.
Excluded/unknown by reason, never zero-filled: replay_disagrees_with_capture,
unresolved_raise_amount, unhandled_action, ante_type_unknown, pots_not_recorded.
