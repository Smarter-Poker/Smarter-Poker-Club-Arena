- **Method:** No purchases were made, no accounts were created and no private back offices were accessed. Sources were official help centers (rendered with headless Chromium, because the ClubGG help center is a JavaScript Salesforce site), official vendor web pages, the public page script that drives the ClubGG union pricing slider, official App Store listings (several storefronts), and secondary guides or forums where no official source exists.

## 0. Conventions

### 0.1 Confidence scale

| Label                                          | Meaning                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **P1: primary official**                       | Published by the vendor: help center, official site or site script, or an official App Store listing in the **US** storefront.          |
| **P2: primary official, non-US or historical** | Vendor-published, but from a non-US USD storefront (for example Uruguay, Jordan or Cambodia), or a dated vendor post that may be stale. |
| **S: secondary reputable**                     | Industry guide or review site. It is not a vendor checkout and cannot certify a claim.                                                  |
| **U: unverified forum**                        | Forum post or anecdote.                                                                                                                 |
| **ND: not documented**                         | Nothing public was found. Unknown is **not** zero.                                                                                      |

### 0.2 Normalization rules (conservative toward the competitor)

- **Smarter:** 1 diamond = US$0.01 nominal. The durations below are 30 days (720 h).
- **Calendar-month prices:** USD per 30 days = monthly price × 30/31. This uses the longest month, so it gives the competitor's _lowest_ possible 30-day cost and makes the claim harder to pass. The flat reading (1 month = 30 days) is also shown.
- **Annual prices:** USD per 30 days = annual price × 30/365. An annual price is a commitment and is not matched to a cancel-anytime 30-day renewal. It is shown only as context.
- **Competitor diamond items:** the consumed value is priced at the vendor's **best official US App Store package rate**, which is the lowest cost to the competitor's customer. The cash needed to buy whole packages is recorded separately (section 2).
- **Taxes:** US App Store prices exclude sales tax. Non-US storefront prices may include local VAT. No taxes were added to either side.
- **20% ceiling (R2 section 4.2):** `max_smarter_diamonds = floor(0.80 × C / 0.01)`. The 25% internal-buffer ceiling `floor(0.75 × C / 0.01)` is also shown. All ceilings were computed with exact rational arithmetic (Python `fractions`).
- **Claim test:** a "20% cheaper" claim is marked **supportable** only when (a) the comparator is P1 (or P2 with its limits disclosed), (b) the capacity is genuinely equivalent, (c) the Smarter price is at or below the 20% ceiling, and (d) no lower verified comparable cost exists (including a free tier).

**Whole-package cash vs consumed value.** Reaching the best rate needs a $99.99 package on each app. A customer buying only what one renewal needs pays more:

- **X-Poker:** a 1,200-diamond renewal consumes $9.2298 at the 13,000 rate. The cheapest exact cash route is 10 × 120 packs = **$9.90** (2 × 600 = $9.98).
- **PokerBROS:** a 1,000-diamond item consumes $15.4592. Small-pack cash is about **$16.83** (17 × 60 packs), or $19.99 (one 1218 pack).

The ceilings use consumed value, the lower of the two figures, so they are conservative.

## 6. Entire-cart checks (R2 section 4.3)

### 6.1 Ten 100-member clubs vs Poker Now Platinum (P1)

- Smarter: 10 × 700 = **7,000 diamonds = $70.00 / 30 d** before any extras.
- Poker Now Platinum: $49.99/mo → **$48.3774 / 30 d**. Annual $499/yr → $41.0137 / 30 d, which is a different commitment and is not matched.
- **Result: Smarter costs 44.7% MORE** than the monthly-matched Poker Now bundle. The 20% ceiling for the whole basket is **3,870 diamonds** (387 per club). Poker Now Platinum also includes club MTTs/SNGs, summary reports, transaction logs and admin roles. Under R2 4.3, those included reports cannot be billed separately in an equivalent comparison.
- Scope caveat: Poker Now is a play-chip home-game product with a different feature set. Features, including approval flows, chip ledger, agents and unions, must be matched before it becomes a binding comparator. The price gap in this basket is still a material risk to any global "cheaper" message.

### 6.2 Union cart vs ClubGG (UBO + club levels)

The ClubGG union master club must be at Level 1 or higher ($19.3452/30 d). Member clubs of ≤100 members are free on ClubGG.

## 8. Caveats, corrections and open items

- **C1 (ClubGG billing period).** The FAQ confirms a recurring subscription with a "next billing date" and platform-specific up/downgrade rules, but no public page states the period. Monthly is assumed. Next action: an in-app or App Store subscription-sheet screenshot of Club Level 1A with its period.
- **C2 (ClubGG storefront).** The US App Store web listing surfaces only 1A ($19.99) among its top 10 items. Levels 2 to 9 were observed only in USD storefronts outside the US (UY, JO, KH). UY 1A equals US 1A ($19.99), which suggests the same A-variant price grid, but this is unverified for the US. Next action: in-app price list or US subscription sheet.
- **C3 (Level-name mapping).** "Club Level nX" in the store is mapped to FAQ "Level n" by name. Suffix letters (A, B, C, E) look like price or region variants. Their meaning is not documented.
- **C4 (UBO Plan 10).** The help-center FAQ gives Plan 10 = 350 clubs. The clubgg.com/unions script uses `max: 1/0` (Infinity) with the label "250+ clubs". Both give $10,000.
- **Correction to R2 4.1 record (ClubGG UBO).** R2 recorded only "$5,000/month for up to 50 clubs; larger plan price not retrieved". The full 10-plan table is now retrieved from two P1 sources (section 3.1, G8).
- **Correction to R2 4.1 record (ClubGG club listings).** The 2E to 7E prices recorded in R2 are confirmed (JO). This pass adds 8E $899.99 and 9E $999.99 (JO), KH variants 7E $799.99 and 8E $999.99, and UY 2A $49.99. The FAQ capacity table (Free 100 through L9 50,000) now allows the A/E SKUs to be mapped to member caps by level number.
- **Secondary-source conflicts.** pokernetai.com says ClubGG has a "$0" club-level fee, which is true only for the Free level (≤100). thepokeragent.com's ClubGG "Level 1 $50 / 250, Level 2 $100 / 1,000" predates the current nine-level system.
- **Not verified in this pass:** PokerStars Home Games limits (the page redirected), the Pokerrrr 2 host tariff, UPoker and PokerTime tariffs, all PokerBROS level prices and capacities, and PPPoker and X-Poker current tariffs. The R2 conditional examples (X-Poker 1,200 → ceiling 738; PokerBROS 1,000 → 1,236; ClubGG export 560 → 447) are re-derived and match exactly. They remain secondary.
- **Chips are out of scope.** ClubGG chips are free and unlimited. PokerBROS and X-Poker chip-for-diamond rates are secondary and are not comparable to Smarter chip obligations (R2 3.6).
