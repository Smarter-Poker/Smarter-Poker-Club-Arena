# D80 Installed-Path Map, Per Offered SKU

R2 test D80 (`CA-DIAMOND-COMMERCE-2026-09-22-R2.md` line 1637): "Final installed-path acceptance maps each offered SKU to its current quote, payer, term, charge/waiver, rights, data visibility, test and release evidence. Unavailable offerings and unresolved claims are not labeled complete."

"Offered" means the SKU has `supported = true` and a published price in catalog v1. There are nine such SKUs (M1:446-455, M1:464-473). The install asserts exactly nine published prices at M1:2049-2052, and RM `catalog v1 publishes nine prices` checks it. The seven unsupported SKUs are listed at the end and explained in `unsupported-offerings.md`.

Short names follow `c1-wiring-map.md`.

## Unresolved claims on the offered SKUs

- **No comparison claim.** Every price is `comparison_verified=false` (`../competitor-evidence-2026-09-24.md`).
- **The candidate price authority is still the one in effect.** Every `price_authority` names the R2 candidate schedule (M1:465-473). No newer owner-confirmed price is recorded (`CHECKPOINT.md:181-184`).
- **Price cliff.** The 100 to 250 member step (700 to 1,500) is recorded on the `capacity_250` price as "not an approved optimum" (M1:467).
- **Not verified.** The platform's store or provider acceptance for this product has not been verified (`activation-matrix.md` section 3).
