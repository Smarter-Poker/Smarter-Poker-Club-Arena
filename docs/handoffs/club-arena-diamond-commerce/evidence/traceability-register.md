## 4. Requirements that nothing in the implementation satisfies

1. **Supported SKUs whose right is not enforced anywhere today.** `union_back_office` and `union_insurance_module` are published and purchasable (M1:471, M1:473). No door checks them: the admission changelog says "Union back office tools (`union_tools`) have no door", and no door calls `union_insurance`. M4 wires only `approve_member`, `open_table`, `club_insurance` and `create_tournament` (M4:295-425). A union that pays for back office or union insurance gets nothing it did not already have. R2 3.2 (line 603) says it "buys actual union-wide oversight...", and R2 line 165 says "do not sell an unfinished capability as available". While enforcement is off, the same is true of capacity and the club insurance module (shadow only). That is the intended rollout stage, but it should be stated to a buyer.

4. **Waiver recorded at catalog price** (R2 2.2 at line 1031): the trial right records `net_paid=0` but not the waived catalog price or waiver reason.
5. **Shared interface with Prompt 1** (R2 1.6 at line 1011): not built.
6. **Delegated sponsor spending** from a club admin session through a trusted server route (R2 1.2 at line 913): not built (`CHECKPOINT.md:73-77`).
7. **Named assignment of covered clubs** to purchased union coverage (R2 5.2 at line 1179): not built.
8. **Operational metrics for ambiguity, duplicate suppression and postcondition failures** (R2 7.5 at line 1301): not persisted (`operational-metrics.md`).
9. **Capability withdrawal does not invalidate open quotes** (D06): `purchase_impl` never rechecks `supported`.
10. **Staff refunds from a browser** remain an owner decision (`CHECKPOINT.md:195-204`). M3 adds the owner-request, staff-decide and consumer-execute route instead.
