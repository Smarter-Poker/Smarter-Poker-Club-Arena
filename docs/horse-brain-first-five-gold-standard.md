## Phase 1: solver trust lockdown

### Built mechanisms

- Retire both unsafe public RPC names, `fn_pio_options_from_solver` and `fn_chart_options_from_memory`, including signature/overload/dependency preflight, revocation, removal and postconditions.
- Prevent resurrection with a database event trigger covering CREATE, replacement, rename and schema movement. Source-order tests prevent a later migration from casually recreating a retired helper. The public compatibility surface returns retirement rather than fabricated advice.
- Keep legacy V1 `f`/`fold` data distinct from the V2 Fold action. A V1 matrix carrying the ambiguous channel is rejected as a whole; deleting only Fold would manufacture a conditional strategy.
- Treat a present V2 payload as authoritative. Invalid V2 cannot silently fall back to V1. A JSON field claiming `source: pio_v2` cannot mint process-local trust.
- Validate 1,326-combo order, checksummed ranges/hand order, solver/convergence envelope, exact board/runout, actor, cumulative Pio contribution targets, pot and remaining effective stack, outgoing legal branches and normalized frequencies before producing the 169-class bridge.
- Carry exact node pot, facing price, contribution baseline, last full raise, all-in state and action-size semantics to consumers. Pio `bNNN` means a cumulative contribution target; it is not always a fresh-street bet amount.
- Route Training/solver-facing consumers through the trusted selector or an independently strict V2 reader; retire unsafe legacy endpoint paths. Invalidate pre-boundary offline caches. Current offline cache version is 3, with account/session/attempt/receipt expiry and complete-pack constraints, stronger than the original version-2 invalidation.

## Phase 2: canonical solver policy service

### Built mechanisms

- One versioned JSON schema and portable fixture/artifact set across JavaScript and TypeScript: `smarter-poker.solver-policy.v1`, policy version `solver-policy-service.1.0.1`.
- A decision key containing variant, betting structure, table size, dealer/blind/hero/opponent positions, stacks and commitments, blinds/antes/straddles, rake, tournament objective, payouts/bounties, street, physical board/holding, ordered public actions, legal sizes and eligible side pots.
- Seven answer kinds: exact, aggregated, derived, chart, curated, heuristic and unavailable. Exact is conditional on complete state, complete source provenance, no approximation and exact legal sizes. A shape-valid answer is not automatically solver-exact.
- Full action distribution and range distribution; action family/source code/label; sizes in chips, BB or pot fraction; action and policy chip EV and tournament utility; explicit measured-EV flags, confidence, missing dimensions, exclusions and fallback reason.
- Relational checks: chip/BB conversion, seat/position identities, duplicate cards and pots, chronological history, eligible seats, legal bounds, action/distribution agreement, EV maps, quality-seal/kind agreement and exact-node semantics.
- The World service reads a bounded canonical catalog, normalizes source records and emits a consumer envelope. The current surface registry distinguishes actual service consumers, delegated routes, strict direct readers, authored references and retired endpoints. It currently has four direct service consumers; the old claim of sixteen uniformly live consumers is not today's topology.
- Preflop chart adapter preserves sparse frequencies, reconstructs all 169 classes and labels them CHART_AUDITED rather than exact Pio nodes. Cash/Tournament × 20 depths × six position/action coordinates gives 240 rows; missing sparse hands fold only after validating the row.
- Club atomically hydrates charts and external portable policies into detached immutable indexes, preserves the last good set after invalid refresh, retries failed loading and keeps I/O off the action clock.
- `GtoCharts` reads the canonical index; `HorseLogic` uses the distribution for unopened ≤15 BB push/fold and BB versus SB open-jam ≤25 BB, with variant, line, straddle and structure guards. Mixed frequencies are sampled; the returned maximum-frequency label is not blindly treated as a pure action. Persona adherence and later utility/safety ownership remain explicit.
- Worker startup loads the real stores before READY, periodically refreshes them, exposes counts/errors/version in health, and stops the loaders on shutdown. Solver-agreement tooling reads the same policy meaning.

### Ownership and limits

World: `SolverPolicyService.js`, `solverPolicyContract.js`, portable contracts, `HorsePokerGTO.js`, `lib/god-mode-service.ts`. Club: `server/src/gto/SolverPolicyContract.ts`, `SolverPolicyArtifactLoader.ts`, `GtoChartCorpus.ts`, `GtoChartLoader.ts`, `GtoCharts.ts`, `HorseLogic.ts` and worker startup.

The chart source is live with 240 rows. It does not provide a complete rake/table/ICM-specific exact solver key, and the adapter discloses missing dimensions. Chart frequency agreement is not measured regret or proof of profitability. Dormant non-chart artifact entries are not proof that live horses consume every possible artifact kind.

New nested malformed-JSON tests reproduced six validator crashes in Club. The repair stops before relational dereferences on invalid shapes and makes malformed card vectors safe in the completeness helper. The matching World repair is tested as an isolated candidate; source integration remains open. Schema-byte equality alone had not prevented this shared defect.

Further caller tracing reproduced three additional ownership/identity faults in World: changing the original V2 combo array changed an already normalized policy; the Horse postflop cache omitted blinds/table size and returned the wrong prior key; and validation accepted one physical card twice under different capitalization. The candidate now detaches/freezes normalized records, keys the Horse cache by the complete resolver request, freezes cached envelopes, expires entries after 60 seconds without extending expiry on hits, and rejects noncanonical card names. The case-insensitive user-input normalizer still produces canonical names. Direct completeness calls with malformed card vectors also reject without throwing. These are tested candidate fixes, not deployed behavior. The 60-second cache limit bounds staleness; immediate source revocation still needs a version/invalidation signal.

### Reusable requirements

The chart-agreement benchmark had mislabeled seats, omitted MP and mapped every raise to a shove and an open-call to a fold. It could also exceed its sample budget. The repair constructs the actual six-seat order, preserves the dealt census/fold history and posted blinds, includes MP, and drives real chart-lookup assertions at each position. Sampling is bounded by a positive integer budget. Because the installed receipt schema only represents binary chart actions, an out-of-reference action now rejects the entire score instead of fabricating a receipt or shrinking the denominator. Full raw-action coverage requires a separately qualified receipt-schema expansion; a rejected score is unavailable evidence, not a passing agreement result.

Publish one semantic contract, version data and code, map every producer and consumer truthfully, compare both runtimes adversarially, and admit complete immutable snapshots atomically. Give every lookup a real supported domain and expose misses. Differentiate approximate strategy, exact strategy, measured EV and unavailable evidence. Prove benchmark labels match the actual state and preserve the action that the horse really took.

- Bind the snapshot to lifecycle generation, hand/turn fence and canonical digest. Current sampling identity excludes observational review fields while full validation still binds them; adding diagnostics must not change a mixed action.

- Every returned decision is legalized against the authoritative menu. Legal liveness fallback is explicit.
