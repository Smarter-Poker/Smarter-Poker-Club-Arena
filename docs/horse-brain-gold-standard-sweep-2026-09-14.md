## New committed-over-10BB audit requirement

The old `HorseHandReview` selects absolute net wins/losses of at least20BB. It does not meet the new requirement. The requested population is cumulative chips committed during a hand strictly greater than10BB, including subsequently returned uncalled wagers, across every variant/format and outcome. Canonical `hand_atomic_commits.post_commit_payload.accepted_hand_facts` retains net contributions and returned-uncalled amounts; action-log summation is not authoritative accounting.

A separate review population must preserve legacy rollup/rate denominators. Full replay, matched reference evidence, causal proposal/holdout/rollback and complete source coverage are still required. Missing facts/reference must produce an unverified finding, never a fabricated GTO verdict. Source timestamps are not complete commit watermarks. This checkpoint has not implemented or published the new audit.
