## Horse access and learning

Membership modes, cash personas, stakes, variants and table limits have actual admission readers. Real-time HorseMind statistics influence ranges and response models. These are distinct from diagnostic review tags and the newer scoped-model reports. A table containing tags does not establish runtime use, and a statistical report does not prove better action value. Tournament membership persona fields still have no runtime optional-entry owner; mandatory fills, ticket starts and freeroll obligations must retain their explicit owners.

The existing strict gross commitment >10 BB sweep ignores whether the Horse won or lost and preserves returned uncalled wagers in eligibility. It remains triage with an unverified GTO verdict. Its three-day revisit, current-profile classification and retained-source bounds do not prove every historical or retired Horse hand. Qualified learning needs original decision inputs, legal alternative actions and uncertainty-aware evidence, with independent holdout and explicit versioned approval. The implementation must not infer an optimal action from the final result of one hand.

The private discard extension proves a physical choice only when the original worker request and actual controller receipt reconcile against a committed hand. It does not add private cards to public events or opponent observations. Prepared all-in choices are not execution; stale or rejected actions cannot become accepted evidence. Runtime pins remain explicitly incomplete; reader results continue to deny full-population, replay, GTO and activation claims.
