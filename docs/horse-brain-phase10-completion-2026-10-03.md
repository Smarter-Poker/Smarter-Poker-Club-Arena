## Statement

Phase 10 is complete and closed as **not promoted**. The PLO4 pack's inputs are bound and naturally verified, its strength was measured once under a contract locked by merge before measurement, and the result does not support promotion: the pooled cash edge is indistinguishable from zero and heads-up play (two dealt at a six-max table) loses about a quarter of a big blind per hand against the reference. The authority path exists, is bound to the running code, and selects nothing.

## Audit Of October 3, 2026

A line-by-line audit of every Phase 10 merge found no path that could apply a PLO4 proposal live. It found, and Phase 10 fixed: the qualification was never compared with the running code (F1, runtime policy digest v2 over eleven files); an occupied button with a dead small blind mislabelled positions and the dead-button wrap assumed ten seats (F3, F5); the range capture ran outside the work budget (F7); an invalid shadow-only receipt could stop the worker client (F8); the contract digest was not pinned by a test (F9); and the records disagreed with code in several places (#5996). Design limits carried to a contract v2, not defects: the league harness differs from live play (mood, modifiers, fresh HorseMind, fixed clock, no second look, opponents off), and the four street-family cells cannot fail in practice (14 of 18 cells are effective).

Observed outside Phase 10 and not changed here: the same small-blind-after-button assumption in `JointActionModel.jointPlayersBehind`, HorseLogic `phase7PlayersBehind` and `classifyPosition`, the table's `positionLabelsFor`, and the Phase 11–13 `OmahaVariantLivePolicy` and `RemainingVariantLivePolicy` dealer check.
