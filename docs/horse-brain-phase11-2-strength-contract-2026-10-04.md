## Objectives

**Cash after rake (measured).** The metric is the paired difference of the hero seat's net chips per hand: final stack minus starting stack, after the engine's rake and BBJ drop, candidate minus reference, reported in bb/100. The candidate is `HorseLogic.decide` with `phase11Omaha: 'candidate'` (OmahaVariantLivePolicy over the pack). The reference is the same call with `phase11Omaha: 'off'`: the action live tables execute today, because Phase 11 runs in shadow, under the harness conditions named below. Both arms of a pair use the same deal seed: identical cards, hero seat, button, seat styles and decision clock. BBJ jackpot awards are excluded; the database pays them from the external pool after the hand.

**Tournament prize and bounty: unavailable external input (contract status `unavailable dependency`), refused by name for every pack.** The verdict returns these in `tournament.reasons`, and the qualification file in `objectives.tournament.reasons`. They are never in the top-level `reasons`, which hold cash failures only and alone decide `qualified` (test: a passing synthetic matrix qualifies cash while the tournament block refuses).

- `tournament:<variant>_whole_tournament_outcome_model_unavailable`. The only coherent outcome model for a tournament objective in source is the Phase 8 paired whole-tournament league (`HorseTournamentLeague.ts`, `realizedTournamentReturn`), and it deals NLH only. The Phase 11 league tournament profile measures single-hand chip EV.
- `tournament:qualified_<variant>_tournament_reference_population_unavailable`. No source-qualified PLO5, PLO6 or PLO8 tournament structure population exists.
- `tournament:<variant>_tournament_thresholds_not_specified`. NLH tournament thresholds are not borrowed, and no number is invented before an outcome model exists.

The workflow's plan job reads the pack's matrix from the contract at the evaluated commit.

It recorded only the paired-difference SD and kurtosis (from the exact power sums) and the time per pair. No held-out seed was used and no mean difference was computed or written. Every validity counter was 0, and all 5,760 pilot hands of each pack (17,280 in all) were independently settled; PLO8 settled 632 low halves. The pilot ran before the contract carried its numbers, so its checkout was dirty by construction; each log carries the sha256 of the league and policy files that played and the provisional digest it ran under.

## Statistic And Uncertainty

Unchanged from Phase 10 and implemented here by the contract's own functions: exact integer chip cents per pair; exact power sums `n, S1..S4` per dealer-relative offset × divergence street per shard, merged exactly as BigInt; divergence street as the street of the first action at which the arms' traces differ (identical traces with a nonzero difference are a paired replay mismatch); the stratified mean with equal weight per profile in a cell; a two-sided 99% normal (Wald) interval `T ± z·sqrt(Σ w_p² s_p² / n_p)` with `z = Φ⁻¹(0.995) = 2.5758293035489004`; trusted only if the first Edgeworth term `|κ|(2z² + 1)φ(z)/6` is at most 0.001 and every profile in the cell has at least 10,000 pairs. A cell failing either guard is `interval_not_trusted` and refused.

## Thresholds And Their Justification

No new number. Each gate is Phase 10's, with its reason in the contract.

| Gate                          | Threshold                                                                                                             | Justification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary, cash after rake      | 99% lower bound **> 0 bb/100**, the pack's five profiles pooled with equal weight                                     | Zero is break-even after the house's take; the lower end of a two-sided 99% interval bounds a false promotion at 0.5%. No effect-size floor, because no calibrated cost of switching exists in source. The reasoning does not depend on the variant       |
| Seed replication              | Each held-out seed block's pooled point estimate **> 0 bb/100**                                                       | Three independent blocks fixed in advance; a zero true effect passes all three with probability 1/8 on top of the primary bound                                                                                                                           |
| Critical-domain nonregression | Every gating cell's 99% lower bound **≥ −10 bb/100**                                                                  | 10 bb/100 is one 100 BB buy-in per 1,000 hands, about a whole winning edge in big-bet Omaha cash (PLO4, PLO5, PLO6 and PLO8 alike). The shard counts plan every gating cell's half-width at or below 8.5 bb/100. No multiplicity relief: all 14 must pass |
| Interval validity             | Edgeworth coverage error ≤ 0.001; ≥ 10,000 pairs per profile in each cell                                             | Phase 10's guard; the planned sizes keep the variance estimate's relative standard error under 0.1 at every pilot kurtosis                                                                                                                                |
| Software validity, per shard  | Illegal actions, conservation, card errors, truncated hands, settlement, deduction and paired replay mismatches all 0 | Any nonzero count means the measurement is not of the policy                                                                                                                                                                                              |

**Gating cells: 14 per pack, all effective.** Five profiles, six positions (`positionForOffset`, the pack's names; heads-up offset 0 is the button) and three depth bands (short 40 BB; standard 100 BB at 2, 4 and 6 dealt; deep 200 BB). Each holds only its own domain's pairs, so its 99% lower bound sits below the true domain mean with probability 99.5%: a true domain loss of 10 bb/100 or more fails with at least that probability (tests: a losing big blind, a losing 200 BB profile and the deep band each fail by name while the pool wins).

**Street families: reported, never gated.** `streetFamiliesDiagnostic` carries each street's contribution to the pooled bb/100 (zero for pairs that diverged elsewhere) with the number of pairs diverging there; the four contributions and the no-divergence pairs sum to the primary estimate (test). Why not a gate: a contribution cell is diluted by every pair that did not diverge on its street, so breaching −10 needs a conditional loss of hundreds of bb/100 (Phase 10's four street cells spanned about ±2.3 bb/100 and could not fail); a conditional cell has the right scale, but late-street divergences are a small, run-dependent share of pairs, so its pair count and skewness are not fixed before the run and its interval cannot be planned to resolve the margin at feasible sizes. A test builds a matrix whose flop contribution's 99% interval lies wholly below −10 bb/100 while every gating cell passes: the pack qualifies and the diagnostic is reported with `gate: false`.
