## The selection rule

The selection is committed at a protected code release (`HorsePhase11AuthoritySelection`: `variant`, exact 40-hex `sourceSha`, `packVersion`, `contractVersion`, 64-hex `contractDigest`, `domain`, `qualificationPath` and `completionPath` under `docs/evidence/phase11/`, their sha256, positive `approvalGeneration`, `issuedAt`, optional `expiresAt` and `withdrawn`). There is no runtime issuer. `admitHorsePhase11QualifiedAuthority(variant, …)` refuses, in this order:

1. no selection: `unselected`;
2. a malformed selection (an unknown variant, a path outside `docs/evidence/phase11/` or with traversal, a completion path without its sha256 or equal to the qualification path, a bad digest, generation or date): `invalid_selection`;
3. a committed withdrawal: `withdrawn` (sticky; only a greater approval generation renews it);
4. a selection whose variant, pack version or domain is not the running pack's (`<variant>-cash-single-board-after-rake-horse-population`): `continuation_mismatch`. This is where a PLO5 selection placed under PLO6 is refused;
5. no running contract digest: `contract_unavailable`; a selection for any other digest: `contract_digest_mismatch`;
6. a running policy digest that cannot be computed: `policy_digest_unavailable`;
7. an expired selection: `expired`;
8. a missing qualification file: `missing_evidence`; any other read error: `unreadable_evidence` (transient, a refresh failure, never a withdrawal);
9. bytes that do not hash to `qualificationSha256`: `hash_mismatch`;
10. not JSON, or not `horse-phase11-qualification-v1`: `evidence_mismatch`;
11. `qualified` is not `true`, `mode` is not `contract`, or `objectives.cash.qualified` is not `true`: `not_qualified` (a development assembly always writes `qualified: false`);
12. the file's `contractDigest` differs from the running digest or the selection: `contract_digest_mismatch`;
13. the file's `policyDigestDefinition` is not `horse-phase11-policy-digest-v2` or its `policyDigest` is not `horsePhase11PolicyDigest(variant)` of the running code: `policy_digest_mismatch`. The digest hashes the variant and its pack version, so a PLO5 file can never satisfy PLO6;
14. the file's `sourceSha` differs from the selection: `source_mismatch`;
15. the file's variant, pack version or domain differs: `continuation_mismatch`;
16. another contract version, an `admissionAlsoRequires` other than the contract's own, or a malformed `evidencePath` or `evidenceSha256`: `evidence_mismatch`;
17. the strength record it names is missing or does not hash to `evidenceSha256`: `missing_evidence` or `hash_mismatch`;
18. the completion record (next section): `completion_evidence_missing`, `unreadable_evidence` (transient), `completion_hash_mismatch`, `completion_evidence_mismatch`, `completion_release_mismatch`, `completion_window_invalid`, `completion_below_floor`.

Only then is a frozen `HorseQualifiedAuthority` admitted (`phase: 'phase11'`, the variant, the pack version as `continuationVersion` and `packId`, the running policy digest, the contract digest, both evidence paths and hashes, and an `authorityKey` over all of them plus the completion definition and floor). `selectedHorsePhase11Authority(variant, admission)` returns it only for that variant, or `null`.

**Mode.** `horsePhase11AdmittedMode` gives `off` when the caller turned the packs off; `shadow` for any decision that is not `cash`, whose variant is not a Phase 11 pack, or whose variant is not the pack of the holder that produced the verdict; `candidate` only when that pack's own worker verdict is `usable`. It is evaluated when each request runs, FAST or DEEP.

**Acceptance.** An applied Phase 11 receipt is accepted by the table only when `liveHorsePhase11Authorities[receipt.variant].check(receipt.authority)` is `usable` in the same synchronous turn as `performAction` (a receipt of a non-pack variant is `mismatched`), and plan effects only after the same check. Any other verdict (`withdrawn`, `stale_generation`, `restarted`, `refresh_failed`, `refused`, `unselected`, `mismatched`, `expired`, `missing_receipt`) executes the shadow baseline, re-selects the witness to it so the exact accepted-wager check holds, and records `withdrawn_before_acceptance` with the verdict. A controller refusal or coercion of a selected proposal withdraws that pack's authority for this engine process; the other packs are untouched.

## The natural completion requirement and its floor

The P11.2 contract measured each pack on a fixed policy clock, so its fallback never fired and every sample completed (`liveConditions.differences[policy_clock]`). It made admission depend on natural evidence that live decisions do complete: "P11.3 must state and meet its own floor for that share before admitting the pack". P11.3 meets that with a committed record per pack and a floor in code.

**The record** (`horse-phase11-completion-v1`, exact keys): `schema`, `definition` (`horse-phase11-completion-definition-v2`), `variant`, `packVersion`, `releaseSha` (40 hex, the engine release the decisions were read from), `policyDigestDefinition`, `policyDigest` (that release's `horsePhase11PolicyDigest(variant)`), `gameMode: 'cash'`, `window: { from, to, releaseUnchanged, source }`, and `streets.{preflop,flop,turn,river}` each `{ eligible, completed, workBudget, samplerBudgetExhausted, sampleUnavailable }` with `eligible = completed + workBudget + samplerBudgetExhausted + sampleUnavailable`. `releaseSha` identifies the window's source release; admission binds the record to the running policy through `policyDigest`, not through `releaseSha`, so a later engine release with unchanged policy code keeps a valid record.

**What it counts** (`horsePhase11CompletionOutcome`, `horsePhase11CompletionCounts`, pure functions over journaled `omahaVariantPolicy` receipts). Population: natural cash decisions of the pack's variant and version; tournament decisions are not counted because they never admit candidate mode. Eligible: the receipt is `eligible`. Not completed: `reason: 'work_budget'` (the policy passed `OMAHA_VARIANT_DOMAIN.liveBudgetMs`, 4 ms, and fell back to the reference), or a consumed live range sample the sampler cut short at its own 3 ms budget (`inputs.range.provenance.work.budgetExhausted`). The contract names only the first; the second is counted too because the matrix priced every proposal on a completed fixed-work sample, so a proposal priced on a truncated one is not the measured policy either. Definition v2 (post-merge audit) adds the third case: a postflop proposal whose live range sample was not consumed at all (`inputs.range.status` other than `consumed`, typically the sampler's 3 ms budget spent before its first completed sample), counted as `sampleUnavailable`. v1 counted such a decision as completed, although the matrix never priced a postflop proposal without a sample. Shadow and candidate receipts run the same policy code, so a shadow release whose policy digest equals the running one measures the share the candidate would see.

**What admission requires of it**: the selection names it and its sha256 (`completion_evidence_missing` otherwise or if the file does not exist; `completion_hash_mismatch` for other bytes); it is well formed with counts that add up (`completion_evidence_mismatch`); it was measured on the running pack's policy: the same variant, pack version, digest definition and policy digest as the running code (`completion_release_mismatch`); its window is a real interval of exact ISO timestamps, the release was unchanged throughout, and the window ended no later than the selection was issued (`completion_window_invalid`); and every street clears the floor (`completion_below_floor`).

**The floor: 0.95 per street, on the 99% lower confidence bound** (`HORSE_PHASE11_COMPLETION_FLOOR`). Justification, also in the code:

- _Why a floor at all._ A decision that falls back executes the reference action, so a hand can mix candidate and reference streets in a line neither arm of the matrix played, and a truncated sample prices a different proposal. A pack that falls back on a large share of decisions is not the policy that was measured, whatever its qualification says.
- _Why 0.95._ No calibrated cost of such a mixed line exists in source (the same reason the P11.2 contract has no effect-size floor), so the floor bounds the unmeasured share rather than its cost: at least 19 of every 20 eligible decisions on every street must be the measured policy. It is a stated bound, not a fitted number; raising it is a reviewed code change, and the floor is bound into every authority key.
- _Why per street._ Sampling runs postflop, mostly on the river, where P11.1 measured 3.5 to 5.7 ms for the full sample against the 4 ms live budget (G8 of the [P11.1 record](horse-brain-phase11-1-input-binding-2026-10-04.md)). A pooled share would let a completing preflop hide a failing river.
- _Why a lower bound._ The share is judged as the Wilson score lower bound at the P11.2 interval's own z (`OMAHA_VARIANT_STRENGTH_CONTRACT.interval.z`, Φ⁻¹(0.995) = 2.5758…), so a street whose true share is below 0.95 passes with probability at most 0.5%, and a small window cannot pass by luck: a street in which every decision completes still needs at least 127 eligible decisions (`n / (n + z²) ≥ 0.95`). Tested: 127 all-complete passes, 126 does not; 100 fallbacks in 2,000 (a point share of exactly 0.95) is refused, 50 in 2,000 is admitted.

The reader that produces a record from the journal for a chosen window is built in two stages: `server/scripts/phase11-completion-extract.py` reads the host journal segments read-only and emits each window decision's receipt, and `server/src/scripts/phase11CompletionRecord.ts` counts them with `horsePhase11CompletionCounts` and computes the release's digest from `git show <release>:server/<file>` (see the [sampler-speed changelog](changelog/2026-10-04-horse-brain-phase11-sampler-speed-and-completion-reader.md)).

## One pack never admits another

- **Admission.** The same PLO5 selection and files, admitted as PLO6 or PLO8, are `continuation_mismatch`; a PLO5 qualification file under a PLO6 selection is `policy_digest_mismatch`; a PLO5 completion record under a PLO6 selection is `completion_release_mismatch`; `selectedHorsePhase11Authority('plo6', plo5Admission)` is `null`.
- **Holders.** A holder of each pack is `usable` only for its own pack's admission; a PLO5 admission in a PLO6 or PLO8 holder, in a PLO4 holder, and a Phase 10 admission in a Phase 11 holder are all `mismatched`.
- **Gates.** A withdrawal, a refresh failure or a worker restart at one pack's gate leaves the other two `usable` (`HorsePhase11Authority.test.ts`); a controller refusal withdraws only that pack (`HorseDecisionEffectCommit.test.ts`); a usable PLO5 authority stamped on a PLO8 receipt is refused at the PLO8 gate (`unselected`) and the baseline executes.
- **Worker.** With only PLO5 selected, PLO6 and PLO8 decisions run in shadow, and a PLO6 or PLO8 receipt that borrows the PLO5 receipt is refused at the worker boundary.

## Phase 7 tournament ownership

The P11.2 contract can grant only the cash objective; it refuses the tournament objective by name per pack. P11.3 enforces that three times:

- **Worker rule.** A tournament decision is never given candidate mode, whatever the authority says. "a %s tournament decision keeps Phase 7 ownership even when the pack would be selected" (PLO5, PLO8) runs one worker with usable test-fixture authority for that pack: the request runs in shadow and its action, Phase 7 receipt and Phase 6 attribution equal those of a worker with no authority.
- **Owner level.** `omaha/OmahaVariantPhase11Selection.test.ts`: "a %s %s tournament decision under usable Phase 11 authority is Phase 7’s decision" on five SNG spots where Phase 7 evaluates and the pack's proposal differs: the action equals the pack-off reference and Phase 7's `selectedAction`, and the receipt is `phase7_evaluated`, `shadow_change`.
- **Boundary.** `horsePhase11SelectionIsValid` refuses an applied receipt whose `utilityOwner` is not `cash`. "even the offline candidate control cannot carry a %s %s tournament selection across the worker boundary" checks it on the receipt HorseLogic actually produces offline.

## Witness and journal

`phase11Authority` on the execution witness has the Phase 8 binding shape: `continuationVersion` (the deciding pack's version, which names the variant), `mode`, `selection`, the worker `authority` receipt, the main scheduler's `verdict`, `candidate` (the pack's proposal) and `reference` (the shadow baseline). The accepted action is the witness's own `acceptedActions` and `executedAction`. It is present for every P11.3 Phase 11 receipt, shadow ones included, and absent on retained receipts (no `selection`) and on every other variant, so no existing witness changes shape. The reviewer rebuilds the witness from the journaled decision and refuses a different proposal, baseline, pack version or worker authority, a selection that moved other than `selected` to `controller_accepted` or `withdrawn_before_acceptance`, a controller acceptance without a usable verdict and an intended execution, a withdrawal without an unusable verdict, a dropped binding and a binding moved to the Phase 10 slot.

## Defects and findings

1. **Fixed (latent, offline-only until authority exists): a legalizer-rewritten Phase 11 candidate was still reported as applied.** The P10.3 finding 1, present in the Phase 11 block: in candidate mode `HorseLogic` legalized the pack's decision and kept `applied: true` even when the legalizer changed the action or amount, so the receipt named a proposal the table would not execute. Now such a candidate is refused (`selectionRefusal: 'illegal_candidate'`) and the reference retained. No live decision changed: candidate mode was never live.
2. **Fixed (latent): an applied Phase 11 receipt had no rule at the worker boundary.** `horseDecisionReceiptIsValid` checked only the P11.1 input binding, so an applied PLO5/PLO6/PLO8 receipt with no authority would have crossed it. Unreachable live (the envelope refuses caller candidate control); now refused by `horsePhase11SelectionIsValid`.
3. **Observation, unchanged (the P10.3 observation 4, here with the opposite outcome).** In an offline tournament candidate run Phase 7 evaluates after the pack, and on the spots tested it chooses the pack's own proposal, so the receipt reads `selected` with `utilityOwner: 'phase7_evaluated'`. Phase 7 owned the decision; the label reflects that the executed action equals the proposal. Such a receipt can never cross the worker boundary (it is not a cash decision) and the live worker never grants tournament candidate mode.
4. **Observation (Phase 10, no effect).** `horsePhase10AdmittedMode` does not look at the variant, so under usable PLO4 authority every cash request (NLH included) is sent `phase10Plo4: 'candidate'`. Harmless, because `HorseLogic` reads that option only when the registry routes the variant to Phase 10. `horsePhase11AdmittedMode` checks the variant and the holder's pack explicitly. Reported, not changed.
5. **Stricter than the contract's wording, by choice.** The contract names only the `work_budget` fallback; the completion record also counts a consumed live sample cut short by the sampler's own budget as not completed, for the reason given above.

## Limits stated plainly

- A selecting release must ship `docs/evidence/phase11/` (the qualification file, the strength record it names and the completion record) in the engine image. The image is built from `server/`; without the files, admission is `missing_evidence` or `completion_evidence_missing` and play stays shadow.
- The completion record is produced from a shadow (or earlier) release whose policy digest equals the running one. The digest covers the 17 policy files P11.2 named, not this authority module (it holds the selection, which must change to select) and not `ServerTableEngineTurns.ts` or the worker; a release that changes only those keeps the digest, which is intended: they do not change what the pack proposes.
- Phase 11 has no live safety sentinel of its own. Its withdrawal sources are the committed `withdrawn` record, a controller refusal or coercion, a worker exit and refresh failure. As in Phases 8 and 10, a local generation proves what this process admitted; no instantaneous global withdrawal is claimed.
