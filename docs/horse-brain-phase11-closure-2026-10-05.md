## Statement

Phase 11 is complete and closed as **not promoted**. The PLO5, PLO6 and PLO8 packs' inputs are bound and naturally verified, their strength was measured once each under a contract locked by merge before measurement, and the result does not support promotion: each candidate loses to the reference on the primary cash cell at 99% (PLO5 −3.81, PLO6 −4.58, PLO8 −9.34 bb/100), on every seed block, and on named gating cells. The authority path exists, is bound to the running code, and selects nothing.

What Phase 11 leaves behind that serves today: the variant sampler and the Omaha card facts every Omaha policy (PLO4 through PLO8 and FLO8) calls on every decision do the same work in a fraction of the engine time, proven output-identical, which matters on a single-core engine running these policies in shadow on every Omaha decision.

What a next round needs before it can promote anything: a new policy design that beats the reference on the primary cell (the round 1 packs do not), a new contract with fresh held-out seeds (these seeds have been spent and must not be reused for tuning), and a predeclared completion window on the release that carries both the card-facts change and that design.
