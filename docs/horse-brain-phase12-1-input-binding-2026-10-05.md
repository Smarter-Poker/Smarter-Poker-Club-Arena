## What the proposal now records, and where

**Receipt: `decision.remainingVariantPolicy.inputs`** (`remaining-variant-input-binding-v1`). Built inside `evaluateRemainingVariantPolicy` from the values the proposal consumed, assembled only after every canonical check passes (a refused proposal records `null` and its named reason), frozen and copied so no array or object of the request, the sampler or the caller is aliased. It is assembled inside `finish`, before the elapsed time is read, so the existing 4 ms work check governs it. **It contains no card value of any kind**: board and hole cards are counted, never listed.

| Field group     | Contents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `mode`          | `cash` or `tournament` (Pineapple tournament is refused by name before any binding)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `pack`          | `version`, `holes` (2/3/2/4), `deck` (36 for Short Deck, else 52), `splitPot`, `bettingStructure` (`no_limit` or `fixed_limit`), `source: explicit_variant_heuristic`, `calibratedConfidence: null`, `seats` [2, the ceiling for the mode], `seatCapOwner`, `maxStackBB` (250 no limit, 1,000 fixed limit), `maxAnteBB` 1, `maxRakePercent` 10                                                             |
| `approximation` | `status` (`explicit_variant_heuristic`, or `..._with_uncalibrated_range_sample` when a sample was consumed), `solverInput: false`, `handShape` (`score`, `cardsScored`, `kind: heuristic_entry_score`, `probability: false`)                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `privateCards`  | `heroHoleCards` (Pineapple: 3 before the discard, 2 after), `heroKnownDeadCards` (Pineapple after the discard: 1, else 0), `postDiscard`, `cardValues: not_recorded`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

**Range provenance** (`remaining-variant-range-provenance-v1`), produced by the live sampler with the sample it describes: `source: variant_public_line_sequential_prior`, `calibration: uncalibrated`, `solverInput: false`, `reads: public_action_line_only`; the prior (`attemptsPerSeat: 3`, `finalAttempt: uniform_escape`, seat draws inside completed samples and how many the uniform escape accepted); the deck (`single_deck_excluding_hero_known_and_board`, its `size` 36 or 52, the dealt opponents that occupy it, folded seats included, and `heroKnownDeadCards`, the count of the hero's private accepted discard excluded from it); `discard` for Pineapple only (`opponents: declared_flop_only_structural_prior`, `hero: accepted_private_discard` after the discard, `actualOpponentDiscardsRead: false`), null for the other three; the work (requested, completed, budget exhausted); and the contesting opponents scored with the public counts read. A draw in an iteration cut by the budget is not counted, so `seatDraws` always equals completed samples times dealt opponents.

**Population check.** A sample whose provenance names a population other than this decision's contesting opponents (seat and user), whose deck is not this variant's deck occupied by every other dealt seat, whose known-discard count is not the hero's, whose discard prior is missing on Pineapple or present elsewhere, or whose work is not the sample's own is refused like a malformed one: `invalid_equity_evidence`, range status `rejected_population`, no equity consumed. A Phase 11 provenance offered to this policy is refused the same way. The live caller builds both from the same seats, so no live proposal changes.

**Witness: `phase12Inputs`** (`horse-phase12-input-binding-v1`, `variant`, `inputSha256` by the shared canonical commitment, `rangeStatus`), frozen; present only for a decision whose Phase 12 receipt carries the field (`null` for a refused proposal), absent on retained witnesses and every other variant.

**Journal.** The reviewer recomputes the witness from the journaled decision and refuses a different or missing `phase12Inputs` (`input_mismatch`).

**Worker boundary.** `horseDecisionReceiptIsValid` now checks a Phase 12 receipt through `remainingVariantReceiptBindingIsValid` (before P12.1 nothing at the boundary looked inside a Phase 12 receipt beyond its ownership record). The P10 audit F8 rule, as Phase 11 applies it: in `client.ts` a shadow-only receipt (shadow, not applied) that fails the strict validator is dropped with its ownership record and counted as `phase12_shadow_receipt_binding_dropped`; the decision keeps its action and the worker stays up. An applied receipt still fails closed.

**Telemetry.** `phase12_range_<status>` counts only. Registered in `HorseDataLedger` as `phase12_range_*` (ratio of `phase12_eligible`, minimum 0.99) with `phase12_shadow_receipt_binding_dropped`.

## Defects found and fixed

Each was reproduced on unmodified main `1157cf08` with the probe archived beside this record ([`p12-1-defects-probe.test.ts.txt`](evidence/phase12/p12-1-defects-probe.test.ts.txt), [`p12-1-defects-main.log`](evidence/phase12/p12-1-defects-main.log), [`p12-1-defects-branch.log`](evidence/phase12/p12-1-defects-branch.log)) and the legal-form harness ([`p12-1-legal-form-main.log`](evidence/phase12/p12-1-legal-form-main.log), [`p12-1-legal-form-branch.log`](evidence/phase12/p12-1-legal-form-branch.log)): real `HandController` hands, the main policy file swapped in, then this branch's.

1. **Tournament dead button refused as unavailable state** (the P10.1 defect 3 / P11.1 defect 1 class). The policy required the dealer to be a dealt seat. On main all six preflop decisions of the production dead-button shape (dealt 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8; dealer 5 empty) refused as `canonical_state_unavailable` for Short Deck, FLH and FLO8. Fixed with `plo4DealerSeatIsValid`; an empty dealer on a cash table is still refused.
2. **Occupied button with a dead small blind mislabelled** (P11.1 defect 2). The dealer-offset `plo4Position` called the lone big-blind poster `small_blind` and the first to act `big_blind`. On main, for the engine hand (button 2, big blind 4 alone, order 5, 1, 2, 4) every pack labelled seat 5 `big_blind` and seat 4 `small_blind`, and Short Deck's seat 5 made a `preflop_entry` wager on that label. Fixed: positions from the posted blinds (`plo4CanonicalPosition`), refusing `blind_seats_unavailable` when the state carries none, `canonical_state_unavailable` when the engine could not have posted them. With an occupied button and a live small blind the canonical positions equal the old labels (all 28,150 seat, button and dealt-subset combinations of a ten-seat table are checked).
3. **A valid census above the pack ceiling named unavailable** (P11.1 defect 3). On main a cash table one seat past the ceiling (Short Deck, Pineapple, FLH at 10; FLO8 at 9) refused as `canonical_state_unavailable`. Now `seat_count_outside_pack`, ownership `outside_domain`.
4. **The receipt echoed the caller's equity object** (P11.1 defect 4). `receipt.equity` was initialised to the evidence argument by reference; on main a preflop receipt given evidence returned the caller's object in all four packs. Now a frozen copy of the validated, consumed sample, or null.
5. **Proposals were not in the legalizer's form** (the Phase 11 audit finding, present here too). The policy floored cash wagers to cents (`unit = tournament ? 1 : 0.01`) while `HorseLogic.legalize` snaps to whole chips whenever the big blind is whole (`chipStep`); it proposed raw calls where the legalizer makes a call that covers the stack the menu's `all_in`, and a bet at 92% of the stack or a raise at 95% an all-in; and it carried float noise in call amounts. Over natural controller spots the real legalizer rewrote 20 to 92 proposals per variant and mode on main (examples: `raise 28.75` became `raise 28` at a 5/10 table; `call 53` with a 53 stack became `all_in`; `call 0.19999999999999998` became `call 0.2`), and 0 on this branch. Fixed at the owner: `remainingVariantLegalForm` mirrors the legalizer for the two structures this pack proposes in (the structural pass, the engine's own `validateAction` check, and the authoritative-menu pass), and every proposal passes through it. Fixed-limit wagers equal the controller's canonical bet, raise or completion amount. Phase 12 has no `illegal_candidate` guard in `HorseLogic` (that guard is Phase 10/11 authority work); with the legal form, a future one has nothing to refuse.

**Behaviour change, shadow only.** Defects 2 and 5 change what some shadow receipts propose (labels in dead-small-blind hands; whole-chip amounts, all-ins and cent-exact calls). Every pack stays shadow, so no live action changes. Defect 1 and 3 change refusals into computed or `outside_domain` receipts; again shadow only.

## P12-A remainder (tests only, owners unchanged)

- **Chosen versus forced at the join** (`PineappleDiscardJoinContrast.test.ts`, real HandController and the real `ServerTableEngine` discard handler, only the worker's answer supplied). Chosen: the engine asks lane `choice`, the controller records the discard on the `pineapple_discard` round under the public node `private_discard_choice`, the private dead card is exactly the answered card, the hand reaches the flop betting decision, and the Phase 12 binding counts that card (`heroKnownDeadCards: 1`) without listing it. Forced: an all-in hand never opens the round; the engine asks lane `forced_runout` before the flop, nothing is private or observed until the controller commits, the commit records the discard outside the round under `forced_discard`, the private dead card is the prepared card, and no seat can act afterwards. In `PineappleAcceptedDiscardChain.test.ts` the hero's real accepted discard restaged outside the round is refused by the real worker as `lack authoritative discard proof`, so a forced-form record can never authorize a betting decision.
- **Stale, crossed and duplicate answers** (the same file). A worker answer with another fence or generation, an impossible index (3, -1, 1.5), a late answer after the hand moved on, after the round was re-armed, or for a seat already folded for its missed discard never reaches the private record: the seat still owes its discard with its three cards, no dead card, no discard record, no execution observation. A second answer after the seat's discard was accepted is refused and the first card stays the private record; a direct second submission is refused by the controller. Mutation-checked: removing the fence/generation and hand-number checks in `ServerTableEngineRunout` fails 3 of 11 cases (restored, `git diff` clean). At the client boundary (`client.test.ts`): a duplicate `DISCARD_RESULT` after the first was delivered fails the client and never re-delivers; a duplicate landing on the next queued discard breaks FIFO by name; another hand's fence is refused as a mismatched lifecycle fence.
- **Still not covered here:** natural accepted / forced / missing-capture receipts from a production window (P12-A asks for them; they need publication and an observation window).
