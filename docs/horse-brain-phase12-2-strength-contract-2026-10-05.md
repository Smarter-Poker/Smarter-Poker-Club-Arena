## Objectives

**Cash after rake (measured).** The metric is the paired difference of the hero seat's net chips per hand: final stack minus starting stack, after the engine's rake and BBJ drop, candidate minus reference, reported in bb/100. The candidate is `HorseLogic.decide` with `phase12Remaining: 'candidate'` (RemainingVariantLivePolicy over the pack, behind the selection guard). The reference is the same call with `phase12Remaining: 'off'`: the action live tables execute today, because Phase 12 runs in shadow, under the harness conditions named below. Both arms of a pair use the same deal seed: identical cards, hero seat, button, seat styles, Pineapple discards and decision clock. BBJ jackpot awards are excluded; the database pays them from the external pool after the hand.

**Tournament prize and bounty: unavailable external input (contract status `unavailable dependency`), refused by name for every pack.** The verdict returns these in `tournament.reasons`, and the qualification file in `objectives.tournament.reasons`. They are never in the top-level `reasons`, which hold cash failures only and alone decide `qualified` (test).

- Short Deck, FLH, FLO8: `tournament:<variant>_whole_tournament_outcome_model_unavailable` (the only coherent tournament outcome model in source, the Phase 8 paired whole-tournament league, deals NLH only), `tournament:qualified_<variant>_tournament_reference_population_unavailable`, `tournament:<variant>_tournament_thresholds_not_specified`. Spin never offers these variants.
- Pineapple: `tournament:pineapple_tournament_format_unavailable`. There is no Pineapple tournament: the catalog excludes it, the tournament allowlist (`20260912044409_remaining_tournament_variant_allowlist.sql`) refuses it, `remainingVariantSeatCap('pineapple', 'tournament')` is 0, and the live pack refuses a Pineapple tournament decision.

## The Selection Guard And What The Candidate Arm Played

Phase 12 had no P10.3 guard: `HorseLogic` legalized the candidate's proposal and played whatever the legalizer returned, so a proposal the legalizer rewrote was still executed under the pack's name and counted as `changed`. This package adds the guard to the Phase 12 block of `variant_policy`, exactly as P11.3 added it for Phase 11: an applied candidate whose legalized form differs from the proposal is refused (`applied: false`, `selectionRefusal: 'illegal_candidate'`) and the reference is retained. Candidate mode is offline only (the worker refuses it), so no live decision moves: off and shadow outputs of the Phase 12 development league are identical to the base commit, and the candidate arm differs only where the guard fired (`p12-2-unchanged.log`). Every shard counts those refusals as `illegalCandidates`, beside and never inside `changed`; the strength record (`selectionGuard`) and the verdict total them. They are reported, never gated, and `illegal candidate count` is listed in `notStrengthGates`.

**Finding from the design pilot (development seed only, no outcome read): the two no-limit packs propose wager sizes the legalizer rewrites.** Short Deck: 133 refused against 698 applied candidate decisions; Pineapple: 96 against 579; FLH and FLO8: 0. Root cause, read from the decisions (`RemainingVariantLivePolicy.ts`, `wager`): the pack sizes cash wagers to the cent (`unit = 0.01`), while the legalizer snaps cash bets and raises to whole dollars whenever the big blind is a whole number (`HorseLogic.ts`, `chipStep` and `snapBetSize`) and turns a raise of at least 95% of the stack into an all-in. The common case is the preflop open to 2.875 BB (5.75 at 1/2), legalized to 5. Under the guard those decisions play the reference, so about one in seven of the no-limit candidate's changes never reaches the table in this matrix. This contract measures the pack as it is and does not change it. The fix (size no-limit wagers on the legalizer's chip step and all-in rule) is a policy change outside P12.2; if it merges before a pack's matrix is dispatched, that run measures the fixed code under this same contract, because the contract binds the pack version and the qualification binds the policy digest of the code actually run.

## The Locked Matrices

Each pack: its profiles × three held-out seeds × the profile's shards. Shard k of a (profile, seed) cell plays pair indices `[k × pairsPerShard, (k + 1) × pairsPerShard)`. `pairsPerShard` is a whole number of rotation blocks: 1,296 (the least common multiple of 4, 16, 36 and 81) for the nine-max packs and 576 (of 4, 16, 36 and 64) for FLO8, so every shard visits each (hero seat, button) pair the same number of times. The workflow's plan job reads the pack's matrix from the contract at the evaluated commit.

Columns: dealt / table seats, stack, depth band, shards per held-out seed, pairs per profile (all seeds), design-pilot paired-difference SD (BB per hand), pilot ms per pair, pilot kurtosis, estimated minutes per shard on a hosted runner (2.5 × pilot), estimated runner minutes over the three seeds.

## Statistic And Uncertainty

Unchanged from Phases 10 and 11 and implemented here by the contract's own functions: exact integer chip cents per pair; exact power sums `n, S1..S4` per dealer-relative offset × divergence street per shard, merged exactly as BigInt; divergence street as the street of the first action at which the arms' traces differ (a Pineapple discard belongs to the flop; identical traces with a nonzero difference are a paired replay mismatch); the stratified mean with equal weight per profile in a cell; a two-sided 99% normal (Wald) interval `T ± z·sqrt(Σ w_p² s_p² / n_p)` with `z = Φ⁻¹(0.995) = 2.5758293035489004`; trusted only if the first Edgeworth term `|κ|(2z² + 1)φ(z)/6` is at most 0.001 and every profile in the cell has at least 10,000 pairs. A cell failing either guard is `interval_not_trusted` and refused.

## Thresholds And Their Justification

| Gate                                                            | Threshold                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Justification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary, cash after rake                                        | 99% lower bound **> 0 bb/100**, the pack's profiles pooled with equal weight                                                                                                                                                              | Zero is break-even after the house's take; the lower end of a two-sided 99% interval bounds a false promotion at 0.5%. No effect-size floor, because no calibrated cost of switching exists in source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Seed replication                                                | Each held-out seed block's pooled point estimate **> 0 bb/100**                                                                                                                                                                           | Three independent blocks fixed in advance; a zero true effect passes all three with probability 1/8 on top of the primary bound                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Critical-domain nonregression, no limit (Short Deck, Pineapple) | Every gating cell's 99% lower bound **≥ −10 bb/100**                                                                                                                                                                                      | One 100 BB buy-in per 1,000 hands, about a whole winning edge in no-limit cash (the Phase 10 and 11 number)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Critical-domain nonregression, fixed limit (FLH, FLO8)          | Every gating cell's 99% lower bound **≥ −4 bb/100**                                                                                                                                                                                       | New, and stated before any run: two big bets per 100 hands (the big bet is 2 BB), about a whole fixed-limit winning edge, where strong edges are one to two big bets per 100. The no-limit −10 would be two and a half such edges and could not catch a domain that loses its whole edge (test: a fixed-limit cell losing about 3.5 bb/100 passes −10 and fails −4). Planned half-widths keep Phase 10's ratio, 0.85 of the margin (3.4) |
| Interval validity                                               | Edgeworth coverage error ≤ 0.001; ≥ 10,000 pairs per profile in each cell                                                                                                                                                                 | Phase 10's guard                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Software validity, per shard                                    | Illegal actions, conservation, card errors, truncated hands, settlement, deduction and paired replay mismatches all 0; `illegalCandidates` and `discards` recorded (a shard without them, or with discards outside Pineapple, is refused) | Any nonzero validity count means the measurement is not of the policy                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

**Gating cells: 15 per no-limit pack, 17 per fixed-limit pack, all effective.** Every profile, the six positions (`positionForOffset`; heads-up offset 0 is the button; at eight and nine seats the extra offsets are middle) and every depth band the pack has. Each holds only its own domain's pairs, so its 99% lower bound sits below the true domain mean with probability 99.5% (tests: a losing big blind, a losing legacy-depth profile and its band each fail by name while the pool wins).

**Street families: reported, never gated,** for Phase 11's reason (a contribution cell is diluted by every pair that did not diverge on its street; a conditional cell's size and skewness are not fixed before the run). A test builds a matrix whose flop contribution's interval lies wholly below −10 bb/100 while every gating cell passes: the pack qualifies and the diagnostic is reported with `gate: false`.

## Independent Settlement For Each Variant

`RemainingVariantStrengthChecks.ts` replaces the Omaha reading for a Phase 12 profile (which has no Short Deck, Pineapple or FLH rules and would mark every such showdown a mismatch). It settles each showdown with `settleRemainingReference` (Phase 12's independent reference: its own scorer with the A-6-7-8-9 wheel and flush over full house for Short Deck, contribution layers, refunds and button-relative odd chips; FLO8 through the exact-two/exact-three Omaha reference, which settles high and low halves), checks every Pineapple discard the controller retained, and the third card of a seat that folded before the discard, as physical dead cards, and checks rake and BBJ against `effectiveRake` and `effectiveBbjDrop` (Short Deck drops nothing). Tests: a Short Deck flush over a full house paid to the full house is caught; a BBJ drop on a Short Deck hand is a deduction mismatch; an FLO8 scoop to the high hand alone is caught; a Pineapple discard colliding with a live card is caught; the Omaha reading of a Pineapple hand cannot certify it.
