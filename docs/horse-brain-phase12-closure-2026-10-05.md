## What the audits found and fixed

Before Phase 12 was built, a line-by-line audit of Phase 11 found that the Phase 10 and Phase 11 candidates proposed wagers and calls the legalizer rewrites (cent sizes where the legalizer uses whole chips, unrounded stack-sized calls), so the selection guard refused them as `illegal_candidate` and played the reference: the October 5 Phase 11 matrices measured a mixed arm (PLO5 8%, PLO6 14%, PLO8 6% of changed proposals on a development shard). Phase 10 and 11 policies now take the owner's legalizer and record and execute the exact legal form (mutation-checked test over real league hands). The same audit added `governorReduced` to the Phase 11 completion definition (v3), `BettingStructure.ts` to the Phase 11 digest (v3), and required a receipt carrying a selection to carry its input binding. The Phase 12 build carried the legal-form rule from the start (P12.1, checked over 8,560 real controller spots; every Phase 12 matrix below counted zero `illegal_candidate` refusals). A read-only audit of the merged Phase 12 then found that both completion readers counted a DEEP second look as a second decision; fixed in #6169 before any window was read.
