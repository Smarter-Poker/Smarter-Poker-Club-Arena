# Horse Brain Phase 13, P13.1: input binding, legal form, positions and receipt completeness, October 6, 2026

Horse Brain only. This record covers package P13.1 of Phase 13 (the joint multiway owner, `server/src/engine/multiway/*`) in the [maintained completion plan](horse-brain-phases6-15-completion-plan-2026-09-17.md): record what each joint proposal actually consumed in the existing receipt, witness and journal; put every joint candidate in the form the horse legalizer executes before it is priced; read the preflop action order from the blinds the engine posted; and complete the receipt. It mirrors P12.1 ([record](horse-brain-phase12-1-input-binding-2026-10-05.md)) and the Phase 10/11 legal-form fix. Status vocabulary: verified now, implemented but unverified, defective, unavailable external input, not applicable with reason, historical only. Nothing is averaged into a percentage.

The packs stay explicit heuristics in shadow mode. The range pack stays `joint-public-range-round1-v1`. The response pack identity is read from `JOINT_ACTION_PACK.version` (the parallel package P13-A changes it to `joint-action-response-round2-v1`; this package imports the constant and never hard-codes it). The joint domain moves to `joint-multiway-round1-v4` because the receipt shape changes. No strength, calibration, solver or promotion claim is made, no authority path is added, every protected release selection stays null, and the live worker still refuses caller `phase13Joint: 'candidate'`.

## Defects reproduced on unmodified source, and fixed

1. **Candidates were priced and ranked in a form the table would not execute** (the Phase 10/11 audit class). `evaluateJointActions` sized wagers on the settlement unit (cents for cash chips) and ranked them at those amounts; `HorseLogic.legalize` snaps to whole chips whenever the big blind is whole, and the joint node legalized the proposal only after the ranking. Over natural spots from a real HandController (`JointLegalForm.test.ts`, 14 hands per case, three to seven seats), on `22efa996` the legalizer rewrote these priced candidates and the ranked winner differed from the recorded, executed action in these cash decisions (examples: `raise 4.5` priced, `raise 4` executed; `raise 4.3100000000000005` priced, `raise 4`; `raise 8.040000000000001` priced, `raise 8.04`):

   | Variant and mode                         | Priced candidates rewritten                               | Ranked winner not executed (of fired) |
   | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
   | NLH cash                                 | 180 of 835                                                | 23 of 159                             |
   | PLO4 cash                                | 393 of 1,076                                              | 77 of 217                             |
   | PLO5 cash                                | 393 of 1,078                                              | 72 of 217                             |
   | PLO6 cash                                | 537 of 1,248                                              | 75 of 247                             |
   | PLO8 cash                                | 393 of 1,080                                              | 77 of 217                             |
   | Pineapple cash                           | 179 of 731                                                | 13 of 136                             |
   | Short Deck cash                          | 192 of 655                                                | 25 of 134                             |
   | PLO4/5/6/8 tournament                    | 1 each (`raise 80` priced, pot-limit `raise 78` executed) | 0 (Phase 7 owns tournament ranking)   |
   | FLH, FLO8, NLH and Short Deck tournament | 0                                                         | 0                                     |

   In candidate mode the rewritten proposal was applied silently: the joint node had no `illegal_candidate` guard. **Fixed at the owner:** `evaluateJointLivePolicy` takes the owner's legalizer (`legalForm`, passed by HorseLogic as `(d) => this.legalize(d, player, gs, vi)`), and `jointLegalCandidates` rebuilds every candidate from its legal decision before any is priced (a cent wager becomes the whole-chip wager, a near-stack wager or a covering call the all-in, duplicates after legalization collapse to one). The proposal is the exact legal decision priced, a call included with its amount. On this branch every row above is 0, in all 17 variant and mode cases. The node now carries the P10.3 law: an applied candidate the legalizer would rewrite is refused as `illegal_candidate`, and a Phase 13 candidate is never applied on top of an applied Phase 10/11/12 candidate (`earlier_phase_applied`); both keep the reference. Mutation-checked: deleting either guard line fails its test (`JointSelectionGuard.test.ts`: "expected 0 to be greater than 0"; the earlier-phase cases in `JointLegalForm.test.ts`: "expected true to be false"), restored, `git diff` clean.

2. **Preflop first actor from a dealer offset** (the P12.1 defect 2 class). `jointPlayersBehind` took the preflop first actor as the third dealt seat clockwise of the button (the fourth with the straddle flag). With the tournament dead small blind (TDA Rule 30) the engine posts only the big blind, the first dealt seat after the button is the big blind, and the offset lands one seat late. On `22efa996`, in a real engine hand (dealt 1, 2, 4, 5; button 2; big blind 4 alone; engine order 5, 1, 2, 4) the first actor's players-behind set was `[p1, p2]` where the engine asks `[p1, p2, p4]`, for NLH, PLO4 and FLH alike: the big blind, who still owes the option, was treated as closed by the response model and by the Phase 7 bridge (`actsAfterHero`). **Fixed:** `jointPreflopFirstSeat` (`JointResponseOrder.ts`) reads the controller's own rule: heads-up the button; otherwise the dealt seat after the posted big blind, one more with the single UTG straddle. A cash table the engine was not told blinds for has them walked from the button exactly as HandController walks them; a tournament state without blind seats is refused (`joint_action_blind_seats_unavailable`), and blind seats the engine could not have posted are refused (`joint_action_blind_seats_invalid`). The dead button on an empty seat, ordinary cash hands, the straddle and heads-up already matched and are now pinned against real engine hands (`JointResponseOrder.test.ts`).

3. **No input binding.** The joint receipt recorded none of the facts it consumed. **Added** `joint-input-binding-v1` (below), wired exactly like Phase 12.

4. **Incomplete receipt.** The receipt did not name the range or response pack it ran, nor the sampler's uniform escapes. **Added** `rangePackVersion`, `actionPackVersion` (from the imported constants) and `uniformEscapes`, plus `selectionRefusal` for the guard. `selection` was not added: Phase 13 has no authority, so there is nothing for it to say.

## What the proposal now records, and where

**Receipt: `decision.jointPolicy.inputs`** (`joint-input-binding-v1`). Built by `buildJointInputBinding` from the values the proposal consumed, assembled lazily inside `finish` (so the 4 ms work check governs it) only once the acquisition's canonical checks have passed and the controller order has been read; a refused proposal records `null` and its named reason. Copied and frozen: no array or object of the request, the sampler or the caller is aliased. **It contains no card value of any kind.**

| Field group       | Contents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `variant`, `mode` | One of the nine joint variants; `cash` or `tournament`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `packs`           | `domain` (`joint-multiway-round1-v4`), `range` (`joint-public-range-round1-v1`), `action` (`JOINT_ACTION_PACK.version`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `approximation`   | `status: explicit_joint_heuristic`, the range source and confidence, `calibratedConfidence: null`, `solverInput: false`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `census`          | Dealer and hero seat, dealt seats and count, live opponents and their seats, folded, away and all-in seats, folded and away counts, board count, bomb flag, physical cards and unknown dealt cards per sample (null when no sample completed)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `positions`       | The posted blind seats (preflop, non-bomb; null otherwise), the street's action order of dealt seats and its first seat, the hero's button offset, the seats still owing a response after the hero, the straddle flag                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `geometry`        | The structure, `settlementUnit` (cents for cash chips, whole for tournaments and diamonds), `legalChipStep` (the legalizer's: whole when the big blind is whole), `legalForm` (`horse_legalizer` or `not_supplied`), big blind, pot, current bet, hero bet and stack, call cost, `minRaiseTo`/`maxRaiseTo`, stack raise-to, exactly one of `noLimit.wagerCap`, `potLimit.wagerCap` or `fixedLimit` (`fixedBetSize`, `wagersCapped`), the rake schedule with the dealt count and timed flag, and whether a bad-beat fee applies |
| `depth`           | Effective, hero and deepest-contesting-opponent cover in BB, the domain ceiling (250, fixed limit 1,000), the basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `boards`          | Street, board count, cards per board, `cardValues: not_recorded`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `ranges`          | `status` (`consumed`, `unavailable`), `acquisition` (`fresh`, or `reused_phase7` when the tournament owner reused the identical Phase 7 population), and the provenance: requested, completed, budget exhausted, uniform escapes, and per dealt opponent its seat, folded/live/all-in flags, public raise, call and check counts and prior                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `objective`       | `cash_net_chips` or `tournament_phase7_utility`, and the asset                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

**Validators.** `jointInputBindingIsValid` checks exact keys, the fixed identities, census coherence, that the action order is a rotation of the dealt ring starting at its first seat, that the posted blinds are ones the engine could have posted (`plo4BlindSeatsStatus`), the structure's single geometry block and its cap, depth arithmetic and ceiling, board counts, and the range provenance (a consumed population has at least the minimum samples and a row for every other dealt seat). `jointReceiptBindingIsValid` binds it to the receipt: binding present exactly when eligible, same variant, domain, street, board count, mode, sample counts and escapes, current pack versions, and a selection refusal only by name and only on an unapplied receipt. A receipt without the fields (retained before P13.1) claims nothing and is not refused.

**Witness: `phase13Inputs`** (`horse-phase13-input-binding-v1`, `variant`, `inputSha256` by the shared canonical commitment, `rangeStatus`), frozen; present only for a decision whose joint receipt carries the field (`null` for a refused proposal), absent on retained witnesses.

**Reviewer.** `reconcileHorseJournalHand` recomputes the witness from the journaled decision record and refuses a changed or dropped `phase13Inputs` as `input_mismatch` (mutation-checked: removing the comparison fails both refusal tests).

**Worker boundary.** `horseDecisionReceiptIsValid` re-checks `jointReceiptBindingIsValid`. The client drops a shadow-only joint receipt whose binding fails, counted `phase13_shadow_receipt_binding_dropped`, keeping the action and the worker; an applied one fails closed.

**Ledger.** `phase13_range_*` (range status of every bound proposal), `phase13_selection_refused_*` and `phase13_shadow_receipt_binding_dropped` are registered.

## The execution join (gap 5)

`ServerTableEngineTurns.ts` sets `jointPolicy.executionStatus` after the worker has journaled the decision, so the journaled receipt reads `pending`. No change is needed for the join: the execution witness is created from the same decision (`createHorseExecutionWitness`), carries `phase13Inputs` and the accepted actions, and the reviewer recomputes `phase13Inputs` from the persisted decision record. The natural reader therefore joins a Phase 13 receipt to its accepted execution by that commitment, exactly as it joins Phase 10, 11 and 12. `review.test.ts` proves it for NLH, PLO4, FLO8 and Short Deck: the persisted receipt reads `pending`, the recovered commitment equals the witness's, the accepted action equals the receipt's final action, and the hand reconciles.

## Integration with P13-A (October 6)

Merged with the P13-A response tree on `agent/claude-horse-brain/phase13-20261006`. The one conflict, in `JointActionModel.ts`, was resolved by taking the P13-A file and moving both P13.1 changes to the files that now own them:

- **Legal form for both response models.** `prepareJointActionInput` (`JointActionShared.ts`, shared by both models) takes the optional `candidateForm` and applies it to the built candidates before either model prices them. `evaluateJointActions(..., withinBudget, { responseModel?, candidateForm? })` passes it to the round-1 model and to `evaluateJointResponseTree` (one extra optional parameter). `JointLivePolicy` calls it through the options object. Re-run under the round-2 default: zero rewritten candidates, zero ranked-but-not-executed winners and zero invalid bindings in all 17 variant and mode cases, with the bounded tree pricing turn and river decisions in every case (from 6 to 192 tree-priced decisions per case).
- **Positions.** `jointPlayersBehind` moved to `JointActionShared.ts` and takes its preflop first seat from `jointPreflopFirstSeat` there. The P13-A response tree runs on the turn and river only; its responders go clockwise from the hero and its river round opens from the first dealt seat after the button, both postflop orders that do not depend on the posted blinds. No change was needed there.

**Response fields on the receipt** (first class, absent on retained receipts): `responseVersion` (the action model's version, null when no model ran), `responseModel` (`one_response_then_showdown` or `bounded_raise_tree`), `responseLimits` (a copy of `JOINT_ACTION_PACK.limits`) and `responseTree`, the top-ranked candidate's summary (`terminalBranches`, `raiseBranches`, `raiseProbability`, `heroCallsRaiseProbability`, `heroFoldsToRaiseProbability`, `raiseLimitedResponders`, `riverRoundProbability`, `riverBetProbability`), null for the one-response model. The validator checks them against the pack and the embedded model, field by field (a journaled receipt is canonicalized with sorted keys).

**Binding.** The response limits are a consumed input, so the binding gains `response` (`model`, chosen by street; `raisesPerTree`, `maxRaiseBranchOpponents`, `maxTerminalBranchesPerCandidate`, `riverRounds`), validated against the pack.

**Reasons and ledger.** The named response refusals (`joint_response_branch_unavailable`, `joint_response_street_unavailable`, `joint_response_street_not_modeled`, `joint_response_illegal_simulated_action`, `joint_response_branch_mass`) pass the worker boundary with their binding and no response model. The new `responseCounts` fields (`raiseProbability`, `meanRaiseShare`, `facedRaise`, `calledRaise`) pass unchanged. A telemetry family `phase13_response_*` (`phase13_response_model_<model>`, `phase13_response_refused_<reason>`) is registered.
