# Horse Brain Phase 13, P13.2: the locked strength contract for the joint multiway owner, October 6, 2026

Horse Brain only. This record covers package P13.2 of the [maintained completion plan](horse-brain-phases6-15-completion-plan-2026-09-17.md): the immutable statement of what a strength run of the joint multiway owner (`server/src/engine/multiway/*`) must measure for each of the nine variants of `KNOWN_VARIANTS`, the machinery that runs one shard, assembles a variant and writes its qualification file, and the sized matrices. It mirrors the Phase 12 strength contract ([P12.2 record](horse-brain-phase12-2-strength-contract-2026-10-05.md)) one for one. It uses the maintained gate vocabulary (verified now / implemented but unverified / defective / unavailable external input / not applicable with reason). Nothing is averaged into a percentage.

Status: **CONTRACT NOT YET LOCKED: it locks when this merges; no held-out hand is dealt before.** Every number below (seeds, thresholds, margins, pairs, shard counts, interval) is fixed in the contract object now, and a variant's matrix may be dispatched only against a commit on main that contains this contract. `promotionEligible` is `false` everywhere and `selectionGuard.gate` is `false`. Everything stays shadow and offline: no protected release selection changed, and the live worker still refuses a caller's `phase13Joint: 'candidate'`.

## Domains and the two arms

One domain per variant, nine separate qualifications: `<variant>-cash-joint-multiway-after-rake-horse-population` (`jointStrengthDomain`). The verdict `summarizeJointStrength(variant, shards)` reads one variant's shards only; a shard of another variant is refused by name (`variant_mismatch`, `unknown_profile`, `not_holdout_seed`, `unexpected_shard`) and never enters a cell. A complete, valid Pineapple matrix handed to the Short Deck verdict gives `shardsUsed: 0` and `cash:no_shard_results` (test).

**Candidate.** `HorseLogic.decide` with `phase13Joint: 'candidate'` at the hero seat: the joint owner over domain `joint-multiway-round1-v4`, response pack `joint-action-response-round2-v1`, range pack `joint-public-range-round1-v1`, behind the P13.1 legal form and the `illegal_candidate` / `earlier_phase_applied` guards. The retained one-response model `joint-action-response-round1-v2` is recorded as a comparison identity only. The contract writes these strings as literals (JointActionModel.ts reaches the Phase 7 owner, whose import closure constructs the database client at load); a test requires them to equal the running constants, and a shard records the running versions so a mismatch is refused by name.

**Reference.** The same call with `phase13Joint: 'off'`. That is the action live tables execute today: Phase 13 runs in shadow and the Phase 10, 11 and 12 candidates are never selected live (every protected release selection is null), so the harness keeps `phase10Plo4`, `phase11Omaha` and `phase12Remaining` off in every seat of both arms, which executes the same action as their live shadow.

**Opponents.** Every other seat plays `HorseLogic.decide` with `phase13Joint: 'off'`, the five production styles drawn per (deal seed, seat) as in Phases 10 to 12, mood on in both arms on the deal-seed clock.

## Statistic, interval and thresholds

- **Statistic.** Per hand pair (identical deal seed, so identical cards, hero seat, button, seat styles, bomb antes and boards, Pineapple discards and decision clock), the hero's net chips after rake and BBJ drop, candidate minus reference, in exact integer cents, reported in bb/100. Strata: profile x seed x dealer-relative hero offset x divergence street, exact power sums. Equal weight per profile within a cell.
- **Interval.** Two-sided 99% normal interval on the stratified mean, z = `JOINT_STRENGTH_Z99` = 2.5758293035489004 (the Phase 12 value), trusted only with at least 10,000 pairs per profile in the cell and a first Edgeworth coverage error of at most 0.001 (both equal to Phase 12, tested).
- **Primary.** The variant's profiles pooled: 99% lower bound > 0 bb/100.
- **Seed replication.** Each held-out seed block: pooled point estimate > 0 bb/100.
- **Non-regression.** Every gating cell's 99% lower bound >= the regression margin: -10 bb/100 for the no-limit and pot-limit variants (one 100 BB buy-in per 1,000 hands), -4 bb/100 for FLH and FLO8 (two big bets per 100 hands), the Phase 12 numbers and reasons.
- **Gating cells.** Every profile, the six positions (`positionForOffset`: three dealt has button, small blind, big blind; seven to nine seats add middle), every depth band the variant has, and each board count (`boards:1` pools every ordinary profile with the one-board bomb hand): 20 cells for the no-limit and pot-limit variants, 22 for FLH and FLO8. Street families are reported, never gated.

## Software validity

Per shard, every one must hold or the shard is refused by name: complete, every offset visited equally, zero illegal actions, conservation errors, card errors, truncated hands, settlement mismatches, deduction mismatches and paired replay mismatches, **zero `illegalCandidates` and zero `earlierPhaseRefusals`**, fixed work proven (governor off, scale 1, fixed policy clock, deal-seed mood clock), no promotion claim. The two guard counters are validity, not strength: P13.1 puts every joint candidate in the legalizer's own form before it is priced and the harness keeps every earlier phase off, so either refusal on a natural decision means the code measured is not the code P13.1 verified. That is why `selectionGuard.gate` is `false` (a refused candidate executes the reference and never moves the paired difference) while the counters sit in `softwareValidity.perShard`.

Reported and never gated: `workBudgetRefusals`, `responseBranchUnavailable` (`joint_response_branch_unavailable`, the reference is played in both arms), `insufficientSamples`, `eligible`, `fired`, `changed`, `eligibleByBoards`, Pineapple `discards`. A missing counter is refused (`<field>_unrecorded`).

**Independent checks.** `JointStrengthChecks.ts` settles every finished hand with `settleJointBoardReference` (the Phase 13 offline multiboard reference: every physical board pot by pot from independently built contribution layers and refunds, ranked by the independent Omaha reference for PLO4/5/6/8 and FLO8 with high and low halves, or the independent remaining-game reference for NLH, FLH, Short Deck and Pineapple with discards as dead cards). No seat may receive more than its independent gross award, losers receive nothing, the shortfall equals rake plus BBJ drop, a board beyond the activated count must never have been dealt, and the deductions must equal `effectiveRake` and `effectiveBbjDrop` on the contested pot.

## Matrix sizes

Rule (`matrixRules`, script `p13-2-sizing.mts.txt`, reproduced byte for byte on the final tree): pairs per shard = rotation block x k, k the largest whole number keeping the slowest profile's shard at or under 60 hosted minutes, a hosted runner taken as 1.5 times faster than this container (so at or under 90 container minutes, three quarters of the 120-minute timeout even with no speedup). Then start every profile at the fewest shards giving 10,000 pairs at every offset and add shards to the member of the widest planned gating cell that narrows it most per runner minute, until it is inside 0.85 x the margin at 1.2 x the pilot deviation, or until the budget (256 jobs, 3 hours of wall time at max-parallel 20) refuses any addition.

1,209 jobs and about 504 runner hours across the nine variants; each variant is one dispatch of at most 256 jobs. Every planned cell keeps at least 11,232 pairs per member profile, and the relative standard error of a member's variance estimate at its pilot kurtosis is at most 0.044 (the test requires under 0.1).

**Power, stated plainly.** FLH reaches its planned maximum (every cell within 3.4 bb/100). For the other eight variants the budget binds first: the widest planned half-width is wider than the margin itself (16 to 31 bb/100 against 10 for the big-bet variants, 4.66 against 4 for FLO8). So with this matrix a variant whose true effect is zero is expected to fail the non-regression gate, and only a candidate with a large real edge in every gating cell can qualify. This is a shortfall of power, never of validity: a false pass stays bounded by the 99% intervals. Narrowing it means a larger budget (more dispatches of the same locked contract are not allowed; a larger matrix needs a new contract version) or a cheaper candidate per pair. The bomb profiles dominate the cost: they are the slowest per pair and among the most dispersed.

## What refuses by name

**The verdict**: `missing_shard`, `duplicate_shard`, `unexpected_shard`; per shard `schema_mismatch`, `variant_mismatch`, `contract_version_mismatch`, `contract_digest_mismatch`, `pack_version_mismatch`, `domain_version_mismatch`, `range_pack_version_mismatch`, `not_contract_mode`, `unknown_profile`, `not_holdout_seed`, `shard_outside_matrix`, `first_pair_mismatch`, `pairs_below_contract`, `incomplete`, `position_coverage_incomplete`, `offset_count_mismatch`, `position_coverage_unbalanced`, `strata_pair_count_mismatch`, `unknown_stratum`, `paired_replay_mismatch`, each nonzero validity counter including `illegalCandidates` and `earlierPhaseRefusals`, `<counter>_unrecorded`, `discards_outside_pineapple`, `fixed_work_not_proven`, `shard_claims_promotion`; then `cash:primary:lower_bound_not_above_zero`, `cash:seed:<seed>:point_estimate_not_above_zero`, `cash:<cell>:regression_margin_exceeded`, `cash:<cell>:interval_not_trusted`, `cash:no_shard_results`.
