## The selection rule

Mode is derived only from admission. `horsePhase13AdmittedMode` returns `off` when the caller says `off` (the only choice a caller has), `shadow` for a tournament decision, an unknown variant or a holder of another variant, `candidate` only when the worker's own holder for the decision's variant reports `usable`, and `shadow` for every other verdict, unselected included. `assertEnvelope` still refuses a caller's `phase13Joint: 'candidate'` and any `phase13EvidenceMode` with "offline candidate controls are forbidden in live decision requests", even when authority is usable.

At the worker boundary `horsePhase13SelectionIsValid` accepts a changed action only as an authority-backed cash selection: running receipt version, a well-formed usable authority whose continuation is `joint-multiway-round1-v4/<variant>` (another variant's joint authority, the bare joint domain, a Phase 11 or a Phase 12 pack never backs it), `mode: 'candidate'`, `changed: true`, `selection: 'selected'`, `utilityOwner: 'cash'`, the leaving action equal to the proposal and the final action, no acceptance-time fields, no selection refusal on an applied receipt, and no applied Phase 10, 11 or 12 candidate on the same decision. An unapplied receipt may carry the named refusals `illegal_candidate` or `earlier_phase_applied` and nothing else.

Composition with the earlier phases: HorseLogic's `earlier_phase_applied` guard (P13.1) stays, and an authority-path test runs natural Short Deck controller spots through the live worker with usable Phase 12 and Phase 13 Short Deck authority both admitted: both modes are `candidate`, the Phase 12 candidate keeps the action, the joint receipt is a usable-authority `shadow_change` with `selectionRefusal: 'earlier_phase_applied'`, the result crosses the boundary, and the same receipt relabelled as applied on top of it is refused.

## The natural completion requirement and its floor

Population: natural chip cash betting decisions of one variant on one engine release, read from the journaled joint receipts (`body.decision.jointPolicy`) of receipt version `joint-multiway-round1-v4`. A receipt is not counted unless it is eligible, cash (`utilityOwner: 'cash'`), of this variant, of this receipt version, on a betting street, and carries a board count of 1, 2 or 3. Tournament decisions never admit candidate mode and are not counted; Pineapple discards are not betting decisions. An eligible Diamond decision (bound objective asset `diamonds`, NLH only) is excluded by name: it is counted in the record's `excluded.diamond` and in no cell, because the P13.2 contract excludes Diamond NLH from the qualified domain and admission never runs it as candidate.

Which streets the owner decides on: the flop, turn and river of every hand, and the preflop of an ordinary single-board hand. A bomb or multi-board hand has no preflop decision (`bomb_hand_has_no_preflop_decision`, never eligible), and a single-board heads-up decision belongs to the variant policy (`heads_up_owned_by_variant_policy`). So the preflop cell holds ordinary multiway hands only, and the two- and three-board cells hold bomb hands only, on the flop, turn and river.

Each counted decision takes the first outcome that applies:

1. `workBudget`: the policy passed its live budget and played the baseline (`reason: 'work_budget'`);
2. `samplerBudgetExhausted`: the sampler's own deadline cut the population short (`sampleBudgetExhausted: true`);
3. `responseBranchUnavailable`: the response model's named limit refusals only (`joint_response_branch_unavailable`, `joint_response_street_unavailable`, `joint_response_street_not_modeled`);
4. `sampleUnavailable`: no complete consumed population (insufficient or unavailable samples, no consumed range binding, or fewer completed samples than requested);
5. `governorReduced`: a complete population whose request was below the table's full count (`jointFullSamples`: 16 up to four dealt players, 8 above, read from `JOINT_LIVE_DOMAIN`);
6. `analysisUnavailable`: a complete full-count population whose proposal did not fire (`fired` is not true): an analysis failure (`joint_action_conservation`, `joint_analysis_unavailable`, `joint_pots_conservation`, `joint_scores_no_winner`, `joint_action_no_legal_candidates`, `proposal_outside_legal_menu`, and any other caught failure) or a response-model defect (`joint_response_branch_mass`, `joint_response_illegal_simulated_action`);
7. `completed`: the policy the P13.2 matrix measured, a proposal that fired.

So in every cell `eligible = completed + workBudget + samplerBudgetExhausted + responseBranchUnavailable + sampleUnavailable + governorReduced + analysisUnavailable`. The Wilson bound reads `completed` only.

Cells: the P13.2 contract asks for the share "per street and per board count". The record therefore carries two tallies of the same decisions: `streets` (preflop, flop, turn, river) and `boardCounts` (`1` pools ordinary and one-board bomb hands, `2` and `3` the multi-board bomb hands), the marginal cells the contract's own board-count gating cells use. Admission refuses a record whose two tallies disagree on any field.

Floor: every cell of both tallies must reach 0.95 on the Wilson score lower bound at the contract z (`JOINT_STRENGTH_Z99`, 2.5758293035489004), never the point share. An all-complete cell needs at least 127 eligible decisions (127 of 127 bounds at 0.95035, 126 of 126 at 0.94998). Why a floor: the matrix measured the candidate only on decisions that complete their fixed work, and a fallback mixes candidate and baseline streets in a line neither arm played. Why 0.95: no calibrated cost of such a mixed line exists in source, so the floor bounds the unmeasured share rather than its cost; it is the P11.3 and P12.3 number and reasoning. Why per street: the response tree runs only on the turn and river, so a completing preflop must not hide a failing river. Why per board count: a two- or three-board decision prices every sample on every board, so common one-board hands must not hide failing bomb hands. A cell with no natural decisions has no evidence and fails closed; a variant whose tables never deal two- or three-board bomb hands cannot be admitted, because admission would let the candidate act there.

The reader. Stage 1 (`phase13-completion-extract.py`, read-only on the engine host, `nice` and `ionice` idle) reads closed archive segments only, verifies each segment's sha256 name, counts only `DECIDE_FAST` decision records (a DEEP second look is `excluded_deep_second_look`), names `excluded_release_other`, windows Pineapple discards by their own `journalContext.requestedAtMs` and names them `excluded_discard_decision`, and prints multi-board and bomb hands with `boardCount`, `bombPot`, `variant`, `dealtPlayers` and `governorScale`. `PHASE13_EXTRACT_SEGMENT_DIRS` replaces the host directories in tests. Stage 2 (`phase13CompletionRecord.ts`) counts with the authority's own functions, computes the policy digest from the release's own sources with `git show <release>:server/<path>`, and refuses by name: `unknown_variant`, `invalid_release`, `invalid_window`, `release_unchanged_not_proven`, `release_sources_unavailable`, `malformed_extract_line`, `decision_outside_window`. An empty window is a record with zero eligible in every cell, which admission refuses as `completion_below_floor`. Stage 1 reads its window bounds as stage 2's `Date.parse` does (`Z` or a numeric offset, fractional seconds truncated to milliseconds) and names Diamond decisions in its summary (`diamond_records`); stage 2 excludes them by name.

## Defects and findings

1. Defective, fixed: the joint owner's runtime closure reached `HandController.ts` and, through it, the database client. `JointDeductions.ts` imported its two pot-scaling helpers from the controller. With the unmodified import the closure test fails: "expected [ ...(125) ] to have a length of 42 but got 125" and "expected [ ...(125) ] to not include 'src/engine/HandController.ts'". The effect was a policy digest that would have had to hash or excuse the whole controller, and an offline completion reader that loaded the database client and never exited. The helpers now live in `WinnerUnitScaling.ts` (no imports), unchanged; `HandController.ts` re-exports them, and `RakeScaling` and `Phase9MultiboardUnits` pass unchanged.
2. Hardened: the selection validator on its own accepted an applied joint receipt that also carried `selectionRefusal: 'illegal_candidate'` (reproduced: "forgery 9: expected true to be false"). The full boundary already refused it through the P13.1 binding check, so no receipt could cross; the validator now refuses the pair itself.
3. Finding, not a defect: the joint wrapper called directly in candidate mode reports a tournament receipt as applied; the HorseLogic node hands every tournament decision to Phase 7, and the boundary refuses a tournament selection whatever it says.

## Audit fixes (October 6, 2026)

An audit of the merged Phase 13 (squash 886cc965) found five defects. Each was reproduced by a test on the unmodified source, fixed at its cause, and the test kept. The changelog `docs/changelog/2026-10-06-horse-brain-phase13-audit-fixes.md` records the reproductions verbatim. No file in `HORSE_PHASE13_POLICY_SOURCE_FILES` or in the Phase 10, 11 or 12 policy digest lists changed, so every policy digest, and every held-out matrix measured against those bytes, is unchanged. The strength contract and its digest are unchanged.

1. High, defective, fixed. The assembler wrote the qualification's `packVersion` as the response pack version (`joint-action-response-round2-v1`), while admission compares it with the joint domain version (`HORSE_PHASE13_PACK_VERSION`, `joint-multiway-round1-v4`) and refused `continuation_mismatch`, so no real qualification could ever be admitted. The test fixture built qualifications with the domain version, so the two halves were never run together. The assembler now writes the contract's `domainVersion` (equal to `JOINT_LIVE_DOMAIN.version`, which every counted manifest is checked against); the response identity remains bound by the policy digest, which hashes the domain, range and response versions, and the strength record still carries the response version under `source.packVersion`. A new test assembles a contract-mode matrix with the real assembler, reads its real qualification and strength bytes through the real admission with a non-null selection, and is admitted; the same file carrying the response version is refused `continuation_mismatch`.
2. Medium, defective, fixed. The contract excludes Diamond NLH from the qualified domain, but `horsePhase13AdmittedMode` gated only on cash and variant, so a usable NLH authority would have run a Diamond NLH decision as candidate, and the completion counting counted Diamond receipts in the NLH cells. Admitted mode now takes the decision's `asset` (absent or null is chips) and keeps anything but chips in shadow; the worker passes `gameState.asset`; the boundary validator refuses a candidate-mode receipt bound to a Diamond objective and an applied receipt without a bound chip objective; the completion counting excludes a Diamond decision by name (`excluded.diamond`).
3. Low to medium, defective, fixed. Stage 2 refused an empty extract (`no_records`, exit 2), so a variant with no window decisions got no record. It now writes a record with zero eligible in every cell, which fails the floor by name at admission. The refusal and its test case were removed; the replacement tests pin the zero record and its refusal.
4. Low, defective, fixed. The completion counting never read `fired`, so an eligible full-sample receipt whose proposal failed in analysis counted as `completed`, and a response-model defect counted as `responseBranchUnavailable`. The new `analysisUnavailable` field takes every complete full-count decision whose proposal did not fire; `responseBranchUnavailable` keeps the three named limit refusals only. The field is in the record, both tallies, the cell sum admission checks, and the counting both readers use; the Wilson bound reads `completed` only.
5. Low, defective, fixed. Stage 1 dropped fractional seconds and failed on a `+00:00` offset while stage 2 used `Date.parse`, so the two stages could read different windows from the same arguments. Stage 1 now parses these forms exactly as `Date.parse` does and refuses a bound without an offset.

Items 2 to 4 change what a completion record counts, so the completion definition is now `horse-phase13-completion-definition-v2`, the record carries `excluded`, and admission accepts only v2 records; a v1 record is refused `completion_evidence_mismatch`. The authority key binds the definition. The predeclaration in `docs/evidence/phase13/declaration-p13-3-completion.txt` names definition v1 and the readers at the squash commit; a record produced exactly as declared there is a v1 record and is refused by the current admission, so a v2 record needs its own predeclared window with the readers at this change. The declaration file is evidence and is not edited.

## Limits stated plainly

No variant's held-out matrix has run, no qualification or completion record exists, and nothing selects. The completion requirement is implemented and tested on real receipts but unmeasured on natural play. The board-count cells make admission impossible for a variant whose live tables deal no two- or three-board bomb hands; that is deliberate.
