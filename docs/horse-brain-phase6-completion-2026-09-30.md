## Whole-window route proof and the discovered blocker

The [completed 6B observation](evidence/phase6b/route-proof-2026-09-30-f92ef579.json) used the same exact serving release and 10:00–11:00 UTC window. The atlas digest agrees with the maintained domain. Of 5,643 route cells, 493 were observed and 5,150 remain unobserved.

76,964 actions bind to their completed-hand observation. The observer separately reports 1,216 execution-unavailable outcomes, which must not be counted as accepted actions, and 43 observation-identity refusals. Those 43 are a concrete Phase 6 closure defect, not a passing fallback. The same-window [read-only database aggregates](evidence/phase6b/observation-identity-diagnosis-2026-09-30.json) find exactly 43 `unavailable_public_node` / `invalid_public_state` actions, all NLH tournament hands with an empty physical dealer-button seat. Database creation time and journal record time differ, so this count agreement is supporting aggregate evidence, not an individual-ID join.

`HorsePublicActionNode` requires the dealer seat to be occupied even in multiway hands. The maintained dead-button rules allow an empty physical button after elimination in a hand with at least three players. The Horse observer therefore rejects a valid public state. `HorseAdaptiveObservation` repeats the occupied-button assumption at the learning consumer. The repair accepts an empty physical button with at least three dealt players, preserves occupied-button structural keys, and assigns no real player position zero for a dead button. Two-player empty buttons and invalid physical seats remain refused. Real HandController regressions reproduce seven failures before the fix and pass all 137 focused cases afterward, including capture on all streets, immutable observation identity, final accepted-action binding and adaptive qualification. Gameplay and settlement are unaffected by this metadata refusal; the accepted action was already validated and recorded.

The observer itself completed on the exact image with a read-only mount, no network, 1 GiB memory, one CPU, exit zero, no OOM and confirmed container/scratch removal. It did not mutate the engine or private journal.
