## 7A: qualify current single-board utility

### Demonstrated source defects to close

The following locations refer to the starting source; implementation can move line numbers.

1. **ICM method selection loses live all-in entrants.** `IcmModel.ts` selected its exact workspace from positive pre-settlement stacks. All-in commitments can leave ten positive stacks in an eleven-player live field. Awards or a permitted recovery can restore eleven positive stacks, but the exact helper returned zero above ten and the workspace labeled it exact with zero error. Retain exact arithmetic for candidates at or below ten; use one deterministic bounded Monte Carlo workspace for candidates above ten. Keep original input ownership, immutable remote stacks, common clocks and prefix limits. The focused regression and existing ICM ownership suites are the local proof; publication and natural coverage are separate.
2. **The ordinary caller omits exact tournament settlement.** `HorseLogic.ts::buildPhase7UtilityInput` did not supply the `settlement` contract. The ordinary branch in `HorseTournamentUtility.ts::settleSample` divided odd chips fractionally, while the real controller uses whole tournament chips and button-based remainders. Reuse the existing exact pot owner already used by the joint bridge. Cover single-board ties, high/low where supported, folded contributions and returned excess with independent expected amounts. Verify the ordinary caller supplies the contract.
3. **Static opening order omits reopened responses.** `HorseLogic.ts::phase7PlayersBehind` used only the seats after hero in the street's opening order. Earlier seats can still owe a raised price. With occupied BTN4 and preflop order 3 → 4 → 1 → 2, an UTG raise, BTN call and SB reraise leave UTG and BTN owing responses after BB hero. The utility caller must include them. Reuse the existing unpaid-wager rule in `jointPlayersBehind` or the same authoritative ring logic. Add a connected caller regression, not only a fixture that manually supplies `actsAfterHero: true`.

The empty physical button is a different limitation. The Phase 6 producer names `dead_button_atlas_unsupported` and marks the tournament context incomplete; `trustedTournamentContext` therefore refuses Phase 7. Preserve that explicit unsupported state. Do not enable dead-button utility by inventing a complete context or changing the atlas during this stage.

### Original inputs, observations and model attribution

- Bind the actual legal menu, stack identities, pots, board/sample population, payouts, bounties, reload eligibility, response inputs and resulting numerical error to the current utility evaluation. Preserve chip and funded-pool units; never add chip EV to prize utility a second time.
- Carry the response-model identity and exact original input identity through the existing utility ledger, worker admission, private journal and final accepted outcome. Serialization or a digest alone does not establish that the model is calibrated or that an input was freshly observed.
- Preserve the read frame selected for the actual decision. Distinguish family/size statistics, pooled fallback, pooled recency, disabled reads and unavailable reads. Do not attach the current table, exact variant or tournament format to a historical pooled counter and call that its original observation scope.
- Add one bounded connected completed-observation → original read frame → changed utility input/calculation → accepted action case. Cover a meaningful contrasting observation and a negative incompatible scope. Same actor at another table or in cash/tournament/variant context must not inherit a falsely claimed observation authority. A valid pooled read can remain explicitly pooled.
- Preserve distinct intended, coerced, fallback, stale/retired, not-executed and unavailable outcomes. Join final controller records to their original request, generation, turn and selected utility. Worker transport, policy selection and accepted action are separate checkpoints.

### Known limits that must remain visible

The current MDF/strength fold-call response formula is a heuristic. Its sampling, equity and ICM error terms do **not** include a calibrated opponent-response model error. The receipt must say that calibration is unavailable, rather than presenting the numerical selection interval as confidence in GTO optimality.

The original Phase 7 release did not retain original wall-clock observation windows in HorseMind lifetime/family-size counters and pooled recency. The [observation-window follow-up](changelog/2026-09-30-horse-phase7-observation-window.md) repairs that directly in the existing capture, persistence, original-frame and receipt owners. It preserves legacy unknown history and describes conservative contribution envelopes. These counters still do not establish table, cash/tournament or exact-variant isolation. The 7A evidence owner records those limitations explicitly, including separate selected-statistics and pooled-recency timestamp coverage. A recently completed hand, complete read-frame hash or refreshed cache cannot fill missing historical timestamps. Do not close a source-window or isolation qualification gate from these counters alone. Record any still-required window/isolation evidence as unavailable with its specific owning work; do not conceal it behind a percentage.

The existing recovery model deliberately refuses unsupported bounty reload/add-on economics and relevant unknown personal eligibility. Current-action utility also preserves the reference when omitted future hero decisions or overlapping numerical uncertainty prevent a supported override. Keep those refusals. No new response calibration, solver corpus or promotion claim is implied by repairing arithmetic and attribution.

Nothing may create that population to obtain the proof.
