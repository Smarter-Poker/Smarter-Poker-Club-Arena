One real defect was found and fixed in [#5711](https://github.com/Smarter-Poker/Smarter-Poker-Club-Arena/pull/5711) (merged b736397c): worker admission accepted a Phase 7 receipt without checking it against the request it answered (shape only), so a joint sample from a different hand state or board count, a multi-board receipt with no shared-deck sample, or evidence naming a player not in the request could reach the execution witness. Admission and the journal reader now rebuild those checks from the request and refuse a mismatch by name (`phase7_foreign_opponent` and its siblings); old receipts without evidence are not refused. Also in that PR: the duplicated 160-sample constant now has one owner, an unreachable sample collector in the multi-board equity loop was removed, and six joint-sampler refusal names that had no test are tested. Focused Phase 7 suites: 1,094 tests across 28 files after the change (1,083 before). Design limitations recorded, not defects: the fractional chip split exists only for callers that supply no settlement rule (both live callers supply one); the Phase 13 joint bridge prices without sample provenance and is offline only; preflop sampling is capped at the opponents the deck can deal; the journal reader checks the stored input hash rather than recomputing it.
