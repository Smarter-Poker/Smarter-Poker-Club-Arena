## Production Context Completeness

Phase 8 refuses a tournament postflop decision with `context_incomplete` whenever the decision's tournament snapshot is not `complete` (`HorseTournamentPostflop.ts`). The snapshot is produced in one place, `ServerTableEngineTurns.horseTournamentContext`, from the lifecycle cache in `TournamentBrainContext.ts`. `stateSchemaVersion` is always 1 there and the worker refuses anything else, so every refusal comes from `contextStatus`.

**Producers and conditions.** The cache (`deriveContext`, `getTournamentBrainContextSnapshot`) reports `incomplete` for: missing tournament type, status or game variant; an invalid player population; `live_field_recovery_pending` (a zero-stack player holding an unexpired rebuy prompt) and the resulting `live_field_stack_cardinality_mismatch`; no payout or ticket structure; no live stacks; an invalid blind structure or level; `level_timing_missing`, `_stale`, `_future` or `_paused`; invalid entry, seat, next-level, re-entry, rebuy, add-on, registration, bounty or mystery terms; satellite entitlement read failure or `satellite_award_depth_unavailable`; mystery inventory read failure; `tournament_context_refresh_not_started`, `_failed`, `_timed_out` or `tournament_context_missing`. It reports `warming` while the first read is in flight and `stale` when the last good read is older than 60 seconds. The table adds `live_blinds_invalid`, `live_seat_state_incomplete`, `dealer_seat_missing`, `dead_button_atlas_unsupported`, `blind_level_cache_lag` and `tournament_id_missing`.

**The October 1 rise is not a producer change.** Phase 8 runs its domain checks only until the safety sentinel disables it after 32 eligible but silent decisions per engine start (`HorsePhase8Safety`); after that every decision is tallied `disabled_eligible_but_silent` (362,980 on 2026-10-01). `phase8_reason_context_incomplete` therefore counts only the decisions in those short post-start windows and follows the number of engine starts and the events running in them. The producers changed once in the period (#5642, 2026-09-30: a dead button moved from `dealer_seat_missing` to `dead_button_atlas_unsupported`, incomplete either way), and the share of all tournament decisions with incomplete context was flat (`phase6_tournament_context_incomplete` 258,919 of 1,968,489 on 09-30, 264,869 of 1,758,890 on 10-01). Status of the October 1 cause split: historical only.

**Defects fixed (this PR).**

1. `blind_level_cache_lag` on a hand dealt just before a level change. A hand keeps the stakes it was dealt with, so when the manager publishes the next level mid-hand the cache describes the new level and the hand is exactly one level behind it. Both facts were authoritative, but the producer treated the mismatch as missing context. Of 2,303 NLH postflop lag decisions sampled from 06:00 to 14:00 UTC, 2,251 were this case (cache level begun a median 0.31 minutes earlier, never more than 1.81). The cache now carries the previous playable level of the same structure (`previousLevel`), and the producer recognises a hand on exactly that level: the snapshot is complete, its current level and stakes are the hand's, and the tournament's current level is its next level with `nextBlindInMin` 0 (due with the next hand). A hand two levels behind, or a different ante rule, still reports `blind_level_cache_lag`.
2. `blind_level_cache_lag` with the cache behind the table (52 of the 2,303) and `level_timing_missing` (379 NLH postflop decisions, every one at level 0, 377 of them on the first cache read, taken before the manager persisted the first level start). The cache refreshed only on a 20 second TTL, so it kept describing a clock this process had already replaced. The tournament manager now asks for a generation-fenced read immediately after it commits a blind level, persists a level start or releases a break with a credited clock (`refreshTournamentBrainContextAfterClockCommit`); a read that started before the commit can never publish over it.

**What stays refused, and why.**

- `live_field_recovery_pending`: not applicable with reason. The settlement sets the prompt only when a rebuy or re-entry is still allowed and the window is open; until the player accepts, declines or the prompt expires, whether that seat returns with a stack is undecided by tournament rule, so the ICM field is not known.
- `satellite_award_depth_unavailable`: not applicable with reason. The award depth is fixed only when entry closes and the entitlements are written (for example 119292f4: refusals at 00:15 UTC, entitlements written at entry close 00:41 UTC).
- `tournament_context_stale`: unavailable external input. The context read timed out at its 5 second bound under database load (35 `TournamentBrainContext.refresh` timeouts in 40 minutes of the engine log), and the last good read passed 60 seconds.
- `dead_button_atlas_unsupported`: not applicable with reason. The worker requires this label for a dead button, and the Phase 8 future-game projection needs an occupied button seat (`position_unavailable`), so relabelling would only rename the refusal.

**Production after release (verified now).** Read only from the decision journal on the production engine host, 2026-10-02 15:03 to 16:20 UTC, after engine `c0b56ffc` (#5825 and #5827) and then `1c155771` (a descendant with no Horse change) were serving: 21,800 tournament decisions carried a Phase 8 ledger. Refusals carrying `blind_level_cache_lag` or `level_timing_missing` fell from 324 and 43 (00:00 to 13:45 UTC, table above) to **0**. The 1,092 `context_incomplete` refusals that remain are the causes named above as staying refused: `satellite_award_depth_unavailable` 1,047, `live_field_recovery_pending` with its cardinality mismatch 33 and `dead_button_atlas_unsupported` 12. Across the four engine processes the candidate was eligible on 4,910 decisions, fired on 1,837 and completed 1,792. With activation off every decision stayed in shadow, and 0 changed an action.

One process (`dc7fd8f3`) latched `repeated_budget_breach` at 15:46:09Z, after three consecutive `budget_exhausted` decisions, and tallied its later decisions as `disabled_repeated_budget_breach` (4,221); 46 decisions were tallied `disabled_eligible_but_silent`. `budget_exhausted` is the 5 ms wall-clock limit, not the 4 ms cooperative work deadline: over the window 743 decisions breached it, with median latency 6.2 ms and simulation 5.9 ms, while the policy step took a median 0.12 ms. The overshoot is in rollout time on a 4-vCPU host running at a load average of about 8. The latch is the specified Phase 8 safety (`PHASE8_SAFETY.budgetFailureLimit = 3`) doing its job. It was not weakened and no budget was raised. A restart returns the process to shadow, and none of this can select a candidate. The raw journal reads (`p8post2`, `p8be`) are kept in the agent evidence archive.
