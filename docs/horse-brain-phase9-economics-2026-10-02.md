Each case drives the real owner. RIT cases start the controller at the ceiling, inject the preflop all-in state, read the table's configuration through `applyRunItTwiceConfig`, take the offer, the chooser's two or three runs and both acceptances through `RunItTwiceEngine`, and settle through `ServerTableEngineRunout.dealAndResolveRIT` from the controller's own deck. As in the existing Phase 9 suites, the contributions are a balanced settled fixture injected at the standing street. They are not proof of a prior accepted betting trajectory, event-to-offer dispatch, persistence or natural Horse use.

## Tournament Bomb Gate

The plan says unsupported offer conditions fail at their existing owners. A tournament never deals a bomb pot, but before this PR nothing in the engine said so: no tournament path writes `bomb_pot_enabled`, the column defaults to false and Diamond tournaments refuse it, yet `fn_update_table_bomb_settings` (`supabase/migrations/20260829161712_bomb_ante_multiplier_is_whole_blinds_and_says_so.sql:31-120`) does not look at the row's kind and the dealer read the switch on any table (`ServerTableEngineDealing.ts:2563`).

`bombPotSettingsFromTable` (`server/src/engine/BombPotScheduler.ts`), the read every hand passes through, now treats a tournament row (`tournament_id` set or `game_type` `tournament`, the same test as `isTournamentTable`) as bombs off and reports it once per table as `BombPotScheduler.tournament_bomb_refused`, naming the table. The manual next-hand claim in `ServerTableEngineDealing.ts` skips tournament tables too. The database function is unchanged. Proof: the new `BombPotScheduler.test.ts` case "reads the switch as off on a tournament row, whichever column says tournament" failed before the change (`enabled` true on a tournament row) and passes after; the file passes 37 of 37, and server `tsc --noEmit` is clean. Status: implemented but unverified in production (not in a released engine).

- Contributions: there is no contribution column. They are replayed from `actions`: `call` and `bet` record the chips added; `raise` and `all_in` record the street total (the same number when the player had no street bet). Each replay step is checked against the totals the next action captured (`publicNode.seats`).

## Left Open

**Chip-cash insurance on Omaha (5 cells): implemented but unverified.** The economics of a contract are spread over owners this package does not drive: pricing comes from the equity worker, the offer and purchase from `InsuranceEngine` through `ServerTableEngineRunout.runInsurancePerStreetFlow`, and the payout and premium from post-hand settlement against the insurance bank (`ServerTableEngineSettlement.ts:724-729` onward). An independent answer would need `OmahaEquityOracle` in `exact_river` mode for the price and a settlement fixture through the bank ledger. For PLO8 and FLO8 the product prices the high half only and voids any split, so there is no supported hi-lo insurance economics to certify; for PLO4/PLO5/PLO6 the test is a named follow-up.
