# Horse Brain Phase 9 P9.2: enabled economics intersections, October 2, 2026

Horse Brain only. P9.2 is defined in [the maintained completion plan](horse-brain-phases6-15-completion-plan-2026-09-17.md#p92--complete-only-uncovered-enabled-intersections-s1s5). This record joins the existing Omaha oracle matrix to the launch and configuration rules in source on `origin/main` `b05c02ab`, names every variant × mode × feature × unit × seat-ceiling cell, says which existing test holds its economics, and adds tests only for the enabled cells that were still missing. Status uses the maintained vocabulary (verified now / historical only / implemented but unverified / defective / unavailable external input / not applicable with reason). No percentage is used. P9.1 (the five prepared seven-seat PLO5 chip-cash accepted-betting cases) is a separate package running in parallel and is not repeated here.

What this record does not claim: run it twice is not supported anywhere merely because the offline evaluator can score two boards; there is no ten-seat cash table, no tournament or heads-up offer, and no insurance claim for the hi-lo games. The oracle's bounds stay as they are: chip units 0.01 or 1, at most ten players and seats 1 to 10, at most three boards, and a one-billion-unit contribution per player (`server/src/benchmark/OmahaReference.ts:139-165`, `OmahaEquityOracle.ts:15-22`).

## The New Tests And Their Independent Answers

One fixture serves every case: three live seats (seat 1 short all in; seat 3 sixty over seat 2 and returned uncalled), seats 4 and 5 folded after putting in the main-pot level, every other seat folded for nothing, button on seat 1. The live hands are `As 2s Jh Td`, `Kh Kd Qc Qd`, `Ah 3h 9s 9d` (PLO5 and PLO6 add the next free cards), and the boards, in order, are `7c Tc Ad 5h 6h`, `3c 4d 8h Kc Qh`, `5c 6d 9h Th Jc`. Every case asserts that the full physical deal is 52 distinct cards.

**Chip deductions, by hand.** Contributions 180, 540, 600, 180, 180: the reference returns 60 to seat 3 and builds a 900 main pot (seats 1 to 3) and a 720 side pot (seats 2 and 3), 1,620.00 gross. The 10/20 schedule row gives rake 10% = 162.00 capped at 15.00 and BBJ 20 × 0.06 = 1.20, so 1,603.80 is paid, exactly 99%. PLO6 collects no BBJ, so its row is 180, 480, 540, 180, 180: 900 + 600 = 1,500.00, rake 15.00, 1,485.00 paid, again 99%. Every chip case asserts that each reference award is a whole number of dollars before taking 99% of it, so the expected cents are exact and no rounding rule is taken from the engine. The test reads the schedule the engine reads and pins it to these numbers.

**Whole units.** Diamond and tournament rows are 181, 541, 601, 181, 181: main 905 (odd), side 720, 60 returned, no deductions. The odd unit reaches the earliest board, the high half before the low and the first seat clockwise from the button, all from `OmahaReference`.

Tournament rows use the PLO4/PLO8/FLO8 tables at ten seats, PLO5 at nine and PLO6 at seven, one board; the extra folded seats change no pot, so their answers are the one-board whole-unit column.

Each case drives the real owner. Classic and bomb cases start `HandController` at the ceiling with the launch rake and BBJ configuration, play legal calls and checks to the river, inject the settled contributions and cards, and let seats 2 and 3 check the river so the controller itself returns the uncalled 60, builds the pots, splits boards and halves in the table's unit, takes the deductions and completes once. RIT cases start the controller at the ceiling, inject the preflop all-in state, read the table's configuration through `applyRunItTwiceConfig`, take the offer, the chooser's two or three runs and both acceptances through `RunItTwiceEngine`, and settle through `ServerTableEngineRunout.dealAndResolveRIT` from the controller's own deck. Assertions: cards per run, pots and eligibility, every award by board, pot and half, winner totals, the `rit_result` distribution and net pot, returned money, final stacks, `HAND_COMPLETE` rake and BBJ, one completion, table conservation including deductions, no error report, no restart and no pending offer.

As in the existing Phase 9 suites, the contributions are a balanced settled fixture injected at the standing street. They are not proof of a prior accepted betting trajectory, event-to-offer dispatch, persistence or natural Horse use.

## Defects

None found. Every new case passed against current source on the first run that had a correct fixture. The one fixture correction was a launch-rule finding, not a defect: PLO6 collects no BBJ (`RakeConfig.ts:319-325`), so the PLO6 chip row is sized separately rather than assumed.

## Observations, Not Changed Here

- **A tournament table has no engine gate against bomb pots.** No tournament path writes `bomb_pot_enabled`, the column defaults to false and Diamond tournaments refuse it, but `fn_update_table_bomb_settings` (`supabase/migrations/20260829161712_bomb_ante_multiplier_is_whole_blinds_and_says_so.sql:31-120`) does not refuse a tournament row and the dealer reads the switch on any table (`ServerTableEngineDealing.ts:2563`). If a host turned it on, the hand would settle in whole chips (held by `Phase9MultiboardUnits` tournament arm). Whether tournaments may ever deal bombs is a product rule, not P9.2 economics, so nothing is changed.
- **Chip-cash seat ceiling in practice.** New template games seat the Omaha family at six; the seven- and eight-seat ceilings are reachable through games adopted at Gate 7. The tests use the ceiling, which is the deck's worst case.

## Left Open

**Chip-cash insurance on Omaha (5 cells): implemented but unverified.** The economics of a contract are spread over owners this package does not drive: pricing comes from the equity worker, the offer and purchase from `InsuranceEngine` through `ServerTableEngineRunout.runInsurancePerStreetFlow`, and the payout and premium from post-hand settlement against the insurance bank (`ServerTableEngineSettlement.ts:724-729` onward). An independent answer would need `OmahaEquityOracle` in `exact_river` mode for the price and a settlement fixture through the bank ledger. For PLO8 and FLO8 the product prices the high half only and voids any split, so there is no supported hi-lo insurance economics to certify; for PLO4/PLO5/PLO6 the test is a named follow-up.
