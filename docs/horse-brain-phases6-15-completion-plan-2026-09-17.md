## Completion target

Every declared supported case must have a traceable chain from authoritative game state and qualified data, through the actual Horse worker and phase calculation, to the accepted controller action and retained private evidence. A producer, tag, import, registry entry, shadow result or passing fixture alone is insufficient.

Reuse the architecture in the [first-five blueprint](horse-brain-first-five-gold-standard.md) and the calculation depth in the [Phase 6/7 playbook](horse-brain-round1-playbook.md). Historical “100%” labels are not a substitute for evidence. In particular, the last inspected certified V31 runtime inventory was empty; World-owned shared solver and Training work has separate integration boundaries. Those dependencies must not be copied into a new completion claim.

The practical distinction for every phase is:

- **Coded:** the required calculation and its failure behavior exist in maintained source.
- **Connected:** the deployed producer, worker, selection, controller acceptance and private reader actually use the same identities and semantics.
- **Qualified:** independent correctness, declared-domain coverage, bounded work, rollback and natural source-matched evidence satisfy the applicable gates.
- **Activated:** only a qualified strategy with current machine-verifiable authority may replace the reference decision. Shadow computation is separately useful evidence and remains labeled shadow.

These states are separate facts, not a percentage. The implementation work is complete only when every applicable gate is evidenced or explicitly classified as an unresolved dependency. A phase with an unresolved core gate remains open.

4. **S4 — Qualified activation and withdrawal.** Build one generation-bound authority path for Phase 8 and Phase 10–13 candidate policies, reusing their current owners. Preserve the worker's prohibition on caller-supplied offline candidate controls. Machine-verifiable qualification must precede live selection; queued requests, deep work, think timers, cached policies and pending effects must retire when authority is withdrawn. The principal dependencies are S1 → per-phase coverage; S2 → meaningful phase receipts; S3 → complete review population and durable plans; qualified independent references + holdouts + S4 → strategy activation. Empty V31 or unavailable licensed solver inputs block certified-solver claims, not all heuristic functionality. No new watcher, cron, release loop or repair service is part of this plan. Existing business schedules may invoke corrected owners; they must not repair correctness that belongs in the transaction.

One concrete reader gap remains in the inspected source: `personaMtt` is loaded into the tag book, but its non-test occurrences are the declaration, tag production and loader; no runtime tournament-persona consumer was found. Do not describe it as an active tournament decision influence.

Reuse `StableHandTags.test.ts`, `HorseSitVerdict.test.ts`, `HorseFleetPolicyWiring.test.ts` and `HorseGameLoad.test.ts`; add only missing row-validation, scope, stale-book and consumer cases.

A future optional-entry timing consumer must use authoritative paying membership and distinguish discretionary entry from mandatory fills, tickets and freerolls. Do not map it into wager aggression by guessing. For current wager style, prove original profile → resolved modifiers → worker key/read frame → actual calculation → accepted action with existing decision-path suites and finite natural receipts.

A tag may legitimately be diagnostic; it must not receive silent permission to alter live policy or the sampling stream.
