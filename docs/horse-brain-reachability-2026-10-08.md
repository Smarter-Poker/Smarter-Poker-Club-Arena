# Horse Brain Reachability Record, October 8, 2026

Horse Brain only. This record answers one question with production evidence: does every Horse Brain phase actually run on every horse decision it is supposed to run on? It maps each phase to its live entry point, measures in production (read-only) how many horse decisions inside each phase's domain the phase reached, names the cause of every decision it did not reach, and records the wiring defects found and fixed. Strategy activation is a separate decision and is not part of this record: no selection, authority, flag or strategy was changed, and every optional owner is still shadow with selection `null`.

Status vocabulary: verified now, historical only, implemented but unverified, defective, unavailable external input, not applicable with reason. Nothing is averaged into a percentage of completion; the reach rates below are counts of decisions.

**Outcome.** Every phase is wired into the live decision path and reached every horse decision in its domain, except where it refused by a named reason (budget, incomplete context, a variant or state outside its declared domain). Two wiring defects were found and fixed at their owners: Phase 10, 11 and 12 receipts lost the whole private decision record when every live opponent was away, and Phase 7 refusals were named only in a telemetry counter that second looks do not write. After the fix, unexplained gaps are zero in the re-measured window.

## Defects Found And Fixed

Both are fixed at their owner in [#6493](https://github.com/Smarter-Poker/Smarter-Poker-Club-Arena/pull/6493), with direct regressions that fail on the prior source and pass on the fix.

1. **Phase 10, 11 and 12 receipts made the decision unjournalable when every live opponent was away.** `Plo4LivePolicy`, `OmahaVariantLivePolicy` and `RemainingVariantLivePolicy` derive depth from the opponents able to respond (unfolded and either present or all-in). When the only unfolded opponent had sat out mid-hand, that set was empty, the depth was `-Infinity`, and the pack correctly refused the state as `depth_or_ante_outside_pack`, but the receipt kept `depthBB: -Infinity`. The private journal refuses a number JSON cannot carry, so `journalHorseDecision` threw and the whole decision record (snapshot, read frame, every phase receipt) was lost while the horse still played. In the window: 5 of 93,835 FAST decisions (PLO4 twice, PLO5 once, Pineapple twice, all cash), which is the whole `phase15_journal_capture_unavailable` count of 5; each lost decision's execution witness and lifecycle survived and name the same refusal. The receipt now records an unavailable depth as `null`. Regressions: PLO4, every Phase 11 variant and every Phase 12 variant, through the policy and through `HorseLogic.decide` into `horseJournalJson`.
2. **Phase 7 refusals had no cause on the decision.** A tournament decision inside Phase 7's domain with no utility ledger named its refusal only through `noteFire`, which runs only on telemetry decisions. Second looks run without telemetry, so 88 DEEP refusals in the window had no recorded cause anywhere, and FAST refusals could only be reconciled in aggregate. The decision now carries `tournamentUtilityRefusal` with the same name as the telemetry suffix on every lane; the worker boundary accepts it only as a finite name, never beside a `tournamentUtility` ledger and never on the brain-exception fallback. Regressions: the incomplete-context, multi-board and work-budget refusals, a lane without telemetry, and both validator refusals.

Neither fix changes a selection, an authority, a flag or a strategy: the depth was already refused, and the Phase 7 refusal was already taken; only what the receipt records changed.
