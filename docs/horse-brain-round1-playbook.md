## What basic functionality means

A first round is a complete, bounded implementation of the phase's core job across its declared production domain. Sophistication can increase later; core functionality, correct integration and proof cannot be deferred. Phase 6 supplies the example of complete context, position/action/depth coverage and tested interpolation. Phase 7 supplies the example of action-specific calculations, reusable ownership, explicit uncertainty and final execution accounting. Equal depth means comparable completeness for each phase's job, not equal lines of code or test counts.

Each phase must satisfy all eight gates:

1. **Freeze the domain and acceptance cases.** Inventory the actual production variants, seats, cash/tournament formats, positions and position pairs, streets, action branches, stack/SPR boundaries, rake, ante/straddle conventions, pot eligibility and board modes. Give every supported coordinate an implemented policy or a specific, tested boundary fallback. A list of labels is not a certification matrix. Ordinary core cases cannot all be fallbacks.
2. **Implement the core calculation.** Demonstrate that phase-specific inputs change the intermediate calculation and resulting action or distribution in contrasting fixtures. Do not replace action utility with descriptive tags, hand quality with calibrated equity, or a future-state model with a display-only projection. Heuristic baselines are permitted when versioned and honestly labeled; claiming solver quality requires solver evidence.
3. **Integrate with the real owner.** Identify the exact caller, validated worker contract, deployment artifact and final action executor. Keep synchronous, bounded, memory-only strategy on the action clock. Keep independent or expensive oracles offline. Variant policy must feed the existing Phase 7 tournament utility owner, not skip all tournament decisions or duplicate ICM. Prove the actual worker-to-execution path with tests.
4. **Preserve safety and observability.** Validate public state, hide opponents' private cards, enforce canonical legality and eligible pots, preserve chip units, and bound work under load. Record eligible, evaluated, changed, retained, unavailable, timed-out and executed/coerced/fallback outcomes with policy version and reason. Verify rollback/disable and rejected stale or malformed inputs.
5. **Certify independently.** Compare rules and settlement against independent constructions, including pot layers and refunds rather than giving production the oracle's inputs. Exercise the phase's real critical branches and dimensional boundaries. Reuse existing independent certification where it already proves the contract; add missing tests rather than duplicate it. Missing external inputs remain explicitly incomplete for the gate they own.
6. **Make evaluation reproducible.** Freeze source, policy/runtime pack identities, benchmark population, seeds, budgets, rake and primary metrics before running. Pin offline adaptive resource governors and record effective work. Pair identical cards and seating between baseline/candidate while independently rotating every dealer-relative position. Test reproducibility under artificial load and assert position/domain coverage. Report losses and uncertainty, not only favorable seeds.
7. **Separate functionality from strength promotion.** A functional baseline is not automatically winning or solver-calibrated. Preserve predefined statistical promotion gates, held-out populations and rejected results. Never enable an unpromoted candidate by renaming it a baseline or weakening a gate. Where learning or candidate activation requires positive evidence, deploy and prove the complete shadow/control path while reporting activation as unapproved/unproven. That does not excuse missing calculations or live callers.
8. **Publish and prove.** Use reviewed local source custody, admitted protected-native checks and separately authorized publication. Record tested source, admitted artifact, successful deployment, exact served source (or verified unchanged descendant), and natural production receipts for the intended supported paths. For an offline-only component, prove the released artifact and its real consumer; do not invent live actions. Local tests, an unadmitted source packet, a health check alone, a dormant feature, or synthetic production chip transactions do not establish completion.

One phase uses one acceptance manifest, targeted checks during implementation, one final integrated gate after the final change, protected publication and focused live verification. Repeat a passed gate only after a relevant change, failure or new concern. Record every unresolved item; never write “100% complete” while a required gate is pending.

Second-audit regressions are part of this standard: carry every affected economic state across simulated hands, price calls using net eligible pots, and distinguish category-best hands from unbeatable hands across all categories. Tests must include cases where blockers eliminate a possible tie and where one hand changes the next hand's bounty. Large matrix counts do not substitute for these independent semantic checks.

## Phase 11: complete PLO5, PLO6 and PLO8 programs

**Core first round.** Separate versioned packs and explicit confidence for PLO5 High, PLO6 High and PLO8, with their actual production seat limits and exact hole-card counts. Use Phase 9's independently certified rules/features. Cover RFI, limp/check option, isolation, defense, call/overcall, three/four/five-bet, squeeze, reshove and call-off, plus checked-to, bet-facing, raise-facing and terminal decisions on every postflop street. Each variant needs its own hand-shape/range calibration or explicitly heuristic variant-specific baseline; copying PLO4/Hold'em percentile thresholds is not an implementation.

PLO8 must calculate separate high/low/combined outcomes with nut-low hierarchy, low qualification, backup low, counterfeit transitions, quartering/sixthed risk, high-only/low-only traps, scoop/freeroll logic and side pots with different eligibility. Unsupported fixed-limit behavior must remain explicitly owned by its rules/policy matrix; do not route it through pot-limit sizing.

**Required proof.** Variant × seats/position-pair × depth/SPR × action/street × rake/ante/straddle × cash/tournament coverage; independent split-pot and blocker/counterfeit fixtures; per-variant public-line-conditioned ranges; one-deck joint sampling; legal sizing and bounded latency. Each variant has its own fixed after-rake paired benchmark, full relative-position coverage, confidence report and critical bucket report. Cash and tournament worker-to-execution fixtures must show the new variant policy is evaluated and Phase 7 retains final tournament utility ownership. Release identity and natural receipts are required for each live variant path, or the unresolved path remains open.

**Later enhancement.** Larger licensed corpora, deeper search, stronger adaptation and statistically promoted strength. A missing licensed corpus does not justify omitting the honestly labeled baseline; it also must not be represented as certified solver output.

## Phase 12: Short Deck, Pineapple and all other enabled variants

**Core first round.** Freeze a registry from the production table creator and server launch validators, including rules and configuration, not names alone. Reconcile every enabled variant with its policy/certification owner. Implement variant-specific deck/ranking/equity/legality, preflop, every postflop street, tournament inputs and settlement. Short Deck must use its actual ranking, ace/straight and deck conventions. Pineapple variants need their actual discard timing, private-card selection, post-discard transitions, idempotence/recovery and canonical worker validation. Do not silently fall through to NLH when a distribution is missing.

**Required proof.** Production and certification registries match; independent deck/ranking/exact-card-use/settlement cases; all discard choices and stage boundaries, including the prior Crazy Pineapple post-discard regression; short all-ins/reopening; actual seat/depth/rake/ante configurations and cash/tournament objectives. Prove the discard path and betting path through their real executors, including reconnect/stale-state handling. Each enabled variant needs a fixed legal, conserved, bounded and position-balanced benchmark plus published source and natural path receipts. An unavailable pack requires an explicit tested conservative fallback or launch restriction with a stated reason; blanket fallback across a phase's core job does not satisfy this phase.

**Later enhancement.** Broader solver distributions and deeper variant exploitation; ordinary discard/ranking and supported-game coverage cannot wait.

## Phase 13: universal multiway, joint ranges, multiboard and bomb pots

**Core first round.** One public-line-conditioned range and confidence per opponent; joint sampling with card removal and folded/dead-card constraints; actual players behind, cover relationships and opponent-specific responses. Calculate each candidate against only its eligible main/side pots, with action-specific resulting stacks and Phase 7 tournament utility. Cover multiway value, bluff/protected check, raise-facing, overcall, squeeze, reshove and multiple-all-in decisions. Support each actual bomb-pot/multiboard launch mode with shared-prefix physical decks, board allocation, per-board distributions and covariance, including scoop/split/quarter/one-board-lock outcomes.

**Required proof.** Independent joint-deck, pot construction/refund, multiboard/high-low and chip-unit tests; contrasted fixtures where opponent count, public line, players behind, pot eligibility or board covariance changes utility; T8 two-all-in and Omaha dominated-draw regressions. The matrix spans supported variants, opponent counts, board modes, side-pot topologies and cash/tournament objectives. Fixed benchmarks must expose worst critical buckets and conservation/latency failures, not only an aggregate result. Live receipts must prove the real multiway and multiboard inputs reached the policy and final executor.

**Later enhancement.** Higher-fidelity joint distributions, deeper continuation and stronger learned responses. Independent opponents without collision correction, average-opponent bands, or one-board EV labeled multiboard are not sufficient first-round substitutes.

## Phase 14: causal self-tuning and confidence-weighted opponent models

**Core first round.** End-to-end observation → scoped model → candidate adjustment → holdout/shadow evaluation → gated activation/rollback. Partition by variant, stakes, seats, position, public action node and tournament stage; decay stale evidence, require samples, shrink to population priors and report uncertainty. Generate bounded node-specific counterfactual proposals with explicit action/EV evidence. Isolate training from holdout, control feedback/self-observation leakage, prevent duplicate updates and preserve provenance across restart. Observational leak tags remain audit signals rather than causal permission to move a dial.

**Required proof.** Sparse/noisy/stale/conflicting evidence, one-outlier/session resistance, node isolation, held-out leakage prevention, calibrated uncertainty, bounded update magnitude and reproducible proposal/rejection/reversion. Every proposal records source window, node, alternatives, counterfactual estimate, sample size, uncertainty, budget and prior/new policy versions. Prove automatic rollback on distribution shift, confidence collapse and negative benchmark movement. Publish and observe the complete shadow pipeline naturally; no live adaptive action is enabled without its predefined held-out/shadow uplift gate. If none qualifies, report that result without inventing uplift or treating disabled tuning as an implemented learning pipeline.

**Later enhancement.** Richer causal models, finer cohorts and broader learned policies. The control pipeline, confidence calculations and real shadow consumer are first-round requirements.
