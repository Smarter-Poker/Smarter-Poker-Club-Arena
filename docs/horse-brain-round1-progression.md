The user requires Phases 8, 9 and 10 to match the functional depth and proof of the completed first rounds of Phases 6 and 7 before Phase 11 begins. The parity audit found that this standard had not been met. Earlier declarations of basic functionality complete are superseded. The failed Phase 8 promotion result remains failed; no unproven candidate is authorized by this correction.

[The common first-round playbook](./horse-brain-round1-playbook.md) now defines the shared acceptance gates and phase-specific requirements. Core calculations, coverage, real callers, verification and publication cannot be deferred as later-round enhancements.
