The former coverage test listed nine owners only inside the test. Runtime dispatch separately compared variant strings, while the worker card-rule check accepted uppercase identifiers. PLO4 and Plo4 therefore reached the worker decision path without the lowercase PLO4 policy dispatch. An explicit unknown variant also completed the direct brain path using fallback card rules.

HorsePolicyRegistry now supplies the actual Phase10/11/12 dispatch owner and existing pack version. The canonical worker and direct schema-v1 brain reject unregistered, aliased or missing identifiers before sampling. Older unversioned inputs keep their documented missing-variant NLH default. All nine table-creator variants and engine-rule entries are reconciled against this runtime registry; a test-only owner map no longer proves coverage.

A compact private ownership record comes from the corresponding policy result. It distinguishes the source-bound legacy reference, an explicit disable, a computed proposal, a known domain boundary, and other unavailability. A disabled pack cannot claim a receipt; an enabled pack cannot omit its receipt or claim another version/variant/owner. The client validates the record against the returned pack and original request variant before constructing the execution witness. The witness freezes its own copy. Finite variant/outcome counters enter the existing telemetry flow. Actual joint and tournament receipts continue to own their own calculations and refusals.

## Remaining acceptance

This registry proves actual variant ownership and accurately records pack refusals. It does not certify every launch configuration, NLH reference dimension, discarded-card executor, out-of-distribution alarm, internal policy distribution, durable decision history, restart replay or fleet strength. A computed shadow proposal is not an activated policy. The legacy reference has no invented pack version; release provenance binds that source. Protected publication and natural ownership/execution receipts remain required. Full Phases14/15 and the first15-phase program remain incomplete.
