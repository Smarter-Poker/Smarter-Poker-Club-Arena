# Qualified committed observations for the adaptive model

The accepted hand transaction now carries its validated hand UUID to the worker observation contract. A new pure qualifier can distinguish a committed action from a legacy replay row, retain its original unfiltered ordinal, and assign the whole dealt session to a fixed training or holdout partition before considering the action. Receipt retries preserve the UUID and action identities; rejected or pending receipts do not enqueue observations. The existing worker consumer is unchanged.

The qualifier validates persisted public JSON, supported variant and chip units, betting rights, physical boards, positions, contribution tuples, action-time deduction rules and captured tournament stage. Missing tournament context cannot imply cash. It produces a frozen, versioned structural scope covering stakes, seats, relative positions, street and earlier public action lines, board texture, stack/price/contribution bands, wagering rights, rake/BBJ rules and public tournament stage. The scope excludes observed outcomes, raw player identities and private cards. Forced money and Pineapple discards remain original ordinal entries without becoming voluntary decisions. Unknown or malformed betting history excludes the remaining public line instead of fabricating an empty line. Human/pre-action and horse-policy observations retain distinct origin labels for the future consumer's leakage controls.

Work is one pass over at most 4,096 input records with bounded per-action public arrays. This is an offline/background whole-hand operation; it must not be placed on the synchronous decision clock. Structural bands are a declared heuristic grouping, not exact pot utility, a solver abstraction or proof of causal action value.

The focused qualifier/receipt group passed 95 tests. The test harness's initial completion/discard handling and native method-name errors are retained in local negative evidence.

Two fresh native processes replayed the same 2,220 completed offline controller hands, spanning all nine variants, cash/MTT/SNG and the actual NLH Spin/HU-SNG formats, 2/3/capped seats, every dealer seat, passive/raise/fold lines, and ordinary/requested-three-board modes. Both qualified all 41,462 captured voluntary observations, retained 1,410 private-action exclusions and produced digest `086f636b7a82d9e3c96d6f55f6b13dfc0533b09fdce92aff419d127a1fe4312f`. JSON replay matched, original inputs and controller state were unchanged, and original observation IDs were unique within each hand. Actual board counts are recorded after the controller's physical-deck downgrade. These are qualification fixtures, not a full game-launch or strength certification.

The first native run overlapped the full test suite: whole-hand qualification p99 was 6.825 ms and maximum 22.870 ms. The second process measured p99 1.071 ms and maximum 2.575 ms. These load-sensitive observations are retained; they do not establish the separate Phase 8 four-millisecond gate or justify adding this work to the decision clock.

This prerequisite does not update a model or establish durable deduplication. The Phase 14 scoped statistical model, priors/decay/uncertainty, independent held-out/shadow evaluation, counterfactual proposals, durable application, activation/rollback and natural complete-pipeline proof remain required. No learner, adaptive strategy or promotion is enabled by this change. Protected review and exact engine publication are separate pending gates.
