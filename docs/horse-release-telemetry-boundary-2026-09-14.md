# Release telemetry access repair

Production release verification reported six browser-reachable definer routines.
Five are operator diagnostics for stalled knockouts, orphaned tournaments,
silent rake rollups, stranded completion and tables unable to deal. Live catalog
inspection found no RLS policy dependency. Their two routine callers are the
service-only definer conservation sweep and incident notifier. Player access is
revoked while their bodies and service execution remain unchanged.

The sixth routine, fn_bbj_allocation_policy, supplies public allocator rates to
the existing jackpot page through bbjPoolFeed. It deliberately remains available
to authenticated players. The function now checks for an account subject or a
service request before returning the same seven rates from policy row1. It
exposes no balances or diagnostic data. No allowlist or verifier exception is
introduced, and no allocation formula or policy value changes.

The native fixture reproduces the prior access, verifies all five diagnostic
denials for both player roles, verifies service access, and compares the old and
new jackpot projection exactly for an authenticated account and service request.
A role lacking an account subject receives no policy. Direct policy-table access
remains denied. Diagnostic bodies are placeholders in this permissions fixture;
their unchanged production hashes and live caller/dependency checks are separate
evidence. This is not an end-to-end PostgREST authentication proof.

This addresses a release verification finding, not the remaining live-table
realtime failures or unfinished Phase14 causal learning pipeline.
