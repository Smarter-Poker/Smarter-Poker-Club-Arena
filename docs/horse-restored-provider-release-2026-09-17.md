## Meaning of the evidence

An observed commitment over 10BB is eligibility for review, not proof of a
poker mistake. The reviewer requires the original accepted hand, original
decision inputs and independently qualified alternative-action evidence.
Historical result tags cannot change a wager or its random sampling stream.
Missing source population, references or authority remain explicit uncertainty.

The current live baseline read on September 17 reported a ready Horse worker
with chart and legacy postflop stores, but zero certified V31 runtime entries.
That observation does not certify every game/format, GTO optimality, natural
Phase 8 timing, durable plan recovery or automatic learning activation.
Those outstanding first-round acceptance requirements remain in the
[playbook](horse-brain-round1-playbook.md) and
[first-five blueprint](horse-brain-first-five-gold-standard.md).

routes. There is no agent-managed numbered release queue. Only actual source

assigned delivery. No new development phase begins as part of publication.
