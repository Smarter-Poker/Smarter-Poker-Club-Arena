## Meaning of the evidence

An observed commitment over 10BB is eligibility for review, not proof of a
poker mistake. The reviewer requires the original accepted hand, original
decision inputs and independently qualified alternative-action evidence.
Historical result tags cannot change a wager or its random sampling stream.
