# server/src/lightning/aBrainChangeMustWinInLightning.law.test.ts

A horse brain change must keep the horses winning in Lightning. The law runs the seeded Lightning strength matrix - the real host, controller and brain, learning like the production worker - against a calling station, a nit and a tight-aggressive regular (the maniac is measured but not gated: its 250-hand sign is noise) who each meet a brand-new table every hand, in NLH and PLO5, and fails if the horses' result against any of them falls under its floor. Since 2026-10-25 each archetype is judged on the mean of three fixed 250-hand deals (the original deal first) instead of one, because one deal is a coin flip, and this file covers NLH; PLO5 is in aBrainChangeMustWinInLightningPlo5.law.test.ts.
