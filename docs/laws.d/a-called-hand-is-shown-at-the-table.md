# server/src/engine/ACalledHandIsShownAtTheTable.law.test.ts

A hand that called the river is shown at the table (Dan 2026-10-25: "show the hand at call down"). In a cash game a beaten hand may still muck, except a hand whose last river decision was a call or an all-in for no more than the bet it faced: the engine tables it win or lose, so the table, the hand history and every player and horse see it. A checked-down loser may still muck, and a call on an earlier street does not count.
