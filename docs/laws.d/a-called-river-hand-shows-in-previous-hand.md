# tests/a-called-river-hand-shows-in-previous-hand.law.test.ts

A hand that called the river and was mucked shows in Previous Hand (Dan 2026-10-25: "WHEN A PLAYER CALLS A BET ON THE RIVER AND HIS HAND IS MUCKED, USERS SHOULD STILL BE ABLE TO SEE THE HAND ... BUT ONLY IF THE HAND WAS 'CALLED' ON THE RIVER"). The newest `fn_ca_called_river_mucked_cards` returns a holding only to a reader dealt into the hand, only for a seat ruled mucked at showdown, and only when that seat's last river decision was a call or an all-in for no more than the bet it faced; checked-down and folded hands stay private and the card tables' policies are unchanged. Every surface marks it as called rather than shown: the replay row is `calledMuck`, the tracker export writes "mucks hand" and "mucked [cards]", and a shared hand link carries the cards with the muck.
