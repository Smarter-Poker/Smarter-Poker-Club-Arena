# tests/a-called-river-hand-shows-in-previous-hand.law.test.ts

A hand that called the river and was mucked shows in Previous Hand (Dan 2026-10-25: "WHEN A PLAYER CALLS A BET ON THE RIVER AND HIS HAND IS MUCKED, USERS SHOULD STILL BE ABLE TO SEE THE HAND ... BUT ONLY IF THE HAND WAS 'CALLED' ON THE RIVER"). `fn_ca_called_river_mucked_cards` returns a holding only to a reader dealt into the hand, only for a seat ruled mucked at showdown, and only when that seat's last river decision was a call; checked-down and folded hands stay private, the card tables' policies are unchanged, and the client adds the cards beside a record that still says the hand was mucked.
