# server/src/tournament/aDecidedGameIsNotWaitingBehindLiveOnes.law.test.ts

A tournament whose field is decided (one table, at most one stack with chips, read from the deciding hand or a GameServer decided recovery) is admitted by the elimination scheduler from a decided lane ahead of live work, never on the last general slot while live work waits, so a Spin or Sit & Go winner is paid in seconds instead of behind every live game.
