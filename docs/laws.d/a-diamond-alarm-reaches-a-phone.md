# tests/a-diamond-alarm-reaches-a-phone.law.test.ts

Before September 29 almost nothing that went wrong in the Diamond books reached
a phone. The Diamond incident store, ca_diamond_incidents, had no trigger, so a
critical Diamond row paged nobody. The one Diamond finding that did reach the
incident board, an unexplained movement in the player Diamond supply, was
recorded and worded in chips.

Now a critical row raises one drift incident for its rule, through the estate's
own door (fn_ca_raise_drift_incident). That incident is recorded in diamonds,
and its amount, if it has one, is the discrepancy. The existing recipient
registry, notify ledger, escalation tick and push carry it the same way they
carry a chip finding. The incident's key is diamond-rule:<rule>:<episode>.
While the rule's incident is open, every new critical row of that rule folds
into it, so a condition the health watch re-files every hour pages once. A new
episode starts when a person closes the incident, or when the rule has filed
nothing for 24 hours (the detector registry's aging floor). Its next critical
row then pages again. Warnings and info rows never fire the trigger. The law is
that a critical Diamond row produces exactly one notification to the registry
and a warning produces none. That was proved live before apply: the rows open
on September 29 (20 critical rows in one rule, DR0:health_critical, plus 7,060
warnings and 15,979 info rows) made exactly one incident and one page.

The page speaks Diamonds, not chips. A critical from a Diamond rule gets through
even when the row carries no amount, because the rule has already judged it
critical. Dan's other gates of September 6 still hold: a fix is not a page, and
anything below critical is not a page. The supply snapshot tags its incident as
Diamonds, and the eight supply incidents already on the board are now recorded
in diamonds. The engine's DiamondCustody alerts carry the Diamonds in doubt, so
an unverified custody call pages with its size instead of being withheld as 0.
The incident board shows a Diamond incident only to platform staff and to the
recipients a Diamond incident has, never to a club or union owner. Every chip
function is changed in place, with its live md5 pinned and the reverse
substitution proved. Chip findings are worded exactly as before. Nothing writes
the arena switches.
