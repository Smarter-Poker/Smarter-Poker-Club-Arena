# tests/a-frozen-engine-minute-is-not-a-stuck-minute.law.test.ts

A tournament detector that pages on "N minutes since X happened" counts live engine minutes, not wall-clock minutes: `fn_ca_engine_live_minutes(from, to)` is the wall interval minus every declared maintenance break (`engine_maintenance_break_log`, plus the break in progress, bounded to 20 minutes), merged so an overlap counts once, and undeclared downtime stays live. `fn_ca_tournament_finished_but_not_completed` pages a decided event on 15 live minutes or on max(3 x threshold, 45) wall-clock minutes whatever the breaks, so a winner is never unpaid 45 minutes without a page; `fn_ca_orphaned_running_tournaments` calls an unleased event orphaned only when no hand has been committed on its tables for the dwell in live minutes, measured to `clock_timestamp()` because the hourly sweep starts at :52 and runs into the :55 break; the absent-player and cannot-deal checks keep their wall prefilter and add the live condition. No severity, schedule or money row changes. Measured cause: 2026-10-02 21:10, ten events decided at 20:53 were paid seconds after the second of two back-to-back breaks (4.6 live of 20 wall minutes), and 20:52, two events named "nobody is dealing it" committed hands that same minute.
