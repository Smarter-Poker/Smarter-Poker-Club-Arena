# server/src/tournament/aHorseAnswersItsRebuyOfferLikeAClick.law.test.ts

A horse answers its tournament rebuy offer as soon as the zero-stack hand completes, on its own path outside the elimination scheduler and its work budget (answering only to manager stop and the maintenance freeze), so its 30 second offer is the same window a human's click gets (2026-10-24: 85 willing horses, 19 rebuys, in one freeroll). A horse's "no" (the draw declines, or a Free Buy allowance is spent) closes the offer through `fn_decline_tournament_rebuy`, the door a human's Cancel uses, so the event's live field is complete again at once rather than ICM-blind for 30 seconds (2026-10-24 horse capability audit); a refused decline leaves the offer to its own clock, and nothing is ever declined for a human.
