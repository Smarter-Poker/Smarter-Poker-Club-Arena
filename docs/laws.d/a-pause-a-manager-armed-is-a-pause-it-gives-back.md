# server/src/tournament/aPauseAManagerArmedIsAPauseItGivesBack.law.test.ts

A tournament manager may not leave a table holding a pause that no living authority will ever release. When a break releases while a co-authority (the satellite qualifier boundary's `terminalCloseoutPaused`) still holds the table, `resumeDealing()` must surrender its own `untilResumed` claim on the deferral path rather than early-returning with `pauseRequiresExplicitResume` still raised — the safety timeout deliberately refuses to lift that claim, so a claim nobody owns is permanent. The law also pins the second half of the same one-shot release: a replacement dealer armed before `resumeFromBreak`'s single sweep and published into `tableEngines` after it is still offered the release, at the park edge the engine itself publishes. Measured 2026-09-28: three cohort satellites (b165b22f, 0e1d340e, e8cc6c78) resumed cleanly and dealt nothing for 14 to 19 hours, and were settled rather than played out.
