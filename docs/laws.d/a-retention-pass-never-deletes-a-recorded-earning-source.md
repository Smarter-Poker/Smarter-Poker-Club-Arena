# tests/a-retention-pass-never-deletes-a-recorded-earning-source.law.test.ts

Hand-history retention is a storage decision about `hand_history` (CLAUDE.md 10.5, eight days since 2026-09-17); the money ledger is not pruned. `sp_prune_hand_history` used to delete `public.rake_attributions` for every hand it pruned, and from 2026-09-25 09:37 every run of `sp_prune_hand_history_10m` failed on `recorded_cash_earning_source_is_immutable` - `accounting_cash_source_immutable` correctly refuses to move an attribution once `accounting_cash_accrual_batches` holds a batch for its rake record. Twelve failures in two hours with zero successes, and retention had stopped. No foreign key ever required that delete, and it also fired `trg_ca_club_rake_daily_user_del`, which would have decremented the per-club per-user daily rake rollup - the rakeback basis - for every horse in every pruned hand. Removed by `20260925143224_four_producers_that_measure_the_wrong_thing`, whose cron succeeded at 14:33 UTC the same day.

The law pins that the removing migration is in the repo and names its cause (the trigger, why the trigger is right, the absent foreign key, the rollup trigger) and asserts its substitution rather than retyping the function; that no migration sorted at or after that version both redefines `sp_prune_hand_history` and carries a `DELETE FROM rake_attributions`; that the three deletes and the candidate conditions the pass keeps are required to survive; and that any later redefinition still reads `hand_history_retention_policy.horse_retention_days` rather than hard-coding the window, which is Dan's to change.
