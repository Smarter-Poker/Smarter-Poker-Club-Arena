# server/src/tournament/aSeatedCapacityTableIsNeverStarvedByTheSweepBudget.law.test.ts

A late-registration capacity table whose players are already seated gets its dealer on the next manager pass of any kind, even when the 5 s sweep work budget is spent (production 2026-10-26: seven tables sat seated with no dealer for up to 27 minutes because admission bailed on the spent budget); only a finished event, a stopped manager, an aborted sweep or the platform freeze may stop it.
Terminal flags are rechecked after receipt reads and acknowledgements, so an in-flight finish cannot admit a dealer or announce expansion.
