# server/src/engine/aShortAllInIsCalledByABigStack.law.test.ts

Dan 2026-10-26, verbatim: "WHEN A PLAYER SHOVES ALL IN WITH LESS THEN 3 BB A STACK OF 20 OR MORE BB SHOULD BE CALLING WITH ALMOST ANYTHING IN A TOURNAMENT." In every tournament (MTT, Sit & Go, Spin, satellite) and every variant, a horse with 20 or more big blinds (stack plus chips already in the pot) facing an all in under 3 big blinds (the shover's total, or what the horse must add) never folds preflop, whatever its style, profile or tightness; postflop it folds only a hand its own Monte Carlo measures as drawing dead (under 5% and under the price). Only a fold is converted; a raise stays a raise. `shortAllInCallFloor` (`HorseShortAllInCallFloor.ts`) is the last action rule in HorseLogic's `wager_rules` graph node, after the PLO paired-board fold and the hold'em premium rule, and again at the commit door in `ServerTableEngineTurns.scheduleHorseAction`. The PLO paired-board fold keeps priority only for a real stack-off (a quarter or more of the horse's remaining stack, or all of it). Cash games, a 19BB horse and a 3BB-or-more shove are untouched. The law replays trash hands in NLH, short deck, PLO4/5/6 and PLO8 across five seeds and five styles and checks each receipt passes the worker client's validator.
