# tests/a-table-that-never-dealt-is-moved-from-its-seated-entries.law.test.ts

A parked tournament table that never dealt a hand must still be movable: `smarter_private.f06_movement_prior` sends a park of a table with no committed hand to `smarter_private.f06_movement_never_dealt_prior`, which admits it only when no hand row of any kind or permit exists on the table and every occupied chair holds a playing, uneliminated registration on exactly that chair whose chips equal the chair stack and exactly what its single 'entry' funding receipt granted (no rebuy, re-entry or add-on), and returns the park proof shape with no hand (`atomic`/`history` null, `first_hand` true). `smarter_private.f06_assert_movement` holds such a proof to "no hand on the table" and checks every other proof with its old boundary statement unchanged.
