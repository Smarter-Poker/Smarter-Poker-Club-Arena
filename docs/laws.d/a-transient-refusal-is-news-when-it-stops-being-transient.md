# server/src/tournament/aTransientRefusalIsNewsWhenItStopsBeingTransient.law.test.ts

A finish refused by a deadlock or a lock timeout (the engine's own transient class) is alerted only when the same tournament reports that reason TRANSIENT_FINISH_REFUSAL_ALERT_STREAK times running, with the streak in the alert; a rule refusal is still news on its first report. The refusal's retry rewinds the sweep cursor to the finish stage so the winner is owed the next admission, 55P03 is transient in isTransientDbError like the deadlock beside it, and the leave_pending post-hand step - whose every throwing path is a read before any cash-out - reports its failure as a warning asserting moves_chips:false instead of a critical drift incident. Measured 2026-10-01: 342 of 342 settled tournaments with an open atomic_finish_refused critical were paid in full, 14 of 14 leave_pending_failed criticals left zero unaccounted seat exits.
