# tests/a-union-wallet-side-always-names-its-column.law.test.ts

A union_wallet side of a journal leg always names which of the wallet's balances moved: the autoledger writes the column the payer declares for its counterparty, every payer that declares a union wallet names it, the union bank is one account whoever names it, and chip_ledger refuses a bare union_wallet side except where the other side names a promo bank or the leg is a journal-only correction.
