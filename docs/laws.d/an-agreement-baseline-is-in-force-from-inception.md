# tests/an-agreement-baseline-is-in-force-from-inception.law.test.ts

An agreement baseline (the 2026-09-14 12:09:27Z snapshot that opened accounting_agreement_history) is in force from inception: when a key has no row at or before an instant and its earliest row is its baseline, that baseline answers. One function, fn_accounting_history_in_force_from_inception, holds the rule, and every producer (fn_accounting_terms_at, fn_accounting_agent_terms_at, fn_accounting_earning_contract) and every verifier (fn_accounting_union_earned_plan, fn_calculate_cash_rakeback_periods) of agreement receipts uses it, so a receipt the producer writes is never refused by the weekly close. A key first seen by INSERT or UPDATE never reaches back. The fixture preimages are byte-identical to production and each edit must reproduce the asserted postimage md5.
