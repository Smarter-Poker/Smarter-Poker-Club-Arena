# tests/an-incident-is-born-with-the-repair-status-it-will-get.law.test.ts

A drift incident is born `auto_repair_status = 'manual_needed'` (20260922141524), because nothing on this platform repairs an incident automatically. Before it, every incident was born `'pending'` and the every-minute `ca-auto-reconcile-tick` existed to rewrite that claim ten minutes later (139 rewrites in 7 days) and to call two rake/BBJ re-drives every minute an incident stayed open (7,774 pairs in 7 days, zero chips moved in 13). Pins the default, the migration's both-direction live proof (both writers inherit the default; NOT NULL and the CHECK were not loosened), and forbids any later migration from making an incident claim a pending or running repair again: no such default, no such write, no DROP DEFAULT. The detector is exercised against the shapes it refuses and allows.
