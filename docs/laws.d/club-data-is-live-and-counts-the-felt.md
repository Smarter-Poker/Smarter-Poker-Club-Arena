Club Data is real time (Dan 2026-10-14): cash rake and cash buy-ins/cash-outs are written to club_table_daily by the rake and wallet insert triggers in the same transaction as the money, the hourly job never rewrites the live UTC day, and ca_club_data_snapshot adds every seated club player's live stack to winnings so chips on the felt never read as a loss; the everyday screen at clubs/:clubId/data re-reads every 15 seconds and never paints zeros for a failed read. Since 2026-10-17 (20261017161545) the club is read from the money records, never guessed: cash rake from rake_attributions at the moment it is attributed, a tournament fee from accounting_tournament_fee_sources, a cash buy-in or cash-out from its chip_ledger leg at that table, and a tournament result from the club the entry was paid from - never a player's newest seat at another table and never their first membership.
