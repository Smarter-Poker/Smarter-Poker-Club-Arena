# tests/diamond-spins-never-start-themselves.law.test.tsx

Dan, 2026-09-21 (R1): "Games can NEVER auto start ... The only 'auto play' is when the user chooses to spin 5/10/25 at a time. Nothing ever auto-plays just because they are in the game lobby." Four clocks used to advance Diamond Spins with no tap: a 30-second idle countdown that pressed Spin, a win reveal that dismissed itself into a won bonus game or upgrade, an effect that opened an unfinished award's game page on load, and an upgrade ring that spun the moment its reveal closed. The law renders the real wheel page and reveal flow under fake timers, lets two minutes pass at each of those points, and asserts no spin was requested, no game was opened and no ring turned: a won game waits on its reveal for Play Game, an unfinished award waits in a visible card for Play Game, the upgrade ring waits under "Swipe Or Tap The Wheel To Spin" for a swipe, a tap, Enter or Space, and a run found open at the server waits for Resume Run. A Diamonds spin is held to the same rule (R15): its reveal waits for Pick A Card, the three cards that open then wait for a tap on one of them, and two minutes at either point turn nothing over. Source pins keep the idle hook deleted and every effect on the page free of navigation.
