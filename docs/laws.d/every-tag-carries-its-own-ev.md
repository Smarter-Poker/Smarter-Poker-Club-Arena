# server/src/services/EveryTagCarriesItsOwnEv.law.test.ts

`horse_review_rollup.leak_net_bb` carries the per-tag net beside `leak_counts`, so `fn_horse_tag_ev` can rank every tagged situation by EV across BOTH outcomes instead of by its loss total - which ranked situations by how often they occur in big pots and made `river_aggr_lost` (-778,000bb) look like the fleet's worst leak while river aggression makes +12.66 bb/hand over 75,811 hands. Both mirror naming shapes must pair (`X`/`X_won` and `X_lost`/`X_won`), and mirror status comes from the fold family named in `HorseHandReview` (`big_bet_fold`, `big_fold_river`, `big_fold_early`, `bet_fold_line` - a folded hand never wins) rather than from whether a `_won` key happened to appear this week, which hid the two worst situations in the fleet. It also cut `fn_audit_river_aggression_ev` from 5,868 ms to 107 ms, which was 86% of the nightly audit against a 15s client abort.
