# tests/horse-capture-drains-in-order.law.test.ts

The horse observation capture queue drains in order and reads only what fits (20261025042223): claims take the oldest request first (created_at, request_key); a slice ends at the 512-hand boundary that the snapshot's own overflow precheck returns, so a success is never followed by a refine; a timeout (source_unavailable) is refined narrower at once, like source_budget_exceeded; each new discovery epoch resumes after the previous epoch's end instead of jumping to the newest boundary, with no backfill job; and the engine loop runs capture slices back to back while the journal has no work of its own. Before this, on 2026-10-24, about 60% of the observations were lost: 256 of 256 slots were occupied, 161 of them part-done, and every epoch skipped two windows.
