# server/src/engine/HorseMadeHandBeatsClassMean.law.test.ts

Dan, 2026-10-26: "I JUST WATCHED A HORSE RAISE AJc ... FLIP THE NUT FLUSH AND CHECK IT EVERY SINGLE STREET INSTEAD OF EVER BETTING FOR VALUE". The compact solver open cells hold the mean of a hand class over every board in a texture class, so they cannot see that a particular A-J suited holds the ace of the board's suit. When the horse holds a set, trips, a straight, a flush or better on the board, the class-mean cell does not answer and the value ladder bets it; every other holding still reads the cell exactly.
