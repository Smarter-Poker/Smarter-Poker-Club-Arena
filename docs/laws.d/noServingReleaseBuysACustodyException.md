# tests/noServingReleaseBuysACustodyException.law.test.ts

The restart certificate in `server/scripts/engine-release-transaction.sh` (`maintenance_certificate`) decides whether a cutover may proceed past a shut `readyForRestart`. On 2026-09-25 (#5266) it gained a one-shot exception admitting the raw `stopped_bank_custody_unconfirmed` reason when the serving release was exactly `778075b4`; it fired, production moved on, and a rollback to that SHA would have re-armed a custody reason in the allow-list, breaking the binding rule that no bank or custody reason may enter it. This pins, by reading the certificate's own source and by driving it with every stopped-bank reason from a list of serving identities, that the certificate never reads the serving release identity, contains no 40-hex SHA literal, admits only the two F06 preparation reasons, and refuses every bank or custody reason from every serving release; and it proves the detector fires on a copy of the certificate with the 778075b4 exception re-inserted.
