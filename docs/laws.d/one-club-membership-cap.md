# tests/one-club-membership-cap.law.test.ts

The club membership cap was a number typed into five SQL functions and two client files, and production migration 20260908125235 raised it from 4 to 10 in the four that enforce it but not in fn_get_club_creation_eligibility, so a player in 4 to 9 clubs was told they could not create a club the server would have created; only the create path locked the player, so two joins at 9 could both land. 20260922153234 makes the cap one authority (fn_club_membership_cap, fn_club_membership_count, fn_club_membership_lock, fn_club_creation_open) and patches the four enforcing functions onto it by exact substitution against byte-exact production preimages. This law pins that the cap is 10 and stated once, that every substitution matches its preimage exactly once and yields the md5 the migration pins, that no src file restates a club membership cap, that no buy grid offers the 'club_creation' purchase nothing reads, and that the client's reading of the server's cap refusal works for any cap. scripts/ci/test-club-membership-cap.py proves the behaviour, including the concurrency, in PostgreSQL.
