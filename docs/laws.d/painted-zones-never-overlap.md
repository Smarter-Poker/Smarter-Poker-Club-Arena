# tests/painted-zones-never-overlap.law.test.ts

Every rectangle a console head prints at the same time as another is checked
for intersection, for all three approved families and all eight shapes of head
content, through the very selector the component renders from. The shark's
title band (y 76-142) and subtitle band (y 126-148) overlapped by 16 rows on a
154-row head, and the riveted pair by 4 rows; both were invisible only because
no live caller passed a subtitle. It also fails when a zone runs off its own
slice, and when the component stops choosing its zones through the selector.
