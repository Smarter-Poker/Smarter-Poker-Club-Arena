# server/src/engine/HorsePloPairedBoardFold.law.test.ts

Dan 2026-10-25, on a PLO5 1/2 Madness horse that called a turn shove with two pair after the Jack paired the board: "HE CALLED THE ALL IN ON THE TURN WHEN THE JACK PAIRED THE BOARD..." and "ITS A FOLD 100% OF THE TIME"; earlier the same day for PLO6, "IN PLO6 IF ITS NOT THE NUTS, ITS PROBABLY NOT GOOD". In PLO5 and PLO6 a horse without a full house or better never calls or raises a committing bet (bettor all in, a quarter or more of the horse's stack, or a call that leaves it all in) on a paired turn or river; it folds, keeping its own think time. Applied in `HorseLogic.decide` after every other layer (`HorsePloPairedBoardFold.ts`), so no equity cap can reopen the call. Token calls needing under 15% equity, hi-lo and multi-board pots are left to the brain. The recorded hand is replayed across seeds and every style.
