# tests/round-three-is-read-once-and-paid-in-batches.law.test.ts

Round three is read once and paid in batches (2026-10-03): in a chunked weekly close, a book whose round 3 has no receipt proves its week's round-3 sources in two-hour windows (fn_settle_accounting_rakeback_window: per (club, player) the count, the non-house count and two 60-bit sums of md5(source type|id|rake credit|rake record|agent)) and reads its admitted periods' latest certificates once (fn_settle_accounting_rakeback_plan: the stage's counts, sums, element-only tests and the same md5 sums of each allocation with the certificate's payer), in attempts of their own; the paying attempt's stage still locks the periods and runs every certificate test live and uses that read (fn_settle_accounting_rakeback_plan_get) only when every locked (period, certificate, club, player) is in it, a disagreeing md5 sum being the stage's any-bad flag; the payout loop pays in the original order until 90 s after it began and commits (batch_committed), a period an earlier batch paid being recognised only by its status, its single payout of the owed amount and its unique round3-period:v3 chip_ledger row with this scope, certificate, payout and amount; each batch locks and funding-tests every unpaid period's wallets and checks each locked wallet against what it paid; the last batch writes the receipt the single loop writes. Outside a chunked close nothing changes.
