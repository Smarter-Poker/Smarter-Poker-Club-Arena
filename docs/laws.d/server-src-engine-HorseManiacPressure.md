# server/src/engine/HorseManiacPressure.law.test.ts

Horses read a maniac in real time, call it off by arithmetic, and never raise a hand they will fold to it (Dan, 2026-10-22: "IF A PLAYER IS MOVING ALL IN ON EVERY HAND THE HORSES NEED TO RECOGNIZE THIS IN REAL TIME ... CALL OFF LIGHTER AND NOT RAISE A HAND WITH THE INTENTION OF FOLDING. SAME GOES IN PLO ... FOR ALL GAMES, LEVELS AND STAKES"). Real HandController hands, five live horses against a seat that shoves every hand or pots every street: the read must be live within eight hands; heads-up against a shove no horse may fold a hand holding more than the price plus six points of equity against the shover's real holding; fewer than 5% of the hands a horse raises against a shover, and fewer than 10% against a potter, may end with that horse folding to it, with at most three preflop raise-then-fold-preflop hands in a run; and heads-up postflop against the potter no fold may hold more than the price plus twelve points of equity, in hold'em and in PLO4. A red law means a horse is again folding to a maniac's bet as if it carried information, or opening hands it gives up to the re-raise.
