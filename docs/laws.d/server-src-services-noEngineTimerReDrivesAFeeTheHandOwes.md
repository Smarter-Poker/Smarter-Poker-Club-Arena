# server/src/services/noEngineTimerReDrivesAFeeTheHandOwes.law.test.ts

No engine timer re-drives, re-queues or back-fills a fee or ledger row that the accepted hand already owes. The rake and the BBJ drop of an accepted hand are carried by its post-commit envelope, which `fn_ca_commit_hand_settlement` (the only hand door the engine can reach) refuses to commit without, and are banked in one transaction by `fn_ca_process_hand_post_commit_obligations`; a bomb-pot hand cannot commit without its award units (`zz_ca_bomb_hand_keeps_its_award_units`). So the fee cycle's hourly `requeueUnbankedCashRake` (it filed equal-split claims after ten minutes and, against a merely late envelope, permanently erased the hand's weighted rake attribution through `atomic_distribute_rake`'s first-write rule), the 30-minute `fn_requeue_unbanked_fees` pass, the queue drain's rake and BBJ-drop re-drive, the hourly BBJ self-heal and the hourly bomb-pot award-unit backfill are retired (2026-09-22). The law pins the two source guarantees in the migrations in force, the engine envelope's rake and BBJ slots, that no engine runtime file calls a repair-, backfill-, re-drive-, heal-, catch-up- or re-queue-shaped RPC beyond a named debt list that may only shrink, that the queue drain reads `bbj_payout` claims only, and that the fee cycle keeps its six read-only audits.
