# server/src/tournament/aNeverStartedPermitDoesNotHoldTheBank.law.test.ts

A terminal tournament engine attests, by id and hand number, the one F06 hand permit it reserved above its stopped custody and never started, and the process-root park closes that permit `never_started` with the ordinary receipt in its own transaction; a permit whose `start` ran is never attested, and the park keeps refusing over it. On 2026-09-26 at 15:07Z six tournaments lost their lease holding one such reserved permit each; `fn_park_stopped_time_bank_custody` counted it as a hand after the custody, every stop failed "retained time-bank custody" (about 16,400 retries per manager), `stopped_bank_custody_stuck` held the restart certificate shut at 26 hourly countdowns, and no engine release was admitted for 24 hours, because the ordinary cancellation door needs the live lease the engine had just lost and `start` is a fence the engine holds locally. The law pins that the attestation is sent only by the process-root park and only for a permit in phase `reserved`, `unknown` or `terminated` of this engine's own table, tournament and lease generation above the custody's hand number; that the database releases only a `reserved` permit with no evidence whose generation no longer holds the lease within `fn_engine_lease_stale_seconds()` and only without a durable start witness (`f06_hand_dispatch`, `hand_atomic_commits`, `hand_history`, an incomplete `hand_state_snapshots` row, `hand_private_state`, `table_hole_cards`), recording the `f06_prepared_hand_cancellations` receipt; that the engine treats its preparation as resolved only when the database names that permit back; and that #5409's exemption of a reserved permit of the caller's own generation, every earlier refusal, the lock wait and the service-actor ACL stay verbatim (migration 20260927153827).
