# server/src/tournament/aTerminalBankIsNotManagerAuthority.law.test.ts

A terminal tournament engine's frozen stopped time-bank custody is written and read as the process, never under its manager's lease generation: on 2026-09-26 every such write and every retirement evidence read of a manager whose lease had lapsed was fenced by `fn_smarter_data_api_pre_request` (698 `TOURNAMENT_MANAGER_FENCED` park writes, 134,904 "retained time-bank custody" stop failures and 100,607 `f06_mixed_bank_evidence_unavailable` in sixteen minutes), managers quarantined, tables stalled and the restart certificate stayed shut until a manual restart. The law pins that the custody write goes through the root-bound `parkStoppedTimeBankCustody` to `fn_park_stopped_time_bank_custody` (service actor only; refuses by name over a later hand, a newer park, an adopted or open F06 mixed transfer; never an `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`), is acknowledged only on a confirmed write, never falls through to the null-snapshot upsert after a refused or unknown answer, is attempted by the manager's stop before it reads `hasUnretiredStoppedTimeBankCustody()`, and that `captureMixedF06Custody` reads `engine_presence_parked` only through the root-bound `readMixedF06PresenceEvidence` while the caller's own authority is restored afterwards (migration 20260926090846).
