# tests/a-closed-target-pays-its-satellite-tickets-in-cash.law.test.ts

A direct satellite ticket whose target finalizes, finishes or is cancelled is paid to its holder in cash in that same transaction, once, from its own escrow, through fn_ca_pay_closed_target_ticket_in_cash; the ticket guard opens only for that door, a ticket never stops a target from closing, and horses are paid exactly as humans.
