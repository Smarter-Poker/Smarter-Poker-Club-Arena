# tests/a-dropped-measurement-is-not-a-money-break.law.test.ts

A dropped measurement is not a money break (2026-10-04): the Diamond trial balance has three outcomes, not two, and each has its own name and its own reader. A money break stays critical with every bit of its force, DR11:trial*balance_break over 1,000 by ruling 13 and a critical health area filing DR0:health_critical on the first tick, paged by the ca_diamond_incident_pages trigger. A clean hour is DR11:trial_balance_summary at info carrying reading_complete true. An hour nobody could measure is its own named outcome and never a money claim: fn_ca_diamond_trial_balance_watch files DR14:trial_balance_unreadable at warning when its pass could not complete (reason read_failed*<SQLSTATE>, with the message) or compared no account (reason no_accounts_reported), where before it raised a warning into the Postgres log and filed nothing; fn_ca_diamond_health gains a fifteenth area, trial balance reading, which reads the age of the hourly summary series and returns unknown, never critical, once the newest reading is older than 95 minutes, a threshold measured from 718 consecutive gaps of which 715 are at most 65 minutes, 3 are one missed tick and none has ever exceeded 2h00m01s; and fn_ca_diamond_health_watch files that un-paged outcome as DR0:health_unknown rather than under the critical's name, escalating to DR0:health_critical at critical only when the next hourly reading still cannot tell, with its resolver reading both names so an unknown row cannot outlive its cause. The migration edits only md5-pinned live function texts by exact substitution, rewrites no settled record, and installs no cron, sweep, backfill, reconciler or healer.
