# tests/a-scheduled-restart-is-not-a-failure.law.test.ts

A scheduled restart is not a failure (Realtime Phase 4, 2026-09-05): the engine restarts inside an announced break at :55 of every hour and is away for two to three minutes, and until this phase nothing on the transport knew it - the maintenance frame went past `EngineStateClient` to `TablePage`, which drew a countdown while the ladder underneath doubled to maxRetries at about three minutes, announced 'failed', and let TablePage's twenty-second failsafe reload the page under a seated player who had just been promised their seat would survive, having first sent every connected browser at GoTrue to ask about a silence the server had already explained. The engine now puts `restart_in_ms` and `resume_expected_at` on that same frame, derived from the pinned break constants rather than guessed; the client latches the window plus a grace that expires, so one announcement cannot disable the failsafe forever; inside the window the ladder never reaches 'failed', polls at a flat cadence instead of doubling so a table that could return at :58:02 does not wait until :58:30, and does not ask GoTrue; a missing table still wins over the window; TablePage independently refuses to reload during a break, because a browser that loaded mid-outage never received the frame and only the database-backed break can reach it; and an engine that does not send the field leaves the ladder exactly as it was.
