# tests/law/DiamondChallengesAreClaimedAndExpireForEveryone.law.test.ts

A completed daily challenge is claimed for every player - a horse's by the engine, its input device, inside the transaction that completed it and through the same claim body a human's click reaches - and expires seven days after completion for everyone, refused by the clock in both claim paths and stamped by a scheduled product rule that pays nothing. A horse is never a certification fixture: the predicate excludes horses by asking the profile, and mis-tagged certification rows are deactivated. Nothing caps what a challenge is assigned; the per-user daily cap and the monthly line govern the claim, and the line carries a measurement written beside it (docs/DIAMOND-RULINGS.md ruling 3 as amended, 17, 18; CLAUDE.md 10.5, 10.12, 10.84).
