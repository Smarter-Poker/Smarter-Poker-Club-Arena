# tests/law/DiamondTransfersGoThroughOneDoorAndAGiftIsOneTransaction.law.test.ts

A player-to-player diamond transfer goes through exactly one door: `send_wallet_diamond_transfer`, defined by 20260909200327, SECURITY DEFINER with a pinned search path, granted to `authenticated` and to nobody else, writing an append-only `diamond_wallet_transfers` row that names both sides and both journal legs, readable only by the two participants. The retired doors stay retired: the legacy unjournaled `transfer_diamonds_deduct`/`transfer_diamonds_credit` pair is dropped, and `fn_arena_deposit`/`fn_arena_withdraw` raise rather than move money, because custody replaced them. The stream gift remains the one social transfer: it moves both legs, the gift row and both journal rows in a single transaction, refuses a replay by the keys it actually writes, classifies both legs as transferred, locks the two profiles in uuid order, never moves purchased diamonds (the refund sub-ledger's collateral), and is authorised by the privileged-column guard. The anti-farming ladder applies to every sender: no hard-coded exempt account, no age or purchase waiver, one policy under one name, and no lift date promised (docs/DIAMOND-RULINGS.md 1, 4, 12; CLAUDE.md 10.12, 10.86).

Dan amended ruling 4 on 2026-09-08 to allow transfers through one atomic, authenticated, journaled path, 20260909200327 built it, and the law had contradicted both the ruling and Phase 4 of the build programme ever since.
