# tests/promo-is-disbursed-by-the-owner.law.test.ts

Promo is disbursed by the owner: fn_promo_disburse is the one door (union owner to a member club or to a player in one; a club owner to a player in that club), every leg declares its counterparty, and an op key replays instead of paying twice. Amended by Dan's ruling of 2026-09-05 (docs/LAWS.md, Resolved conflicts): to a CLUB it lands in the club's Promo Wallet (clubs.promo_balance), never the Club Bank; to a PLAYER it lands as ordinary cashable chips, never a playthrough lock; a club inside a union spends its own promo wallet. The union-funded leaderboard declares its counterparty like the club-funded one, and wallet_transactions knows the word leaderboard_payout without which every leaderboard round with a winner aborted
