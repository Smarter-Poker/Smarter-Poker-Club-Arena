# tests/the-arena-is-one-club-and-it-mints-nothing.law.test.ts

Phase 5 of the diamond roadmap. Creating a club forced a 100,000 CHIP opening
grant on every non-union club regardless of asset, so the Diamond Arena would
have minted chips inside a diamond club; there is exactly one platform club, by
a partial unique index; an arena deposit and withdrawal are transfers, not a
burn and a mint, so the register no longer destroys money parked on the felt;
the diamond identity counts arena wallets and the chip supply snapshot excludes
the platform club; and the house-board rule is derived from is_platform so a
horse may play in the arena (10.5) while an ordinary club still refuses one.
