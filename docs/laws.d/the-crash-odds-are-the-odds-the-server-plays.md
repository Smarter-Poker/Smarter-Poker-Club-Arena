# tests/the-crash-odds-are-the-odds-the-server-plays.law.test.ts

Every Diamond Crash figure a player reads is computed from the rule the server plays, and a live round is sealed in the database (fairness audit 2026-09-22: the game recomputed 16 of 16 production rounds, and the lobby was still printing "Instant Crash 1 In 5" from before migration 20260919034436 made every round fund a guaranteed minimum out of its own odds). The crash point is X = L + (0.8 - L) / u with L the round's minimum over its stake, so the instant crash is 1 - (0.8 - L) / (1.01 - L): 1 in 4.2 to 4.3 on an ordinary award, 1 in 2.4 on a Super one, and the lobby derives both from fn_diamond_bonus_minimum's mirror across every stake an award can be played at (`awardInstantCrashChances`, `oneInRangeLabel`) instead of typing a figure. "Up To" is the largest multiplier the server quotes on a playable bet right now, never `config.max_multiplier_cents`, and a figure with nothing behind it is not shown at all. `verifyCrashRound` and `crashPointCentsFromRoll` take the round's bet and minimum as required inputs, and every caller passes both, because the old defaults (bet 1, minimum 0) recomputed the retired no-minimum game and disagreed with four of six award rounds. Migration 20260922173914 gives crash_rounds and diamond_game_commits the lock Donkey Cross rounds have had since 20260914102113: a round still open may move only the settlement columns fn_crash_decide writes and only to cashed or crashed, a ticket is spent once (consumed_by NULL to its round) and deleted only by the expired-unused sweep, the app.ledger_maintenance escape and the minimum rule are unchanged, and `scripts/dev/test-accounting-delivery.sh` runs the real settlement and award-start paths plus `tests/sql/diamond-crash-round-is-sealed.sql` on top of it. Migration 20260922173916 makes fn_diamond_game_quote_max price the one setting fn_choice_mode names, so the owner console stops quoting retired mines counts and road ladders.
