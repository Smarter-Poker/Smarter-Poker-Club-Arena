# tests/the-daily-free-spin-leaves-the-building.law.test.ts

The daily free spin that the one-time welcome spin replaced (Dan 2026-09-10) is retired whole rather than left standing beside its replacement, because a half-retired feature is what the 2026-09-11 audit kept finding: free_spin_daily_budget_diamonds was read by nothing, sat at 2000 on both hosts and could still be typed into by an operator; free_spin_enabled had quietly become the WELCOME spin's switch while keeping the retired feature's name; fn_wheel_free_history and fn_wheel_free_spin_result still answered over tables nothing writes; and the DiamondWheel component still carried a `free` prop that re-inked the rim by amount rather than by kind, a rule written for a five-prize all-diamond table that the welcome spin does not draw, so it was about to paint the real wheel by the wrong scheme. So the dead column is dropped and no later migration adds it back, the switch is renamed to welcome_spin_enabled, the three live doors are renamed and the two dead ones dropped outright with the new names revoked from PUBLIC and anon and the internal window helper revoked from authenticated too, wheel_free_spins and wheel_free_segments are registered as deprecated in both the database and the source-scanning gate so a future read fails the build, the client stops naming any of it, and welcome_budget_period_days arrives with its own non-negative floor and a door that refuses a negative in words before the constraint has to.
