the mini has existed, and reports NULL rather than zero when a reserve is not

on a club, and both refuse a club inside a union, so the larger pool on this
platform had neither; the union's own pair authorizes with fn_is_union_operator,
resolves its pool on the predicate that is actually unique, derives the same
floor bound, and has words for every reason it can refuse.
