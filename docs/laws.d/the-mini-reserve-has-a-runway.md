# tests/the-mini-reserve-has-a-runway.law.test.ts

The mini's runway measures income and spend over ONE window, never longer than
the mini has existed, and reports NULL rather than zero when a reserve is not
draining. The per-pool floor is a control whose lower bound is derived (one
payout at the largest enabled tier), authorizing before it explains. The mini
has its own players-dealt floor and its own near-miss record. The floor a NEW
pool starts with is derived from the largest enabled tier rather than typed as
a literal, and moves no floor a club has already set.
