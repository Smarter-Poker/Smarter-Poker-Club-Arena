# tests/the-open-week-rake-basis-is-refreshed-two-hours-at-a-time.law.test.ts

The open-week rake basis is refreshed two hours at a time (2026-10-03): fn_union_rake_basis_refresh (job 123, union-integrity-sweep) takes its basis detail from fn_union_rake_basis_windowed, which proves each two-hour window of the open week with fn_accounting_union_earned_plan_window, keeps each closed window (its end at or below the settler's accrual cursor) in union_rake_basis_windows with the input stamp it was proved under (bank rows, cash bank receipts, fee recognitions, recognized fee sources, accrual batches, agreement history observed before the window's end, clubs scope digest), reuses it while that stamp is unchanged, starts no closed window 120 s and no tail window 180 s into the statement (answering windows_in_progress, the snapshot keeping its previous refresh), and combines the windows exactly as fn_accounting_union_earned_plan_combine; an unproved window, a failed conservation test or a final settlement witness sends the refresh to fn_union_club_rake_basis as before. Its whole-week input stamp counts accrual batches and recognized fee sources instead of reading accounting_payable_earning_sources.
