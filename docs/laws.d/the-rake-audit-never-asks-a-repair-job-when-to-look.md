# tests/the-rake-audit-never-asks-a-repair-job-when-to-look.law.test.ts

`fn_rake_bbj_invariants`, the money alarm behind `rake-bbj-invariant-audit-hourly`, takes the grace for I5 (BBJ drop not banked to its pool) and I7 (raked hand never banked) from the writer's own five minutes, never from when a repair job last ran. The old lookup read `cron.job_run_details`; a retired job answered NULL, the fallback was `now() - 2 hours`, and at `p_hours = 2` the window was empty, so I5 read zero by construction once `ca-bbj-repair-unbanked-15m` was retired, and retiring `rake-repair-unbanked-hourly` would have blinded I7 the same way. The rake of an accepted hand is owed by its post-commit envelope and banked in one transaction by `fn_ca_process_hand_post_commit_obligations`: I7 counts a raked hand with no rake record and no pending envelope (lost), and I9 counts an envelope that still owes rake or a BBJ drop past the grace, with no lower time bound (late, not lost).
