# tests/the-register-is-a-supply-ledger.law.test.ts

The Mint register's per-wallet `SUM(mint) - SUM(burn)` is issuance attributed to that wallet, not its balance: the pre-2026-09-03 diamond supply was acknowledged as one `circulation` row and deliberately never attributed per holder (DIAMOND-ACCOUNTING-STANDARD lane B, "backfill NOTHING"), so 1,068 wallets read short by exactly their opening stock and all of them lean the same way. The law pins the two reporting functions added by `20260930164146` as read-only with three verdicts (`opening_stock_only` is COULD NOT TELL, never "balanced"), forbids a later migration from re-attributing the baseline per holder or turning the report into a sweep or back-pay job, requires the balance chain to be read by value rather than by the last `created_at` (dozens of rows share one), and keeps the Diamond Arena statement's `sessions === 0` branch so a player who has never played is never told every session reconciles.
