# server/src/services/TheTunerDoesNotFightTheFloor.law.test.ts

A table with a VPIP floor (`nit_game` + `maintain_percent_min`) stands a seat up after ten hands under the floor, horses included, so a horse there is REQUIRED to play 40-70% of hands. The floor travels from `ServerTableEngineSettlement` through `logHandHistory` into `HorseHandReview`, which keys the `horse_daily_play` row by `floored` so required-loose play is accumulated apart from free play; `HorseSelfTuner.loadPlayRows` and `fn_horse_frequency_leaks` read `floored = false` only, and the `BENCH` bands do not move. On 2026-09-05, 216 of 383 tuned horses were tightened for "too loose" (104/429 on 09-03, 180/386 on 09-04) because 15 of 72 cash tables were floored at a mean 49.3% and `tightness` is a global dial. `fn_audit_tuner_health` is the production twin (critical over 40% tightened in a night); `fn_audit_frequency_leaks` reports the floored share so the exclusion stays visible.
