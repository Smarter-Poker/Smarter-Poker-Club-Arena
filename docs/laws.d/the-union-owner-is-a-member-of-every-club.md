# tests/the-union-owner-is-a-member-of-every-club.db.law.test.ts

The union owner is a member of every club in the union (Dan, 2026-10-12): every union_clubs INSERT or reassigning UPDATE makes the union's owner an active member of the club in the same transaction (trg_union_owner_joins_every_club; a new row is an ordinary zero-chip player row, a pending, inactive or departed row is activated, an active row is left alone), a change of unions.owner_id adds the new owner to every club (trg_union_new_owner_joins_every_club), and while the club is in the union the owner's membership cannot be removed, banned, suspended, left, deactivated, moved or deleted (trg_union_owner_membership_is_kept, UNION_OWNER_MEMBERSHIP_REQUIRED). A union owner banned or suspended in the club refuses the join (UNION_OWNER_BANNED_IN_CLUB, UNION_OWNER_SUSPENDED_IN_CLUB) instead of being reinstated. The source gate admits 'union_owner_membership' and the 10-club cap lets only that source past (migration 20261012112707; qualified by scripts/ci/test-union-owner-membership-db-postgres.py in accounting_postgres shard 2). The client words each refusal for the club owner.
