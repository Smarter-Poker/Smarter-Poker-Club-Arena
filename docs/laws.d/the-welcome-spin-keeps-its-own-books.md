All paid for by the promo wallet") is the real wheel at the real price, once per member per host ever, and it keeps books of its own so that a spin with no intake can never flatter or drain the paid wheel it now shares a table with: it SAYS it is one (fn_wheel_spin_result carries free, so the page and the history label it and a spent spin is not offered again), its payout is charged to welcome_chips_paid against the host's declared welcome budget and never to chips_paid, it adds nothing to the intake, it neither spends nor is gated on the paid wheel's diamond float, the realised-return windows exclude it the way they exclude fixtures, and it is counted in no rate kept over paid spins; once is a partial unique index on (host_id, user_id) rather than a check two requests can both pass; an unfunded budget offers no spin and an owner may not welcome itself; and the flag that makes a spin free is an argument of fn_wheel_spin_core, which no browser role may execute, reached only through two doors that each ask auth.uid() before they delegate.
