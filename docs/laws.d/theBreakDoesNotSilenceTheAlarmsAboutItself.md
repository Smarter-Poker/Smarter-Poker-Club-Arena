# tests/theBreakDoesNotSilenceTheAlarmsAboutItself.law.test.ts

Fleet alerts carry the maintenance-break guard (CLAUDE.md section 13 rule 6), which mutes from the :53 announcement to six minutes after the :00 thaw and resets every pending `for`. Measured over the 24 hours to 2026-09-26 04:10 UTC the guard muted 39.5% of the day (48 breaks, median unmuted gap 29.8 minutes, longest 46.9; an ordinary hour leaves 47). That muted the alarms about the break itself (`EngineCannotBeReplaced`, `EngineReplacementWatchdogBlind`) and made `for: 1h`/`for: 2h` guarded alarms (`TournamentRunningWithNoOwnerCritical`, `EngineSheddingPrecisionForHours`) structurally unable to fire on any day. This pins that no release-route alarm carries the guard; that every guarded alarm's `for` is at most 40 minutes, inside the ordinary gap, with longer persistence measured inside the expression over unmuted minutes; that `PlatformFrozenTooOften` reads the flag's 3-hour share unguarded at the measured 0.30; that the promtool timing cases in `tests/monitoring/break-does-not-silence-its-own-alarms.test.yml` run in CI; and, as a negative proof, that the checker flags each forbidden shape.
