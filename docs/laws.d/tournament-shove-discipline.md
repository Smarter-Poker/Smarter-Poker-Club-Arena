# server/src/engine/HorseTournamentShoveDiscipline.law.test.ts

Dan, 2026-10-25: "BIGGER STACKS SHOULD NOT BE OPEN RIPPING 35+ BIGS UTG OR PERIOD!" A tournament horse shoves preflop only where shoving is the real strategy: at most 15bb effective first in, 25bb over one raise, 40bb over a 3-bet. Deeper, the shove becomes a standard sized raise (kept as a shove only when that raise would already commit 40% of the effective stack), and a stack that covers every live opponent raises to exactly what the biggest of them holds instead of moving its whole stack in. Applied as a wager rule at the end of the decision and again at commit, so it holds whichever policy owner proposed the shove. Cash, pot-limit, fixed-limit, all-in-or-fold tables and call-offs are untouched.
