# tests/vip-is-not-a-ladder.law.test.ts

VIP is `is_vip` + `vip_tier` ('lifetime' | 'monthly') + `vip_expires_at`, resolved only by `src/utils/vipStatus.ts`. There are no bronze/silver/gold/platinum/diamond tiers, and `profiles.tier` ('Newcomer' on all 1,310 rows) may never gate an entitlement, badge or avatar ring. There is no tier ladder at all: `src/constants/vipTiers.ts` (bronze/silver/gold/platinum/diamond/royal, with rakeback percentages, point multipliers and tournament tickets) and its three renderers were deleted 2026-09-05 because nothing implemented any rung, and a member with 426,490 points was being told they were Royal. A VIP gets exactly what the server meters: 100 rabbit hunts, 120 time-bank seconds, 1,200 emojis, 1,000 tags, and three per-use charges waived. Nothing is ever "Unlimited".
