## Decisions For Club Arena

Keep the approved icon in its existing HUD slot, B hotkey, private one-request full runout, visible price, VIP/pack rules and replay access. Competitors support the short optional interaction, but do not justify another per-street charge or a longer table pause.

Fix source-level continuity defects: stable identity and original slots for rabbit cards while only ghosts remain at next preflop; actual-card-bound visible count for a shortened completed board; one coherent disappearance for retained and live copies; opaque pending feedback with price retained; private hypothetical caption. Keep all live engine events and action clocks flowing.
