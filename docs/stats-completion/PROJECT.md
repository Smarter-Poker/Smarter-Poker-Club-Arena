# Club Arena Stats Completion Program

## Vision

Make `/stats` a secure, authoritative, club-aware poker analytics product. Every displayed value must identify its scope, source, coverage and confidence, and every actionable result must lead to authorized evidence.

## Users

- Players reviewing their own results across one or more clubs.
- Players viewing a deliberately limited shared-club profile comparison.
- Club agents and operators reviewing authorized rake/downline information.
- Platform operators reconciling facts, rollups and ledgers.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- Database authorization is the final access boundary.
- Horses and humans receive identical Stats treatment.
- Exact settlement and ledger values outrank reconstructed actions.
- No incompatible fallback may masquerade as the selected scope.
- No mock, stub or fabricated analysis may produce a user-facing success claim.
- Every phase is tested, committed, pushed, merged, published and production-verified before completion is reported.
- Club Arena publishes only through the protected Club Arena pull request and
  the Club Arena-owned Hetzner publisher; World Hub is routing only.

## Success

The program is complete only when the clean-schema, multi-club, accuracy, privacy, recovery, performance, accessibility and production E2E gates in Phase 10 pass against the deployed build.
