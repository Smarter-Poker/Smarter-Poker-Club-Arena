# Stats Completion Roadmap

## Phase 1 Of 10: Foundation, Truth And Access Boundary

Versioned metric/scope/quality contracts; reproducible production SQL; owner-only browser RPCs; incompatible fallback removal; explicit private cross-profile state; demo Assistant removal.

## Phase 2 Of 10: Exact Facts, Durability And Repair

Canonical settlement-derived money; fact outbox and retries; missing-fact reconciliation; duplicate/void/correction handling; exact/reconstructed coverage and historical repair controls.

## Phase 3 Of 10: Club-Scoped Aggregation Contract

Club-aware facts and daily/lifetime aggregates; authorized shared-club reads; consistent date/timezone semantics; range-correct tournaments; versioned caches and payloads.

## Phase 4 Of 10: Multi-Club Product Surface

All Clubs/club selector; sortable comparison; URL persistence; every panel, export and share artifact receives club/range scope; truthful per-section loading/error/coverage states.

## Phase 5 Of 10: Evidence And Navigation

Server-side evidence filters before cursor pagination; exact replay links; heatmap/session/rival/trophy/tournament drilldowns; stale-response guards; Back/Forward and scroll restoration.

## Phase 6 Of 10: Cash Intelligence

Opportunity-correct preflop/postflop metrics; stack/table/position/opponent filters; period and club comparisons; confidence and leak impact; lifetime achievements and anti-farming evidence.

## Phase 7 Of 10: Tournament, Session And Financial Reporting

Ledger-backed tournaments, sessions, rake, rakeback and bankroll; currency/unit separation; refunds, tickets, bounties and corrected results; session notes and overlap.

## Phase 8 Of 10: Assistant, Goals And Study Workspace

Persisted real analysis; evidence-linked leak lifecycle; goals and progress; study collections; saved/custom dashboards; controlled alerts and privacy presentation mode.

## Phase 9 Of 10: Experience, Accessibility And Operations

Distinct casino-realism interface; mobile/accessibility/browser completion; realtime/provisional state; degraded/offline behavior; exports; performance budgets; operator quality center and telemetry.

## Phase 10 Of 10: Certification And Production Proof

Clean-schema replay; historical backfill/reconciliation; database fixture suite; full tab/filter/club/export/Assistant E2E; hostile cache/network/auth testing; live production verification and final audit.
