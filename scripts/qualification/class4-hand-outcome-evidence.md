## Remaining rollback outcome boundary

A future private DB-generated rollback receipt would need owner-only insert authority at the actual atomic exception boundary, exact original request identity and DB hash, immutable event/receipt identity, replay/collision handling and canonical readback. A row created inside the failed subtransaction rolls back with it. A row created in the exception handler survives only if the outer RPC transaction commits. Earlier lease/validation refusals and later outer-wrapper failures are different outcomes. Response loss or an outer abort must remain unknown until a committed receipt is found. Generic financial-alert JSON cannot manufacture this authority. This separate design touches shared atomic/lease/accounting ownership and is not implemented by stage 1.

The two historical resolved originals remain byte-identical. Their absent current exact hand receipt is not proof of original loss, and their reported rollback is not relabelled as a successful commit. Cause investigation and original operational inbox disposition remain with the parent.
